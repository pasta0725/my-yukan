アーケロンは2,980円。友達は無料で遊べます。
2026年11月9日
アーケロンは11月28日(土)に早期アクセスを開始します。
価格は日本円で2,980円
、その他の地域では各地域に合わせた価格を設定します。
新作のPvPゲームでお金をいただくのが、どれだけハードルの高いことかは承知しています。最近の対戦ゲームはどれも基本プレイ無料のようですし、気軽に試せますからね。
しかし、基本プレイ無料のゲームでも、時間という投資は必要です。しかも、新しい対戦ゲームを覚えるには、とにかく膨大な時間がかかります。面白いゲームはかつてないほどあふれていますし、既にお気に入りのタイトルもあるはず。
あなたの時間には、2,980円とは比べものにならないほどの価値があります。
では、なぜ私たちは、アーケロンにはあなたの時間を費やす価値があると考えているのでしょうか？
私たちは何年もの歳月をかけて、アーケロンを自分たちが心から愛せる、手に汗握る対戦ゲームへと磨き上げてきました。戦闘はスピーディーで、至近距離での戦い、ひとつひとつの駆け引きが濃密です。すべての一撃に自ら狙いを定め、立ち位置や戦いの行方に気を配りながら、一瞬の判断を重ねていきます。相手が投げたスローイングナイフを跳ね返したり、致命傷を受ける寸前に姿を消したり、壁を飛び越えて向こう側のチーム全員を気絶させたり。そんな場面も生まれるでしょう。
試合のたびに自分だけのビルドを組み上げ、アイデアも駆使して相手を出し抜いていきます。スパイアーズで3vs3の戦いを制し、アセンションでほかの14チームを相手に生き残り、勝利するたびに、タワーの頂へ一歩ずつ近づきます。
私たちは、今のアーケロンにはこの価格に見合う価値があると信じています。みなさんがいつも遊ぶゲームの仲間入りを果たせるよう、ぜひチャンスをいただければうれしいです。そのためには、実際にアーケロンを手に取って、ご自身で判断していただくのが一番だと考えています。そこで、購入前にアーケロンをお試しいただける方法をいくつかご用意しました。
オープニングウィークエンド（11月28日(土)～30日(月)）
：どなたでもすべてのコンテンツを無料でプレイできます。制限なし、条件なし、購入も不要です。
フレンドパス（いつでも）
：無料のフレンドパスをダウンロードすれば、チュートリアルモードとトレーニングモードをプレイできます。さらに、アーケロンを購入した人のパーティに参加すれば、スパイアーズとアセンションも遊べます。
Discordパートナーサーバー
：対象のコミュニティでは、オープニングウィークエンドの終了後も、メンバーを期間限定の無料プレイに招待できます。
Xbox Game Pass
：加入者は、11月28日(土)から始まるXbox Game Previewの最初の3か月間、すべてのコンテンツをプレイできます。
フレンドパスのご紹介
対戦ゲームの好きなところのひとつは、友達と一緒に遊ぶと何倍も面白くなることです。覚えたい戦略、試したいビルド、みんなであれこれ研究したくなる反則級のコンボ。やることはいつだって尽きません。
こてんぱんにやられる試合もあります。かと思えば、命からがら戦闘を切り抜け、チーム全員を蘇生させて、なぜか最後には勝ってしまう試合も。勝っても負けても、そのすべてを分かち合い、あとで一緒に笑い合える仲間がいます。
とはいえ、友達に新しいゲームを試してもらうだけでも一苦労なのに、購入してもらうとなれば、なおさらです。
だからこそ、フレンドパスを作りました。
チームの誰か1人がアーケロンを購入していれば、全員で一緒に遊べます。
友達はフレンドパスをダウンロードしてチュートリアルモードとトレーニングモードをプレイし、そのあとゲームを購入した人とパーティを組めば、スパイアーズやアセンションを楽しめます。
もちろん、フレンドパスはクロスプレイに対応しています。Steam、PlayStation5、Xbox Series X|Sのどれで遊んでいても、みんなで一緒にプレイできます。
Discordパートナーサーバー：みんなで強くなろう
いいゲームギルドやコミュニティに入ったことがある方なら、それだけでゲーム体験がどれほど変わるかをご存じでしょう。始めたばかりで、どのアイテムの相性がいいのか誰かに教えてほしい、という方もいるはずです。あるいは、何百時間も遊んだ今でも、トッププレイヤーがどんなビルドを使っているかをDiscordでチェックしたり、新しい戦略を考察したり、調整される前に使い倒せる反則級のコンボを探したりしているかもしれません。自分がまだ気づいていないことを見つけている誰かが、必ずどこかにいるものです。

Join an Arkheron Partner Server. Play Free Through December 6.
Nov 18, 2026
When Arkheron launches, we want your Discord communities to come with you. 
We’re making it easier to find each other and party up in-game, and working with Partner Servers to give you and your friends more communities to join.
You probably already know which Discord you’d open first. Maybe it’s a small server with longtime friends who come with you to every new game. Maybe it’s your college gaming club, where you sneak in a few matches between cramming sessions for finals. Or a women’s gaming community where saying “hello” in voice chat doesn’t derail the entire lobby. Maybe you belong to all three, and which one you open depends on who you feel like playing with that night.
We believe everyone who enjoys Arkheron deserves to play with people they feel comfortable with, in a community where they belong. Players have already built those communities on Discord, with their own expectations for how they play together and treat each other. Here’s what we’re doing to make it easier to bring those communities into Arkheron and find more people you’ll enjoy playing with:
Bring your Discord communities into the game
. Connect your Discord account to find open parties in servers you already belong to, or make your own party available to other members.
Find a community through our Partner Servers
. We’re working with creators and gaming communities ready to welcome Arkheron players from day 1. Bring your friends, meet other players, and choose a community to represent in matches and upcoming server competitions.
Keep playing free after Opening Weekend
. Members of participating Partner Servers can play free November 30 - December 6, without needing someone who owns Arkheron in their party.
Connect your Discord communities to Arkheron
Connect your Discord account to Arkheron and add the servers you want to play with. From there, you can:
Open your party to your community.
 Let other members of a connected server know you’re looking for teammates so they can join you.
Find an open party
. See which parties have room for you in the social panel. An indicator on the main screen lets you know when someone in your communities has a party open to join.
You can add multiple servers, so you can find teammates in whichever community you feel like playing with that night. These features work on Steam, PlayStation 5, and XBOX Series X|S, with crossplay so you can team up across platforms.
Playing on Steam? You can get a party together from Discord, too
. With Discord activity sharing enabled, friends can see what you’re playing and join your party or custom lobby from your profile. You can also post a game invite in a Discord channel, so people can join while you’re all hanging out and chatting. If their game isn’t running, accepting the invite launches Arkheron and brings them into your party.
Introducing Partner Servers
We’re excited to introduce our Arkheron Partner Server program. We’re working with creators and gaming communities who are getting ready to welcome new players at Early Access launch, giving you and your friends more people to play with and learn from as you get into the game.
If you’ve ever gotten really into a game, you probably know the Discord where the patch notes are already being dissected before you’ve finished reading them. There are reaction videos, arguments about the nerfs, and confident predictions about the new meta from people who haven’t played a single match yet. A few channels over, someone’s rounding up players for customs, while others are hanging out after a stream and deciding whether to try the game themselves.
Our partners will include communities built around creators, college clubs, and other gaming groups. You might already follow a creator but have never played with anyone from their Discord. Or you and your friends might find a group that shares your sense of humor and enthusiasm for the game. You can jump in for a few matches, find people to practice with, or become one of the regulars helping new players find their footing.
Rep your server

rep
,” and its tag appears alongside your name in matches. When you need teammates, matchmaking will try to pair you with players of similar skill repping the same server.

Your favorite streamer thinks their community has the better players. You’ll have a chance to compete alongside other members for bragging rights and in-game cosmetic rewards. Something to show off after everyone’s done arguing about the results. We’ll share more about the competitions soon.

Arkheron Early Access Starts November 27: $24.99, a Free Opening Weekend, and Free Play Through December 6

Arkheron enters Early Access on Friday, November 27 for $24.99 in the US, and Bonfire Studios has stacked so many free ways in that you can play the full game at no cost from launch day through December 6 if you line them up right.
Launch:
 Friday, November 27, 2026 on Steam, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with crossplay. Bonfire hasn’t announced an unlock hour.
Price:
 $24.99 in the US, local pricing elsewhere. Supporter Edition is $49.99.
Free Opening Weekend:
 November 27 to 29, full game, no purchase.
Partner Server Free Play Week:
 November 30 to December 6, through Discord communities.
Also free:
 Friend Pass, and the full game on Xbox Game Pass for the first three months of Xbox Game Preview.
This is a debut game from 
Bonfire Studios
, the independent Orange County, California team founded by Rob Pardo. Arkheron is a fast, isometric PvP game where teams of three fight through the Tower, collect items that shape a new build each match, and take on rival teams. Bonfire names two modes: Ascension, where you survive against 14 other teams, and Spires, which is 3v3 fights. Tutorial, Training and Custom Games round it out.
Arkheron – Everything You Need To Know Before Early Access, on the 
LaserBolt channel
.
Why the $24.99 price is a real question
Let’s be honest about the pitch. A paid PvP game in a market full of free-to-play shooters is a hard sell, and Bonfire knows it. In its 
November 9 post
, the studio called charging for a new PvP game a big ask and argued that learning any competitive game costs you a lot of time, whatever the sticker says.
Its answer isn’t a discount. It’s a long list of ways to try the game before you spend anything, and a promise that cosmetics won’t buy power. Bonfire says no pay-to-win, no loot boxes and no gacha mechanics, and that every Collection, its version of a Battle Pass, will be earnable for free during Early Access.
One thing to weigh before you buy: Bonfire says it expects the price to go up as it adds content and gets closer to 1.0. If you like the game, the launch price is the lowest it’s likely to be. If you’d rather wait, the studio says that’s fine too.
Every way to play Arkheron for free
Here’s Bonfire’s own list, in the order you’ll hit it.
Opening Weekend, November 27 to 29.
 Everyone can play the full game free. Bonfire’s wording is no limits, no requirements, no purchase necessary.
Friend Pass, anytime.
 It’s a free download. On its own it lets you play Tutorial and Training solo. Party up with someone who owns Arkheron and you can play Spires, Ascension and Custom Games too. Only one person on the team needs to own the game, and Friend Pass supports crossplay across Steam, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
Partner Server Free Play Week, November 30 to December 6.
 Members of participating Partner Servers can play Ascension, Spires and Custom Games free, solo or in a team, without an owner in the party. Per Bonfire’s 
November 18 post
, you need to do three things: download the free Friend Pass Edition, join a participating Partner Server on Discord, and connect your Discord account to Arkheron. You don’t have to rep the server to qualify.
Xbox Game Pass.
 Subscribers get the full game for the first three months of Xbox Game Preview, starting November 27. Bonfire’s post doesn’t say which Game Pass tier that covers, so check your own plan before you count on it.
After December 6, regular Friend Pass rules return: Tutorial and Training solo, and the rest only alongside an owner.
Teams of three climb the Tower in Arkheron. Official screenshot, Bonfire Studios.
The free-through-December-6 math
Here’s the part we want to flag clearly, because it’s our reading of two separate announcements rather than a line Bonfire wrote. Opening Weekend covers November 27 to 29. Partner Server Free Play Week starts November 30 and runs through December 6. Put them back to back and a player who joins a participating server can play the full game free for the first 10 days of Early Access.
There’s a catch worth knowing. Bonfire says it will share the list of participating Partner Servers before launch, along with a step-by-step guide to joining a server and connecting it to Arkheron. That list isn’t out yet, so we can’t tell you which communities are in. The studio describes the partners as communities built around creators, college clubs and other gaming groups.
How Partner Servers and Discord fit in
Bonfire is leaning hard on Discord. Connect your account and you can find open parties in servers you already belong to, or open your own party to your community. A social panel shows which parties have room, and an indicator on the main screen tells you when someone in your communities has a party open. You can add multiple servers, and it works on Steam, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S with crossplay.
On Steam, Discord activity sharing lets friends join your party or custom lobby from your profile.
The Partner Server program adds an identity layer on top. Rep a server and its tag appears next to your name in matches, and matchmaking tries to pair you with similar-skill players repping the same server. Bonfire also plans competitions between Partner Servers, with in-game cosmetic rewards, and says more details are coming.
Supporter Edition and the Armored Swan bonus
The Supporter Edition costs $49.99 and includes the game plus two cosmetic packs, The Siren and The Night’s Advocate. Either pack is $14.99 separately. Bonfire says there won’t be a full cosmetic store at Early Access launch.
If you buy any edition from November 27 through November 30, you also get the Armored Swan pack free, including a special chroma exclusive to the Early Access opening. Bonfire says that chroma is gone forever after that window, and the rest of the pack goes back into the vault until it’s released for purchase later.
Which option is right for you
You just want to see if it clicks:
 play Opening Weekend, November 27 to 29. It’s the full game, no strings.
You have one friend who’ll buy it:
 everyone else grabs Friend Pass and parties up with them.
You want to keep going past the weekend with no purchase:
 download Friend Pass Edition, join a participating Partner Server, and connect Discord before November 30. Watch for Bonfire’s server list.
You’re a Game Pass subscriber:
 the full game is included for the first three months of Xbox Game Preview.
You already know you’re in:
 buy before the end of November 30 and take the Armored Swan chroma.
Early Access means unfinished, and Bonfire says so
Don’t go in expecting a polished live game. Bonfire is blunt that performance, stability and combat feel all need more work, and that Arkheron is still a long way from the competitive experience it wants at 1.0. The to-do list includes making the game easier to learn, making fights easier to read, adding depth to Ascension and Spires, and building out progression. The studio says it will take bugs and cheating seriously.
It’s also a small team. More Eternals are on the backlog, but Bonfire warns not to expect the update pace of an established live game right away. Before launch it has promised to reveal the Early Access Eternal roster, including a new Eternal, and Prologue, the start of the narrative arc with the first Collection and quests. Neither is detailed in what we’ve seen, so the full roster is still unknown.
Live at DreamHack Stockholm
Early Access also gets a live-event send-off. DreamHack’s owner, ESL FACEIT Group, announced that Arkheron will launch live at DreamHack Stockholm, November 27 to 29 at Stockholmsmässan, in 
a release published by Inven Global
. 
Dot Esports
 reported the same news.
Per the organiser, there’s a dedicated Arkheron HQ in Hall A with 84 PC stations all three days, with controller and mouse-and-keyboard play. First-timers get guided through their opening matches, and there will be PvP and mini tournaments. An Arkheron stage hosts developer panels and Q&As, creator showmatches and fans playing the devs, streamed live on Twitch and YouTube. A festival-wide quest in the DreamHack app sends visitors to four locations, including three themed photo booths, and a creator tournament runs at the Creator Hub.
Pardo is quoted in the release: “Arkheron is a game built to be played with friends, and there’s no better place to open the Tower than in a hall full of players.”

 Intel Core i5-9600K or AMD Ryzen 5 3600 or an equivalent 6-core, 8 GB RAM, and an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 6GB, AMD Radeon RX 580 8GB or RX 5500 XT 8GB.

 Intel Core i7-9700K or AMD Ryzen 7 3700X or an equivalent 8-core, 12 GB RAM, and an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070 8GB or AMD Radeon RX 7600 XT 16GB.
