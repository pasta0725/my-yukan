Wemade Connect Opens Pre-Registration for Lost Sword in Japan
YoungSeok "Lavii" Yang
lavii@inven.co.kr
Nov 17, 2026
Korean (한국어)
KO
Add us on
ⓒ Wemade Max
Lost Sword, a mobile subculture RPG published by Wemade Connect, opened pre-registration in Japan on November 18. Upon reaching 150,000 pre-registrations, the 5-star character 'Flame Emperor K' will be rewarded.
Wemade Max announced that its subsidiary Wemade Connect has opened pre-registration in Japan for Lost Sword, a mobile RPG developed by CodeCat. Pre-registration is available on the official Japanese brand website, and the official release date will be revealed later on the official site and social media channels.
Rewards will be provided in tiers based on the number of pre-registrations. Reaching 30,000 participants will grant 1,000 Diamonds, 50,000 will add 1,000,000 Gold, and 100,000 will award an additional 3,000 Diamonds. In addition, all promotion participants will receive the 'Feast of Flames' costume exclusive to 'Flame Emperor K.'
Lost Sword was first released in South Korea in January 2025. It later launched global versions including North America and Europe in July of the same year, followed by Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Macau in August, and is now gearing up for its Japanese release.
Wemade Connect plans to introduce the game to Japanese players through the pre-registration campaign and build an initial player base ahead of launch. By highlighting its key visuals and character design, the publisher aims to expand its reach with local players via the official brand site and social media channels.
CodeCat CEO Kim Je-heon said, "Building on the support we received from players in Korea and globally, we are delighted to introduce the unique action and charming characters of 'Lost Sword' to players in Japan," adding, "We will do our best to ensure the game becomes a long-beloved title through localization tailored for the Japanese market and reliable live service."
