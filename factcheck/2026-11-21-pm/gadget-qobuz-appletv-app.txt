QobuzがApple TV向けアプリ。最大192kHz/24bit、「リビングでのリスニング体験を想定」
酒井隆文
2026年11月20日 14:51
 Qobuzは、Apple TV向けアプリの配信を開始した。tvOS 26以降を搭載した第4世代以降のApple TVに対応し、最大192kHz/24bit再生をサポートする。 
 なお、第2世代Apple TV 4Kの場合、最大48kHz再生に制限される。 
 「リビングルームでのリスニング体験を想定して設計した」といい、ホームページにはミュージックチームによるセレクション、ニューリリース、アルバム・オブ・ザ・ウィーク、Qobuzissime(コバズィシム)、Qobuz名盤、レーベルページ、Qobuzプレイリストやラジオに加え、試聴履歴に基づいてパーソナライズされたおすすめやミックスが集約される。 
 アルバムページでは、アルバム情報とトラックリストが並べて表示され、一目で全体を確認できる。 
 サブスクリプション会員のライブラリ(アルバム、アーティスト、プレイリスト、お気に入り)は他のデバイスと同期される。「Qobuz Connect」を使えば、Apple TVで音楽を再生しながら、スマートフォンやタブレットから再生操作も可能。 
 Qobuzの最高製品責任者(CPO)であるアクセル・デスタニョール氏は「サブスクリプション会員の皆様からは、以前よりApple TV向けのQobuzアプリを望む声を多くいただいていました。これからはリビングルームの大画面向けに設計されたインターフェースで、ご自身のライブラリや編集部厳選のセレクション、そしてQobuzならではの高音質をお楽しみいただけます」とコメントしている。

Qobuz is now available on Apple TV
18 Nov
Written by 
Qobuz
The app brings the entire Qobuz catalog in lossless quality—from CD to Hi-Res—along with its editorial selections, to the big screen.
Paris, November 18, 2026 -
 Qobuz, the independent French music streaming and download platform, announces the launch of its app on Apple TV, available on the App Store for compatible models.In 2026, Qobuz introduced a redesigned player with synchronized lyrics and expanded access to recording credits and editorial content. The continued expansion of Qobuz Connect, now integrated by more than 100 audio partners, lets listeners stream directly to compatible audio equipment from the Qobuz apps, while its Android TV app brings the catalog to the big screen. 
Designed for living-room listening, the app showcases Qobuz’s music for streaming and editorial content. The homepage brings together editorial picks, New Releases, Albums of the Week, Qobuzissimes, Ideal Discography selections, label pages, Qobuz playlists and radios, as well as personalized recommendations and Mixes based on listening habits. Album pages display credits and tracklists side by side, so everything can be seen at a glance.
Track lyrics appear during playback, scrolling line by line in time with the music, complete with translations. Subscribers' libraries (albums, artists, playlists, and favorites) are synced across devices and Qobuz’ catalog is entirely lossless, with audio quality ranging from CD to Hi-Res. With Qobuz Connect, users can control playback from their smartphone or tablet while Apple TV streams the music.
"Our subscribers have been regularly asking us for a Qobuz app on Apple TV. Now, they can enjoy their library, our team's editorial selections, and Qobuz audio quality in their living room, within an interface designed for the big screen,"
 says 
Axel Destagnol, Chief Product Officer at Qobuz.
Compatibility and audio quality
Rolled out across all Qobuz countries, the app is compatible with Apple TV models from the 4th generation onward, running tvOS 26 or later. It supports audio playback up to 24-bit/192 kHz, with a 48 kHz limit on the 2nd-generation Apple TV 4K.

Independent music streaming service 
Qobuz
 has officially launched on 
Apple
 TV, and it’s bringing more than just hi-res music streams to the big screen.
Qobuz
 has reportedly designed this app for "living-room listening", with everything from its full catalogue of lossless quality tracks to its editorial picks and personalised recommendations based on your listening habits.
Qobuz curated playlists (which focus on human-led curation rather than algorithms) are also featured, as is the service's Qobuzissimes feature, which highlights new, up-and-coming songs and artists.

Thanks to expanded real estate that comes with viewing the service on a TV or projector, Qobuz presents album covers, tracklists, and credits side-by-side, meaning everything is now viewable at a glance.
Lyrics are presented on-screen and in sync with the track, meaning singing along is certainly possible (though we should stress, very much optional), and your Qobuz libraries (including tracks, albums, artists and playlists) will sync across devices, meaning any changes you make on the Apple TV app will reflect on your smartphone, tablet or computer.
Last, but by no means least, the app supports Qobuz Connect, meaning you can start playing music on a mobile device and then beam it to your Apple TV provided you're on the same wi-fi network.
What’s more, you can continue to control the music on your phone while the Apple TV handles playback.

The Qobuz app is available to download from the App Store now, although we should note that playback capabilities vary by model.
It's available on all Apple TVs that can run tvOS 26, with the 2022 and 2026 Apple TV 4K supporting playback up to 24-bit/192 kHz. The 2022 model is limited to 24-bit/48 kHz, which is a shame. Still, we're always pleased to see a new service join the Apple TV roster, especially when it focuses on high-quality music streaming.
