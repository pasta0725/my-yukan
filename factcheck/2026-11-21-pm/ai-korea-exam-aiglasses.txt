韓国大学入試で電子機器不正111件摘発　AI受験疑惑巡り死者も発生
公開
2026年11月20日 17時48分
[産経新聞]
印刷する
Googleで優先するソースとして追加
　韓国教育省は11月20日、19日に実施された大学修学能力試験（修能、日本の大学入学共通テストに相当）で、スマートグラス（AI眼鏡）など電子機器の扱いを巡り111件の不正行為が確認されたと明らかにした。
韓国の国旗
　教育省によると、試験中のAI眼鏡や小型カメラ、スマートフォンの使用で5件摘発された。受信を拒否できない緊急災害アラートを発する不正対応策で確認された電子機器の持ち込みは54件あった。
　19日にはソウル市内の宿泊施設で、教育系インフルエンサーとして活動し、法科大学院進学に必要な適性試験でAI機器を使用した不正受験疑惑が浮上していた男性の死亡も確認された。警察当局は解剖の結果、事件性は確認されなかったとしている。
　眼鏡に代表されるAI機器の普及で、不正のハードルは急速に下がりつつある。記者が中国製のAI眼鏡を着用し、2025年の修能で出題された制限時間100分の数学問題に挑むと、答案作成は30分余りで完了。誤答は1問のみだった。
　朝鮮日報は20日の社説で、「事実上すべての試験が不信を持たれる状況」にあると指摘。「急変するAI時代において暗記中心、5択中心の試験制度を維持するのか、根本的な議論を始める必要がある」と訴えた。（ソウル＝時吉達也）

 SEOUL, Nov. 20 (Yonhap) -- A total of 111 cases of alleged cheating involving electronic devices took place during the annual national college entrance exam this week, the education ministry said Friday, sharply raising the final tally from the 98 announced the previous day.
 Of the total, 54 cases were identified after the government sent disaster alerts to mobile phones nationwide as part of strengthened anti-cheating measures for the College Scholastic Ability Test (CSAT) on Thursday, according to the ministry.
A smartphone shows a state-issued disaster alert sent to detect any unsubmitted phones during the annual college entrance exam as Vice Education Minister Choi Eun-ok holds a briefing at the government complex in Sejong on Nov. 19, 2026. (Yonhap)
 Authorities also found 52 cases through other methods, such as by detecting Wi-Fi signals, while five were caught red-handed using smart devices.
 The government stepped up measures to root out cheating this year following widespread public outrage over the recent confession of the top scorer of this year's tax accountant exam that he had cheated with the help of artificial intelligence (AI) and digital devices.
 Across three disaster alerts that rang loud alarms even for muted devices, authorities detected 31 cases in the first alert, 11 in the second and 12 in the third.
 Among those caught red-handed, a test taker in South Chungcheong Province used smart glasses, while a test taker in Gyeonggi used a miniature camera and wireless earphones.
 An education ministry official said its Thursday tally of 98 was a tentative figure of those caught through the fourth period of the five-period exam.
 The ministry, which has warned of actively seeking criminal punishment against AI-assisted cheaters, plans to determine the level of punishment by examining each of the cases.
 The government has said it seeks to ban test takers caught cheating with AI from taking the CSAT again for up to 10 years.

111 Cases of Cheating Detected... Pan-Government TF Launches Full Operations
Jung Banseok

  [Anchor] 
 It has been additionally tallied that 111 cases of individuals possessing or utilizing smart devices were caught during yesterday's (November 19) College Scholastic Ability Test (CSAT). The government fully launched a pan-government task force to respond to CSAT cheating this afternoon. 
 Reporting from reporter Jung Banseok. 
 [Reporter] 
 The Ministry of Education announced that a total of 111 cases of cheating were detected during yesterday's CSAT. 
 This is an increase of 13 cases from the 98 cases reported as of yesterday evening. 
 First, 54 cases involving smart devices were caught following emergency disaster text messages that went off three times. 
 There were 31 cases during the first alert before the start of the first period, and 23 cases from two text alerts sent in the afternoon. 
 Apart from the dispatch of disaster text messages, a total of 52 smart devices were caught on-site. 
 Cases where smart devices were utilized during the test totaled 5 cases, comprising 1 case involving smart glasses, 1 case involving a small camera and wireless earphones, 1 case of photographing test questions with a smartphone, and 2 cases of smartphone usage inside the restroom. 
 The Ministry of Education, together with the Korea Institute for Curriculum and Evaluation, plans to determine final disciplinary levels through a cheating deliberation committee depending on the severity of each case. 
 The pan-government CSAT cheating response TF, formed under the chairmanship of Prime Minister Han Sung-sook, held its first meeting this afternoon at the Seoul Government Complex. 
 Prime Minister Han stated that all individuals who engaged in cheating will have their CSAT exams nullified this year, and that the severity of their misconduct will be closely examined to pursue additional administrative and criminal responsibilities. 
 [Han Sung-sook / Prime Minister : Starting today, we are launching the CSAT Cheating Response TF. We will mobilize all pan-government capabilities to thoroughly uncover cheating that occurred in this CSAT and take strict measures in accordance with laws and principles.] 
 It was stated that suspicious Wi-Fi and Bluetooth signals recorded and stored at the test sites yesterday would be analyzed, and cases suspected of cheating would be requested for police investigation. 
 Minister of Education Choi Kyo-jin, who had recently kept a low profile and avoided official appearances amid successive controversies, also attended today's meeting.
