Shopping with AI is becoming less of a futuristic idea and more of something companies expect us to do. Walmart is the latest retailer making that shift, teaming up with Meta to let customers shop its Black Friday deals through Muse, Meta’s AI agent.
The partnership, 
announced
 Tuesday, gives shoppers another way to find products, compare prices, and make purchases without browsing Walmart’s website themselves. Instead, customers can simply tell Muse what they are looking for and let the AI do the work.
The announcement highlights how differently Walmart and Amazon are approaching AI shopping. While 
Amazon
 has taken steps to stop third-party AI agents from making purchases for customers, Walmart is leaning into the idea. 
Image Credits:
Walmart
To get started, users open the Muse app, sign in to their Walmart account, and return to the assistant to begin shopping. They can ask it to find specific products, compare options, or track down deals. Once a customer makes a request in Muse, the assistant sends it to Walmart, which returns relevant products and helps build a shopping cart.
Walmart says it has built privacy protections into the experience. For instance, Muse doesn’t get access to a customer’s Walmart shopping history or other personal information. Shoppers can also set spending limits, require approval before purchases, and review orders placed by the agent. 
Plus, the company notes users can always delete their agent’s profile if they want to.
The experience is powered by Walmart’s Agent Gateway, a new system that allows outside AI assistants to connect directly with the retailer’s online store. This 
launched
 in October, and the company is planning to open it up to more developers and AI platforms, meaning Muse could eventually be just one of several assistants capable of shopping Walmart’s online store.
Walmart is not the only company teaming up with Meta’s Muse. Instacart also 
recently joined forces
 with the AI assistant.
Beyond Muse, Walmart has recently rolled out more AI-powered features to its own shopping assistant, Sparky. 
Some of the
 newer additions 
include turning handwritten shopping lists into shopping carts, using photos and voice commands to find products, and helping customers get around their local store. Shoppers can search for an item and use a pin on the store map to see exactly which aisle it’s in. There’s also a “Flash Price Tag” button that makes the item’s shelf label light up.

Walmart will now let Meta’s Muse shop on your behalf, allowing the AI agent to find and purchase Black Friday deals via Walmart’s Sparky AI assistant. The retail giant is rolling out a live implementation of the 
Agent Gateway it announced last month
, which helps AI agents find relevant products for you without sharing your purchase history, according to Walmart.

AI agents are ready to shop. Some retailers are wary
Muse
 can make purchases through Walmart’s “Agent Profiles,” which provide a dedicated space for agent activity within your Walmart customer account. These profiles also allow you to set spending limits, approve purchases, and check the purchase history of everything bought by Muse. While other online retailers like 
Amazon
 and 
eBay
 have blocked AI agents from browsing their platforms, Walmart says that support for Meta’s Muse is “just the beginning,” and that additional Walmart shopping experiences for other AI agents and apps are “on the way.”
Here are Walmart’s instructions for shopping its website via Muse:
1. Tell Muse what you need.
 For example, ask it to find a great TV deal or show you which Walmart Black Friday deals are available for delivery. Muse can help you explore products and build your basket.
2. Set your shopping rules.
 Muse will take you to Walmart to sign in, then bring you back to Muse. The first time you add an item to your cart, you’ll set up your Agent Profile on Walmart within your Walmart account, including a spending limit you’re comfortable with.
3. Check out.
 Complete your purchase within the conversation using a card saved in your Walmart account, with the same Walmart prices you’d find on Walmart.com. And if something isn’t quite right, Walmart’s return policy still applies.

Black Friday shopping has come a long way from lining up outside stores before sunrise. This year, there’s a new way to shop Walmart’s deals: Put an AI agent to work.
Starting today, customers can shop Walmart’s Black Friday deals directly through 
Muse
, Meta’s personal AI agent, which can help them find items, build a basket and complete their purchase.
Powered by the 
Walmart Agent Gateway
, this new Walmart shopping experience is a big step forward for shopping with AI, bringing product discovery and purchasing in one experience.
Your shopping list. Your Muse. Your rules.
Imagine asking Muse to find a Black Friday TV deal under $500, track down the season’s hottest toys or help you check off everyone on your holiday gift list. Instead of searching for each item yourself, Muse connects to the Walmart Agent Gateway, where it passes your shopping list to Walmart. Walmart then does the shopping, finding products that match your request.
You’ll find Walmart’s Every Day Low Prices and Black Friday deals, along with the membership benefits you’re used to, like free delivery.
The Walmart Agent Gateway lets you decide what your agent can and can’t do.
For more than 60 years, Walmart has worked to earn customers’ trust by helping them save money and live better. The Walmart Agent Gateway brings that same commitment to AI shopping, helping agents find relevant Walmart products without sharing customers’ broader purchase history with others.
And with Agent Profiles, you’re in control. You can set spending limits, approve purchases and check your purchase history to see which purchases were made by your agent.
How to shop Walmart with Muse — including our Black Friday Deals
Tell Muse what you need.
 For example, ask it to find a great TV deal or show you which Walmart Black Friday deals are available for delivery. Muse can help you explore products and build your basket.
Set your shopping rules.
 Muse will take you to Walmart to sign in, then bring you back to Muse. The first time you add an item to your cart, you’ll set up your Agent Profile on Walmart within your Walmart account, including a spending limit you’re comfortable with.
Check out.
 Complete your purchase within the conversation using a card saved in your Walmart account, with the same Walmart prices you’d find on Walmart.com. And if something isn’t quite right, Walmart’s return policy still applies.
Black Friday is changing. Walmart is ready.
Customers have more ways to shop Black Friday than ever before. Some love browsing the aisles. Others prefer searching Walmart.com, asking Sparky for help or putting an agent like Muse to work. This year, 
Sparky can do even more for shoppers
 — like turning a handwritten list into a shopping cart, finding their way through the store and using images and voice to find what you need.
However customers choose to shop, they can count on the same great prices, assortment, convenience and trusted experience they expect from Walmart.
And Muse is just the beginning. The Walmart Agent Gateway will make it possible to shop Walmart through more apps and agents, with additional experiences on the way.
This Black Friday, you can hunt for the deals in stores and online, as well as with Sparky, Muse and agents. Either way, Walmart is ready to help you save.
