臼田勤哉
2026年11月17日 10:39
 音声AIのElevenLabs(イレブンラボ)は17日、日本の音声AIスタートアップKotoba Technologiesの買収を発表した。ElevenLabsにとって日本で初の買収となり、Kotobaの従業員15名がElevenLabsに加わる。買収により、日本における音声エージェントと音声インターフェースの普及拡大を目指す。買収完了は、2026年第4四半期後半を見込んでいる。 
 ElevenLabsは、AI音声・オーディオモデルを中心に、クリエイティブツールと、営業、カスタマーサポートなどの対話型エージェントを手掛ける企業。Kotobaの買収については、音声分野の研究力と日本市場への深い理解を兼ね備えた「理想的なパートナー」としている。 
 Kotobaが開発する音声モデル「Koto」は、日本語の音声合成(Text-to-Speech)、音声認識(Speech-to-Text)、同時通訳のための音声間変換(Speech-to-Speech)に優れ、グローバルな大手企業での採用が進んでいることを評価。Salesforceの「Agentforce Voice」では日本語での会話で採用されているという。両社の研究を結集することで、世界の多くの企業が日本語の複雑な会話にも対応できるエージェントを構築できるようにする。 
 また今回の買収は、日本展開強化の一環としており、今後数カ月で、国内の営業責任者の体制を拡充し、日本でのインフラ投資を継続していく。

We’re excited to announce that ElevenLabs is acquiring Kotoba, the leading Japanese voice AI lab. Together, we’ll accelerate the adoption of AI agents and voice interfaces in the world’s fourth-largest economy. 
The way we speak and interact with each other is deeply local. We talk in different languages, dialects, and accents, and our behavior is governed by a complex, often unstated, mix of local social and cultural norms. 
There are few places where local nuance is more important than in Japan. Sentences don’t always contain a subject and an object, and vocabulary changes depending on who you’re speaking to. Apologies are common because they carry little liability, and pauses signal thought rather than awkwardness. Praise is typically deflected, and saying “no” directly is often avoided. Japanese even has a phrase, 
kūki o yomu
, or “reading the air,” which means people are expected to pick up what isn’t said.
Our goal is to solve how people interact with AI in every language and culture. That means building AI that feels natural and intuitive across different cultures and contexts, so everyone can access and benefit from it.
With deep voice research and knowledge of the Japanese market, the Kotoba team is a natural partner in this mission. The company was founded by AI researchers Noriyuki Kojima and Jungo Kasai in 2023, with the aim of developing voice AI for a multilingual world. As 
they put it
, “Speech shouldn't stop where language does.”
“To bring the value of AI to every person, it needs to be natural and intuitive to interact with,” said
 Mati Staniszewski, co-founder and CEO of ElevenLabs
. “The language people speak and the social norms they follow need to be reflected in the technology’s architecture from the very start. Kotoba has done exactly that for Japan, building state-of-the-art voice models that have already been adopted by some of the world’s leading enterprises. Together, we can take that even further together and build the full interaction layer for AI in Japan.”
“When we founded Kotoba, our goal was to build a frontier AI lab from Japan,” said
 Noriyuki Kojima, co-founder and CEO of Kotoba Technologies, Inc
. “Along the way, we’ve seen the enormous potential of voice AI, and three years later, we’re more determined than ever to build technology that benefits society. I’m incredibly excited for Kotoba to join ElevenLabs. Together, we can accelerate the development and adoption of voice AI, bringing tangible benefits to people and businesses in Japan and beyond.”
Kojima, who goes by Nori, holds a PhD in computer science from Cornell. He worked as a research scientist at Hugging Face, and co-built Japan’s Fugaku-LLM, an open-source model trained from scratch on the RIKEN Fugaku supercomputer. Kasai holds a PhD in computer science and engineering from the University of Washington, and was an assistant professor at Toyota Technological Institute at the University of Chicago. He is one of Japan’s most-cited researchers.
“Kotoba’s team has done exceptional research on the Japanese language,” said
 Piotr Dąbkowski, co-founder and CTO of ElevenLabs
. “It is an incredibly intricate language that poses a whole range of technical challenges when building voice models. Bringing their research together with ours will improve Japanese across the entire stack ElevenLabs builds.”
“I’m incredibly excited for Kotoba and ElevenLabs to join forces and lead the next wave of voice AI in Japan and East Asia,” said
 Jungo Kasai, co-founder and CTO of Kotoba Technologies, Inc
. “Since I began my AI research career, I’ve dreamed of building a frontier AI research lab in Tokyo and creating products that transform people’s lives, from voice AI agents to simultaneous translation. Our journey is just beginning.”
Kotoba’s voice model, Koto, excels in Japanese text-to-speech, speech-to-text, and speech-to-speech for simultaneous interpretation. Several major global enterprises already use Koto, including Salesforce, where it powers Japanese-language conversations in Agentforce Voice. With Kotoba joining ElevenLabs, more of the world’s leading companies will be able to build agents that handle complex conversations in Japanese naturally. 
“Voice is emerging as a powerful new interface for AI, with potential to transform how people interact with technology,” said 
Nowi Kallen, a partner at Salesforce Ventures
. “We’ve been proud to back both ElevenLabs and Kotoba, and together, they’re uniquely positioned to bring more natural, intuitive AI experiences to businesses and consumers worldwide.”
This is just the first step in our long-term plan to make AI intuitive and trusted for everyday interactions across Japan. In the coming months, we plan to build out our local sales leaders and continue to invest in infrastructure in Japan. With Kotoba as part of ElevenLabs, we look forward to partnering even more closely with Japanese enterprises, and multinationals operating and expanding in Japan.
The acquisition is expected to close later in Q4 2026, subject to customary regulatory approvals and closing conditions.

Kotoba Is Joining ElevenLabs
Kotoba Technologies is joining ElevenLabs. Together, we will build the future of voice AI from Tokyo, accelerating research and product development.
Since day 1 of Kotoba, our ambition has always been to build a frontier AI lab in Japan and bring voice AIto the world. Joining ElevenLabs marks a major step toward that goal, and our entire team is excited to begin this next chapter.
A Shared Vision for the Future of Voice AI
ElevenLabs was founded in 2022 by Polish entrepreneurs Mati Staniszewski and Piotr Dąbkowski. Drawing on their respective backgrounds at Palantir and Google, they set out to help people share their voices and express themselves across language barriers. They have since built a global leader in voice generation and conversational AI.
What resonated with us was ElevenLabs’ deep commitment to research and product development, alongside its vision of making interactions between people and AI more natural through voice—and opening up new possibilities for society.
Everyone should be able to interact naturally with AI in their own language. Making that possible takes teams who understand the nuances of local languages and cultures, respond to regional needs, and move quickly to build exceptional technology and products. Connecting research rooted in that local understanding with resources on a global scale is exactly the approach Kotoba has pursued.
Accelerating “from Research to Products” 
Kotoba was founded in July 2023 by two AI researchers, Dr. Noriyuki Kojima and Dr. Jungo Kasai. With our speech foundation model, Koto, at the core of our work, we have focused on turning research into products people use.
Our work spans Japanese-language voice agents for Salesforce’s Agentforce Voice, voice AI powering products at major companies in both Japan and the United States, simultaneous interpretation technology supporting artists such as YOASOBI, and our simultaneous interpretation app, Kotoba. These efforts have put us at the forefront of voice AI.
Along the way, we have seen both the promise of voice AI and the challenges of making it work in practice. Conversations leave subjects unstated. Levels of politeness shift depending on who is speaking to whom. Brief acknowledgments and pauses carry meaning. For voice AI to feel natural in everyday life and at work, accurate speech recognition and generation are only the beginning. It must also understand context, follow the flow of a conversation, and know when to respond.
Accelerating the Path from Research to Real-World Use
Joining ElevenLabs brings together Kotoba’s expertise in East Asian language research and product development with ElevenLabs’ research capabilities, computing resources, and global business infrastructure. With these resources, we will improve voice AI across East Asian languages, starting with Japanese and extending to Korean and others, while accelerating its adoption in products and services.
For businesses, this will create more opportunities to bring voice agents capable of handling complex Japanese conversations—and technology that bridges language barriers—into everyday operations. Drawing on our understanding of Japanese language, culture, and customer needs, the Kotoba team will continue to work across research, product development, and deployment support. As a frontier AI lab with global ambitions, we will also contribute our research to the models and products ElevenLabs delivers worldwide.
Building the Future of Voice AI in Japan, for the World
We want to help strengthen Japan’s role as a center for cutting-edge voice AI research and development. By collaborating with researchers around the world and drawing on large-scale computing resources, we will bring insights from real-world use in Japan into our models and products. Our next challenge is to deliver the benefits of that work to people everywhere.
This next chapter shows a path for an AI startup based in Japan to develop distinctive technology, join a global leader, and continue its research and product development on a much larger scale. We hope it offers researchers and entrepreneurs in Japan another model for growth—and another way to bring their technology to the world.

いいね！数を読み込み中です
音声基盤モデルを開発するAIリサーチ・プロダクト企業のKotoba Technologies（本社：米国サンフランシスコ、研究開発拠点：東京都千代田区、共同創業者兼CEO：小島熙之、以下「Kotoba」）は本日、AIリサーチ・プロダクト企業のElevenLabs（本社：米国ニューヨーク）による買収に合意したことをお知らせします。本件はElevenLabsにとって日本で初めての買収です。Kotobaの従業員15名はElevenLabsに加わり、日本における音声エージェントおよび音声インターフェースの普及を加速させます。
Kotoba チーム
■ 背景：日本語に根ざした音声AIの必要性
 人と人とのコミュニケーションのあり方は、言語や文化によって形づくられ、それぞれの地域に深く根ざしています。日本語では主語や目的語が省略されることが多く、話す相手によって言葉遣いが変わります。聞き手には、言葉にされない部分を文脈から汲み取ることが求められます。
Kotobaは2023年の創業以来、こうした日本語の特性に向き合い、音声分野の研究を重ねてきました。あらゆる言語と文化においてAIとの対話を自然なものにすることを目指すElevenLabsと、Kotobaが持つ音声研究の知見および日本市場への深い理解が組み合わさることで、このミッションの実現を大きく前進させます。
■ 音声モデル「Koto」について
 Kotobaの音声モデル「Koto」は、日本語の音声合成（Text-to-Speech）、音声認識（Speech-to-Text）、そして同時通訳向けの音声間変換（Speech-to-Speech）において高い性能を発揮します。Kotoはすでにグローバルな大手企業に採用されており、Salesforceでは「Agentforce Voice」の日本語による会話を支えています。
今後は両社の研究を結集し、世界をリードするより多くの企業が、日本語の複雑な会話に対応できるエージェントを構築できるよう支援していきます。
■ 今後の展開
本買収は、日本における日常のあらゆる対話でAIを直感的に使え、信頼できるものにするというElevenLabsの長期的な計画の第一歩となります。ElevenLabsは今後数か月のうちに国内の営業リーダー体制を整え、日本でのインフラ投資も引き続き進める予定です。KotobaはElevenLabsの一員として、日本企業や、日本で事業を展開・拡大するグローバル企業との連携をこれまで以上に深めてまいります。
 2023年の創業以来、多くの投資家の皆さま、お客様、パートナーの皆さまに支えられてまいりました。これまでいただいたご支援に、心より御礼申し上げます。ElevenLabsへの参画後も、Kotobaの挑戦は続きます。今後とも変わらぬご支援を賜りますよう、よろしくお願い申し上げます。
