Google is putting AI chats right on the homepage
You can now view your recent AI searches by opening the sidebar on Google’s homepage.

is a news writer who covers the streaming wars, consumer tech, crypto, social media, and much more. Previously, she was a writer and editor at MUO.
Google is updating its homepage to include AI searches. In 
a post on X
, Google’s vice president of product, Robby Stein, shows how you can now open a collapsible sidebar to view and continue your AI chats.
Based on the GIF shared by Stein, it looks like you can open the sidebar by clicking the gray bar that appears on the left side of your screen. From there, Google will show a list of the searches you’ve made using AI Mode, along with the ability to start a new thread, search your threads, or connect a Gemini Notebook.
It’s yet another big change for Google’s homepage, which has long presented you with a single bar to kick off your web search. The company brought its 
AI Mode button
 to its search bar last year, which opens up a chatbot-like search experience within Google. In May, 
Google updated
 its search box to accommodate longer queries and added AI-powered autocomplete.
Stein says Google is rolling out the option because “people are coming to Search with more questions that aren’t just one and done,” adding that “in testing, this sidebar made a big difference for making progress & continuing to explore the web.” The new homepage is rolling out globally on desktop.

Google homepage rolling out AI-focused redesign that includes your Gemini chats [Video]
Ben Schoon
 | Nov 19 2026 - 2:25 pm PT																
								5 Comments							
The Google Search homepage is embracing Gemini in a big way, with a new sidebar redesign that puts your chat history just a click away.
Google’s AI ambitions have been anything but subtle in their integration with Search, but Gemini and Search have had some clear separations. But, with its latest update, Google is very much intertwining the two. 
Confirmed by Google’s Robby Stein 
on Twitter/X
, Google is now rolling out a desktop homepage revamp that gives Gemini near-top billing. 
At first glance, you’ve got the typical blank canvas with a Google logo and Search bar at the center, but the new changes start with Gemini-powered shortcuts underneath for actions such as creating images, analyzing files, and “brainstorming” with Gemini. 
Advertisement - scroll for more content
These replace the existing “Google Search” button.
Beyond that, a new sidebar pulls your Gemini chat history and tasks into the Search homepage as well as including a “New chat” shortcut and everything else you’d find in the Gemini desktop app/site. The AI-heavy adjustment is very perhaps one of the biggest embraces of AI in Search since Google launched AI Overviews and AI Mode. 
Stein explains this change is because users are coming to Search with questions that aren’t “one and done.”
People are coming to Search with more questions that aren’t just one and done – and in testing, this sidebar made a big difference for making progress & continuing to explore the web.
The new Google Search homepage is now rolling out globally, Stein says, though we’re not seeing it live just yet on our devices. Sound off in the comments if you’re seeing the new look and whether or not you like this functionality.

Google は 2026 年 11 月 20 日、デスクトップ版の Google 検索のホームページに、最近の AI モードの検索を探して再開できる折りたたみ式のサイドバーを追加したことを

検索には 1 回の質問で終わらない質問を持ち込む人が増えており、テストではサイドバーによって検索を続けやすくなるといった違いがあったとしています。
