The Binding of Isaac: Repentance+
2026/11/20 12:12
ニュース
「The Binding of Isaac: Repentance+」，現世代コンシューマ機向けに発売。10年以上の進化を凝縮したローグライクアクションの集大成
ライター：奥谷海人
　インディーデベロッパのNicalisは，ローグライクアクション
「The Binding of Isaac: Repentance+」
を
Switch2
 / 
PS5
 / 
XBOX Series X|S
向けに
発売
した。なお，日本国内での販売は確認できていない。
　コンシューマ機ではシリーズ初となる
最大4人のオンライン協力プレイ
に対応し，友人とのプレイはもちろん，知らないプレイヤーとのマッチングやクイックマッチも楽しめるようになった。
「The Binding of Isaac」
は，母親から逃れるために地下室へと潜り込んだ少年
アイザック
を主人公に，ランダム生成されるダンジョンを探索するローグライクアクションだ。
涙を武器
に異形の敵と戦いながら，さまざまなアイテムを集めて能力を強化し，何度も挑戦を繰り返していく。
エドモンド・マクミレン（Edmund McMillen）氏
が生み出したシリーズは，2014年発売の
「The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth」
以降も拡張を重ねてきた。
　今回の「The Binding of Isaac: Repentance+」は，
「Rebirth」
の本編に加えて，
「Afterbirth」「Afterbirth+」「Repentance」
の各拡張コンテンツと，「Repentance+」で新たに用意されたコンテンツを収録した，集大成版ともいえるバンドルパッケージだ。
　700種類以上のアイテム，34人のプレイアブルキャラクター，330種類以上の敵，120種類以上のボスやミニボスが登場。アイテムの効果をゲーム内で確認できる説明機能も追加されている。また，ローカルでも最大4人での協力プレイに対応する。
　PS5とXBOX Series X|Sでは，既存の
「The Binding of Isaac: Repentance」
の所有者向けに9.99ドルの有料アップグレードが用意されている。Switch2では，59.99ドルのダウンロード版と69.99ドルのパッケージ版が販売されるほか，対応する既存作品の所有者向けに，同じく9.99ドルの有料アップグレードが提供されるという。なお，Switch2のパッケージ版は，ゲームキーカードではなく，ゲームデータを収録した通常のゲームカードとなっている。
　一方，PC（Steam）版「The Binding of Isaac: Repentance+」は，2024年11月から「The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth」の無料DLCとして配信されている。
　さらに，今後のアップデートでは，Steam版のセーブデータをコンシューマ機版に引き継げるようになるほか，PCを含む異なるプラットフォーム間でのオンライン協力プレイを可能にするクロスプレイ機能も実装される予定だ。
　過去にはMac，Switch，PS4，XBOX One，iPhone向けにもリリースされ，発売から10年以上にわたって進化を続けてきた「The Binding of Isaac」が，より幅広いプレイヤーに楽しまれるようになるのは間違いないだろう。

IT’S DANGEROUS TO GO ALONE: 
THE BINDING OF ISAAC: REPENTANCE+
 HITS NINTENDO SWITCH 2, PLAYSTATION 5 AND XBOX SERIES X|S
Online Console Play For Up To Four Players For The First Time, Cross-Saves, New Item Descriptions And 
Hundreds Of Additions And Improvements
Nov. 19, 2026 —
 Today, Nicalis officially announced that 
The Binding of Isaac: Repentance+
 is has launched for Nintendo Switch 2, Sony PlayStation 5 and Microsoft Xbox Series X|S. PlayStation and Xbox Players can purchase a DLC expansion to upgrade 
The Binding of Isaac: Repentance 
to 
Repentance+ 
while Nintendo Switch 2 owners have the option to purchase a digital or physical version of the complete game.
See the launch trailer for 
The Binding of Isaac: Repentance
+
: 
https://youtu.be/Fe2hQIWjOpo
The new online functionality multiplies the mayhem by a factor of four. "That's kind of the magic of 
Isaac
, just watching everything go horribly wrong,” 
says 
Isaac
 and The Game Awards nominated 
Mewgenics! 
designer and creator Edmund McMillen. 
“Now you can do that with up to four friends online, assuming you have more than one.. :|"
Since 
The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth 
launched in 2014, its mix of dungeon crawling, twin-stick shooter action and randomized replayability has made the 
Isaac
 series a landmark gaming experience for millions of players. 
Repentance+
 includes hundreds of additions and improvements that have been implemented into the game since the original 
Repentance
 DLC expansion launched in 2021. Over 700 different items to collect, 34 unique playable characters, more than 330 individual enemy types, 120+ terrifying, deadly bosses and mini-bosses. 
Additional features include:
Online Co-op In Every Mode For Up To Four Players: Play online with friends (if you have any), start or join a match with strangers, or jump into a Quick Match with online players chosen at random. 
Repentance+
 also has four-player local support for nights when friends join you on the couch.
Cross Saves (coming in post-launch update): Players will be able to import their 
TBOI
 Steam save data. This is a huge benefit for 
Isaac
 PC players who want to play on console. 
Crossplay (coming in post-launch update): The 
Repentance+
player base will be able to play together regardless of platform, including on PC where the game saw over 150,000 current daily players in 2026.
Item Descriptions: This new feature helps players with the hundreds of items in the game, making it easier to maximize each run. No more having to keep the 
Isaac
 wiki open on a tab on your phone to remember what an item does!
The standalone Nintendo Switch 2 version of 
The Binding of Isaac: Repentance+
 is available now in both digital ($59.99) and physical ($69.99) versions. The physical version comes on a standard Game Card (a real cartridge that doesn’t have to be downloaded); it includes a reversible cover sheet insert, an instruction manual and a PVC Isaac and Guppy strap. 
Repentance+
 is also available as a $9.99 downloadable expansion (DLC) update for players who own 
The Binding of Isaac: Repentance
 on Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S.

and Studio Pixel’s 
Cave Story
. Nicalis specializes in bringing distinctive gaming experiences to audiences worldwide across PC and console platforms.

What Carries Over, and What Does Not
Owners of the 2021 console version.

PC players.

Crossplay.

Online subscriptions.

's way – which, for most of us players, has meant a whole lot of new games to play in September and October and very few to choose from in November. One of the few brave enough to take on GTA 6 head-to-head is Binding of Isaac developer Edmund McMillen, and he's not 
too
 worried about going up against the titan.
The Binding of Isaac: Repentance+ is a free DLC expansion that launched on PC back in 2024, and is now coming to consoles on November 19, the same day that GTA 6 launches. Apparently, this was the idea of publisher Nicalis, and McMillen agreed that it would, if nothing else, be pretty funny, as he tells

"There's a possibility [Repentance+'s release date] wasn't that stupid an idea," McMillen says, "and if it was a stupid idea, it's a funny stupid idea." But he does have experience with having a game's thunder stolen by a much bigger competitor, as was the case when he launched strategy roguelike Mewgenics just a few weeks ahead of roguelike deckbuilder Splay the Spire 2 earlier this year.

"My heart says it doesn't matter, but my brain has shown me it does," he says. "When Mewgenics came out it blew up; we went past two million sales almost right off the bat.
