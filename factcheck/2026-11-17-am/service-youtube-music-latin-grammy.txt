This week, Las Vegas became the global capital of Latin music and YouTube touched down with special initiatives championing diversity, innovation and celebrating the artists transforming global culture. Here’s a look at how we celebrated the week’s biggest moments.
Sandra Jimenez makes history
YouTube’s Regional Director & Head of Music for LATAM & US Latin was honored as one of the Latin Recording Academy’s Leading Ladies of Entertainment 2026, becoming the first Brazilian woman ever to receive this distinction.
Fireside chat with Daddy Yankee
As an official sponsor of the 2026 Person of the Year Gala, YouTube hosted an intimate conversation with the “Big Boss,” revisiting his historic role in taking urban music from Puerto Rico to every corner of the globe while celebrating his new album “La Postal,” which serves as a love letter to Latin identity, romance and global culture. Daddy Yankee ranked among the top 50 most streamed artists this year with over 41 million subscribers. His single “BOTZ” charted in over 10 markets, and his recent collab with Diljit Dosanjh, “POKITO,” reached #1 on the Global Daily Music Videos chart.

AmplifyHER & YouTube Music Nights
Expanding our women-in-music initiative (which has impacted over 375 artists, managers, and executives across Mexico, Colombia, and Argentina) we hosted a special Las Vegas session welcoming +100 female guests. This was followed by an exclusive YouTube Music Nights pop-up alongside partner labels featuring Aria Vega.
