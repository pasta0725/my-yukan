Grand Theft Auto VI officially launches on November 19th and the community on YouTube couldn’t be more hyped. To date, 
the Grand Theft Auto series has reached over 900 billion views on YouTube, with pre-launch videos related to GTAVI already topping 40 billion views.

In celebration of GTAVI’s imminent launch, YouTube and Rockstar Games are partnering to bring the community together with Creator content and interactive features.
Here's how fans can take part in the action:
Eligible viewers tuning into select creator live streams can claim and send a Grand Theft Auto VI gift on us while they last.

GTAVI: Visit Vice City Shorts Effect
Fans can drop themselves directly into Vice City using the 
GTAVI: Visit Vice City
 Shorts Effect. The feature includes multiple iconic backgrounds, making it easy for creators and fans to build their own launch-week videos.
Unique creator POVs
Our global creators are bringing you everything from deep tactical breakdowns and immersive storytelling to behind-the-scenes access throughout launch week in Miami. Stay tuned to watch your favorite creators dive into Vice City!
The sound of Vice City
Music has always been a defining part of the Grand Theft Auto experience. Listeners can find the official 
GTAVI
 soundtrack (featuring Morgan Wallen’s “Last Thing You Need”) and the complete GTA series anthology playlist directly on the YouTube Music homepage throughout launch week.
YouTube’s Culture & Trends team published an in-depth look at Grand Theft Auto's lasting cultural impact as we inch closer to the GTAVI era.
New Culture and Trends Report
Today, YouTube’s Culture & Trends team published an in-depth look at Grand Theft Auto's lasting cultural impact as we inch closer to the GTAVI era. The report analyzes how the uniquely cinematic and immersive world has evolved digital storytelling and opened new avenues for creative expression.
