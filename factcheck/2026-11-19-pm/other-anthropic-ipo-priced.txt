===== https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering
(status 200)
Today Anthropic, PBC priced its initial public offering of its Class A common stock at $83.00 per share. The offering consists of 980,000,000 shares of Class A common stock, 956,351,354 of which are being sold by Anthropic and 23,648,646 of which are being sold by certain selling stockholders. Anthropic will not receive any proceeds from the sale of shares by the selling stockholders.
The shares are expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on November 19, 2026 under the symbol “ANT.” The offering is expected to close on November 20, 2026, subject to the satisfaction of customary closing conditions. In addition, Anthropic has granted the underwriters a 30-day option to purchase up to an additional 147,000,000 shares of its Class A common stock.
A registration statement relating to these securities was declared effective by the Securities and Exchange Commission on November 18, 2026. The offering is being made only by means of a prospectus. Copies of the final prospectus may be obtained, when available, from: Goldman Sachs & Co. ===== https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-above-projected-range/
(status 200)
Anthropic has priced its long-awaited IPO at 
$83 per share
, above its initial range of $70 to $78, signaling strong investor appetite for one of the companies at the center of the AI boom, despite market jitters following the departure of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, which shook confidence in the industry. The Claude maker sold 980 million shares in the offering, up from the 825 million initially planned, generating $81.3 billion in proceeds and surpassing SpaceX’s $75 billion record to become the largest IPO in history by proceeds raised.
At the IPO price, Anthropic is valued at roughly $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis, a significant increase from the $965 billion the company reached in May when it raised $65 billion in its 
last private market round
, but below the $2 trillion it was reportedly aiming for several months ago.
Anthropic will begin its life as a public company on Thursday, trading on Nasdaq under the ticker symbol “ANT.” A strong first day of trading would signal that investors remain optimistic about AI adoption, despite recent volatility in AI-driven stocks.
The company’s performance is expected to set expectations for private AI companies, including Anthropic’s main rival OpenAI, which was last reportedly valued at 
$1.25 billion
 but has yet to announce plans for an IPO.
Although none of Anthropic’s venture capital investors holds more than a 5% stake in the company, the IPO is a massive windfall for its 
numerous backers
. Investors who own approximately 1% of the company reportedly include Sequoia and former Google CEO Eric Schmidt. Singaporean sovereign wealth fund GIC also holds about a 1% stake in the company, according to a person familiar with the matter. Other backers set to make billions are Iconiq, Menlo Ventures, Coatue, and Sound Ventures, a firm launched by Guy Oseary and actor Ashton Kutcher, and Spark Ventures, the only VC firm known to hold a board seat at the company.
Some of the company’s existing investors, namely Sequoia, Iconiq, Lightspeed, Accel, and Altimeter Capital Management, are so enthusiastic about Anthropic’s future that they were 
reportedly planning
 to buy billions of dollars’ worth of the company’s shares in the IPO.  
Yet the investors’ holdings in Anthropic pale in comparison to those of the Claude maker’s 
strategic partners
, Amazon, which owns nearly 15% of the company, and Google, which holds almost 14%. A group of major tech companies, including AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia, and TSMC Partners, have also agreed to purchase $26 billion worth of non-voting shares through a private placement alongside the IPO.
The company’s 
seven co-founders
, including CEO Dario Amodei, each sold nearly 3.4 million shares in the IPO, according to the S-1 filing, bringing in about $282 million for each.
