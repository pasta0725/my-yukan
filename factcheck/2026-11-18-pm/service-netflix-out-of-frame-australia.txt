Netflix Calls on Australia's Next Generation of Visual Storytellers for Out of Frame MasterClass
Social Impact
17 November 2026
Global
Australia
Link copied to clipboard
Ten creators will train with Netflix’s Photo and AV Studio in Sydney this December, then get 12 months of ongoing support.
Netflix is looking for 10 independent Australian photographers, videographers and social creators for its Out of Frame Masterclass in Sydney on 9–10 December 2026. Applications are open now and close at 11.59pm on Monday 23 November.
Run in partnership with 
Bursty
 and 
Media Mentors Australia
, the masterclass is led by Netflix’s Photo and AV Studio, the team behind Netflix’s key art and behind-the-scenes content. It shows creators how their skills translate to film and TV. No previous screen credits are required.
“If you know how to capture raw emotion in a split second at a high-stakes event, or craft a scroll-stopping reel right from your phone, you already possess the instincts that electrify a film set. Out of Frame is designed to supercharge those instincts and show you exactly where they can elevate your business,” said Abby Morgan, head of PAVS Netflix ANZ. 
Participants will pitch and produce promotional content from a mock Netflix brief, working with actors and getting feedback from industry professionals and Netflix teams. They’ll also get guidance on finding freelance work, tour an active Netflix set (subject to production schedule) and attend an industry networking event on the night of 10 December.
After the masterclass, participants join a year-long alumni group with ongoing support to turn what they’ve learned into work.
“This is an incredible opportunity to build on your career and we’re thrilled to be supporting the successful applicants over the next 12 months. The combination of training, community and support is unprecedented and we cannot wait to see the creative talent that exists across Australia,” said Denise Eriksen from Media Mentors who will be working with the cohort for an extended period to help them turn their learning into business opportunities and success.
Who can apply
Applicants need at least two years of professional creative experience, full availability for both days and the evening event, and their own professional gear. Two of the 10 places are reserved for First Nations creatives and two for disabled, Deaf and/or neurodivergent creators. Flights and Sydney accommodation will be covered for selected interstate participants.
How to apply
Submit an expression of interest at 
Out of Frame
 by 11.59pm on Monday 23 November. Applicants will be screened for experience and invited to submit creative examples, and a shortlist will be interviewed.

Netflix is opening the door for Australian photographers, videographers and content creators looking to expand their careers into film and television. 
The streaming giant is offering 10 places in a new Sydney masterclass next month, with successful applicants also receiving 12 months of ongoing industry support.
Applications are now open for Netflix’s Out of Frame masterclass, which will take place on 9 and 10 December in partnership with Bursty and Media Mentors Australia.
- Advertisement -
The two-day program will be led by Netflix’s Photo and AV Studio, the team responsible for producing promotional photography, key art and behind-the-scenes content for the streaming platform.
It is aimed at independent creators with at least two years of professional experience, although previous work in film or television is not required.
Participants will be given a mock Netflix brief and tasked with developing promotional content alongside actors, with feedback provided by industry professionals and the company’s creative teams.
The program will also cover opportunities for freelance work in the screen industry, alongside a networking event and a potential visit to an active Netflix production set, depending on filming schedules.
Netflix ANZ Head of Photo and AV Studio 
Abby Morgan
 says the initiative is designed to help creators recognise how their existing skills can translate into opportunities in film and television.
“If you know how to capture raw emotion in a split second at a high-stakes event, or craft a scroll-stopping reel right from your phone, you already possess the instincts that electrify a film set. Out of Frame is designed to supercharge those instincts and show you exactly where they can elevate your business.”
Beyond the two-day masterclass, the 10 selected participants will receive a further 12 months of support through an alumni program, with Media Mentors Australia helping them develop professional connections and explore potential business opportunities.
- Advertisement -
Media Mentors Australia’s 
Denise Eriksen
 says the extended program will give participants an opportunity to build on their experience beyond the initial training.
“This is an incredible opportunity to build on your career and we’re thrilled to be supporting the successful applicants over the next 12 months. The combination of training, community and support is unprecedented and we cannot wait to see the creative talent that exists across Australia.”
Of the 10 available places, two are reserved for First Nations creatives, while another two are allocated to disabled, Deaf and/or neurodivergent creators.
Applicants must have their own professional equipment and be available for both days of the masterclass, including the evening networking event on 10 December.
Flights and accommodation will be covered for selected interstate participants, with applications open to eligible creatives from across Australia.
The selection process will involve an initial assessment of professional experience, followed by examples of previous creative work and interviews with shortlisted applicants.
Expressions of interest can be submitted through the official Out of Frame application page 
HERE
.
Applications for Netflix’s Out of Frame masterclass close at 11.59pm on Monday, 23 November, ahead of the two-day program in Sydney on 9 and 10 December.
