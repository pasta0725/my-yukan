OHB and Astroscale Sign Agreement to Strengthen International Cooperation on Space Security
Posted
November 3, 2026
Posted in
Announcements
Astroscale and OHB have signed an MoU to explore cooperation on capabilities that protect critical infrastructure in space while supporting the safe, sustainable and responsible use of the orbital environment 
The MoU focuses on collaboration between Astroscale’s UK entity and OHB in Germany, following the ratification of the Kensington Treaty in October 
Astroscale’s operations in the UK and Japan also offer a link between the two countries’ industrial capabilities and their wider international partnerships 
Tokyo, 3 November 2026 
– OHB SE and Astroscale Holdings Inc. have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to explore cooperation on capabilities that protect critical infrastructure in space, strengthening industrial ties between Germany, the United Kingdom and Japan.
Announced Wednesday 28
th
 October 2026 at the German Embassy in Tokyo during the German-Japanese industrial collaboration event, the agreement brings together the companies’ complementary expertise across in-orbit servicing, assembly and manufacturing (ISAM), space domain awareness and related space security applications. It provides a framework to explore joint opportunities that support secure and sustainable operations in orbit.
A key focus is closer cooperation between Astroscale’s UK entity and OHB in Germany, creating potential export opportunities for capabilities developed in the UK and Germany and opportunities for both companies to address emerging customer needs. The agreement also provides a basis to explore cooperation in Japan and on a global scale, drawing on Astroscale’s leading presence in the region.
The collaboration comes as the UK and Germany deepen their strategic partnership through the Kensington Treaty and Trinity House Agreement. It aligns with the ambition set out in the 
joint statement
 on the 8th October 2026 to strengthen cooperation in space across civil, commercial, security and defence interests. By connecting industrial expertise across borders, OHB and Astroscale aim to support these shared priorities while opening new routes to growth.
Astroscale’s operations in the UK and Japan also offer a link between the two countries’ industrial capabilities and their wider international partnerships. The MoU demonstrates how cooperation between companies can help translate relationships among like-minded nations into opportunities for innovation, trade and greater resilience in space.
Nobu Okada, founder and CEO, Astroscale: 
“The future of space security will be built on trusted partnerships between allied nations, while sovereign capabilities will remain important, many of the challenges facing spacefaring nations are too complex and too interconnected for any single country to address alone. 
“By bringing together complementary expertise from Germany, the United Kingdom and Japan through this collaboration, we are helping to build the industrial relationships and capabilities needed to support the long-term security, resilience and sustainability of the space domain.”
Markus Moeller, CSO at OHB SE: 
"Space infrastructure has become indispensable to modern society and must be protected accordingly. Combining our expertise with Astroscale's unique capabilities in in-orbit servicing will allow us to explore innovative approaches supporting secure and resilient space operations. We see significant potential in working together to address evolving customer requirements and future challenges in orbit."
With space becoming increasingly contested and congested, trusted international partnerships are essential to the future security and sustainability of the domain. This agreement brings together global expertise to examine how we can better protect the infrastructure on which our societies depend.

OHB and Astroscale sign agreement to explore space security cooperation
by Clarence Oxford
Tokyo, Japan (SPX) Nov 06, 2026
SPX
Germany's OHB SE and Japan-headquartered Astroscale Holdings Inc. have signed a memorandum of understanding to explore cooperation on capabilities that protect critical infrastructure in space. The companies say the agreement strengthens industrial ties between Germany, the United Kingdom and Japan.
The memorandum is a starting point, not a deal. It commits the two companies to explore joint opportunities, and no joint project, contract or value has been announced.
The agreement was announced on 28 October 2026 at the German Embassy in Tokyo, during a German-Japanese industrial collaboration event. The companies' release about it is dated 3 November 2026.
According to the companies, the memorandum provides a framework to explore opportunities that support secure and sustainable operations in orbit. It draws on their expertise in three linked areas. In-orbit servicing, assembly and manufacturing, known as ISAM, covers work done on or with spacecraft once they are already in space. Space domain awareness is the job of knowing what is in orbit and what it is doing. The third area is what the release calls related space security applications.
Since its first launch in March 2021, Astroscale has demonstrated rendezvous and proximity operations technologies in orbit on its ELSA-d and ADRAS-J missions. Those technologies let a spacecraft manoeuvre close to another object in orbit. Its services include life extension, in-situ space situational awareness, end-of-life and active debris removal. The release says Astroscale spacecraft have been selected for missions with JAXA, the U.S. Space Force, the European Space Agency, the UK Space Agency and Eutelsat.
The companies say a key focus is closer cooperation between Astroscale's UK entity and OHB in Germany. They say this creates potential export opportunities for UK-developed capability, and opportunities for both companies to address emerging customer needs.
The agreement also provides a basis to explore cooperation in Japan, drawing on what the release calls Astroscale's leading presence in the region. Astroscale was founded in 2013 and has subsidiaries in the United Kingdom, the United States, France and Israel. According to the release, its operations in the UK and Japan offer a link between the two countries' industrial capabilities and their wider international partnerships.
The companies place the memorandum within a deepening UK-German relationship. They cite the Kensington Treaty and the Trinity House Agreement, and the release says the memorandum follows the ratification of the Kensington Treaty in October. The release also says the agreement aligns with the ambition set out in a joint UK-Germany statement on 8 October 2026 to strengthen cooperation in space across civil, commercial, security and defence interests.
Nobu Okada, founder and CEO of Astroscale, said: "The future of space security will be built on trusted partnerships between allied nations, while sovereign capabilities will remain important, many of the challenges facing spacefaring nations are too complex and too interconnected for any single country to address alone."
He added: "By bringing together complementary expertise from Germany, the United Kingdom and Japan through this collaboration, we are helping to build the industrial relationships and capabilities needed to support the long-term security, resilience and sustainability of the space domain."
Markus Moeller, CSO at OHB SE, said: "Space infrastructure has become indispensable to modern society and must be protected accordingly. Combining our expertise with Astroscale's unique capabilities in in-orbit servicing will allow us to explore innovative approaches for supporting secure, resilient and sustainable space operations. We see significant potential in working together to address evolving customer requirements and future challenges in orbit."
The memorandum signals intent by two companies to look for joint work across three countries in an area they describe as space security. It does not establish a programme, a mission or an order.
