===== https://macdailynews.com/2026/10/20/apples-homepod-software-27-2-brings-album-synced-sound-glow-lighting-to-existing-homepods/
(status 200)
Apple’s HomePod mini
Apple is preparing to roll out HomePod Software 27.2, which extends the new Sound Glow visual feature beyond the recently announced HomePod mini 2 to several existing speakers. The update, currently available as a release candidate for developers and public beta testers, is expected to reach the general public next week.
Sound Glow transforms the illuminated top surface of compatible HomePods into a dynamic light display that pulses in time with audio playback. Colors are extracted from album artwork, creating a unique palette for each track. The effect works with songs, podcasts, Apple’s ambient soundscapes, and other audio. On the HomePod mini 2, Apple has described it as turning the top into a visual canvas that reacts in real time to bass and treble, with bespoke animations for soundscapes such as rain. Early observations indicate that typically two or three dominant colors appear, with light elements that shift and bounce across the surface. The brightness is relatively modest, but in a darkened room the glow can cast subtle color onto nearby walls.
The feature will be available on the second-generation HomePod, the original HomePod mini, and the HomePod mini 2. It is not supported on the original full-size HomePod, a limitation linked to differences in the LED array on that earlier model. Owners of the first-generation HomePod will continue to receive software updates, however; the device has already enjoyed roughly eight years of support.
After installing version 27.2, users can activate Sound Glow in the Home app by long-pressing the speaker and enabling the new toggle in its settings. When HomePods are paired with an Apple TV, the feature can also be toggled from the Apple TV Control Center.
HomePod Software 27.2 includes additional improvements. It adds a Lock Controls option that disables most touch interactions on the top surface, preventing accidental play/pause, volume changes, track skips, or Siri activation from phantom touches or curious hands while still allowing certain functions such as intercom, timers, alarms, and FaceTime responses. The update also enables select HomePods — specifically the second-generation model and HomePod mini 2 — to serve as the default audio output for compatible Macs (those with Wi-Fi 6E or later), offering a more seamless and lower-latency alternative to standard AirPlay. In addition, it lays groundwork for using compatible HomePods in multi-speaker surround or whole-room audio setups with the new fourth-generation Apple TV 4K.
The Sound Glow capability was first highlighted alongside the HomePod mini 2, which Apple introduced earlier this month and which is scheduled to begin shipping on October 30th. By bringing the lighting effect to older hardware via a free software update, Apple is expanding the visual experience to a broader installed base ahead of the public release of version 27.2.

===== https://www.ithinkdiff.com/homepod-software-27-2-sound-glow-lock-controls/
(status 200)
Apple’s HomePod Software 27.2 release candidate brings Sound Glow to more speakers than HomePod mini 2. HomePod (2nd generation) and the original HomePod mini pick up the glowing top, and every HomePod model gets a new Lock Controls setting.
The change is available in the 
iOS 27
.2 release candidate. Sound Glow works on every HomePod except the original, which is the 2018 model, and Lock Controls works on all of them.
Sound Glow reaches HomePod (2nd generation) and the original HomePod mini
Sound Glow debuted on 
HomePod mini 2
, where the top of the speaker glows and pulses with the music. The light takes its colors from the album artwork in Now Playing and reacts to bass and treble in real time. On HomePod mini 2, Apple also lets you pick a soundscape such as rain for softer animations.
With the release candidate, Sound Glow works on three speakers:
HomePod (2nd generation)
HomePod mini, the 2020 model with the S5 chip
HomePod mini 2, which has it out of the box
You need iOS 27.2 on the iPhone and HomePod Software 27.2 on the speaker. Sound Glow then shows up as a single toggle in the HomePod’s settings in the Home app, and it displays ambient light matched to the colors and rhythm of what is playing.
The original HomePod does not get Sound Glow. It also misses the 
HomePod surround sound setup
 in the same update, which supports only HomePod mini 2 and HomePod (2nd generation) speakers in matching pairs.
Lock Controls turns off the touch controls on every HomePod, including the original
Lock Controls stops a HomePod from playing audio or taking action when the top of the speaker is touched. Intercom, timers, alarms, Check In, and FaceTime calls still respond to touch.
With the setting on, tap to play or pause, volume changes, and touch and hold for Siri stop working until you switch it off. The setting first appeared in 
iOS 27.2 beta
 4, and in the Home app it sits directly below the Sound Glow toggle.
HomePod Software 27.2 should arrive before HomePod mini 2 ships on October 30
Apple seeded the HomePod Software 27.2 release candidate on October 19, alongside the 
iOS 27.2 release candidate
. A release candidate is normally the last build before the public release. Apple has not announced a date or published release notes for HomePod Software 27.2.
The update has to be live before HomePod mini 2 reaches customers, since the speaker runs HomePod Software 27.2. HomePod mini 2 costs $149 in the US, pre-orders are open, and it goes on sale Friday, October 30.
