Remembering What Matters
With Muse Wonder, we introduced Active Memory and Active Focus. We’re bringing both of these to our specialized models in Muse and Meta AI.
Active Memory enables Muse and Meta AI to remember what matters and recognize when earlier information becomes relevant, making it easier to build off your past conversations. We’ve trained our models to recognize what type of information is temporary versus permanent, to reason across time, and to distinguish between what is still relevant and what has become stale. For example, our improved models in Muse show a 2.4x reduction in forgetting critical information necessary for helping you achieve your goals.

Working Toward Your Goals
Active Focus enables Muse to continue working toward a goal, even when faced with unexpected challenges. We’ve trained our models on a variety of bottlenecks reflective of the real world, so that they can try harder, recover, or find alternatives on your behalf. In Muse, our models show a 67% improvement in completing everyday tasks where unexpected issues arise.
We’ve also developed our specialized models to leverage Muse Wonder’s performance on behaviors that are especially important to Muse. For example, they learned to be excellent multi-taskers that know when to push on your goals independently or collaborate live with you. They can also better adapt to steers and clarifications in the middle of running tasks without getting confused.
In Meta AI, the same improvements make for a better thinking partner and collaborator. It can break complex problems into clear steps, ask sharper follow-up questions, and keep up when you change direction mid-conversation.
By adding these specialized models to 
Muse
 and 
Meta AI
, we aim to give more people access to even more capable superintelligence. We can’t wait for you to experience it for yourself.

===== https://about.fb.com/news/2026/11/introducing-muse-ai-voice-expressive-avatars/
(status 200)
At Connect 2026, we previewed real-time conversations with your Muse. Now we’re rolling it out in the Muse app on mobile, web and on Mac — just tap the phone icon in the top-right corner to start talking.
Talk It Through With Muse
Sometimes it’s easier to think out loud. With voice, you can talk through an idea with your Muse and let it take shape without worrying about the exact prompt.
Planning a trip? Start with the half-baked idea: the city you’ve always wanted to see, the kind of place you’d want to wake up in, the one meal you’d plan the rest of the trip around. Your Muse picks up the thread and works while you talk — on a walk, making dinner, or wherever you are.
Some things are better rehearsed out loud. Prepare for a job interview, run through a presentation, or talk through a pitch — hearing a response and answering in the moment makes it feel more like the real thing.
https://about.fb.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/01_Muse-Realtime-Voice-technology.mp4
See Muse Respond in Real Time
As your Muse speaks, the avatar uses subtle expressions and gestures that match the tone of the conversation. It reacts while you talk, so you can tell it’s following along. And you choose how present you want it to be: voice only, the avatar alongside your conversation, or full screen when you want to talk face to face.
Give your Muse a voice with some personality. Want the energy of a New Yorker or the flair of a pirate? With hundreds of voices to choose from, you can pick a style that feels right for your mood and change it whenever you want.
Our 
Muse Realtime Avatar
and Muse Realtime Voice technology lets you have fluid conversations in real time. Speak to your Muse, as you do with real people. Hear responses right away. You can even interrupt if you need to. Wherever the conversation goes, Muse keeps up with you.
What’s Next
Voice and expressive avatars are rolling out now in the Muse app in the US to start. Voice conversations with your Muse are coming soon to AI Glasses and Muse Charm.

===== https://about.fb.com/news/2026/11/introducing-spaces-customize-muse-app/
(status 200)
Today, we’re introducing spaces, an interactive way to build a more personalized Muse. A space is a custom experience you create inside Muse by describing your idea, like a holiday gift tracker, a daily word game, or a planning board for your next DIY project. It’s shaped around your routines, goals, and interests, and can be as practical or entertaining as you want. Every space you create becomes part of Muse, so you can keep it where you want and come back to it any time. 
From An Idea to Something Real
Have an idea you’ve been meaning to try? Tell Muse about it, and it builds it for you. Your initial idea is just the starting point. As you use your space, you can keep adjusting it with Muse, whether that’s adding what’s missing, trying a new look, or changing how it works.
You can also choose where your spaces live in Muse. Add them to your sidebar, move them into your main tabs, hide the ones you don’t need right now, and schedule when they appear. For example, keep your weekly planner up front during the workweek, then swap it for your weekend plans on Saturday.
https://about.fb.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/01_Design-the-Muse-App_Just-for-You.mp4
What You Can Build
Spaces can help you get things done, have a little fun, or give your creative projects a home. Here are a few ideas:
A shopping hub 
that brings holiday and birthday gift ideas, wishlists from Instagram saves, and your Marketplace listings and buyer messages into one view
A school dashboard 
that finds the events relevant to your kids in school emails and adds them to your calendar
A music tracker
 for the artists you love, with new releases and upcoming shows
A wedding planner
 with your RSVP list, vendor invoices, and to-dos 
A trivia game
 that quizzes you on your favorite movies, music, or sports team
A podcast studio
 to keep your episode ideas, research, and scripts together
An order tracker
 for your small business, with new orders, customer messages and what needs to ship today 
A client hub
 for your freelance work, with projects, deadlines, and invoices 
A trip planner
 with your flights, hotel, reservations and a day-by-day itinerary. 
Got a half-finished idea sitting in your notes app, a spreadsheet you keep meaning to clean up, or a hobby you want to dive into? Chances are you can turn it into a space.
A Space That Keeps Up With You
Your spaces are personal from the start. Connect the apps you use, and your spaces keep themselves up to date in the background, so new concert dates, price changes, or savings progress show up without you having to ask. You can check things off, add notes, and make edits right in your space, and everything will be there when you return.
Looking Ahead
Starting today, spaces are available everywhere Muse is available. We’re also exploring ways for you to share your spaces with others to build a more collaborative experience in Muse.
We’re also rolling out specialized 
versions of our models
 to Muse, trained with learnings from Muse Wonder, to make your overall experience even better.
