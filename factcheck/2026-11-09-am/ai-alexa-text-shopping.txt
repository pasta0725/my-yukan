===== https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/after-blocking-muse-amazon-now-lets-users-text-alexa-to-help-them-shop/
(status 200)
Amazon is jumping on the currently most popular bandwagon for interacting with AI agents: texting. The company on Friday said it is launching a feature that lets customers chat with its Alexa AI agent over text messages to help them shop, much like you can with AI agents like Muse or Instinct.
The move comes after Amazon 
blocked Meta’s Muse from querying its site in September.
 The company is rolling the features out just before the start of the holiday shopping season.
The e-commerce giant said select U.S. customers will get a text asking them to enable the shopping feature. Those with access can text Alexa to shop on Amazon using natural language queries, spanning everything from searching for items, adding them to cart, and purchasing.
Image Credits:
Amazon
The text agent builds on the recent shopping-focused 
upgrades to Alexa
, and so can manage lists, keep tabs on prices, search for items across the web, compare products, and check the status of orders. Users can also share a picture of an item or someone wearing an outfit to search for similar listings on Amazon. 
The company said over time, Alexa will learn users’ preferences and suggest items accordingly.
The company said users who don’t get invited to use this feature can 
sign up to a waitlist
. The feature will be rolled out more broadly in the coming weeks.
As the market for consumer-focused AI agents balloons, platforms like Amazon are building their own to tap this new avenue of user interaction. Last month, delivery company DoorDash said that third-party agents had so far 
only placed hundreds of orders
 in total on its platform, and that it plans to concentrate on developing its own text-based agent, 
launched in September
, further.

Meta’s AI agent has been blocked from using Amazon.com
Sunday night, users of Meta’s AI assistant Muse started getting a strange error message when they tried to buy goods on Amazon. 
As spotted by GeekWire
, the error message read: “Continued access by an unauthorized AI agent violates Amazon’s Conditions of Use, to which our customers have agreed.”
In other words, Muse isn’t welcome as a shopper — and anyone trying to buy something on Amazon through the AI agent will have to find it somewhere else.
It’s easy to see the block as a tête-à-tête between tech giants. Amazon has 
its own cohort of foundation models
, after all, along with 
one of the most popular inference platforms on the internet
. As long as they’re under no legal obligation to open the doors to Muse, why would they?
But there are also substantive reasons Amazon might not want to be in the agentic commerce business just yet. If Muse makes a bad order, Amazon is going to be the one to clean it up — dealing with both the angry customer and the angry vendor. Muse has 
one of the lower hallucination rates
, as AI models go, but it’s still pretty far from zero. Even if Amazon sees an opportunity in agent-driven commerce, they may want to wait a few more release cycles before rolling anything out.

Amazon updates Alexa to help you manage shopping lists and subscriptions and update delivery instructions
Ahead of the holiday season, Amazon has added more than 100 new shopping-related capabilities to Alexa, including managing subscriptions and lists, updating delivery instructions, solving payment issues, and answering questions about Prime subscriptions.
The update allows you to use Alexa to create lists, register them, see what’s on them, and add or edit items. You can also manage subscriptions with the AI bot — for example, you could ask it to change deliveries for an item to every three months instead of every month — and edit delivery instructions, such as change an address or add drop-off details.
Users also can now ask Alexa about their Prime or Audible subscriptions, and ask it questions about billing or payment issues.
Amazon has launched various AI-centric shopping features this year, including 
a shopping assistant
 and a way to notify users about 
new products and offers
.
The company said that since the start of the year, the number of people using agentic features has grown 8x, and over the last 12 months, over 400 million consumers have used Alexa for shopping.

===== https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/30/doordash-launches-an-ai-agent-you-can-text-to-order-food/
(status 200)

Image Credits:

Aisha Malik
DoorDash launches an AI agent you can text to order food
DoorDash 
announced
 on Wednesday that it’s launching a text-to-order AI agent that lets users place orders through Apple Messages. The new tool allows users to send prompts like “order my usual,” and the agent will understand that they mean their Friday night order.
DoorDash says users can also ask for a specific dish and request a local recommendation. The agent will then search local spots and suggest a cart based on the prompt, and even text photos of the food it recommends. When ordering for a group, DoorDash says the agent can handle mixed dietary preferences and different quantities in one order. 
By launching an AI agent for food ordering, DoorDash is looking to gain an edge over rivals Uber Eats and Grubhub. The launch also comes amid a growing push to adopt personal AI agents that can handle tasks without requiring users to navigate apps or websites themselves.
DoorDash is opening up a waitlist for users in the U.S. to try out the new feature. 
In addition to the new AI agent, DoorDash announced that it will begin testing its delivery drones with select restaurants in Northern California.
