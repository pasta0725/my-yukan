Android 版「Google ウォレット」アプリにて展開を確認した「海外 QR 決済（Traveler QR）」は、シンガポールに本社を置く Ant International（アント・インターナショナル）が提供するクロスボーダー決済ネットワーク「Alipay+」を介して、旅行先で現地の QR コード決済に対応する加盟店で支払いができる機能です。現地のアプリや、銀行口座 / 電子ウォレットのアカウントなどは必要なく、「Google ウォレット」に登録済みの Visa カードにて、QR コード決済を通してそのまま支払うことができます。
なお、「海外 QR 決済」を利用するには、Android 版「Google ウォレット」アプリの設定内に新設された「海外 QR 決済」の「利用許可を更新」から、「位置情報」権限を ON にする必要があります。

「海外 QR 決済」
なお、「海外 QR 決済」で支払いができるのは、今のところシンガポール国内の「Alipay+」パートナーネットワークのみです。利用対象は、日本を含む APAC やオーストラリア、カナダ、ヨーロッパ、イギリス、アメリカなど、「Google ウォレット」のタッチ決済が利用可能な国から渡航するユーザーとなっています。
「海外 QR 決済」の支払い方法は 2 通りで、「スキャンして支払う」から店舗の QR コードを読み取るか、「コードを表示」から自分の QR コードを提示して、店舗の決済端末で QR コードを読み取ってもらいます。店舗の QR コードを読み取る場合は、現地通貨で金額を入力すると為替レートと自国通貨による合計金額が表示され、内容を確認してから支払いを承認する流れです。どちらの方法でも、レジでは「Alipay+」で支払う旨を伝える必要があります。
It is accepted at Alipay+ partner networks in Singapore.

「海外 QR 決済」の利用条件は、Android 版「Google ウォレット」アプリのタッチ決済用に登録された Visa のクレジット / デビットカードを持っていること、カードの海外利用が有効になっていること、Android 版「Google ウォレット」アプリの「位置情報」と「カメラ」の権限が ON になっていることの 3 つです。Android 版「Google ウォレット」アプリの「位置情報」権限を ON にしている場合、対象地域に渡航すると自動で有効になる仕様です。
「海外 QR 決済」を使用した場合でも、Google 側で手数料は一切発生しません。ただし、為替レートや手数料は「Alipay+」のパートナーとカード発行会社が決めるため、カード発行会社の海外事務手数料が別途発生する場合もあります。このほか、支払いにはトークン化された決済情報が使われ、実際のカード番号が「Alipay+」や加盟店に共有されることはありません。
ちなみに、当サイトオリジナルコンテンツ「アプリ解析」にて 
2026 年 7 月に報じた際
は、Visa と Mastercard の両方に対応するとみられていましたが、展開開始時点で支払いに使えるのは Visa のみです。Visa 以外のカードで支払おうとすると、「このお支払いを続行するには、Visa カードを追加してください。」と案内されます。
また、
2026 年 9 月に追加情報として報じた際
は、タイの「PromptPay」と日本の「PayPay」に関するリソースコードも確認されていましたが、今のところは「PromptPay」「PayPay」を「海外 QR 決済」にて利用することはできません。

Make payments with Traveler QR
Important:
Available for global Google Wallet users traveling from 
countries where tap to pay is available
 including APAC, Australia, Canada, Europe, the UK, and the US.
Traveler QR supports payments through the Alipay+ network only. Google Wallet facilitates sending your payment requests securely, but does not process, hold, clear or transfer funds directly for transactions.
Foreign exchange (FX) conversion rates and transaction fees are determined based on the rate offered by Alipay+ network's banking and card scheme partners and your card issuer, inclusive of any conversion margins or spreads determined by the Alipay+ network. Google does not charge any fees for using this feature.
About Traveler QR
Traveler QR connects with local payment networks through the Alipay+ network so you can make payments to participating offline merchants while traveling. You do not need to download any additional apps, or have a local bank or ewallet account. Google Wallet securely transmits your payment credentials to the Alipay+ network, which connects directly to local QR networks to process your payment using your saved Visa cards.

An Android device.

Location and camera permissions turned on for Google Wallet.
It is accepted at Alipay+ partner networks in Singapore.

Commonly asked questions
How do currency exchange rates work when paying by QR code?
Google Wallet provides the platform to facilitate secure payments, but does not process or hold funds directly for transactions. Payment processing, currency exchange and merchant settlement services are operated by your card issuer, Alipay+ network's banking and card scheme partners, the Alipay+ network and its local partners. Payments are settled between you, the merchant, and your card issuer through the Alipay+ network and its local partners.
You will always find the real-time exchange rate and total cost in your home currency before confirming payment when you scan a merchant’s QR. Google Wallet does not charge any fees for overseas QR payments. The exchange rate and transaction fees are determined by the rates offered by Alipay+ network’s associated banking and network partners and your card issuer, inclusive of any margins applied by the Alipay+ network. Your card issuer may charge additional international transaction fees. Please check with your bank for their foreign transaction fee policy.
When you present your QR to the merchant, you can view the total cost, including exchange rate, after the merchant has scanned your QR code and the transaction is completed.
Are there fees for international QR payments?
Google Wallet does not charge any fees for overseas QR payments. However, you may be subject to charges from the other parties processing your transaction. The total cost of your transaction may include base exchange rates and international transaction fees determined by your card issuer and Alipay+ network’s banking and card scheme partners, as well as currency conversion margins applied by the Alipay+ network. Check with your bank for their specific foreign transaction fee policy.
Is my payment information secure when making a QR payment through Alipay+ network using Google Wallet?
Google Wallet helps keep your payment information secure. When you pay, Google Wallet does not share your actual credit or debit card number with Alipay+ or the merchant. Payments are processed using secure, tokenized digital credentials protected by Google Pay.
Fix issues
QR code scanner screen is black
Google Wallet requires camera permissions to scan store codes. Go to your device’s application settings for Google Wallet to verify camera access is set to "Allow while using the app".
Asked to "Add a supported card"
This happens when your default payment method is on an unsupported network. It also happens if the card is a domestic-only debit card or is not set up for contactless tap-to-pay in Google Wallet.
To fix the issue, add or select an eligible card such as a Visa card and make sure foreign transactions are enabled with your bank.
Scanner says "Unsupported QR code"
The camera scanned a personal bank transfer code instead of an official merchant payment code, or the code belongs to an unsupported network. Ensure the QR code displays an accepted merchant partner logo such as Alipay+.
Bank notified me of a charge after a failed payment
Google Wallet provides the platform to facilitate the connection and does not hold or process transaction funds directly. If Google Wallet shows the payment failed but a pending charge appears on your banking app, it is usually a temporary pre-authorization hold, meaning no funds were actually deducted.
Because pre-authorization holds are managed entirely by your financial institution, please follow up directly with your card issuing bank for details on when the hold will be released
Entered the wrong amount on a static QR code
Inform the cashier and show your in-app transaction receipt to request a void or a refund directly from the merchant. Because Google Wallet does not process or hold the funds, we cannot cancel or reverse the transaction on your behalf.
Request refund for returned item
Google Wallet provides the platform to facilitate secure payments, but does not process or hold funds for transactions or process refunds directly.
Returns and refunds are subject to the individual merchant's store policies. If the store allows returns, you may be able to request a refund by presenting your in-app receipt to the cashier so they can process the return on their register. Once the merchant processes the return, please check with your card issuer for the refund time
