『MLB The Show 27』（英語版）の最新情報へ進む前に、まずはこれまでの歩みを振り返ってみましょう。
2026年を振り返り、改めてふたつのことを実感しています。ひとつは、「MLB The Show」コミュニティーの皆さんによる並外れた情熱とサポート。そしてもうひとつは、今後コミュニティーの皆さんとより良いコミュニケーションを図り、皆さんからのフィードバックにより適切に対応していく必要があるということです。何十年もプレイしてくださっている方から、今年初めて参加してくださった方まで、皆さんのおかげでこのシリーズの歴史に特別な1章を加えることができました。
『MLB The Show 26』コミュニティーの統計データ
私たちは、今年チームで成し遂げた成果を誇りに思っています。しかし、「MLB The Show」の中心にあるのは、いつの時代もコミュニティーの皆さんです。発売以来、皆さんが打ち立てた驚異的な記録をご覧ください！
ホームラン：1,058,402,460本
三塁打：55,168,197本
一塁打：1,639,448,296本
また、123,300,616回の四球を選び、2,130,551,161回もの三振を記録しました。どうやら、私たちは選球眼をさらに磨く必要がありそうです！
とはいえ、三振ばかりではありません。皆さんはPerfect-Perfectヒットを18,666,758本記録し、2,506,757,548の得点を挙げています。
これらの数字だけでも十分に驚異的ですが、今年コミュニティー全体で「MLB The Show」シリーズをプレイした時間は、合計でなんと31,541年分に相当します。
今年はいくつかの課題もありましたが、今後数ヶ月の間に、私たちがさまざまな面でどのような改善を行なってきたのかを皆さんにお見せできればと考えています。皆さんの体験談、フィードバック、創造性、情熱は、私たちがこの仕事に取り組む理由を改めて思い出させてくれます。皆さんが費やしてくださる時間、寄せてくださる信頼とご支援を、私たちは決して当たり前だとは考えておりません。これほどまでにゲームを大切にしてくださるコミュニティーに恵まれていることを、とても幸せに感じています。
San Diego Studioのスタッフ一同、今年も忘れられない1年を共に過ごしてくださった皆さんに、心より感謝申し上げます。これからの展開に胸を躍らせながら、皆さんと共にこの旅を続けていけることをとても楽しみにしています。
『MLB The Show 27』（英語版）に備えよう
『MLB The Show 27』（英語版）に導入される魅力的な新機能や改善点を、皆さんに早くお伝えしたいところですが、詳しい内容を明かすのはもう少し先になります。しかし、今すぐお伝えしたいことがひとつあります。それは、『MLB The Show 27』（英語版）で、Tech Test（技術テスト）が正式に復活するということです！
Tech Testは、ゲームの開発過程において非常に重要な役割を果たします。開発チームはTech Testを通じてバグを特定し、パフォーマンスを評価し、ゲームの正式リリース前にコミュニティーから有意義なフィードバックを集めることができます。
ここで、皆さんは疑問に思うかもしれません。「Tech Testがそれほど重要なら、なぜ『MLB The Show 23』（英語版）を最後に実施していないのか？」と。それは、もっともな疑問です。
ゲーム開発は決して簡単なものではなく、毎年さまざまな難しい判断や、優先順位の調整を迫られます。そうした判断の結果、ここ数年はTech Testの実施を見送っていました。皆さんのご理解と情熱、そして変わらぬご支援に心より感謝しています。開発プロセスに皆さんが再び参加してくださることを楽しみにしています。Tech Testの詳細は、年明けに改めてお知らせいたします。
“Now & Laterパック”が復活！ 『MLB The Show 26』を引き続きプレイしよう
『MLB The Show 26』には、まだまだお楽しみが待っています。“Now & Laterパック”も復活し、『MLB The Show 26』（英語版）をプレイすることで、『MLB The Show 27』を有利にスタートすることができます。
“Now & Laterパック”には、『MLB The Show 26』（英語版）で”Now（いますぐ）”使えるアイテムが含まれているほか、『MLB The Show 27』で”Later（あとから）“使える”The Showパック“を追加料金なしで引き換えられる特典も付いています。
コンソール版『MLB The Show 26』（英語版）のDiamond Dynastyのコンテンツをプレイして“Now & Laterパック”を獲得すると、1パックにつき“Now & Laterバウチャー”が1枚付与されます。このバウチャーは、コンソール版『MLB The Show 27』（英語版）の通常版リリース時に同作へログインした際、自動的に“The Showパック”へと引き換えられます。必ず『MLB The Show 26』（英語版）で“Now & Laterパック”を獲得したアカウントと同じアカウントで『MLB The Show 27』（英語版）にログインしてください。
今回お伝えする情報は以上です。『MLB The Show 26』（英語版）で“Now & Laterパック”を集めて、一足早く次回作に備えましょう。

				Product Development Communications and Brand Strategist, San Diego Studio			
Before we dive into new information on MLB The Show 27, let’s take a look back.
As we reflect on 2026, two things stand out above everything else: the incredible passion and support of the MLB The Show community, and our need to better communicate and react to community feedback going forward. Whether you’ve been playing for decades or joined us for the first time this year, you’ve helped make this another special chapter in our franchise’s history.
Community stats from MLB The Show 26
While we’re proud of what our team accomplished this year, we recognize that the heart of MLB The Show has always been its community. Since launch, you’ve hit an astonishing:
1,058,402,460 home runs
55,168,197 triples
1,639,448,296 singles
You’ve also drawn 123,300,616 walks and racked up a staggering 2,130,551,161 strikeouts. It seems we have some work to do on our collective plate discipline and pitch recognition!
But it’s not all strikeouts. You’ve also recorded 18,666,758 Perfect-Perfect hits and scored an incredible 2,506,757,548 runs.
And if those numbers weren’t impressive enough, the community collectively spent the equivalent of 31,541 years playing MLB The Show this year.
This year was not without some issues and hopefully over the next few months we’ll show you how we’ve made improvements in a host of areas.Your stories, feedback, creativity, and passion remind us why we do what we do. We don’t take your time, trust, or support for granted, and we’re incredibly fortunate to have a community that cares so deeply about the game.
From all of us at San Diego Studio, thank you for another unforgettable year. We’re excited about what lies ahead and look forward to continuing this journey together.
Get ready for MLB The Show 27
So, what lies ahead? We’re eager to share the exciting new features and improvements coming to MLB The Show 27, but it’s still a little too early to reveal what’s in store. However, there’s one announcement we can’t wait to make: The Tech Test is officially returning for MLB The Show 27. 
Tech Tests are invaluable to the development process. They help our team identify bugs, evaluate performance, and gather meaningful feedback from the community before the game officially launches.
Now, you might be wondering: If Tech Tests are so important, why haven’t we held one since MLB The Show 23? That’s a fair question.
Game development is challenging, and each year brings its own set of difficult decisions and competing priorities. Some of those decisions resulted in us not holding Tech Tests for the past few years. We appreciate your patience, passion, and continued support, and we’re looking forward to having you be part of the process again. We will provide more details on the Tech Test in the new year. 
Now & Later Packs return, so keep playing MLB The Show 26
There is still more ahead for 
MLB The Show 26
, and the time you spend playing can give you a head start in MLB The Show 27. Now & Later packs are coming back.
Now & Later Packs contain items you can use now in MLB The Show 26, with the added bonus of allowing you to redeem The Show Packs “later” in MLB The Show 27 at no additional cost.
Each Now & Later Pack you earn playing Diamond Dynasty Content in MLB The Show 26 on console will give out a Now & Later voucher that will automatically be redeemed into The Show Packs when logging into MLB The Show 27 on console during its standard release. Make sure you log into ‘27 with the same account you used to earn Now & Later Packs in ‘26.
While that’s all for today, in the meantime, get a head start and start earning those Now & Later Packs in MLB The Show 26.
We look forward to sharing more soon and are excited about what’s to come in 2027. In the meantime, for the latest MLB The Show news, head to 
TheShow.com
, sign up for 
The Scouting Report
, and follow our official social channels.
MLB The Show 27
San Diego Studio

MLB The Show 27
 has officially been announced, with San Diego Studio sharing the first details about its next annual baseball release. Although the developer isn’t ready to reveal new gameplay features or major improvements just yet, it has confirmed the return of a community testing program that hasn’t been part of the franchise for several years. The announcement also gives players an early reason to keep playing 
MLB The Show 26
, with rewards carrying over into next year’s installment.
			Videos by ComicBook.com		
In a 
new post on the official PlayStation Blog
, San Diego Studio’s Ramone Russell reflected on the past year before outlining some of the plans for 2027. Russell acknowledged that the studio needs to improve how it communicates with players and responds to community feedback, suggesting that the developer is looking to address some of the concerns raised throughout the latest release.
MLB The Show 27 Brings Back Tech Tests and Now & Later Packs
Image courtesy of San Diego Studios
One of the biggest announcements is that Tech Tests are officially returning for 
MLB The Show 27
. The program allows players to get hands-on with an upcoming release before launch, helping San Diego Studio identify bugs and collect feedback that can inform further development. The feature hasn’t been available since 
MLB The Show 23
, with the studio explaining that difficult development decisions and competing priorities prevented it from holding tests over the past few years. More information about the next Tech Test is expected to arrive sometime in the new year.
San Diego Studio also shared some impressive statistics from the 
MLB The Show 26
 community. Since launch, players have collectively hit more than 1 billion home runs and recorded over 1.6 billion singles, alongside approximately 123 million walks. The community has also scored more than 2.5 billion runs, with players spending the equivalent of 31,541 years playing the latest installment. These numbers offer a glimpse into how active the franchise’s player base remains, even as the studio begins shifting its attention toward next year’s game.
Image courtesy of San Diego Studios
Players who aren’t ready to move on from 
MLB The Show 26
 will also have another incentive to keep playing. 
Now & Later Packs
 are returning, allowing Diamond Dynasty players to earn items for the current game alongside vouchers that can be redeemed for The Show Packs in 
MLB The Show 27
. Packs earned through Diamond Dynasty content on console will automatically convert when players log into the next installment during its standard release. However, players must use the same account across both games to receive their rewards.
San Diego Studio is keeping most of 
MLB The Show 27
‘s new features and improvements under wraps. The developer says it looks forward to sharing more information in the coming months, giving players concrete information and a better idea of what to expect when the next entry arrives in 2027.

Is there an MLB The Show 27 Tech Test?
Yes. In the 
PlayStation Blog post
, San Diego Studio's Ramone Russell says the Tech Test "is officially returning for MLB The Show 27". It's the first Tech Test since MLB The Show 23. Details on dates, platforms and how to sign up are coming "in the new year".
How do Now & Later Packs work in MLB The Show 27?
Packs you earn in 
MLB The Show 26's Diamond Dynasty on console
 give you a voucher. When you log in to MLB The Show 27 on console at its standard release, the voucher turns into The Show Packs. Two catches: it has to be the 
same account
 you used in '26, and it works on console. So it's worth keeping up with Diamond Dynasty this season.
When is the MLB The Show 27 release date?
It hasn't been announced. The studio also hasn't named a cover athlete or confirmed platforms, editions, prices or early access, and says it's "still a little too early" to reveal new features. Recent MLB The Show games have launched in March, ahead of the MLB season, but that's a pattern, not an announcement. We'll update this page when San Diego Studio confirms the date.

 and 
2,130,551,161 strikeouts
 so far.
Is there an MLB The Show 27 trailer?
