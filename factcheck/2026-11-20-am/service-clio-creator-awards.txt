Last night at the iconic Dolby Theater in downtown Los Angeles, the biggest brands, creators and leaders across the industry gathered to celebrate the inaugural 
Clio Creator
 awards. As the presenting sponsor of the awards and home to the creators shaping culture today, YouTube is proud to champion the craft and expertise that goes into creator partnerships and creator-led work.
YouTube creators were at the center of the celebration, taking home seven Grand Clio awards. With more than 3 million creators in the YouTube Partner Program and brand deals growing by double digits year over year, it’s clear brands and creators are continuing to find business success on YouTube.
Grand Clio winners for brand and creator collaborations included:
iShowSpeed
 x
Expedia
 - Grand Winner, Integrated Campaign:
Fans around the world are captivated by iShowSpeed’s unscripted travel streams, so Expedia teamed up with the creator for three epic 12-hour live streams, including a single-day sprint across five Caribbean countries. Expedia integrated directly into the streams to show how easy it was to book travel and the videos drove 
9.6 million views in just the first 24 hours
 alone.
Michelle Khare,
Challenge Accepted: I Tried Tom Cruise's Deadliest Stunt
 - Grand Winner, Creator & Brand Collaborations:
In partnership with Adobe and Red Bull, Michelle Khare attempted to become the second person after Tom Cruise to perform the famous plane hang from 
Mission: Impossible - Rogue Nation.
MrBeast
,
I Granted 100 Kids Their Biggest Wish
- Grand Winner, Public Relations:
In partnership with Disney and to raise funds for 
Stand Up to Cancer
, the most-subscribed YouTube creator granted the wishes of 100 kids who had been impacted by childhood cancer, giving them the awesome days they all deserved.
Animated short of bruised characters like pinatas in a support group
Spaceskits
 x
Morgan & Morgan
,
The Injury Club
- Grand Winner, Written Content:
Legal disclaimers don’t usually grab attention from viewers. Morgan & Morgan decided to change that by giving Cliff Benfield, the animator behind Spaceskits, the creative freedom to take inject humor into disclaimers and gather bruised everyday characters—from piñatas and punching bags to scarecrows and crash-test dummies—into a witty YouTube Short.
Jordan The Stallion
 x
Blenders Eyewear
,
Jordan is the CCO
 - Grand Winner, Creative Execution:
Blenders Eyewear took creator partnership a step further by naming Jordan The Stallion Chief Content Officer and embedding him directly into the business. Working out of Blenders HQ, shopping in stores, collaborating on product design, and training the in-house marketing team on how he crafts content, Jordan helped shape the brand’s creative vision from the inside out.
Grand Clio winners for creators included:
Kylie Kelce,
Not Gonna Lie
 - Grand Winner, Podcasting:
Despite her best efforts to remain on the sidelines, being part of football’s favorite family means the world has found out about Kylie Kelce. Each week on 
Not Gonna Lie,
 Kylie shares her personal story beyond being just a “football wife,” setting the record straight on gossip and speaking her truths on topics like modern parenting, social media trends, women in sports and more.
fern
,
The Taliban Highway: From the Last Nomads to the New Silk Road
- Grand Winner, Video:
This groundbreaking documentary reported first-hand impressions from one of the most remote regions in the world, providing a human perspective to life and political conflict in Afghanistan.
A huge congratulations to every creator, brand, and agency honored last night. Spanning across Shorts, live streams, podcast and cinematic content for the biggest screens, these winners show that creators on YouTube are taking their content and brand partnerships to new heights.

The Clios expanded its recognitions beyond excellence in advertising and marketing to honor creators with its inaugural Clio Creator Awards this year. 
Host Terry Crews ushered the audience through the show Wednesday night as creators were awarded for their notable impact videos, brand partnerships and creative executions from the past year. 
The awards show streamed live on YouTube from the Dolby Theatre with special performances from Julien Cohen and his five-piece band, a sweet moment between Crews and Internet-famous Brodie the dog and a live rendition of Romeo Bingham’s viral Dr. Pepper jingle.
Among the night’s top honors, Bingham was named Creator of the Year following her breakout success that helped catapult her career. The soda company was named Brand of the Year, while Deutsch, the creative agency behind the viral ad, was recognized as Creative Agency of the Year.

“Creators aren’t just participating in culture, they’re shaping it,” said Nicole Purcell, CEO of the Clios. “The inaugural Clio Creator Awards recognize the incredible talent and originality driving this industry forward, from creators building their own communities to brands finding new ways to connect with audiences. These are storytellers whose influence extends far beyond their platforms, and we’re proud to give them the recognition they deserve.”
The organization also handed out two speciality awards. Dhar Mann took home the 2026 Clio Creator Impact Award for his growth in the kids and family sector. The creator announced partnerships with Fox Creator Studios and Disney within the past year.  
Meanwhile, the 2026 Clio Creator Rising Star Award, which honored an emerging creator who has demonstrated exceptional creative craft, rapid breakthrough momentum and distinct cultural influence, was presented to short-form storyteller 
Grace Wells
.
Other notable creator winners included Best Creator & Brand Collaborations for Michelle Khare for her Challenge Accepted series “I Tried Tom Cruise’s Deadliest Stunt” in collaboration with Red Bull.
Jordan Stallion won best creative execution for his “From Glasses Thrower to Chief Content Officer” advertisement by Greenlight Group. Kylie Kelce took home the podcasting award for “Not Gonna Lie with Kylie Kelce,” and Wave Sports & Entertainment accepted on her behalf.
MrBeast’s Make a Wish campaign video “I Granted 100 Kids Their Biggest Wish!” won for best public relations, while fern’s “The Taliban Highway: From the Last Nomads to the New Silk Road” won for best video. 
Clio Creators accepted submissions across Instagram, LinkedIn, Snapchat, TikTok, YouTube and other emerging content creation platforms and were voted on by a committee of creators, advertisers and more.

Clio Creators
, a new global awards program honoring creative excellence in digital content, revealed the 
2026 Clio Creator Award
 winners last night during a ceremony hosted by actor and TV presenter 
Terry Crews
 at the Dolby Theatre in Hollywood. The event was streamed live.
Among the night’s top honors, 
Romeo Bingham
 was named 
Creator of the Year
 following her breakout success with the viral Dr Pepper jingle that helped catapult her career. 
Dr Pepper
 earned 
Brand of the Year,
 while the brand’s partner 
Deutsch
 was recognized as 
Creative Agency of the Year
.
Presented by 
YouTube
 and powered by 
Influential
 with 
MOCEAN
 as a creative partner, Clio Creators celebrates how digital content makers drive innovation and commerce through ideas that shape culture across platforms, brands, communities and industries.
“Creators aren’t just participating in culture, they’re shaping it,” said 
Nicole Purcell
, CEO of the Clios. “The inaugural Clio Creator Awards recognize the incredible talent and originality driving this industry forward, from creators building their own communities to brands finding new ways to connect with audiences. These are storytellers whose influence extends far beyond their platforms, and we’re proud to give them the recognition they deserve.”
The evening also celebrated two creators making a significant impact on the industry. The 2026 Clio Creator Impact Award was presented to 
Dhar Mann
, founder of Dhar Mann Studios, whose audience-first entertainment company reaches more than 170 million followers worldwide. The 2026 Clio Creator Rising Star Award honored short-form storyteller 
Grace Wells
, recognized for her “Making Epic Commercials for Random Objects” series and its distinctive blend of cinematic storytelling, humor and brand advertising.
Unlike other recognition programs in this space, Clio Creators accepts submissions across Instagram, LinkedIn, Snapchat, TikTok, YouTube and other emerging platforms. As more brands and agencies rely on creator-led content to reach their audience with messages that resonate, the Clio Creator Awards celebrate the creator’s contribution and point of view in a unique format that centers them as the award recipient.
The full list of Grand, Gold, Silver and Bronze Clio Creator Award recipients is available at
