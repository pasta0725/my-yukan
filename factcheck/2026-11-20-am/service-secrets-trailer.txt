The Past Doesn't Stay Buried:  Sha Ine Febriyanti and Abimana Aryasatya Face Off in Netflix's 'Secrets'
Entertainment
19 November 2026
Global
Indonesia
Link copied to clipboard
Some people are impossible to forget. Others are impossible to escape.
Sixteen years ago, Parni (Sha Ine Febriyanti) walked away from an abusive relationship and the man who once controlled her life. Now a single mother, she has built a new life for herself and her daughter, leaving behind a past she hoped never to revisit.
But when Jamal (Abimana Aryasatya) returns, he brings more than painful memories. Driven by revenge and armed with knowledge of her deepest secrets, he threatens to unravel everything Parni has fought to protect.
In Netflix's upcoming Indonesian psychological thriller 
Secrets
, the most dangerous person isn't always a stranger. Sometimes, it's the one who knows you best.

He Knows Her Past. She Knows What He's Capable Of.
At the heart of 
Secrets
 is a dangerous game of power and control between two people bound by a deeply troubled history.
Two of Indonesia's most acclaimed actors, 
Sha Ine Febriyanti 
and 
Abimana Aryasatya
, appear together for the first time as Parni and Jamal, who are locked in a confrontation where every decision has consequences.
Like a game of chess, their relationship is defined by shifting power dynamics, calculated moves, and an intimate knowledge of each other's vulnerabilities. But while Jamal is determined to reclaim control over the woman who escaped him, Parni is no longer the same woman he once knew.
For Abimana, Jamal's cruelty lies not only in his capacity for violence, but also in the possessiveness and manipulation that shape his relationships.
"For Jamal, ownership is everything. Throughout the film, we see how he regards his partners as objects that belong to him
,
" Abimana explained.
Secrets unit still
She Escaped Him Once. This Time, She's Fighting Back.
While Jamal represents a past that refuses to disappear, Parni is determined to not let that past define her future.
Told through Parni's perspective, 
Secrets
 explores the lasting psychological effects of abuse without reducing its central character to her suffering. Beneath her carefully maintained composure is a woman shaped by trauma, but also driven by fierce determination to protect the life she has rebuilt.
For Sha Ine, portraying Parni meant navigating the tension between vulnerability and strength, revealing a character whose survival instincts gradually resurface as the danger around her grows.
“I had to convey how trauma can remain embedded in a person’s body, even years after the experience. After experiencing trauma, Parni doesn’t move much because she is in a state of freeze. It’s as though everything is turned inward,” she said.
As Jamal closes in, Parni must confront the man she once escaped — and decide how far she is willing to go.

When Survival Becomes a Psychological Thriller
Following his acclaimed films 
27 Steps of May
 and 
Jermal
, filmmaker Ravi Bharwani returns with 
Secrets
, marking his first venture into the psychological thriller genre.
Inspired by 
Rahasia dari Kramat Tunggak
 (
Secrets from Kramat Tunggak
), a short story by Indonesian author Dewi Kharisma Michellia, the film draws from the world of Jakarta's Kramat Tunggak red-light district in the 1980s. The story's vivid atmosphere and complex characters left a lasting impression on Ravi, inspiring him to bring its world to the screen.
“When I read the short story, I could feel the mud, smell the odors and see the color red. I wanted to stay true to that concept,” Ravi shared.
With 
Secrets
, Bharwani brings a darker, more suspenseful dimension to his storytelling. 
The newly released main trailer offers a closer look at the escalating confrontation between Parni and Jamal, where the past threatens to collide with the present and every secret carries a consequence.
Also starring Shenina Cinnamon and Ruth Marini, 
Secrets
 premieres globally on December 3, 2026, only on Netflix.

The story unfolds from Parni's perspective as she fights to protect the life she has rebuilt for herself and her daughter.
