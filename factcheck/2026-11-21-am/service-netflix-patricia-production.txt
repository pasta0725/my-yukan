Netflix Announces 'Patricia,' a Film Produced by and Starring Penélope Cruz and Directed by Juan Diego Botto

Global

STARRING AND PRODUCED BY PENÉLOPE CRUZ WRITTEN AND DIRECTED BY JUAN DIEGO BOTTO
Patricia
 is the first film produced by Moonlyon, the audiovisual company created by Penélope Cruz in partnership with IMAGINA
 Download the image
Netflix today announced the start of production on 
Patricia
, the second film written and directed by 
Juan Diego Botto
 and starring 
Penélope Cruz
 (
La Bola Negra, Bunker, Volver, Pain and Glory)
  who also produces the film through her audiovisual company Moonlyon. The two reunite after 
En los márgenes
.
Penélope Cruz
 plays 
Patricia
, a woman who escapes the hell of a twelve-year abusive relationship only to face another hell in the courts, where she fears for the wellbeing of her children.
The cast also includes 
Goya Toledo
 (
Amores Perros, Marsella
),
 Rafa Castejón
 (
The Good Boss
), 
Pilar Gómez
 (
Tarde para la ira
), 
Dylan Radley
 (
The Nest
) and 
Ramón Barea
 (
Lullaby, Everybody Knows
), among others.
In the words of Juan Diego Botto
: 
"After two years of writing and a long research process, which included hours of interviews with women who trusted us with their stories, it is a privilege for me to tell this story with this cast and this creative team."
In the words of Penélope Cruz
: 
"After more than two years developing what will be the first narrative feature from my production company, Moonlyon, and just about to start shooting, I feel enormous excitement. It has been a beautiful and intense journey alongside my dear Juan Diego Botto. Juan has written a wonderful script, and it is an honor to tell this story with him. It is a very necessary story, and an immense privilege to play this character. Patricia represents so many other women who, like her, suffer this kind of violence every day. I am also very grateful for the incredible response from Netflix in Spain since they read the script. This is my first film with them, and we couldn’t be happier with the care and attention they have shown us."
In the words of Diego Ávalos
: 
"Some stories matter to us for what they tell and how they are told, and Patricia is one of those rare and unforgettable stories: brave and courageous, brought to life by an extraordinary team. I’m truly honored to join Juan Diego Botto and Penélope Cruz in their first film for Netflix in Spain, and for a story born at home to move audiences here and around the world."
About Moonlyon
In 2022, actress Penélope Cruz announced the launch of her production company, Moonlyon, under the umbrella of IMAGINA (the new name for Grup Mediapro as of January 1). It is an international, independent label dedicated to producing both fiction and non-fiction content, allowing the actress to take her career a step further by leading new areas of the industry. Moonlyon has several projects in development led by the actress, and Patricia, directed by Juan Diego Botto, is its first narrative feature.
