iPhone Duoで「顔認証によるロック解除」が追加へ　iOS 27.3 Beta 2で確認、ただしFace IDとは別物
»
2026年11月20日 18時25分 公開
[
田中聡
，
ITmedia
]
　iOSの次期バージョン「iOS 27.3」のパブリックβ版（Beta 2）が配信され、「iPhone Duo」の顔認証によるロック解除が利用可能になった。
iOS 27.3 Beta 2が配信されている
　iPhone Duoの生体認証はこれまでTouch ID（指紋認証）のみで、Face IDには対応していない。ロックを解除するにはTouch IDに指を当てるか、パスコードを入力する必要があり、これまでFace ID対応のiPhoneを使っていた人からすると利便性が大きく低下した。この顔認証によるロック解除が正式実装されるとしたら、iPhone Duoユーザーにとっては朗報といえる。
　具体的な設定方法を見ていこう。iOS 27.3 Beta 2では、iPhone Duoのロック解除に新たな項目が追加されている。「設定」→「Touch IDとパスコード」にアクセスすると、「Unlock with Front Camera」という項目が追加されているので、これをオンにする。すると、注意書きや顔認証のセットアップについての説明が現れるので、顔を登録していく。
「Touch IDとパスコード」の「Unlock with Front Camera」から顔の登録を進めていく
　頭を「左」「右」「上」「下」の4方向に動かし、最後に正面を見ると登録が完了する。Face IDは頭で円を描くように登録していき、顔全体を精密に読み取っていることが分かるが、今回の顔認証は4方向のみと簡易的なものにとどまる。4つの角度から顔を学習することで精度が高まるが、4つ全ての角度から登録できない場合、一部の角度のみで登録することも可能だという。
4つの角度から登録すればよい
　顔認証で利用するのはアウターディスプレイ側のインカメラで、インナーディスプレイのインカメラでは登録できない。もちろん、顔認証によるロック解除もアウターディスプレイのインカメラでしか行えない。
インナーディスプレイのインカメラでは登録も解除もできない
　マスクを装着して試してみたが、「顔の一部が覆われている」という理由で登録できなかった。Face IDで可能な、マスクを着用した状態での登録方法は用意されていない。登録できる顔情報は1つのみで、顔認証を登録してからオフにして再度オンにすると、再び顔を登録する必要がある。この点はβ版ということで仕様が固まっていないのかもしれないが、一度登録した顔情報の設定は保持してほしいところだ。
マスクを装着した状態では、3つの角度までは認識されたが、最終的には登録できなかった
　顔を登録した後にロック状態から画面をタップしたところ、インカメラ下の鍵アイコンが解錠され、速やかにロックが解除された。
顔を登録した後は、ロック画面に顔認証のアイコンが現れる
　なお、今回の顔認証はTouch IDやFace IDよりも安全ではない旨が、顔の登録前に表示される画面に明記されている。Face IDで使用する「TrueDepthカメラ」は、何千ものドットを顔の上に投射して顔の深度マップを作成した上で正確なデータを読み取る。そのため、紙の写真や顔のデジタルデータでは本人と認識せず、なりすましを防げる。
　対する顔認証で使うiPhone DuoのインカメラはTrueDepthカメラではなく、立体的な顔の情報を読み取るものではないと推察される。試しに登録時に、他のスマートフォンに表示した自分の顔をカメラに向け、「一部の角度のみで続ける」オプションを選んだところ、登録できてしまった。なお、実物の顔を登録し直した後に、顔写真の画像を読み込ませてロック解除することはできなかった。
他のスマートフォンに表示した写真でも登録できてしまった
　Face IDよりも安全性で劣るため、顔認証で利用できるのは画面ロック解除のみで、Apple Payやアプリ起動時のロック解除などでは従来通りTouch IDを利用する必要がある。また顔認証は一定時間がたつと無効になり、再度パスコードやTouch IDでロックを解除する必要がある。実際にiPhone Duoを約45分放置したところ、顔認証でロック解除できなくなった。
　今回の顔認証は、あくまで簡易的なロック解除の手段として使う形になるが、Face IDが恋しいiPhone Duoユーザーのストレスを軽減するアップデートといえる。

Apple has released iOS 27.3 public beta 2, bringing Face Unlock to the iPhone Duo, smarter Siri AI and a clearer picture of how Apple Intelligence usage limits will work. 
iOS 27.3 Public Beta 2: What’s New
Apple released iOS 27.3 public beta 2 two weeks after the first public beta arrived on November 5, 2026. It brings most of the features we saw in 
iOS 27.3 developer beta 2
 earlier this week, and Apple has also pushed out a revised beta 2 build for developers.
Image Credit: X/ @Aaron Zollo
Image Credit: X/ @Aaron Zollo
The biggest additions are for the 
Apple iPhone Duo
. A new Unlock with Front Camera option for the iPhone Duo lets you unlock the phone using the selfie camera on the outer display. But this only works when the phone is folded. 
You’ll still need Touch ID or your passcode for Apple Pay, passwords and app authentication, and Touch ID is required again if you haven’t unlocked the phone for 45 minutes.
Image Credit: MacRumors
Image Credit: MacRumors
There’s also a new Attention Aware feature for the outer display, which keeps the screen awake while you’re looking at it and lowers the volume of some alerts. You don’t need to set up Face Unlock to use it. Apple has also added a separate option that locks the iPhone Duo as soon as you close it.
Additionally, 
Siri AI
 gets smarter in this beta. It can now answer questions about your Health data, such as your step count, heart rate and Activity rings. 
It can also use an entire Safari webpage as context, including the parts you haven’t scrolled to yet, so you can ask questions about long articles without reading through them first.
Image Credit: Apple
Image Credit: Apple
iOS 27.3 public beta 2 also brings Apple’s new usage limits for Siri AI and other 
Apple Intelligence features
. Plans with 200 GB or more of iCloud+ storage get higher daily limits, while the 50 GB plan sticks with the standard limits. 
Other Changes in iOS 27.3 Public Beta 2
The update also brings a few smaller changes:
Calendar:
 The search and new-event buttons have moved to the bottom of the app.
Apple TV:
 Adds video podcasts and a feed of vertical clips from Apple Originals.
Apple News+:
 Puzzles are expanding to Australia, French Canada and the UK, though the available puzzles vary by region.

===== https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/16/apple-adds-face-unlock-to-the-iphone-duo-but-its-not-face-id/
(status 200)
One of the top complaints from tech reviewers about Apple’s new foldable, the iPhone Duo, has been its reliance on Touch ID, a technology that’s been largely replaced by Face ID across the company’s iPhone lineup.
With Monday’s release of iOS 27.3 beta for developers, Apple is addressing that complaint with a new “face unlock” feature that lets iPhone Duo users unlock their phones using the front camera, rather than waiting for a future hardware upgrade.
To be clear, this feature is not Face ID.
Due to hardware constraints, the iPhone Duo shipped without support for Face ID technology primarily because its thin, foldable design doesn’t offer enough space for the TrueDepth sensor array that Face ID requires. 
A recent report from Bloomberg 
suggests that the company is working to change that in a future version of the iPhone Duo. In the meantime, the Duo uses Touch ID for unlocking, authentication, and Apple Pay.
The new face unlock feature, however, will allow users to quickly unlock the device using the front camera when the phone is closed. It only works if users have recently authenticated with Touch ID or a passcode. Apple notes that Apple Pay and passwords will still require Touch ID, and users will have to authenticate with Touch ID again after 45 minutes of inactivity.
The move is meant to make it easier for people to use the Duo in the way they’re accustomed to, after years of unlocking an iPhone just by looking at it.
Apple isn’t going so far as to allow face unlock to work across the entire device, though, because the technology isn’t as secure as Face ID. The latter uses Apple’s TrueDepth 3D sensing hardware, which includes a flood illuminator and a dot projector that casts over 30,000 invisible infrared dots onto the user’s face to build a unique 3D map of the face. The system combines this map with attention-awareness checks, making it resistant to attempts to fool it with photos or other 2D images.
The software update addresses another complaint by incorporating 
one of the “hacks” iPhone Duo early adopters were using 
to force their phone to lock when they shut it. That involved creating a shortcut that would automatically run when the phone was closed. Now, Apple says users can enable this feature directly in Settings, under Display and Brightness.
Alongside the software update, Apple announced that more than 200,000 apps are now optimized for the iPhone Duo, up from 
over 100,000 earlier this month
.
Other new features in this latest software release include an option to ask Siri questions about the content of a web page, even portions that aren’t visible without scrolling. Apple is also introducing video podcasts in Apple Podcasts in the Apple TV app, catching up with rivals like YouTube and Spotify.
Apple is also expanding access to Apple News puzzles for News+ subscribers in Australia, the U.K., and for French-Canadian users. Australian News+ subscribers will get access to a daily quick crossword, crossword mini, and Sudoku, as well as Apple’s original puzzles, Quartiles and 
Emoji Game
. U.K. users will get the quick crossword, the crossword mini, and Sudoku, while French-Canadian subscribers will get access to Apple’s original puzzles.
The iOS 27.3 software update is currently available to developers and will soon arrive for public beta testers. It will roll out more broadly to all users after further testing is complete.

iOS 27.3 adds new face unlock feature for iPhone Duo
Chance Miller
 | Nov 16 2026 - 10:13 am PT																
								18 Comments							
In today’s iOS 27.3 beta release, Apple is introducing two changes for iPhone Duo users. Most notably, it adds a new face recognition option that lets you unlock your iPhone Duo by looking at it.
Apple is also adding an option to have your iPhone Duo lock when you close it.
Face unlock comes to iPhone Duo
You can enable the new option to unlock your iPhone Duo with the front camera in Settings, under Touch ID & Passcode, after updating to iOS 27.3. When enabled, your iPhone Duo will unlock after recognizing your face.
In the Settings app, Apple says: 
Use the front camera, along with a recent Touch ID or passcode authentication, to quickly unlock iPhone Duo when it’s closed. Face recognition data never leaves your device. 
This isn’t Face ID. This new option on iPhone Duo uses the front camera on the outer iPhone Duo display to recognize your face. The iPhone Duo doesn’t have the hardware needed for Face ID.
Because this isn’t as secure as Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode, Apple has implemented a few guardrails. First, the setting only works to unlock your iPhone Duo. To use Apple Pay, access your passwords, unlock apps, and do other things that require authentication, you’ll have to use Touch ID or enter your passcode.
This face recognition feature also has a time-based limitation. After 45 minutes of inactivity, you have to authenticate with Touch ID or your passcode. That is, if you go 45 minutes without unlocking your iPhone Duo, the facial recognition feature is disabled until you enter your passcode or use Touch ID.
Finally, this new feature also doesn’t work when using the iPhone Duo unfolded. When unfolded, you still have to use Touch ID or your passcode to unlock the device.
Lock when closed
Second, iOS 27.3 adds an option to lock your iPhone Duo when you close it. You can enable this in Settings, under Display & Brightness. Once enabled, your iPhone Duo will lock when you fold, rather than transition from the inner display to the outer display.
Many iPhone Duo users (myself included) have said Apple should do this. Personally, I’ve been 
using a shortcut
 to mimic this behavior, but I’m glad to see that Apple has listened to user feedback and added a default setting for it.
