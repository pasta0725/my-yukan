Netflix Releases Trailer for 'Jão: Tales From the Void,' a Documentary Featuring Unprecedented Access to the Singer, Premiering December 3
Entertainment
19 November 2026
Global
Brazil
Link copied to clipboard
 The production follows intimate moments in the artist’s life, from his career break and the grief over his father’s death to his long-awaited return to the stage
Netflix today unveiled the trailer for 
Jão: Tales From the Void
, a documentary premiering on December 3 that offers audiences an exclusive glimpse into a more intimate side of the singer, one of the most celebrated artists of Brazil’s new pop generation. Created and produced by UFO Sounds, the film follows Jão through one of the most vulnerable chapters of his journey, shaped by a pause in his career, the grief of losing his father, and the lead-up to a long-awaited reunion with fans in an epic live show.
Between the spotlight and the silence, the stage and the life beyond it, the trailer reveals fragments of a journey in which life and art become deeply intertwined. Away from the energy of live performances and the demands of constant public exposure, Jão finds space to turn inward, process his emotions, and reconsider his relationship with music. The film is a portrait of an artist in transformation, revealing what unfolds behind the scenes of a career when life calls for a pause — culminating in his most mature album yet and a triumphant return.
Jão: Tales From the Void
 premieres on December 3, only on Netflix.

Netflix has set December 3, 2026, for Jão: Tales from the Void. The documentary is listed as 
Jão: Memórias Póstumas
 in Brazil. Netflix’s new trailer follows the singer through a break from public life, the loss of his father, work on an album and a return to the stage.
Where to find the trailer and watch the film
Netflix’s title page
 is the place to look for the trailer and the film itself. The December 3 date comes from Netflix’s Brazilian announcement. Netflix’s English press release confirms the name 
Tales from the Void
. The announcements give no exact streaming hour or full list of regions and subtitle languages.
The teaser and the new trailer are separate releases. The teaser debuted at Jão’s September 25 comeback show in São Paulo. The November trailer arrived with the December premiere date. That September show matters to the story too, since Netflix describes the film as following the period that led back to the stage.

How the album and the book fit in
Memórias Póstumas
 is also the name of Jão’s 14-track album, released on July 26, 2026. The documentary follows the making of that record among other events. If you want to hear the music before watching the film, 
the album’s tracklist on Apple Music
 is a useful starting point.
In a July interview with Hugo Gloss, Jão traced the album title to Machado de Assis and said he already had the idea for the title song in 2024, before his father died. That chronology matters because the loss became part of the record’s story after the idea had taken shape. Machado’s novel appears in English as 
The Posthumous Memoirs of Brás Cubas
. The literary connection is through the title, while Netflix’s film follows Jão’s own life.
