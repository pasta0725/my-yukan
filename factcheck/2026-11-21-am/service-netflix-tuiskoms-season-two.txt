Fleur and Werner are getting ready for forever, but life in Wilderness rarely goes to plan. Tuiskoms, 
Netflix
's Afrikaans-language romantic drama, is back for a second season. Here is when it arrives, who is in the cast and what the new season is about.
Tuiskoms season 2 release date and where to watch
Tuiskoms season 2 premieres on Netflix on Tuesday, December 8, 2026. Netflix 
South Africa
 announced the date, and an official 
trailer
 followed on November 19. The first season is streaming on Netflix. Netflix has not shared how many episodes the new season has.
Tuiskoms season 2 cast
Amalia Uys as Fleur
Armand Aucamp as Werner
Kim Syster
Jane de Wet
Clint Brink
Cantona James 
Zane Meas 
Cantona James and Zane Meas were reported as part of the cast by entertainment commentators on social media, and Netflix has not confirmed it. The characters played by the other cast members have not been mentioned.
Tuiskoms season 1 recap
The first season followed Fleur, a widow who leaves behind a failed restaurant in Gauteng and moves back to her parents' home in Wilderness, in the Western Cape, with her daughter. She takes over management of a flower shop owned by Werner, a charming womanizer she keeps clashing with, and the two slowly grow closer. By the final episode, Werner proposes to Fleur and she accepts.
What is Tuiskoms season 2 about?
Season 2 picks up with Fleur and Werner preparing for their 
wedding
, before their carefully planned future takes an unexpected turn. At the same time, a free-spirited woman with a complicated past returns to Wilderness after years away, hoping to reconnect with the family she left behind. According to the official synopsis, old wounds, new beginnings and unexpected connections reshape the lives of those who call the town home.
Tuiskoms season 2 creative team
The series was created by Louis Pretorius and Albert Snyman for Netflix, where it is the second original Afrikaans-language series. Season 1 was produced by Infinity Films and shot on location in Wilderness. The late Michelle Botes, who played Fleur's mother, Abigail, in season 1, passed away during its post-production.
With season 1 only seven episodes long, there is still time to catch up before the new season arrives.
