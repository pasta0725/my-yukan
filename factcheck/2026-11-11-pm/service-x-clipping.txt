===== https://www.eyerys.com/articles/news/x-starts-allowing-users-clip-short-pieces-long-videos-directly-inside-app
(status 200)
People have been pulling short bits out of long videos and sharing them online for years. 
Podcasts, interviews, and talks that run for 20 or 30 minutes often get broken into smaller pieces that spread more easily. Until recently, doing that on X usually meant downloading the video, cutting it in another app, and uploading the piece as a new post. 
Those clips did not always point back to the original video, and the person who first posted it did not earn anything from them.
X 
added a way to clip videos
 right inside the app. 
The tool works on pre-recorded videos that are at least two minutes long. It does not work on live streams. A user taps the repost button on the video, chooses the clip option, and slides the handles on a timeline to pick a section. 
The clip has to be between 5 and 60 seconds long, and it cannot be longer than 30 percent of the full video. 
A two-minute video can produce a clip of up to 36 seconds. Longer videos can support a full 60-second clip.
Once the user picks the part they want, they add their own text and post it. The clip shows up in the timeline like any other video. It includes a label that says "Clip from" and links back to the original video at the exact moment that was clipped. 
Anyone can tap that label and jump straight to that point in the full video.
If the person who made the original video is in X's Original Content Rewards program, they can earn from views on the clips. 
The person who makes the clip can also earn from views on their own post, as long as they are in the same program. Original Content Rewards is the system X uses to pay people for views on their posts. 
It looks mainly at views from people who pay for Premium and see the post in their main feed. Clips do not count toward a separate bonus that exists for some video creators.
Right now the feature is only in the X apps for iPhone and Android. X has said the web version is coming later. 
Creators cannot turn the feature off for their own videos yet, though X has mentioned that an option for this is being worked on. If someone deletes the original video, the clips made from it disappear too. Some people said the clip option did not show up for them right away even after updating the app.
This change removes the need to download a video and edit it somewhere else. It also keeps the clip connected to the original post, so viewers can find the full video more easily, and both the original poster and the person who made the clip can receive payments if they are enrolled in the rewards program.

X on Monday introduced a new video clipping feature as part of its efforts to attract more influencers and strengthen its creator ecosystem. The feature enables users to choose scenes from a longer video, trim them into smaller sections and upload them straight to the platform.
