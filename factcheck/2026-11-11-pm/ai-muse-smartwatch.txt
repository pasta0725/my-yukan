MetaのAIエージェント「Muse」がApple WatchとWear OSに対応　手首から音声で呼び出し
»

　米Metaは11月9日（現地時間）、パーソナルAIエージェント「Muse」が米Appleの「Apple Watch」と米Googleの「Wear OS」搭載スマートウォッチに対応したと
公式Xアカウントで発表
した。同社の最高AI責任者（CAIO）、アレクサンダー・ワン氏も
Xに投稿
した。
 Metaの公式発表投稿
　Museは、Metaが開発するAIアシスタントサービス。米OpenAIの「ChatGPT」やGoogleの「Gemini」のように質問に答えたり、文章を作成したりできるほか、ユーザーの指示に応じて外部サービスと連携し、さまざまな作業を代行するAIエージェントとしての機能も備える。特徴の1つは、かわいらしいキャラクターを使った対話機能で、キャラクターの表情や動きを見ながら音声で会話できることだ。10月23日には日本でも提供が始まった。今回のスマートウォッチ対応により、スマートフォンを取り出さずにMuseに話しかけたり、作業を依頼したりできるようになる。
　スマートウォッチ版では、文字盤のコンプリケーションからMuseを起動し、音声やテキストで対話できる。両アプリの紹介画像では、映画のチケット予約を依頼する様子が示されており、Wear OS版では、会議と歯科医院の予約が重なっていることをMuseが知らせ、予定の変更を提案する場面も紹介されている。
 Apple Watch版Museの画面。文字盤からの起動やキャラクターとの対話、映画チケットの予約などが可能（画像：App Storeより）
 Wear OS版Museの画面。映画のチケット予約や、予定の重複を知らせて調整を提案する機能などを紹介（画像：Google Playストアより）
　Museは、質問への回答だけでなく、ユーザーに代わって予定の調整やメールの管理、レストランの予約、情報収集などを実行できるAIエージェントだ。ユーザーが許可した外部サービスと連携し、アプリを閉じている間もタスクを進められる。
　Apple Watch対応版はApp Storeで、Wear OS対応版はGoogle Playストアでそれぞれ配信されている。iPhone版はiOS 18.0以降、Apple Watch版はwatchOS 9.0以降に、Android版はAndroid 10以降に対応する。Wear OSの対応バージョンは記載されていない。アプリは無料でダウンロードでき、追加機能を利用できる有料プランも用意されている。

Meta’s Muse AI agent is now available on Apple Watch and Wear OS. Users can give it instructions, check progress and approve actions without pulling out their phone.
Meta announced the smartwatch rollout yesterday. The Muse version 15.0 update adds the Apple Watch companion app, while Meta has confirmed that a Wear OS version is also available.
What Muse can do on a smartwatch
On Apple Watch, users can type a request, dictate it or start a voice conversation with Muse. A watch face complication provides quicker access, while the animated Muse character appears inside the app.
Meta’s examples show Muse searching for movie tickets and detecting a scheduling conflict between a meeting and a dentist appointment. The agent can suggest a change and then carry out the task once the user approves it.
That is the main difference between Muse and a normal chatbot. Muse can navigate websites, fill in forms, manage calendar events and make bookings through connected services. It can also continue working after the app has closed and return when it needs more information or permission.
The Wear OS app appears to offer the same core interaction methods. However, Meta has not published yet a proper compatibility list.
The watch is a remote for the agent
Of course, Muse does not run its main AI model on the smartwatch. The company says the agent operates inside a cloud-based computer called Muse Secure VM, which has its own browser and stores the services connected by the user.
The Apple Watch and Wear OS apps only act as remote controls for that system. This lets users start a task, read an update or approve an action from the wrist, while the actual work happens in the cloud.
No surprise there. A watch is not a good place to supervise a complicated booking, but it works well for short instructions and quick decisions. We previously looked at why 
smartwatches could become natural access points for AI agents
, and Muse is a practical example of that model.
Meta has not announced direct access to smartwatch sensors as part of this update. The bot can connect to health and fitness services when users give it permission, but there is no indication that the new apps independently analyse heart rate, sleep or workout data from the watch.
The update also puts Meta on the wrist without waiting for its own device. The company has reportedly been developing a 
smartwatch focused on AI and neural controls
, but Muse can reach existing smartwatch owners much sooner through software.
Muse will sit alongside Google Gemini on compatible Wear OS watches. The latter benefits from being built into Google’s platform. Muse, on the other hand, focuses more heavily on longer tasks that cross between different apps and websites, such as researching options, filling in forms and completing a booking.
There will still be overlap between the two. Both can handle reminders, schedules and general questions, so the more useful assistant may depend on which services someone already uses.
Muse also becomes more capable as users connect email, calendars, financial services and other accounts. Meta says users can control those connections, review an activity log and approve sensitive actions before anything gets sent, shared or purchased.
Even then, some supervision will be necessary. Meta’s Google Play listing warns that Muse may provide inaccurate information or take unexpected actions, and advises users to check its work.
