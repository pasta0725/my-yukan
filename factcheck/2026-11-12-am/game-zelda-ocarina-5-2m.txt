任天堂について
日本の京都に本社がある任天堂株式会社は、1889年に花札の製造・販売で創業して以来、娯楽に関わる商品を手がけ続けてきました。1983年にファミリーコンピュータを発売して以降は、Nintendo Switch 2 を発売する現在にいたるまで、主にゲーム専用機のハードウェアおよびソフトウェアを開発・製造・販売しています。任天堂はこれまで、世界中で8億7000万台以上のハードウェアと、62億本以上のソフトウェアを送り出し、ハード・ソフト一体型の展開を通じて、マリオ、ドンキーコング、ゼルダの伝説、メトロイド、カービィ、どうぶつの森、ピクミン、スプラトゥーン等を生み出しました。任天堂は今後も「娯楽を通じて人々を笑顔にする」ために、世界中のお客様に「ユニークで安心な娯楽として任天堂を選んでいただく」ことに挑戦し続けます。

(status 200)
Nintendo has announced that The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake sold 5.2 million units in its first four days.
The 
Switch
 2 exclusive was released on November 5, and according to 
Nintendo
 it went on to hit 5.2 million sales by the end of November 9.
Nintendo’s total includes both physical and digital sales – reports from other tracking and charting companies will report lower sales numbers, because Nintendo doesn’t provide digital sales data, meaning they tend to only report on physical sales.
According to Nintendo, this makes it the fastest-selling first-party Switch 2 game globally so far. While it’s likely it’s also the fastest-selling Switch 2 game overall, Nintendo can only claim it’s the fastest-selling first-party title because not all third-party publishers – especially those who release multi-format titles – share sales figures per console.
The current total means the Ocarina of Time remake has already outsold some other Zelda titles in less than a week, including Switch game 
The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom
 (4.09 million after seven months), 3DS title 
The Legend of Zelda
: A Link Between Worlds (4.26 million) and DS games Phantom Hourglass and Spirit Tracks (4.13 million and 2.61 million respectively).
It still has quite some way to go to overtake the two best-selling Zelda games of all time, however, with 
The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild
 and its sequel 
The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom
 sitting at 35.76 million and 22.71 million copies respectively.
