===== https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/google-searchs-ai-mode-can-now-tap-into-drive-and-wallet-for-more-personalized-responses/
(status 200)
Google 
announced
 on Monday that it’s expanding Personal Intelligence in Search’s AI Mode with integrations for Google Drive, Google Wallet, and the Gemini app. Personal Intelligence is designed to offer users more personalized, context-aware answers by connecting to their Google apps and drawing on information from their digital lives. The feature can already connect to apps like Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Photos.
By bringing Personal Intelligence in AI Mode to Drive, Wallet, and the Gemini app, Google is leveraging the wealth of user data that’s already within its ecosystem. Since users rely on these services, the tech giant can deliver more personalized experiences that rivals aren’t able to match. Of course, not everyone wants AI looking at their Wallet and Drive, so connecting the apps is optional, and you can turn Personal Intelligence on or off at any time.
Google says that connecting Google Wallet to Personal Intelligence lets users track their financial goals, cut everyday spending, and make better use of credit card rewards. The tech giant notes that if users choose to connect their Wallet, AI Mode can only read information from accounts and isn’t able to move or manage their money. 
Image Credits:
Google /
As for Drive, the company says important information related to a project or major decision is often scattered across documents and spreadsheets. By connecting Google Drive to Personal Intelligence, AI can draw on those files to provide more relevant, personalized answers, such as creating a tailored plan for tax season.
Users will also be able to share context between their Gemini app and AI Mode in Search, which means that if they explore an idea in the Gemini app, they won’t have to start over with AI Mode because it will already have the information, and vice versa. 
Additionally, Google says users will soon be able to view the context AI Mode uses via a new “Personalization” icon that will appear below responses. 
The company also announced on Monday that users will start to see more personally relevant responses in AI Overviews and AI Mode that will be tailored to users’ recent search context.

Google’s graphic for connecting Wallet, Drive and other apps to Personal Intelligence in Search.
Credit: Google
Google is rolling out an opt-in connection between Google Wallet and Personal Intelligence in Search’s AI Mode, with Drive and the Gemini app to follow in the coming weeks. The read-only Wallet link lets AI Mode suggest ways to cut spending and use card rewards.
Google Search’s AI Mode can now use your Google Wallet to give more personal answers, the company 
announced
 on Monday. Drive and the Gemini app will follow in the coming weeks. It is the latest step in Google’s 
AI overhaul of Search
, unveiled at I/O in May.
The opt-in connections are part of Personal Intelligence, which already lets AI Mode draw on Gmail, Calendar and Photos. With Wallet linked, AI Mode can suggest ways to trim spending and make the most of cash-back and reward points on cards stored in Wallet. Google says the link is read-only, so AI Mode cannot move, manage or invest money.
With Drive connected, AI Mode can use documents, spreadsheets and PDFs, for example to draw up a plan for tax season. Linking the Gemini app will let ideas explored in one carry over to the other.
Google launched the same 
Wallet link in Gemini
 for US users ten days ago, and OpenAI has let some ChatGPT users 
link bank accounts
 since May. Users can switch each app on or off under Connected Apps in Search Personalization, and a new icon will show what personal context a response used.
The added data could matter for ads too. Since October, Google’s privacy policy has let content from apps linked to Personal Intelligence 
shape the ads
 users see, though not directly their inbox or photo library. The feature is limited to personal accounts and is not available in Europe or the UK.
