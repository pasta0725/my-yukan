Release Date First Revealed in CBT Thank-You Broadcast, Adding 2 New Divers and Chapters
'VOID DIVER' Set for Full Steam Release on January 20, 2027

ⓒSTUDIO NEMO
'VOID DIVER' will officially launch on Steam on January 20, 2027.
STUDIO NEMO revealed the release date on the 23rd during a Closed Beta Test (CBT) thank-you broadcast on its official YouTube channel. Previously, at Tokyo Game Show 2026, the team had only announced a January 2027 window, making this the first time the exact release date has been disclosed.
'VOID DIVER: Escape from the Abyss' is a 2.5D pixel art extraction action RPG developed by LoadComplete's STUDIO NEMO and published by RED BRICK HOUSE.
The game is set in a city swallowed by anomalous phenomena. Familiar places such as police stations, hospitals, bus stops, and parks blend with an otherworldly realm to form dungeons. Players become the shopkeeper of the antique shop BALUSHA, sending Divers—each possessing unique skills and backstories—down into the Abyss. Divers must fight through monsters and traps, recover artifacts, call the Diving Booth, and escape before the countdown ends. The game can be played solo or with friends in co-op supporting up to three players.
In the video, the developers explained that two new Divers and additional chapters will be added for the full launch version. Following Chapter 3 (The Sunken Place) featured in the CBT, Chapters 4 and 5 will be introduced alongside personal stories, unique weapons, and a variety of build combinations.
Meanwhile, according to the Steam store page, Korean language support includes interface and subtitles, but not audio. Producer Baek Ho-young noted in the broadcast that news will be shared via Steam Community and Discord, encouraging viewers to add the game to their wishlists.

About This Game
'Void Diver: Escape from the Abyss'
 is a 2.5D pixel art extraction action RPG: dive into a city swallowed by the otherworld, recover artifacts, and make it out alive.
Alone or with friends, fight through dungeons teeming with aberrations and madness, grab what you can, and call the Diving Booth to get out. All for the sake of completing your requests and returning alive.
DIVE TOGETHER: Up to 3-Player Co-op
Party Play:
 Team up to take on dungeons and bosses, with everyone's skills colliding on the same screen.
Diverse Divers:
 Choose from Divers with unique skills and stories, each offering a different combat style.
Or Go Solo:
 Prefer the lone-wolf life? Explore the depths on your own.
EXPLORE & EXTRACT: Escape the Distorted City
Otherworldly City:
 Police stations, hospitals, bus stops, parks... explore familiar cityscapes fused with another dimension.
Artifact Recovery:
 From super-scientific devices to forbidden magic relics, find the valuables hidden throughout the dungeon.
Extraction:
 Call the Diving Booth and hold out until the countdown ends. Only then do you make it home with your loot.
SURVIVAL: Aberrations, Madness, and Curses
Bosses That Rule Each District:
 Urban legends, rampaging machines, and humans driven to madness. Read their patterns and survive.
Mental Pressure:
 Dungeons consume not just the body, but the mind. Manage the stress that triggers strange phenomena when it hits its limit.
The Paradox Curse:
 Survival includes finding cures for 'Paradoxes', dreadful curses that linger even after an expedition.
GROWTH: Build Your Own Legend
Character Development:
 Combine exclusive skill trees and traits to maximize your Diver's potential.
Gear Customization:
 Mix weapons, accessories, and relics from the dungeons to create a build that's all your own.
MANAGEMENT: Antique Store 'BALUSHA'
Your Base:
 Run 'BALUSHA', the antique shop where Divers gather, in the heart of a city where anomalies are part of daily life.
Negotiation:
 Read each customer and play negotiation cards to trade artifacts at a better price. As you deal in forbidden relics, the city's dark secrets slowly come to light.
