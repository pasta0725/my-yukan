===== https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/wave-race-blue-storm-is-the-12th-gamecube-game-to-be-added-to-switch-onlines-library/
(status 200)
Nintendo has added a 12th title to the GameCube library on Nintendo Switch Online.
Wave Race: Blue Storm is the latest game which can be found in the console’s 
Nintendo
 Classics: 
GameCube
 app, which is available as part of a 
Nintendo Switch
 Online + Expansion Pass subscription.
A launch title for the GameCube when it was released in late 2001, Wave Race: Blue Storm is the third game in the Wave Race series, after the Game Boy original and its iconic sequel Wave Race 64.
Success in its races involve navigating between red and yellow buoys, which builds up the player’s turbo meter and makes it possible to get speed boosts.
The game also had a cheat code which let you unlock a sarcastic announcer who would insult the player all the time. Some of his comments can be heard in the new trailer for the 
Switch 2
 release, seen below.
The addition of Wave Race: Blue Storm brings the total number of GameCube games on Switch 2 to 12, meaning the full list is now as follows:
Chibi-Robo!
F-Zero GX
Fire Emblem: Path of Radiance
The Legend of Zelda
: The Wind Waker
Luigi’s Mansion
Pokémon
 XD: Gale of Darkness
Soulcalibur II
Star Fox
 Adventures
Super Mario Strikers
Super Mario Sunshine
Wario World
Wave Race: Blue Storm
Nintendo also previously announced that Pokémon Colosseum is coming to the library, but hasn’t yet confirmed its release date.
GameCube games are available to Switch 2 owners subscribed to the Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pass service.
NES, SNES, Game Boy and Game Boy Colour games are available as part of the standard Switch Online subscription, which costs £3.49/€3.99/$3.99 for a one-month membership, £6.99/€7.99/$7.99 for a three-month membership, and £17.99/€19.99/$19.99 for a 12-month membership.
Switch Online’s Expansion Pack add-on, which is needed to play GameCube games on Switch 2, as well as 
Nintendo 64
, Game Boy Advance and 
Sega
 Mega Drive / Genesis games, costs $49.99 / £34.99 / €39.99 per year.
