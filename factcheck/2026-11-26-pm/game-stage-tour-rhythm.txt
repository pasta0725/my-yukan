ニュース
楽器コントローラ対応の新作リズムアクション「STAGE TOUR」，ゲームシステムを紹介する最新映像が公開。全95曲を収録予定
ライター：奥谷海人
　RedOctane Gamesは，2026年12月10日に発売を予定している新作リズムアクション
「STAGE TOUR」
（
PC
 / 
Switch2
 / 
PS5
 / 
XBOX Series X|S
）の
ゲームシステムや発売後のコンテンツ展開を紹介する，30分以上にわたる最新映像
を公開した。
　「STAGE TOUR」は，
「Guitar Hero」
シリーズを生み出したRedOctaneの元スタッフや，NeversoftやHarmonixなど，楽器コントローラを使ったリズムゲームに精通するメーカー出身のメンバーたちが集結したRedOctane Gamesの最新作だ。開発にはThird Kind GamesやEidos-Montréalも協力している。
　ギター，ベース，ドラム，ボーカルを担当する
最大4人のプレイヤーでバンドを結成
できる。また，専用コントローラを持っていなくても，ゲームパッドやキーボードで演奏できる
「Arcade」
が用意されている。GibsonやEpiphone，Kramerといった実在のギター180種類以上と，50人以上のバンドメンバーが収録されており，自由なバンド編成も楽しめそうだ。
　発売時点では，
約60年におよぶ音楽史から選ばれた95曲が収録される予定
で，5フレット式のリズムアクションゲームのローンチ時セットリストとしては，過去最大規模になるという。
　映像の公開に合わせて，Polyphia feat. Sophia Blackの
「ABC」
，Sixpence None the Richerの
「Kiss Me」
，Two Door Cinema Clubの
「What You Know」
，そしてBostonの
「Smokin'」
の4曲が収録曲として新たに発表された。
　今回の映像では，通常の演奏を楽しむ
「Quickplay」
に加え，演奏の正確さを100万点満点で競う
「Judgements」
，音程を気にせず歌える
「Karaoke」
，楽曲の一部分を繰り返し練習できる
「Practice」
といったモードが紹介されている。楽曲の演奏速度やノートの配置を変更するモディファイアも充実している。
　ユニークなのが，ローグライトの要素を取り入れたシングルプレイ用の
「Tour」
で，ランダムに提示される3曲から演奏する楽曲を選んでいく。獲得したスターで特殊効果を持つエフェクトペダルや消耗品を購入しながら，
5つの会場で計15曲を演奏するツアーの完走を目指す
という。
　RedOctane Gamesは，「STAGE TOUR」を長期的な音楽ゲームプラットフォームとして育てていく考えで，年4回の大型コンテンツ拡張や，追加楽曲パックの販売を予定するほか，新たなゲームモードの追加やシステムの拡充は無料アップデートで行うという。また，専用ドラムキットの打面を交換できる張力調整式のメッシュヘッドも開発中とのことだ。

Stage Tour Gives Us The Deepest Dive Into All Of The Tour Modes Coming
by David 'Hades' Becker [ Wednesday, 25th of November 2026 - 10:00 PM ]
Stage Tour
A new developer diary is out there for Stage Tour to take us deep into all of the gameplay to expect in Stage Tour
With just a few more weeks until we get 
Stage Tour
 in our homes, it feels about right to dig further into all of the modes, songs, and other bits that we can expect to have at launch. That seems to be the feeling over at 
RedOctane Games
, as we are going backstage once again to have a look at all of that. Here we have the deepest of dives into 
Stage Tour
 to cover all the hardware, tours, and features we have seen before. Along with a few looks at what is coming down the line for the game. Especially since this is slated to be the one-and-only version of the game, with hardware and DLC in the future building into it. That is some interesting news to have, indeed.
To start with the basics, 
Stage Tour
 will have the quickplay and basic modes that we have seen in every rhythm game up until now. All with the new twist and look to it for this version of the genre. That is to be expected. This is where we will be able to grind out all of the gear, bandmates, and venues in the game. That is not to say that the Karaoke and Tour Modes will not allow for that too. The Karaoke Mode sounds fairly straightforward, but the Tour Mode does offer us a roguelike bit of gameplay, where if we fail a song in the list, the Tour is over. That feels a little harsh to have in 
Stage Tour
, but that will make for some solid replay when not just rocking out with your friends, in general, in the core loop of the game. All so we can unlock those achievements and other little cosmetics that we have grown to love.
In addition to all of that, the following deepest dive into 
Stage Tour
 offers up a tease of things for the future of the game too. It looks like there are some new hardware bits on the way, specifically with the modular drum kit that we will have. I am not a musician, so I do not know what having a Mesh Head will mean for the drums, but it is something coming next Summer. Then we have all of the DLC and songs that might be coming to 
Stage Tour
. There is nothing listed as of yet, but this is where the team is looking to keep the game going on in perpetuity. With paid songs and DLC packs offering up new bandmates, venues, and the like, but also free DLC that will offer us new modifiers and modes to experience in the future. That way, they never need to make a sequel to this one and can just keep updating as long as we keep on playing.

Stage Tour Community Update: Getting Ready for December 10
Hey Rockstars!🤘
We hope you enjoyed our latest Deepest Dive and if you haven't checked it out yet here it is:
We’re hoping it answered a lot of your questions, but no doubt there will be more. Over the next two weeks, we’ll show you more of the game and share some tips and tricks to help you get started on 
December 10
.
Before we get into all of that, a huge thank-you to everyone who’s pre-ordered Stage Tour, whether that’s bundles or hardware on StageTour.com, through our retail partners, or the game digitally on your platform of choice.
The response has gone well beyond what we expected or planned for. We’ll get into hardware availability and shipping further down, but we wanted to say up front how much we appreciate you backing us.
Getting Ready for December 10
The team has been heads down for the last few months getting Stage Tour ready, and we’re proud of the game you’ll get to play on launch day. With December 10 now just around the corner, everyone is busy with the final rounds of polishing and testing as we prepare for launch.
There are 95 songs on the Stage Tour launch setlist, making it the biggest officially licensed launch setlist in five-fret rhythm game history. Rock and metal are at the heart of Stage Tour, and there’s plenty to discover across genres, from some of the biggest names in music to independent artists making their mark. Check out our latest setlist reveal and 
explore all 77 songs we’ve revealed so far
.
We also want to give a huge shout-out to our amazing note charters, who have done an incredible job crafting the charts for the game. It’s a massive undertaking across 95 songs, and they’ve also taken on the challenge of creating an entirely new way to experience rhythm gaming through our innovative Arcade Mode, with charts designed specifically for gamepad and gaming keyboards. We’re incredibly proud of what they’ve achieved, and we can’t wait for you to experience their work.
For a team our size, launching on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Nintendo Switch 2, plus Steam and the Epic Games Store, is a big undertaking. Add cross-platform online play and support for a wide range of new and older instruments, and there’s been a lot to get right across all five.
A few of you have asked about our closed beta. We’ve made the difficult decision to cancel it. As we got closer to launch, we focused our time on internal playtesting, expanded QA across all launch platforms, focused playtests with experienced players from the community, and getting the game into players’ hands at conventions. The Stage Tour team has contact information for everyone who won beta access at an event this year and will reach out to each winner directly to arrange an alternative.
A big thank-you to everyone who played and shared feedback along the way. You’ve helped shape the game you’ll play on December 10 and given us plenty to think about for future updates. We’re looking forward to hearing what the rest of you think once you’ve had a chance to play.
It’s funny how much has changed in 20 years. Back when we were making those rhythm games and instruments, we’d ship the game and get straight to work on the next title. Today, we can keep building on the base game as we hear from players, as technology improves, and as we improve and evolve as a team. Developing this way feels more liberating, and it constantly has us thinking about all the possibilities for Stage Tour going forward.
Hardware Pre-Orders & Shipping
Demand for Stage Tour hardware remains incredibly high, and most of our initial launch allocation of guitar bundles and band soft bundles on StageTour.com is already spoken for. More hardware is on the way, and we’ll update the estimated shipping windows for new orders as each batch fills up.
If you’re ordering for a specific date, check the estimated shipping window for your platform and bundle on the product page or at checkout. That’s when we expect your order to ship, so please allow time for delivery too.
We’re also starting to process select orders early as we ramp up fulfillment across three warehouses around the world. If you get an early notification that your order is processing, your estimated shipping window still applies. You will receive a separate email with shipping  tracking separately once your order has shipped.
If your guitar bundle arrives before launch day, hang on to the QR code attached to the guitar body, as shown below. You’ll need to scan it on December 10 to redeem your copy of Stage Tour.
And if you’re looking for your wireless dongle, it’s tucked inside the guitar neck, as shown below.
Guitar Bundles at Retail
Still looking for a Stage Tour guitar bundle? Check availability with participating retailers in your region. In the US, check 
Amazon
, 
Best Buy
, 
GameStop
, 
Walmart.com
, and 
Target.com
.
Some guitar bundles may reach select retailers slightly later than planned due to unforeseen shipping delays. We’re working with our retail partners to make those bundles available as soon as possible. If you’ve already pre-ordered through a retailer, check your order details for the latest shipping estimate.
We’re also continuing to work on bringing Guitar and Game bundles to more regions, 
including Brazil
, and will keep you posted.
Digital Game & Pre-Orders
Stage Tour will be available digitally from December 10 for $39.99 USD SRP, with regional pricing in local currencies outside the US, on the PlayStation Store, Xbox Store, Steam, Epic Games Store and Nintendo eShop for Nintendo Switch 2.
You can pre-order now with 10% off on the PlayStation Store, Xbox Store, Steam and Epic Games Store.
No guitar required! You can jump straight into Arcade Mode, with charts made specifically for gamepad and keyboard. Stage Tour also supports a wide range of existing instruments, so you might already have what you need to play.
What Comes After Launch
December 10 is the beginning for Stage Tour. We’re in this for the long run. We’ll keep building on the game based on what you tell us once you’ve had time to play, and we’ve got a few ideas of our own we’re excited to try.
As we shared in our last update, new gameplay features and ways to play will come through free updates.
The Official Stage Tour Game Guide
In time for launch, we’ll release version 1.0 of the official Stage Tour Game Guide. It’s free for everyone. We’ll email it to customers, and anyone will be able to download it from StageTour.com.
The Stage Tour Companion App
We’ll also be releasing a companion app where you can read the game guide, watch tips videos, catch up on all our deep dives, and find new content as we release it. In the Gear section, you’ll be able to update the firmware on your Stage Tour hardware and try neat little enthusiast tools like our Drum Tuner.
We’re also working on a simple way for you to share your feedback and suggestions, along with any bugs you’d like to report. 
This gives you another way to share thoughtful, constructive feedback and gives us one place to track reports, spot any recurring issues, and decide what to tackle as we continue to evolve Stage Tour. And of course, we’ll still be in Discord chatting with you about the game.
A Sneak Peek at Mesh Drumheads
In the Deepest Dive, we gave you a 
quick look
 at the mesh drumheads we have in development. We’ve been looking at mesh heads for a while, and the performance and feel of the latest prototypes have been nothing short of impressive.
When we designed the Stage Tour drum kit, we wanted you to be able to replace parts when you need to and upgrade the kit over time. Mesh heads are one example of what that design lets us do.
For many of you, buying a kit is a big investment in Stage Tour and in the future of rhythm gaming. We take that seriously. We’ve learned a lot from making hardware over the years and from hearing what players want to see done better. Our goal is to build on the gear you already own at a pace that lets us get things right and keep prices reasonable.
Thanks for all the support on the road to launch. We can’t wait to see what you play first.
The RedOctane Games Team
Written by Jack Guinchard
