Diablo IV 'Season of Tyrael's Gambit' Launches December 2

Blizzard Entertainment's Diablo IV will launch 'Season of Tyrael’s Gambit' on December 2 (KST). This season features a revamp of 'War Plans' alongside a new progression tree and Legendary items.
The revamped 'War Plans' allows players to customize endgame activities and rewards according to their goals. The newly introduced 'War Plan Tactics' is used to increase the likelihood of preferred activities and rewards appearing. If goals change during progression, players can use 'Marks of Wisdom' to alter their previous choices.
For character progression, a new seasonal tree called 'Tyrael’s Boons' is added. Through this tree, players can empower rewards, currencies, materials, and merchants obtained throughout their journey. For build crafting, a new type of Legendary item known as 'Horadric Items' will be utilized. Horadric affixes attached to these items allow players to receive Talisman set effects in new ways.
The new questline follows players teaming up with Tyrael to track down the mystery of the Death Cult. As the adventure unfolds, the cult's scheme within Sanctuary and the threat facing both Sanctuary and Tyrael are revealed. Risen enemies appear throughout War Plans, and a new boss battle features the Corrupted Reaper and Fractured Reaper joining forces.
Content from the previous 'Season of Hell’s Legacy' will also continue. Players can still encounter the Prime Evil Council battle, world bosses Diablo and Moophisto, Cow Island, and Legacy Unique items in both the Seasonal Realm and Eternal Realm.

According to every Diablo 4 player known to man, I screwed up by skipping its 
last season
. Blizzard shoved just about every iconic 
Diablo 2 boss and item

On December 1, when the 
Season of Tyrael's Gambit

Blizzard scored a major win with Diablo 4's nostalgic season 15, which coincided perfectly with the series' 30th anniversary and the triumphant return of BlizzCon, not to mention the announcement of 
Diablo 5
. Season of Hell's Legacy pulled many of its headlining features from Diablo 1, 2, and 3, including 
a questline recreation of the first game's main storyline
. The season has been a hit with players, so it made sense when 
Blizzard confirmed
 that some of its features would stay in the game permanently. Now, we know which features exactly, and it's clear Blizzard is doubling down on the nostalgia.
"We heard overwhelming requests for features from Season of Hell’s Legacy to carry forward as permanent game features," reads Blizzard's 
announcement
 of Diablo 4 season 16, now officially titled Season of Tyrael's Gambit. "While the entire season couldn’t come along for the ride, we are implementing the following features with the launch of Season of Tyrael's Gambit that will carry forward in both Eternal and Seasonal realms:"
By far my favorite addition in season 15 was the beautifully reimagined Old Tristram, and that'll remain accessible, along with its side quests and Deckard Cain's books, via the portal in the Imperial Library. Real ones will know about Cow Island, a callback to one of the series' most beloved Easter eggs, and that will also remain accessible along with the Moophisto world boss summon. Likewise, the big Uber Diablo World Boss summon is coming along for the ride in season 16, as well as the Annihilus Seal he drops. And speaking of items, the Legacy Uniques from older Diablo games are sticking around, as is the Hellfire Torch Charm, a nod to Diablo 2.

Arguably the most notable season 15 feature getting stamped into the game permanently is the Prime Evil Council fight, which puts you up against Diablo, Mephisto, and Baal simultaneously. Blizzard says that battle will work like a typical Lair Boss in terms of summoning and claiming rewards, and that you won't need any materials to access the encounter. All players in the battle will need their own Wirt's Lucky Coin to get into the Council's Hoard chest and reap the rewards.
Meanwhile, season 15's Capstone Dungeons will be available in Torment 10+, with all spawns being tuned to their equivalents in Torment 13. Blizzard says "these will be the best source for earning Prime Evil Splinters," which are what you need to get those sweet Prime Evil powers. Speaking of sweet, season 15's Waking Nightmares dungeons, which are normal-size dungeons inspired by iconic moments from previous Diablo games, are being made permanent, but only through Chronicle of Nightmares dungeon keys and not in the overworld. Finally, head to the Pit for a chance to fight "monster families from previous Diablo games."
Diablo 4 season 16, kicking off December 1, is all about the evolving threat posed by the Úllo Sar death cult, which is exciting enough, but it'll be really hard to top season 15 for me as a fan of the series from the beginning. Luckily, Blizzard has confirmed I'll be able to go back to Old Tristram and read Deckard Cain's journals any time I please. I think I'll stay a while.

. Last night’s

 answered two questions left open after 
October’s PTR
. How much say will players actually have over their War Plans, and how much of Season 15 can we keep? The answer to the second one is quite a lot, although Runewords will be sitting this season out.
War Plans give you a better chance of getting the activities you want
War Plans Tactics lets you favour particular activities and rewards. The bonuses at each rank have risen from 25/50/75% on the PTR to 200/400/600% at launch.
You have 12 points to spend at maximum War Plans rank, with up to three per node. You can respec using Marks of Wisdom earned by completing War Plans.
Four existing activity trees have been reworked: Lair Bosses, the Pit, Kurast Undercity and Whispers. Tactics is available on both Seasonal and Eternal realms for 
Lord of Hatred
 owners.
When we 
covered the PTR in October
, the appeal of Tactics was being able to spend more of an evening doing the activities you enjoy. The developers acknowledged last night that the original bonuses were too low to deliver on that. The 600% figure is an increase to the chance of seeing your chosen activity when a plan is generated. There’s still some luck in what comes up.
The Lair Boss changes address an older problem. Since 
Lord of Hatred
, there have been nodes intended to let players earn boss Uniques in other activities. Blizzard admit those nodes weren’t much of an incentive to leave what one developer called “Lair Boss jail”. If you’d sooner spend your evening in the Pit, the idea is that you can now favour the Pit in Tactics and put points into the Lair Boss tree to bring some of those rewards with you.

Tyrael’s Boons give you more to do with Obols, salvage and Forgotten Souls
All seasonal players can use Tyrael’s Boons, including those without 
Lord of Hatred
. Killing Úllo Sar advances both the Boons and the seasonal reputation board.
Curious Addition improves gambling at the Purveyor. Merchant of Mystery introduces a Torment vendor who sells caches for Forgotten Souls. Scent of Gold increases rewards including gold, experience and crafting materials.
Trash for Treasure unlocks the Greater Temper Cube recipe, which upgrades a Tempered Affix to a Greater Affix. Its final node also gives salvaged Ancestrals a chance to yield a Scroll of Restoration.
One detail worth knowing before you spend your points is that you can pursue all four paths, choosing up to four of the six nodes in each. A player who wants Greater Temper can still improve their gambling odds. There is a trade-off at the end of the gambling path, though. The final node triples the Obol cost in exchange for a better chance of an Ancestral.

Helltide Cinders stay, and the cap has risen to 5,000
Aberrant Cinders carry over when a Helltide ends. The cap has gone up from 3,000 on the PTR to 5,000.
Helltides in Nahantu and Skovos are offset by 30 minutes from those in the original regions, keeping a Helltide active somewhere.
Helltide chests give better rewards at higher Torment levels. Cinders are also shared between eligible characters. The example given on the stream was two Seasonal Hardcore characters on the same account.
The developers gave a familiar example of why the timing matters. You start a War Plan, reach the Helltide activity and Sanctuary informs you it’s at peace. The staggered starts should remove that wait. Keeping Cinders solves the problem at the other end of the run. Once you’ve finished your War Plan objective, you can move on without first having to find a chest or write off the Cinders you’ve earned.
Horadric gear makes two five-piece Talisman bonuses possible
Horadric jewellery counts as one piece towards the Talisman set named on its affix. One matching charm and one matching piece of jewellery will activate the two-piece bonus.
With the right combination of charms and jewellery, you can activate two five-piece set bonuses at once.
Horadric weapons and armour gain effects that activate with a matching two-piece bonus. Their affixes have been split into weapon and armour pools since the PTR. Blizzard has no new class Uniques or Legendary Aspects lined up for this season.
Being able to equip two sets raised a question for the dev team. What happens if your class has a second five-piece set you can fit, but little reason to use it? The Paladin’s Conviction Aura set was designed for group support. Blizzard had kept its numbers low precisely because the whole party could benefit. It now also increases the Paladin’s own damage, giving solo players a reason to consider it alongside another set.
That also puts the lack of new class Uniques in context. One of the better questions from chat was whether Mythics would be more meaningful this season. The team hasn’t substantially changed Mythic Uniques. What 
has
 changed is the decision when one drops. If equipping it costs you a five-piece bonus, does it still deserve the slot?

Season 14’s Corrupted Reaper returns with company
Tyrael’s questline continues the Pandemonium story and brings back the Corrupted Reaper from 
Season of Death Awakening
 (Season 14).
The Corrupted Reaper now fights alongside the Fractured Reaper. After the quest, the pair can be fought again as Lair Bosses in Zarbinzet.
As in Season 14, Blizzard identifies the Reaper fight as a source of Mythic Uniques, particularly at higher Torment levels.
If you farmed the original Reaper, the thing to watch on your return is how the two bosses combine attacks. That idea also turns up on the way to the fight. Some of the reworked Pandemonium cultists pass their power to nearby allies when they die, giving you a reason to pay attention to which enemy you kill first.

What stays from Season 15, and why Runewords are leaving
Uber Tristram and the Prime Evil Council stay. Fighting the Council requires no materials, but each player needs their own Wirt’s Lucky Coin to open the reward chest. The Prime Evil Capstones also remain.
Waking Nightmares stay, accessed through Chronicle of Nightmares Dungeon Keys. Legacy Uniques, the Hellfire Torch, the Annihilus Seal and the Diablo World Boss summon carry over.
Moophisto and Cow Island stay. If you’ve unlocked the Cow Island waypoint, you keep it. Deckard Cain’s books will be collected on a pedestal in Tristram.
Season 15 Runewords will be absent at launch. Blizzard would like to bring them back, but gave no date.
There’s a distinction here for anyone deciding what to finish before 1 December. Blizzard had

around

Runewords got the more revealing answer. The developers described them as a love letter to fans, but said they’d bent some of Diablo 4’s item rules to make them work for the anniversary season. Enchanting was the example they gave of a system that still doesn’t interact with them properly. The team wants to resolve those problems before deciding how Runewords could fit into the game permanently.

Patch 3.3.0 Notes
A Dual Reaper Threat Emerges
In Season of Death Awakening, the Corrupted Reaper terrorized Sanctuary. In Season of Tyrael’s Gambit, she brings reinforcements.
The Fractured Reaper will lend his strength to Pandemonium’s war against Sanctuary by merging his abilities with that of the Corrupted Reaper. You won’t have just the power of two formidable but separate entities at the same time– you’ll have two eldritch powerhouses working together synergistically, abilities combining to annihilate all but the most powerful challengers.
After defeating these twin terrors during the Seasonal questline, you’ll have the opportunity to fight both as a Lair Boss pair in Zarbinzet. Like the Corrupted Reaper fight in Season 14, these Reapers will be an excellent source of Mythic Uniques, particularly on the highest Torment levels.
Back to Top
Engage with Revamped War Plans
Battling the Úllo Sar requires more planning than ever before, and you’ll have two new War Plans trees to do so. War Plans Tactics allows you to increase the chances of your favorite activities and rewards appearing, while Tyrael’s Boons modifies non-combat activities within your Seasonal journey.
These two trees will continue beyond Season 16.
Customize War Plans with War Plans Tactics
Availability
: Both Eternal and Seasonal content. 
Requires Lord of Hatred.
War Plans Tactics is a new meta progression system that allows you to increase the likelihood of getting the content and rewards from War Plans that matter most to you.
Earn points from completing War Plans activities then invest them into nodes to improve the chance of preferred activities or rewards appearing. This allows you to tailor your War Plans more closely with both your goals and playstyle.
Spend up to 3 points into any War Plan Activity node to give it a better chance to appear in your offered War Plans over other types. Allocating at least one point in Helltides or Nightmare Dungeon nodes also opens a choice to increase the chances for Greater Helltides or Escalation Dungeons.
Assign up to 3 points into any type of Reward node for improved chances to select your preferred rewards.
Respec Costs
: Requires Marks of Wisdom.
Change from PTR: The chances for both Activities and Rewards have been increased from 25/50/75% to 200/400/600%.
Respec Tactics and Activity Trees with Marks of Wisdom
Instead of spending Gold, you’ll use Marks of Wisdom to respec War Plans Tactics and all Activity trees, except for Tyrael’s Boons. Earn this new currency by completing War Plans; the higher the difficulty, the more Marks of Wisdom you’ll earn.
Marks of Wisdom are capped at 20.
Tailor Seasonal Progression with Tyrael’s Boons
Availability
: Seasonal Only. 
Does not require Lord of Hatred.
A new Season-themed War Plans tree called Tyrael’s Boons allows you to tailor other in-game activities. For example, you’ll have season-specific choices such as gaining more Seasonal reputation or currency, or more generalized perks such as reducing Obols costs at the Purveyor of Curiosities.
Earn points by completing Seasonal activities – in this case, slaying Úllo Sar in various activities across Sanctuary.
In Season 16, there are four paths to follow, and you can choose up to 4 of 6 nodes within each path. You can select any and all of the four paths:
Curious Addition
: Modifies the Purveyor of Curiosities to sell extra items or gear of improved quality.  The last choice triples the cost of gambling, but you have a higher chance of getting Ancestrals.
Merchant of Mystery
: Adds a new merchant at Torment difficulty selling valuable caches for Forgotten Souls. The final node guarantees that an Ancestral Archetype Cache is always in stock. Item quality, Forgotten soul costs, and Horadric Gems chances have all been improved.
Scent of Gold
: Increases valuable things you find – extra Gold, experience, gem fragments, and crafting materials. The last node increases your chance of finding Legendary items by 50%.
Trash for Treasure
: Reduces Transmuting costs at the Alchemist and improves chances for Forgotten Souls, Obducite, and Scrolls of Restoration or Salvage to drop. At the end of this branch, salvaging Ancestral Items gives a chance to grant a Scroll of Restoration. It also opens a new Horadric Cube recipe, Greater Temper, which upgrades the Tempered Affix on an item to a Greater Affix.
Respec Costs
: 5,000,000 Gold per point.  Tyrael’s Boons uses Gold instead of Marks of Wisdom, since players who do not have the Lord of Hatred expansion are unable to earn that currency.
Revamped Activity Tree Nodes
Lair Boss, The Pit, Whispers, and Undercity Activity Trees all received changes, including:
creating new nodes
forging new connections
swapping node positions to remove unnecessary prerequisites
rewarding better loot
Examples include Cursed Machines in the Pit increasing your Glyph Upgrade Success Chance as 5 tiers higher and Whispers now offering Ancestral Caches or Junk instead of Greater Caches.

Supercharge Talisman Sets with Horadric Items
A new type of Legendary called Horadric Items arrives, courtesy of the Úllo Sar. Horadric affixes modify a specific Talisman set, with varying effects based on the item type.
Jewelry items with Horadric affixes increase your set bonus count by 1 for the specific Talisman set. Unlike Seal of the Diamond Mind, you only need to have 1 charm equipped from a set to enjoy the 2-piece set bonus of that Horadric affix. You’ll be able to activate a larger bonus with fewer charms, freeing up slots for multiple set bonuses or new charm combinations.
Armor and Weapon items with Horadric affixes add new effects that activate if you have at least a two-set bonus from a defined Talisman set. These can include perks such as skill rank bonuses, bonus damage, and improved defensive capabilities. 
Since the PTR, Horadric Affixes have been split into Weapon and Armor categories to encourage choice and limit the impact of some of the most powerful effects.

Improvements to Helltides
To aid the War Effort against the Ullo Sar, Helltides have been improved to reduce downtime, prevent interruptions, and avoid wasting Aberrant Cinders.
One Helltide will always be up in the original zones for one hour. Another will be up in either Nahantu or Skovos, offset by 30 minutes. This ensures there will always be a Helltide up in one of those two regions.
Aberrant Cinders no longer reset once a Helltide ends. Instead, there will be a cap on the amount you can carry. Once you hit the cap, you will not be able to earn more Cinders until you’ve spent them, and you will no longer see Cinders on the ground.
PTR change: Aberrant Cinders cap has been raised from 3,000 to 5,000.

Features Moving Forward from Season of Hell’s Legacy
We heard overwhelming requests for features from Season of Hell’s Legacy to carry forward as permanent game features. While the entire season couldn’t come along for the ride, we are implementing the following features with the launch of Season of Tyrael’s Gambit that will carry forward in both Eternal and Seasonal realms:
Bosses and Challenges
The Prime Evil Council becomes permanently available in Uber Tristram and works like a traditional Lair Boss for summoning and claiming rewards. No materials are required to fight the Council, but each player will need their own Wirt's Lucky Coin to open the Council's Hoard chest.
Season of Hell’s Legacy’s Capstone Dungeons are available in Torment 10+. Monsters in these dungeons are always tuned to be equal to Torment 13. These will be the best source for earning Prime Evil Splinters.
World Boss Diablo Summon
Moophisto
Dungeons and the Pit
The larger Waking Nightmares Dungeons will be available, but only through the Chronicle of Nightmares Dungeon Keys. These dungeons will not appear in the overworld. We’ve also added the ability to Salvage these keys.
In The Pit, there is a chance to spawn levels with monster families from previous Diablo games.
Items Remaining in Game
Legacy Uniques
Annihilus Seal
Hellfire Torch Charm
Discoveries
Deckard Cain’s books have been gathered together and will be available on a book pedestal in Tristram for your perusal anytime.
Cow Island remains to wait on you hand and hoof. Those who have opened this waypoint will not have to learn it again.
Other popularly requested features are being evaluated for rework and may appear in the future.

Earn New Rewards from Seasonal Ranks
Complete the nine Seasonal ranks to earn valuable rewards. Various levels include items such as Experience Tomes, caches, crafting and Masterworking materials, Horadric Cube reagents, Boss/Lair Keys, and Smoldering Ashes to invest in Season Blessings. In addition, you’ll earn the following, depending on your progress:
Up to 11 Skill Points
Up to 42 Paragon Points
Up to 13 Resplendent Sparks
Up to 5 Mythic Unique Journey Caches
Titles: (Tyrael’s, Warmaster) at Rank 1 and (Nameless, Revenant) at Rank 8
Emblems: Sword of Justice at Rank 2 and Mail Time! Stag at Rank 5
Standard of Justice Mount Trophy at Rank 3
Stamper (Stag) Pet at Rank 6
Seasonal Title Laurels at Ranks 3, 7, 8, and 9
Within Season Rank, you’ll have the ability to unlock Season Blessings with Smoldering Ashes. Smoldering Ashes can be earned from both Seasonal Objectives and Chapter Rewards. Once you’ve earned Ashes, they can be spent on these Seasonal Blessings:
Urn of Tyrael’s Blessing: Boosts the amount of reputation earned from all sources.
Urn of Reclamation: Boosts the chance of rare materials from salvage.
Urn of Ancestral Whispers: Boosts the chance for an Ancestral Cache to appear when turning in Whispers.
Urn of Masterworking: Boosts the chance to drop additional Obducite.
Urn of Glyphs: Grants a chance to earn an extra upgrade when improving glyphs.
