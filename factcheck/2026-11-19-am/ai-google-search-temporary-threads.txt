Google’s search engine has long offered an incognito mode for all those search queries you don’t want logged or associated with your account. Now, Google’s AI Mode in Search is getting a similar feature. In 
a blog post
, the company announced a new feature that brings “temporary threads” to AI Mode, where the conversations you have won’t be resurfaced later in areas like your search history or recommendations.
Many leading AI chatbots already offer the option for temporary chats, Google Gemini included. However, there was not yet an option to have a temporary chat when you’re searching Google and are dropped into its conversational interface, known as AI Mode. 
Image Credits:
Google
Google says that users won’t have to adjust their settings to take advantage of the new option. Instead, they’ll be able to click a temporary threads icon in the upper right corner of AI Mode to engage in a temporary chat. This essentially works the same as a temporary chat would in the stand-alone Gemini chatbot.
The option could be convenient to use at those times when you start digging deeper into a particular topic via AI Mode and realize that you don’t want the discussion to become a part of your search history because of its personal nature. 
Once the temporary thread is started, the session becomes detached from your account, Google says. This means that the responses the AI provides won’t be informed by past preferences, nor will they impact future personalization. That could also be useful for those times when you want to learn about something as more of a one-off, but don’t want Google to start to think of the topic as one of your main interests. 
When you close out of the thread, you will return to searching as usual.
The company says the option to engage in temporary chats in AI Mode is rolling out globally on desktop and mobile across all supported languages.

Temporary threads are now available in AI Mode in Search.
Nov 17, 2026
We’re introducing temporary threads in AI Mode, where you can explore and ask questions — whether that’s brainstorming a surprise gift or looking something up you don’t want resurfaced later like in your history or recommendations.
There’s no need to adjust your settings — you can quickly jump into a temporary thread for any query you don't want saved, and switch right back when you're done. To get started, simply click the temporary threads icon in the upper right corner of AI Mode and start asking your questions. Because your session is completely detached from your account, your responses won’t be informed by past preferences, affect future personalization, or be saved to your history. Once you close the thread, it’s gone, letting you go back to searching as usual.
This feature is rolling out globally on desktop and mobile browsers across all languages supported in AI Mode.

TL;DR
Google is rolling out temporary threads in AI Mode, similar to temporary chats in the Gemini app.
Temporary threads don’t appear in your history, draw on past preferences, or influence future personalization.
The feature is rolling out globally on desktop and mobile browsers in all languages supported by AI Mode.
Google has spent much of 2026 improving AI Mode’s ability to get to know you. Earlier this year, the company 
brought Personal Intelligence to AI Mode
, allowing Search to draw on your Gmail and Google Photos data for more personalized answers. More recently, we even uncovered evidence that Google is working on 
letting you pin AI Mode conversations
 for easy access. As useful as these features are, there are times when you’d rather keep certain searches out of your history, and that’s where Google’s new temporary threads come in.
Starting today, Google is rolling out temporary threads in AI Mode, letting you ask questions without those conversations appearing in your search history or influencing future recommendations. This could come in handy when researching a surprise gift, exploring a topic out of curiosity, or looking up something personal that you don’t want Google bringing up again.
The Gemini app has offered a solution to this problem since last year in the form of 
temporary chats
. Google is now bringing essentially the same experience to AI Mode with temporary threads.
You don’t need to change any of your existing privacy or personalization settings to use the feature. Simply select the temporary threads icon in the upper-right corner of AI Mode and start asking questions. The icon should look familiar if you’ve used temporary chats in Gemini.
Google explains that each temporary thread is completely detached from your account. This means AI Mode won’t use your past preferences to tailor its answers, and anything you discuss won’t influence future personalization. Once you close the thread, the conversation disappears from your view, and you can return to your regular, personalized search experience.
Temporary threads are rolling out globally on desktop and mobile browsers in all languages supported by AI Mode.
