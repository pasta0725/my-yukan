Today, Netflix shared a first look at 
The Åre Murders
 and announced that the series returns for a second season on December 27. Fans will be reunited with Carla Sehn and Kardo Razzazi as Detectives Hanna Ahlander and Daniel Lindskog, who are back in the snowy mountain town of Åre. Joining the cast this season is Simon J. Berger as Henry Sylvester.
The first season of 
The Åre Murders
 became an international hit, reaching the Top 10 in 84 countries, taking the #1 spot in 32 of them, and generating over 38 million views globally. For season two, the story draws on two of Viveca Sten’s bestselling crime novels: Hidden in Memories and Hidden in Lies. 
Season two picks up one year after the events of the first season. As Hanna and Daniel are drawn into two new investigations, buried secrets begin to surface within the close-knit community, and the line between the personal and the professional becomes increasingly difficult to maintain.
ABOUT THE ÅRE MURDERS SEASON 2
Synopsis: 
Christmas is approaching in Åre. Hanna has found her place at the local police station, but her feelings for Daniel leave her torn between work and her personal life, and she is even considering leaving Åre. When real estate developer Charlotte Wretlind is found dead in a suite at the luxurious Copperhill Mountain Lodge, old conflicts resurface. Shortly after, twenty one-year-old seasonal worker Filippa Smedsås is found dead in the icy dawn. What first looks like a tragic accident leads the detectives into a web of secrets and toxic relationships among the young seasonal workers. Stretched thin, and with more lives at risk, Hanna and Daniel must confront both those responsible and their own emotional entanglements.
