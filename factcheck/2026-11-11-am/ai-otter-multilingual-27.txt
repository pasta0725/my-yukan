===== https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/otter-ai-can-now-follow-conversations-across-30-languages/
(status 200)
Otter.ai,
 the AI-powered notetaking tool, announced Tuesday that it’s expanding its multilingual capabilities, including a new multilingual transcription feature that can follow conversations as speakers switch between languages during the same meeting.
The company is also adding support for 27 languages, its largest language expansion to date. Once the rollout is complete, Otter will support 31 languages in total, making the platform more accessible to international teams.
The upcoming feature will automatically detect language changes and continue transcribing without requiring users to adjust settings. For example, a call could move from English to Korean to Portuguese, with Otter following the conversation throughout.
The update aims to address a common challenge for global teams, where language differences can make meetings and collaboration more difficult.
“Otter is breaking down language barriers so any company, no matter where they’re based, can build a system of record for the decisions for voice AI communication,” an Otter spokesperson told TechCrunch. 
Thirteen of the new languages are available now: Mandarin Chinese, Korean, German, Italian, Cantonese, Portuguese, Russian, Indonesian, Malay, Dutch, Turkish, Vietnamese, and Polish.
The remaining 14 languages are coming soon and include Swedish, Danish, Finnish, Arabic, Hindi, Thai, Hebrew, Norwegian, Greek, Macedonian, Persian, Czech, Hungarian, and Romanian.
The newly added languages run on Otter’s own speech models. The company told us it trained them using publicly available datasets and worked with native-speaking volunteers to review transcripts and check accuracy. 
The announcement comes as Otter continues to expand beyond transcription and into other workplace AI tools, following its 
$320 million
 funding from General Catalyst.
It recently introduced 
Work Mode
, which uses meeting history and connected apps to help users prepare for upcoming calls. For instance, the feature pulls information from previous customer conversations, outstanding action items, notes from teammates’ similar calls, and recent Google Drive files.
Another recent addition is Live Assist, which provides real-time coaching during meetings. It analyzes the conversation and offers relevant suggestions based on what has already been talked about and what is happening in the moment.

===== https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/20/otter-ai-raises-320-million-from-general-catalysts-growth-fund-for-its-meeting-tool/
(status 200)
Meeting notetaking tool 
Otter.ai
 announced on Tuesday that it has raised $320 million from General Catalyst’s Customer Value Fund (CVF), which provides capital to startups without diluting equity and gets returns on the results of marketing spend.
Otter.ai said that it will use these funds for enterprise growth, go-to-market strategy, international expansion, and product innovation.
The startup, which has been around for nearly a decade, has raised $50 million in total funding with its last round in 2021, according to PitchBook data. 
Last year, Otter hit a revenue milestone after crossing 
$100 million in annual recurring revenue (ARR)
. The company said it’s now approaching new milestones but didn’t provide updated figures.
The company said that 40 million users have used its product to date, up from 25 million last year. However, given the crowded space among meeting notetaking products, a pertinent challenge for Otter would be to have its customers stick around. Unfortunately, the startup didn’t provide any numbers around its monthly active users or churn.
“Voice is one of the biggest untapped opportunities in enterprise AI, and Otter is at the forefront of it — a conviction that’s only deepened watching Otter’s growth,” said Pranav Singhvi, partner and co-founder of CVF, General Catalyst. “When Otter enables enterprises to capture critical voice data and turns it into actionable business insights, teams move faster, decisions get sharper, and productivity compounds across the organization, and that’s why it’s a strong partner for CVF.”
There is a lot of competition in the market with well-funded meeting notetakers like Fireflies.ai, 
Granola
, and 
Read AI
; new entrants like 
Calendly
, 
Wispr
, and 
Superhuman, which acquired Fathom
; and other notetakers like Circleback, Talat, and Quill.
Most of these notetakers want to connect to enterprise systems and, based on data captured in meetings, automate workflows. Otter is also heading in that direction. In the last few years, it introduced 
a meeting assistant
, 
a search function that works across apps
, and 
integration with other AI tools
.
Otter’s newly appointed CMO, Alex Gay, who comes from Superhuman, told TechCrunch at a sit-down at the HumanX conference last month that the company sees a big opportunity in turning transcripts into an actionable roadmap for people who are in back-to-back meetings. 
“When we start thinking about what is actually the bigger problem that people are facing right now, we saw this large pain point in meeting-heavy roles, where people are back-to-back in conversations. These meetings are the work for them. That’s either customer conversations or from a sales standpoint. That’s where strategies are discussed. But meetings are terribly inefficient because when you come into a meeting, you spend the first 20 minutes recapping what was said before to catch everybody up,” he said.
The company is now experimenting with providing insights before, after, and during meetings. It has been testing a feature to send a prebrief to users with details and background information, with context from previous conversations across surfaces. Plus, it is also trying out a Live Assist feature to let users ask questions to the AI assistant during the meeting to get knowledge.

Mountain View, CA, November 10, 2026 - 
Otter.ai
, the company behind the leading AI notetaker and conversational knowledge platform, today announced the largest multilingual expansion in its history: 27 new languages, 13 available today and 14 coming soon, and multilingual transcription that recognizes and transcribes conversations as speakers move between languages, all within a single meeting.
Global teams rarely work in just one language: 
Capterra’s 2024 Collaboration and Productivity Survey 
found that 42% of international knowledge workers named language barriers as one of their top challenges when collaborating. That’s why Otter is expanding now, and why it is doing so deliberately. Unlike other notetakers, Otter’s speech models are trained, tuned, and run by Otter on its own infrastructure, not wrapped around a third-party AI service, so each new language can be built with the accuracy teams need to trust their notes.
27 new languages
In addition to the four existing languages (English, French, Spanish, and Japanese), Otter now supports 13 new languages: Mandarin Chinese (Simplified), German, Italian, Cantonese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Indonesian, Malay, Dutch, Turkish, Vietnamese, and Polish.
Fourteen more are coming soon: Swedish, Danish, Finnish, Arabic, Hindi, Thai, Hebrew, Norwegian, Greek, Macedonian, Persian, Czech, Hungarian, and Romanian. Once all are live, Otter will support a total of 31 languages.
Meetings don’t stay in one language, and neither should the transcript. Coming soon, Otter’s multilingual option will follow conversations as they unfold, transcribing accurately as participants switch between supported languages, with no manual setting changes. For example, a sales call can move from English to Portuguese to Mandarin and back without anyone pausing to change a language setting.
Because Otter builds and runs each language model in-house, it can account for what makes each one distinct. In English benchmark testing, Otter averaged 96% accuracy on the Open ASR Leaderboard. Otter also does not send your audio to third parties for processing, a commitment that has held for over a decade and now extends to every new language as it becomes available.
A complete workflow in every supported language
Otter’s new languages work across the whole workflow, not just the transcript. Before the call, Work Mode, one of Otter’s
 newest capabilities
, pulls past conversations and connected apps into a brief. During it, summaries, action items, speaker identification, AI Chat, and search work across supported languages. Afterward, Work Mode turns the conversation into follow-up emails and CRM updates, in whichever language the meeting happened.
“Many large international companies are already using Otter to improve collaboration and productivity for teams in different time zones and speaking multiple languages,” said Sam Liang, co-founder and CEO of Otter.ai. “We built our speech recognition in-house from the beginning, so we can build every language the right way, with leading accuracy and private audio. That’s what lets us bring 27 new languages to Otter.”
Availability
Users can select a supported language in their Otter settings. The new languages come at no additional cost across Otter plans and available across desktop, mobile, and browser. For details on supported languages and how to select one, visit

About Otter.ai
Otter.ai is the AI notetaker and conversation knowledge engine for the modern enterprise. With over 40 million users, including more than 86% of the Fortune 500, and 1 billion+ meetings transcribed, Otter captures, connects, and activates the knowledge from every conversation. Otter’s proprietary ASR and speaker identification, built in-house, deliver industry-leading accuracy, turning meetings into searchable intelligence, automated action items, and agentic workflows that sync directly to the tools teams already use. Backed by Spectrum Equity, Horizon Ventures, and Draper Associates, and most recently General Catalyst’s Customer Value Fund (CVF) with $320 million in growth financing. Otter.ai is headquartered in Mountain View, California.
