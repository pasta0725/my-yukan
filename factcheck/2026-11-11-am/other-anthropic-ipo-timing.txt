Anthropic reportedly plans to move forward with IPO plans
Launching a major tech IPO would seem risky right now, shortly after one of Nasdaq’s biggest sell-offs and amid revelations that a CEO central to the AI boom had an affair with a suspected Chinese spy. But that isn’t deterring Anthropic, which is moving ahead with its listing plans, according to 
The Wall Street Journal
.
Anthropic plans to set a valuation range this week and begin trading on Thursday or Friday of next week, the Journal reported.
Investors will look to Anthropic’s valuation to gauge the strength of the AI boom. Some institutional investors are now pushing for a valuation below the $1.5 trillion target discussed as recently as last week, a figure that was itself considerably below the $2 trillion floated a few months ago, the report said.
OpenAI, Anthropic’s main rival, was reportedly valued at 
$1.25 trillion
 last week after the ChatGPT maker raised $11 billion in fresh capital, short of the $20 billion it had initially sought.

OpenAI reportedly raises an additional $11 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation
OpenAI has reportedly raised $11 billion so far in a new funding round that values the AI lab at $1.25 trillion.
Bloomberg 
reported on
 the new funding on Friday, citing sources familiar with the matter. The $11 billion is part of a larger funding round that hasn’t closed yet, according to the outlet. The round is expected to close in the coming weeks and could total about $20 billion, a source told Bloomberg.
In late September, Bloomberg 
reported 
that OpenAI was looking to raise $30 billion in the upcoming round. The current figures fall well short of that target.
TechCrunch has reached out to OpenAI for comment and will update this story if it responds.
The news comes only hours after Bloomberg 
also reported
 that potential buyers of shares in the IPO of OpenAI’s chief competitor, Anthropic, are eyeing a substantially lower valuation than Anthropic had targeted. Bloomberg writes that some potential buyers believe Anthropic’s valuation could be as low as $1.1 trillion, compared with the
 $2 trillion
 that investors were said to be expecting roughly a month ago.
OpenAI previously intimated that it would go public this year but subsequently delayed its plans. There are expectations that the company 
will IPO next year
 at some point.
The market for AI hangs in a particularly precarious place. The Democratic landslide in the U.S. midterms — and overwhelmingly negative voter sentiment around data centers — has 
caused jitters throughout the market
, with many anticipating potential regulatory challenges in the future.

===== https://www.anthropic.com/news/roadshow-launch
(status 200)
Today Anthropic, PBC launched the roadshow for its proposed initial public offering of its Class A common stock.
The proposed offering consists of 825,000,000 shares of Class A common stock. Of these, 801,351,354 shares are being offered by Anthropic and 23,648,646 shares are being offered by certain existing stockholders. Anthropic will not receive any proceeds from the sale of shares by the selling stockholders. Anthropic also expects to grant the underwriters a 30-day option to purchase up to an additional 123,750,000 shares of its Class A common stock.
The initial public offering price is expected to be between $70.00 and $78.00 per share. Anthropic has applied to list its Class A common stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol “ANT.”
The proposed offering will be made only by means of a prospectus. Copies of the preliminary prospectus may be obtained from: Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC, Attention: Prospectus Department, 200 West Street, New York, NY 10282, by telephone at (866) 471-2526, or by email at prospectus-ny@ny.email.gs.com; or Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC, Attention: Prospectus Department, 180 Varick Street, 2nd Floor, New York, NY 10014.
A registration statement relating to these securities has been filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission but has not yet become effective. These securities may not be sold, nor may offers to buy be accepted, prior to the time the registration statement becomes effective. Today’s announcement does not constitute an offer to sell or the solicitation of an offer to buy these securities, nor shall there be any sale of these securities in any state or jurisdiction in which such offer, solicitation, or sale would be unlawful prior to registration or qualification under the securities laws of any such state or jurisdiction.

Anthropic seeks $1.4 trillion valuation in highly anticipated IPO, reportedly aiming for Nov. 19 or Nov. 20 debut
Ines Ferré   
·
 Senior Business Reporter
Tue 10 November 2026 at 6:50 am GMT-8
3 min read
Anthropic (
ANTH.PVT
) is seeking a valuation of roughly $1.4 trillion in its highly anticipated IPO, setting the stage for one of the largest public-market debuts during the AI boom. The stock is expected to begin trading on Thursday, Nov. 19, or Friday, Nov. 20, of next week, according to the 
Wall Street Journal
.
The AI developer said 
in an updated filing Tuesday
 that it plans to sell 825 million shares at between $70 and $78 each. At the top of that range, the IPO would generate up to $64.35 billion in proceeds. On a fully diluted basis, which includes employee stock options and other securities, the frontier AI lab could be valued between roughly $1.3 trillion and $1.45 trillion.
That would mark a substantial increase over the $965 billion valuation that Anthropic announced in a May funding round, but would be far below recent Wall Street estimates, which reached as high as 
$2 trillion 
in early October
.
Read more: 
Everything you need to know ahead of Anthropic's IPO
On top of the public offering, Anthropic is locking down an extra $26 billion on the side from tech's biggest heavyweights, including Nvidia (
NVDA
), Microsoft (
MSFT
), Google (
GOOGL
), TSMC (
TSM)
, Broadcom (
AVGO
), AMD (
AMD
), and Micron (
MU
). Those companies are buying nonvoting Class N shares at the same price public investors will pay. Because this is a private deal, those tech giants won't face the standard six-month lockup window that typically prevents insiders from selling right after the debut.
Anthropic plans to list on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT.
The IPO comes as investors have grown more cautious about the enormous valuations attached to AI companies. Anthropic will also debut against a backdrop of growing political resistance to the build-out of data centers and other infrastructure needed to support AI.
The company's public debut would follow SpaceX's (
SPCX
) record-setting IPO earlier this year. SpaceX raised $85.7 billion in its offering, and the company now has a market capitalization of $1.9 trillion.
Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei started the company in 2021 alongside a group of former OpenAI researchers. The startup has since emerged as a frontier AI lab and competitor to OpenAI.
Anthropic recently revealed revenue grew $1,206% to $33.2 billion through the first nine months of the year. The company also noted a 
staggering aggregate committed spend of $895.4 billion
, which it said is primarily related to compute and infrastructure.
But the AI growth story was put to the test last week as investors weighed a Democratic congressional win against the impact of the AI data center build-out. The market turmoil prompted prospective buyers to discuss 
lowering Anthropic's valuation to a range of $1.2 trillion to $1.3 trillion,
 with some considering as low as $1.1 trillion, according to Bloomberg.
