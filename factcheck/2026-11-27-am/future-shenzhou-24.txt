Update: China launches Shenzhou-24 spaceship to Tiangong space station with first foreign astronaut on board
Source: Xinhua
Editor: huaxia
2026-11-25 11:50:15
The Shenzhou-24 crewed spaceship, atop a Long March-2F carrier rocket, blasts off from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in northwest China, Nov. 25, 2026. (Photo by Wang Jiangbo/Xinhua)
JIUQUAN, Nov. 25 (Xinhua) -- China launched the Shenzhou-24 crewed spaceship on Wednesday, sending three astronauts, including a Pakistani payload specialist, to its orbiting Tiangong space station.
The spaceship, atop a Long March-2F carrier rocket, blasted off from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in northwest China at 8:18 a.m. (Beijing Time), according to the China Manned Space Agency (CMSA).
About 10 minutes after the launch, the spaceship separated from the rocket and entered its designated orbit, the CMSA said, noting that the launch of the Shenzhou-24 crewed spaceship was "a complete success."
The spaceship will later perform a fast, automated rendezvous and docking with the space station complex, and the Shenzhou-24 crew will then enter Tiangong.
The space station has entered the docking orbit, with good working conditions for docking and astronaut entry, the CMSA said.
The astronauts aboard the spaceship are mission commander and spacecraft pilot Tang Hongbo, flight engineer Wu Yi, and Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, the first-ever foreign astronaut to visit Tiangong.
The Shenzhou-24 mission marks the first joint flight consisting of Chinese and foreign astronauts in the history of China's manned space program.
According to Zhang Jingbo, spokesperson for the CMSA, more than 90 scientific experiments and technology tests are planned for the Shenzhou-24 mission, with these covering space life sciences, space material science, microgravity fluid physics and combustion science, fundamental physics, space medicine and new space technologies.
Four China-Pakistan cooperative projects in the fields of biotechnology and materials will also be conducted during the Shenzhou-24 mission, and Ali will conduct an in-orbit experiment on photosynthetic cyanobacteria, the spokesperson revealed at Tuesday's press conference.
After completing a short-term joint flight, the Pakistani astronaut will return to Earth aboard the Shenzhou-23 spacecraft alongside two Shenzhou-23 crew members. The other two Shenzhou-24 astronauts will stay in orbit for roughly six months along with Shenzhou-23 astronaut Zhu Yangzhu, who has already spent six months in orbit and will remain aboard Tiangong to complete a year-long stay experiment.
So far, China has sent 31 Chinese astronauts and one foreign astronaut into space. Wednesday's mission marked the 680th flight of the Long March carrier rocket series.

For the first time ever, a foreign astronaut has set foot inside China's Tiangong space station.
That pioneer is Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, who arrived at 
Tiangong
 aboard the 
Shenzhou-24
 spacecraft early this morning (Nov. 25).
"The Chinese space station is always something to look forward to!" the China Manned Space Agency (CMSA) wrote in 
an update today
 (in Mandarin; translation by Google). "China's 'Tiangong' welcomes partners from all over the world to visit!"
Ali, a former pilot in Pakistan's armed forces, is a payload specialist on Shenzhou-24. He found his way onto the mission thanks to a space-cooperation agreement that China signed with Pakistan in Islamabad 
in February 2025
.
Ali and his two Shenzhou-24 crewmates — commander Tang Hongbo and
flight engineer ​Wu Yi — enjoyed a short and smooth ride to their orbital destination. They 
lifted off
 atop a Long March 2F rocket on Tuesday (Nov. 24) at 7:18 p.m. EST (0018 GMT on Nov. 25) and reached Tiangong a little over six hours later.
Tang and Wu will live aboard the outpost for the usual six-month stint. Ali, however, will come home in just a week, hitching a ride with two of the three Shenzhou-23 astronauts, who 
arrived at Tiangong in May
.
Ali will be busy during his short space stay, conducting a set of joint Chinese-Pakistani experiments.
"Four collaborative projects in biotechnology and materials are planned, with Ali also scheduled to conduct an in-orbit experiment involving photosynthetic cyanobacteria," The Times of India 
wrote today
.
The third Shenzhou-23 crewmate, Zhu Yangzhu, will remain on the station until Shenzhou-24 comes home. Zhu will therefore set a Chinese record, becoming the first astronaut from his country to live in 
low Earth orbit
 for a full year.
Astronauts from the Soviet Union/Russia and the United States have already blazed this long-duration trail. The global record for longest continuous space stay is 437 days, set by cosmonaut Valeri Polyakov aboard the 
Mir space station
 from January 1994 to March 1995.
Another look at the Shenzhou-23 and Shenzhou-24 astronauts aboard Tiangong on Nov. 25, 2026. 
(Image credit: CMSA)
Ali is not the first person from Pakistan ever to reach space, by the way. That distinction belongs to adventurer Namira Salim, who 
traveled to suborbital space
 on a tourism flight with 
Virgin Galactic
 in October 2023.
Shenzhou-24 is the 12th crewed mission to Tiangong, a three-module, T-shaped outpost that China finished assembling in late 2022. Well, "finished" might not be the right term; China 
plans to expand Tiangong
, which is currently about 20% as massive as the 
International Space Station
.
