GPT-6.2の使用量「誤って割引されていた」　修正後はAstraで上限に40％早く達する見込み

　米OpenAIのプロダクト責任者ティボー・ソティオ氏（通称Tibo）は11月25日（現地時間）、17日にリリースしたAIモデル「GPT-6.2 Astra」（以下、6.2 Astra）と「GPT-6.2 Sol」（以下、6.2 Sol）の使用量が実際よりも少なく計算されていたとXで明かした。
　この誤りは既に修正されており、修正前と比べて6.2 Astraでは利用上限に40％早く、6.2 Solでは25％早く達することになると説明している。
　Tibo氏は今回の誤りへの「ささやかなお詫び」として有料プランユーザー向けに利用枠のリセット権を1回分付与するとしている。
　6.2 Astraは難度の高い専門業務向けの最上位モデル、6.2 Solは
「GPT-6.1 Sol」
の後継に当たる日常業務向けのモデル。いずれも難しいタスクにおいて、前世代モデルより少ない出力トークンで、より良い結果を出せるとうたっている。

OpenAI has admitted to a billing mistake with its new GPT-6.2 models
You’ll now hit your usage limits up to 40% faster
OpenAI is offering a one-time usage reset as compensation
If you've been enjoying using OpenAI's new 
GPT-6.2 models
 over the past week, especially since they also now 
work in Chat mode
, I've got some bad news: OpenAI has admitted that it made a billing mistake when launching the models — and now that it's corrected the error, you'll reach your usage limits much faster.
In a post on X
,
 OpenAI's Thibault Sottiaux explained that the company had incorrectly discounted usage of its new GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol models for most users. As a result of this mistake being corrected, users will now hit their usage limits 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol, compared with last week.
OpenAI says it won't retrospectively charge users for the difference in usage and, by way of an apology, is giving Go, Plus, Pro, and Business subscribers a one-off usage reset.

We made a billing mistake at launch of GPT-6.2 that incorrectly discounted GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol usage for most users.
We’ve fixed this now, so the same work will use more of your plan’s usage allowance. Comparing to last week, you will reach your limits 40% faster on GPT-6.2 Astra and 25% faster on Sol. You should still see these go further in usage compared to 6 Astra and 6.1 Sol. Other models weren’t affected, and we won’t charge you for the difference on past usage.
As a small token to apologize for the mistake, we’ll be adding one banked reset for everyone on Go, Plus, Pro, and Business.
November 26, 2026
Hitting usage limits
When OpenAI launched its new GPT-6.2 models — Sol and Astra — it
 made a point of saying
 that “Both models deliver better results on demanding tasks with fewer output tokens, so you can get more done within your usage limits.”
While Sottiaux maintains that this is still true when GPT-6.2 is compared to its predecessors, if you’ve spent the last week getting accustomed to the new models and how far your usage stretches, you’re going to have to revise those expectations.
The one-time usage reset is a welcome gesture, but it won’t change the situation in the long term.
It’s important to note that reaching your usage limits 40% faster is not mathematically equivalent to having your usage limits reduced by 40%. From my experience of using GPT-6.2, I’d say it’s very unlikely that you’ll hit your usage limit if you’re simply chatting to ChatGPT. However, if you’re a heavy user of Codex for writing code, then you’re much more likely to run into usage restrictions.

User reaction
Reaction from the 
Reddit community
 to the announcement was understandably skeptical about OpenAI’s motivations. One user said “At this point I think they are actively driving users away because they don't have enough compute even for paying users”, while another said “One banked reset as an apology for burning through Astra 40% faster from now on is pretty thin lol”.
One recurring theme does seem to be that it’s now unclear how much usage you get with your subscription. One user commented: “One thing I hate about AI is how confusing it is to know how much you are getting for your subscription. There’s no way to know how much a request will burn, and then there are both 5 hourly and weekly limits. It’s designed to be confusing so they can lessen what they offer each month without people noticing.”
I think that last comment gets to the heart of the matter. When I pay for a subscription to a streaming service, I have a pretty good idea of what I’m getting for my money. With an AI subscription, it’s much harder to know how much use I’ll actually get out of it, particularly when different models and tasks consume different amounts of my allowance.
OpenAI may well have made an innocent billing mistake here, but the whole episode highlights just how difficult it is for people to judge whether an AI subscription represents good value for money.

OpenAI「GPT-6.2」利用枠上限に達するのが最大40％早くなるように
2026年11月26日 15時15分更新
文● G.Raymond　編集●ASCII
ポストする
　OpenAIの利用枠リセット担当者として知られるティボー・ソティオ氏は11月26日、GPT-6.2の提供開始時に利用枠の消費量を計算する処理でミスがあり、大半の利用者について「GPT-6.2 Astra」と「GPT-6.2 Sol」の利用量が誤って少なく計上されていたとXで説明した。修正は完了しており、今後は同じ作業でもプランの利用枠をより多く消費するようになるという。
　説明によれば、先週に比べてAstraは「40％早く」、Solは「25％早く」利用上限に達すると説明しており、従来と同じペースで使っていても、上限に達するタイミングが早まることになる。
　ソティオ氏は、修正後も「6 Astra」や「6.1 Sol」と比べれば、同じ利用枠でより多くの作業ができるはずだとしており、他のモデルには影響せず、過去の利用分について差額を請求することもないとしている。
　お詫びとして、ソティオ氏はGo、Plus、Pro、Businessの全利用者に、必要なときに使える利用枠のリセット権を1回分追加する方針も示した。ただし、一時的に利用枠を回復できても、修正後の消費量が継続的に増える点は変わらない。GPT-6.2を前提に仕事を組んでいた人にとっては、リセット1回分では補いきれない変化となりそうだ。
