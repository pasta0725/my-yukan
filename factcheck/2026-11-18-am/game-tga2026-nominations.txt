　2026年にリリースされた優秀なタイトルに贈られる“The Game Awards 2026”（TGA 2026）のノミネート作品が、2026年11月18日（日本時間）午前2時から発表された。
広告
　ファミ通を含む、世界のメディアの投票により選ばれるThe Game Awardsのノミネート作。その年のもっとも優れたタイトルに贈られる“Game of the Year（ゲーム・オブ・ザ・イヤー）”の候補作など、30部門近くの候補作が選出された。“Game of the Year”にノミネートされたのは、以下の6タイトル。
007 ファーストライト
CONTROL Resonant
グランド・セフト・オートVI（GTA6）
Phantom Blade Zero
バイオハザード レクイエム

This year’s nominees are led by 
Grand Theft Auto VI
 with eight nominations, including Game of the Year and Best Game Direction, followed by 
CONTROL Resonant
 and 
Resident Evil Requiem
 each with six nominations, and 
Phantom Blade Zero
 with five nominations.
Users can vote now in all categories via authenticated online voting on 
official website

December 9 at 6:00 p.m. PT / 9:00 p.m. ET

PS5
, Prime Video, 
Roblox
, Steam, TikTok Live, Twitch, Twitter, YouTube, and many more.

Grand Theft Auto 6 has received multiple nominations at The Game Awards 2026, providing an early indication of the critical response to Rockstar’s long-awaited release.

The Game Awards
 2026 nominations during a live stream on Tuesday, which, as usual, were chosen by a voting jury of media publications, including this website, VGC.
More than 30 awards are up for grabs this year (see the full list of nominees below), and the winners will again be determined by a blended vote between the voting jury (90%) and public voting (10%).
In total, 
Grand Theft Auto 6
 is up for 8 awards, including Game of the Year, Best Game Direction, Best Narrative, Best Art Direction, Best Score, Best Audio Design, and

.

 (8) and
