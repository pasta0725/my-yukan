「Apple TV」アプリで動画ポッドキャストを視聴可能に、米国など5カ国で
            CNET Japan Staff
2026年11月17日 17時02分

　Appleは近いうちに、「Apple Podcast」で配信されている動画ポッドキャストを「Apple TV」アプリでも視聴できるようにすると発表した。
     提供：Apple
  ※クリックすると拡大画像が見られます
　これにより、「Apple TV 4K」、対応するスマートテレビ、「iPhone」「iPad」「Mac」、および「CarPlay」に対応した車両で動画ポッドキャストを視聴できるようになる。対象地域はオーストラリア、カナダ、メキシコ、英国、米国の5カ国で、日本は含まれていない。その他の地域では、引き続きApple Podcastアプリで動画ポッドキャストを視聴できる。
Apple
は最新のOSで提供するとしているが、具体的なOSバージョンや開始日は記載していない。
MacRumors
はこの機能が「iOS 27.3」や「iPadOS 27.3」で追加されると報じている。
　なお、購読者限定のポッドキャストはApple TVアプリでは再生できない。有料で購読している番組のコンテンツは、tvOS向けのApple Podcastアプリや、その他の対応機器で利用できる。
　配信者にとっては、Apple Podcastで一般公開している有効な動画ポッドキャストが、自動的にApple TVアプリにも配信されることになる。追加の契約や規約への同意は不要で、既存の配信フィードを引き続き利用できる。広告の管理権も配信者が保持する。
　大画面での番組紹介に向けた素材も用意する。「Universal Show Art」は横長の画像と白い番組名ロゴを組み合わせるもの。「Show Preview」は、出演者や番組の雰囲気を伝える音声付きの30秒の紹介映像だ。

Apple will soon be adding a video 
podcasts

Apple TV

Apple is bringing video podcasts to its Apple TV app, taking its rivalry with YouTube and Spotify onto television screens as the tech giant tries to reclaim ground in a market it helped create.
The company is beginning developer testing of a dedicated video podcast section Monday before making it available to consumers for free, even without an Apple TV subscription, according to 
The New York Times
. No public launch date has been announced. The feature is expected to reach more than 1 billion devices with the Apple TV app, including Roku players and smart TVs.
The move underscores how podcasting has changed since Apple helped popularize the format two decades ago. Video has become an increasingly important battleground for audiences and advertising, with YouTube and Spotify overtaking Apple as preferred podcast destinations. According to 
Edison Research
, YouTube ranked first among weekly U.S. podcast consumers in the third quarter, followed by Spotify at 17% and Apple Podcasts at 9%.

Apple 
introduced enhanced video podcast capabilities
 in February, allowing users to switch between watching and listening, download videos for offline viewing and access improved playback features. The update also gave creators the ability to dynamically insert video advertisements, including host-read spots, while retaining control over their content and monetization.
The company said at the time that it would not charge creators or hosting providers to distribute podcasts, though participating advertising networks would face impression-based fees for dynamically inserted video ads. Apple has partnered with providers including Acast, Amazon’s ART19 and SiriusXM to support the technology.
Competition is also expanding beyond traditional podcast platforms. 
Netflix has added more than 50 video podcasts
 through partnerships with Spotify, iHeartMedia and others, bringing shows including “The Bill Simmons Podcast” and “The Breakfast Club” to its streaming service.
Apple’s latest move puts those shows in front of a potentially much wider television audience. But reaching viewers is one thing; persuading them to leave YouTube and Spotify, where many podcast creators have already built substantial followings, is another.

===== https://podcasters.apple.com/support/5643-video-podcasts-apple-tv-app

Apple Podcasts App
 Video podcasts in the Apple TV app
Coming soon, video podcast episodes in Apple Podcasts will be available on the Apple TV app. Viewers can discover and watch your video episodes on Apple TV 4K, supported smart TVs, iPhone, iPad, Mac, and CarPlay-enabled vehicles.
What this means for your show
If your show already publishes video to Apple Podcasts, it will automatically be available through Apple Podcasts on the Apple TV app. Your feed stays the source of truth, and you stay in control of how you earn from it.
Automatic distribution.
 Every valid, publicly available video podcast distributed to the Apple Podcasts catalog automatically appears in the Apple TV app. No additional agreement or terms are required.
Full ad control.
 Your ad campaigns work the same way they do with HLS today. You can deliver dynamic or baked-in video ads, now with playback for the TV audience. 
Availability
Video podcasts in the Apple TV app are available with the latest OS releases in Australia, Canada, Mexico, United Kingdom, and the United States. Viewers in other regions can continue watching video podcasts directly in the Apple Podcasts app.
Video podcasts are not included in the Apple TV subscription service itself. Subscriber-only podcast playback is not supported on the Apple TV app. Subscribers can access content from their subscriptions on the Apple Podcasts tvOS app and across other supported devices. 
New artwork for big screens
The living room offers an entirely new canvas for your video show. To help your show look great on widescreen displays and unlock featuring opportunities in the Apple TV app, we have introduced new artwork options tailored for the big screen:
Universal Show Art
: Designed specifically for widescreen TV layouts, this pairs a full-bleed horizontal background image with a standalone, solid-white title logo. 
Show Preview
: A 30-second sound-on motion teaser that hooks viewers by putting your hosts and energy front and center.
Best practices for video podcasts
You can work with your hosting provider to ensure you are delivering the best experience for new TV audiences for your show.
Deliver high-definition video.
 High-resolution video enclosures (1080p or 4K) for living room displays.
Include closed captions and subtitles.
 The Apple TV app supports publisher-provided captions through native playback accessibility controls.
