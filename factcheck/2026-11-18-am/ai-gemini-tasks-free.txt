How it’s changing my daily routine
If your emails, calendar, passwords, photos, files, and payment cards are already in the Google ecosystem, Gemini is your best choice as a personal assistant because this sensitive data already lives at Google securely, and you don’t have to send it elsewhere.
Here’s how I’m using it.
Recently, our minivan’s check-engine light came on. Ugh. I pulled out Gemini and told it, "Our minivan is overdue for a service appointment. Please call the two nearest dealerships and figure out how to get an appointment early Friday morning over the next month or so."

How we protect your data
As Gemini becomes more capable, we continually advance our security defenses. When you assign Gemini a task, it uses 
Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine
 to work in a hardware-isolated virtual machine that’s dedicated to your account and never shared with other users. Each virtual machine is sandboxed, so Gemini works in a separate, isolated environment that’s regularly tested by our security red teams. This sandbox is just one of multiple layers of defense, including prompt-injection filters that scan search results, webpages, and more alongside Google Safe Browsing to limit which links Gemini can open.
As Gemini works on a task, you can jump in to give it more instructions at any point, and it’s also designed to pause and check with you before critical steps. For example, Gemini is designed to ask for your confirmation or pre-approval before clicking "Buy," and will ask clarifying questions if an instruction is ambiguous. For routine stuff you already trust, like adding those Cheerios to your grocery cart, you can set that to “Always allow” so it doesn’t pester you.
And finally, we give you access to settings and tools that help you safeguard your data and protect your privacy. You can adjust task settings, disconnect Google and third-party apps, or delete chat history anytime. For a closer look at how we’ve implemented a multi-layer defense-in-depth security strategy, read our 
technical paper
.
And please participate in our bug bounty program through Google’s 
AI Vulnerability Reward Program (AI VRP)
 to advance the security of agentic systems.
How you can help us improve
While we’ve tested tasks extensively to minimize mistakes, we haven't eliminated all of them. You may encounter situations where a task fails to complete or misses the mark. When you see this, please provide feedback by giving the response a “thumbs down.”
For example, the way personal assistants navigate the web is still evolving as sites are still adapting to these new use cases. On some sites, Gemini can make it most of the way through and then hand the screen back to you to click the final button. We’re partnering directly with major websites and security providers to help the web evolve so it works well for site owners and personal assistants. For decades, bots were blocked by default because most were malicious. Personal assistants require a more nuanced approach, and we believe open, verifiable standards will benefit everyone.

eventually became

Getting the function up and running is easy. Users merely flip a toggle within the app from Chat to Tasks, which enables the feature. Users can also send texts to Gemini or call the app to give it commands.

===== https://www.ithinkdiff.com/gemini-tasks-free-six-countries/
(status 200)
Google is making Gemini Tasks free in the United States, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia, and India starting November 17, removing the subscription requirement for a feature that was previously available only through Google AI Pro and Ultra. The Task toggle in the Gemini app was previously called Gemini Spark.
You switch the toggle on the Gemini home screen from Chat to Task, and Gemini works through jobs like booking appointments, managing bills and ordering groceries using your email, calendar and payment methods. Google announced the change in a Gemini Tasks blog post, and the feature runs on 
Gemini 4 Hydrogen
, the model Google released on November 12.
Gemini Tasks Features Start in the US
Google is rolling out a set of advanced features starting in the United States, though it has not listed them. Texting Gemini works on Android and iPhone, but you need a US phone number with an eligible carrier. You open Settings, tap Channels, tap Set up under Text with Gemini and verify the number.
Forwarding emails to gemini@google.com also works only in the United States. Calling businesses through Gemini is blocked in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana and Nebraska, and businesses can opt out of receiving AI calls.
Free accounts have been 
limited to Flash-Lite
 in the regular Gemini model list since October 9. Google has not said whether free accounts have a limit on how many tasks they can run.
Gemini Books, Pays and Shops for You
Google’s Josh Woodward shared several examples of how Gemini can handle everyday tasks on a user’s behalf. In one case, Gemini called two dealerships about a check-engine light on his 2019 Honda Odyssey, found an available slot and booked an early Friday appointment in under five minutes. It also accessed his utility portal through Google Password Manager and traced a $389 electricity bill to a heat pump running around 4 p.m., when electricity rates were higher.
Gemini also compared USB-C chargers based on wattage and color preferences, completed a purchase through Google Pay and found a discount code that saved an additional $10. In another example, it added forwarded school emails to his calendar in under a minute and flagged a scheduling conflict with a practice session. A text message and photo were also enough for Gemini to fill an Instacart order.
Apple is reportedly working on a similar approach with an 
agentic Siri
 that can continue working on tasks for users.
Services Gemini Can Use
Google Password Manager gives Gemini access to sites such as a utility portal.
Google Pay and the Agent Payments Protocol handle purchases, with digital tokens that work with Mastercard and Visa, and American Express coming soon.
Shopping Graph compares products, and Google Maps finds nearby dealerships.
Gmail and Google Calendar supply your messages and schedule, and Personal Intelligence adds details such as your vehicle and location.
Instacart handles grocery orders.
Google Wallet stores receipts and tracking info from Gemini purchases, and it expands to iOS on November 17.
The Connected Apps Platform links Gemini to more than 60 apps, and developers can apply to join.
Each Task Runs in Its Own Virtual Machine
Every task runs in a hardware-isolated virtual machine dedicated to your account, built on Google Cloud’s Agent Compute Engine. Google says security red teams tested the sandbox, and prompt-injection filters plus Google Safe Browsing limit which links Gemini can open.
Gemini pauses for your confirmation before critical steps such as clicking Buy, and you can step in at any time. You can set routine actions to Always allow, adjust task settings, disconnect apps or delete chat history, and you can set a hard spending cap.
