広告

ニューダンガンロンパV3 みんなのコロシアイ新学期

「学級裁判」に関するゲームシステムを紹介！「キョウキモード」では、新システム「議論コレクト」が登場！各要素を動画で見られるトレーラーも本日公開！
株式会社スパイク・チュンソフトは、ハイスピード推理アクション『スーパーダンガンロンパ２×２』（Nintendo Switch 2 / Nintendo Switch / PlayStation5 / Xbox Series X|S / Steam / Epic Games Store）について、ゲーム内の重要なパート「学級裁判」に関するゲームシステムを紹介いたします。
新シナリオが展開する「キョウキモード」の学級裁判では、新システム「議論コレクト」が実装され、真相解明に向けて議論を整理する楽しみが加わります。
また、学級裁判を含めゲームシステムの各要素を動画で見ることができる「システムトレーラー」も本日公開いたします。
学級裁判の新システム「議論コレクト」！
生徒たちの間で殺人事件が起きたとき、一定時間の捜査が行われた後、事件の真相を明らかにする「学級裁判」が開廷します。本作に含まれる新シナリオ「キョウキモード」には、学級裁判においても新システムの「議論コレクト」が実装されます。
学級裁判の中では、参加者それぞれが思い思いの論点で発言し、議論が荒れて収拾がつかなくなってしまうことがあります。そこで発生するのが「議論コレクト」。発言内容の正否を見極め、「肯定」か「否定」、いずれかのコトダマをぶつけて議論を整理し、議論の方向性を定めていきます。
次々と流れてくる発言を正しく整理できたら、最後に、今後話し合うべき内容を決める3つの選択肢が表示されます。ここでの選択が適切であれば「整論」となり、「議論コレクト」は成功。定めた方向性に沿って、その後の学級裁判は進んでいきます。

Danganronpa 2×2 Slayhem Mode’s Class Trial and New Akimbo Consensus System Revealed
    By sceditor on November 16, 2026 6:00 PM    
Share
    Tweet
Follow @SpikeChunsoft_e
Danganronpa 2x2 New System Trailer Released
HUNTINGTON BEACH, Calif. (November 16, 2026) – Spike Chunsoft, Inc. today revealed new details about the Class Trials featured in 
Danganronpa 2x2
’s new Slayhem mode. In addition to a completely original scenario, Slayhem mode introduces Akimbo Consensus. This system is an entirely new mini game that adds the engaging element of weighing testimonies in the pivotal Class Trials.
Along with today’s announcement, Spike Chunsoft, Inc. has released a new <trailer name> that offers a closer look at some of the game's mechanics, including the Class Trials.
The high-speed deductive action game is scheduled for release on January 14, 2027 in North America and Europe for Nintendo Switch™2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation®5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam® and Epic Games Store.
A new Class Trial system: Akimbo Consensus
When a murder occurs among the students, an initial investigation takes place, followed by a Class Trial. This is where Slayhem mode introduces the new Akimbo Consensus system.
During a Class Trial, parties may voice various, often conflicting, testimonies, causing the discussion to become chaotic and difficult to follow. This is the point where Akimbo Consensus comes into play. You must evaluate the validity of each statement and use either an "Affirm" or "Refute" Truth Bullet to address it, thereby organizing the discussion and steering it in the right direction.
Once you have correctly organized the statements, three options will appear at the end, allowing you to decide what should be discussed next. If you make the appropriate choice, it is considered a "Consensus," and the Akimbo Consensus is successful. The Class Trial will then proceed in the direction you have established.
Slayhem mode also utilizes a familiar system from a previous title

Game publisher Spike Chunsoft just dropped a new trailer for the upcoming “psycho tropical” mystery game 
Danganronpa 2×2
, detailing the Class Trial mechanics of the new Slayhem Mode. 
Scheduled to launch on January 14, 2027, Danganronpa 2×2 is a full remake of Danganronpa 2: Goodbye Despair. The new game features an Original mode, which follows the exact same story as the original 2012 title with improved graphics, animations and smoother gameplay, as well as the highly-anticipated Slayhem Mode. 
Slayhem Mode takes the familiar setting of Danganronpa 2: Goodbye Despair, but adds new motives, culprits, victims, and tricks across a completely new scenario. It also welcomes the mysterious 
Sakutaro “John Danganronpa” Higashi
 to the cast, though we have yet to see how this new character will impact the overall storyline.  
According to the new trailer, Slayhem Mode’s Class Trials will also introduce the “Akimbo Consensus” mechanic to the game. During the Class Trials, debates tend to get super chaotic, so the “Akimbo Consensus” will help you take the lead and stir the discussion in the right way. While other classmates present their theories on how the murders happened, you’ll have to use the special “Affirm” and “Refute” truth bullets to endorse or debunk statements as you go. 
After you’ve correctly organized the statements, you’ll be presented with three different options to choose from, allowing you to decide the next topic of discussion. A correct choice will establish a “Consensus,” and the trial will proceed in the direction you have established. 
Considering that Slayhem Mode will have a fixed difficulty level, which is apparently going to be more difficult than the base game’s Hard mode, mastering the Akimbo Consensus could be crucial to avoiding getting “eaten up” in the chaotic Class Trials. 
In addition to the new Akimbo Consensus, Slayhem Mode will also bring back the Debate Scrum from Danganronpa V3: Killing Harmony, a mechanic in which students split into two opposing groups and debate over their clashing opinions.
