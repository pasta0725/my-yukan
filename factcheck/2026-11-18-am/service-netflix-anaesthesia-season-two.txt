'Anaesthesia' Will Be Back With a Second Ceason. Netflix Renews Hit Medical Drama Starring Leszek Lichota 
Entertainment
17 November 2026
Global
Poland
Link copied to clipboard
Following the success of Netflix's first Polish medical drama, it’s official: 
Anaesthesia 
is returning for a second season. Just 24 hours after its premiere, the series claimed the number one spot on the platform's Top 10 list in Poland and has held onto it ever since. The show's triumph almost immediately took on a global dimension. 
Anaesthesia 
is successfully climbing the charts in other countries around the world (including Hungary, Slovakia, the Czech Republic, Argentina, France, and Belgium) and currently ranks 5th on Netflix’s global Top 10 list of Non-English TV.
In the new episodes, Leszek Lichota will return as Dawid Łazarz. Will the consequences of his past decisions give his battle against rigid procedures a completely new context, forcing him to find his place in the medical world all over again?
About the series 
Anaesthesia:
Anesthesia is the first Polish medical drama on Netflix, immersing the viewer in a tense world of hospital turmoil. Dr. Łazarz (played by Leszek Lichota) is a brilliant neurosurgeon whose career at a major hospital comes to an abrupt end following a bold, yet unsuccessful surgery. Forced to leave the large medical center, he takes a job at a provincial hospital. Getting to know the tight-knit local community and its unfamiliar rules, Łazarz discovers that his willingness to break the rules to save patients sparks both admiration and controversy. His confidence and lack of humility make him an exceptional doctor, but they also put him in conflict with the system and ethics, highlighting the weight of responsibility he bears for the lives of others. To ensure that 
Znieczulenie
 accurately reflects the procedures and realities of modern medical practice on screen, medical consultants oversaw the production. Creative input was also provided by Neal Baer — a physician and producer known for his work on the cult classic series 
ER
 — who shared his extensive storytelling and dramaturgical experience with both the creators and the cast.

Netflix isn’t hanging around with its newest medical drama. Just 13 days after it debuted, 
Anaesthesia
 has officially been renewed for a second season, with Leszek Lichota confirmed to return as the brilliant but disgraced neurosurgeon, Dr. Dawid Łazarz.
Medical dramas are having a moment right now, and when 
we previewed the series ahead of its release
, we asked whether it could be Netflix’s answer to 
The Pitt
. All eight episodes dropped on November 4th, and the show quickly found an audience well beyond its home country of Poland, where it was one of the headline titles in 
Netflix’s 12-title local slate for 2026
.
×
For those yet to check it out, the show follows a gifted, arrogant neurosurgeon whose career at a major hospital implodes following a bold surgery that goes wrong. Forced out, he takes a job at a provincial hospital, where his willingness to bend the rules to save patients earns him admirers and enemies in equal measure.
The series also has some serious genre pedigree behind it. Neal Baer, the physician and producer best known for his work on 
ER
, provided creative input to the writers and cast, while medical consultants oversaw production to keep the procedures accurate. Łukasz Ostalski directs.
How well has Anaesthesia performed on Netflix?
In short, it’s been a chart-topper at home and has traveled better than most international medical dramas manage, although the raw viewing numbers are more modest than the rankings suggest.
As we covered in 
our latest top 10 report
, 
Anaesthesia
 drew 3.5 million views in its opening week. That puts it in the middle of the pack among recent Polish titles that also premiered on a Wednesday, ranking fourth of the seven.
It’s well behind the two standout hits: 
Colors of Evil: Black
, which opened with 14.1 million views (four times as many), and 
High Water
, which opened with 10.2 million. It also fell just short of 
Lead Children
 (4.2 million) but outperformed 
Heweliusz
 (3.3 million), 
The Doll
 (2.5 million), and 
It Takes a Village
 (2.4 million).

So it’s a solid but unspectacular start on views alone. Where the show has impressed is in its staying power and reach in the daily charts. Netflix says it hit number one in Poland within 24 hours and currently sits fifth on the global Non-English TV top 10.
FlixPatrol data backs that up. Across its first 12 days, 
Anaesthesia
 has featured in the TV top 10s in 60 countries and hit the number one spot in nine of them: Poland, the Czech Republic, Slovakia, Hungary, Argentina, Chile, Uruguay, Venezuela, and Réunion. In Poland, it’s a perfect run, holding number one every single day since release.
Central Europe and Latin America have been the show’s strongest regions, while it also peaked at number two in Germany, Mexico, Turkey, and Ukraine. It’s been a harder sell in English-speaking markets. The show peaked at seventh in the United Kingdom and eighth in Canada before dropping out, and it hasn’t cracked the top 10 in the United States at all.
It’s also worth noting that the show is now on the way down in most regions. As of today, it remains in the top 10s in 33 countries, but outside of Poland, it’s no longer in the top three anywhere.
Still, that’s evidently enough for Netflix, particularly for a local-language title where domestic performance matters most.
What to expect from Anaesthesia season 2
Details are thin for now. Netflix has only confirmed Lichota’s return, teasing that the consequences of Łazarz’s past decisions will give his fight against rigid procedures a new context and force him to find his place in the medical world all over again.
No release date or filming schedule has been announced, and it’s unclear who from the supporting cast, which includes Karolina Gruszka, Aleksandra Popławska, Filip Kowalewicz, and Dorota Kolak, will be back.
The renewal gives Netflix a returning medical drama at a time when its roster in the genre remains small. It also joins a growing list of Polish Netflix Originals to score further seasons, including 
1670
, 
Go Ahead, Brother
, and 
The Mothers of Penguins
.
