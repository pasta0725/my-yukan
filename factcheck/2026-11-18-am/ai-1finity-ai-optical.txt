背景
生成AIをはじめとするAI技術の急速な普及に伴い、AIの学習や推論に必要な計算リソースやデータ量は急激に増加しています。一方、データセンターでは電力や設置スペースなどの制約が課題であり、複数のデータセンターに計算リソースを分散配置し、それらを連携させて効率的に活用することへのニーズが高まっています。

1Finity NICは、長距離においても大量のデータを高速かつ低遅延で転送する1Finity独自のデータ転送技術xRDMA（Extend Remote Direct Memory Access）と高速光インターフェースを搭載したネットワークインターフェースカードです。サーバの拡張インターフェースとして広く普及しているPCI Expressに準拠しており、対応する既存サーバに搭載するだけで容易に利用できます。例えば、離れた場所に設置されたデータセンター間においても、各拠点のサーバに1Finity NICを搭載するだけで、伝送距離や通信量の変動に応じた調整を行わずに、遠隔地のサーバ間を容易に接続できます。既存サーバの構成や対応要件に応じて1Finity NICを装着できるため、分散コンピューティング環境を効率的に構築できます。これにより、AIの学習や推論をはじめ、大量のデータ転送を必要とするコンピューティング環境において、地理的に分散した計算リソースやデータの効率的な活用を支援します。
1Finity NICの主な特長は以下の通りです。
1. 大容量かつ高速伝送を可能にする400Gbps × 2ポートの高速光インターフェースを搭載

Fujitsu launches 'world first' optical NIC with 1Finity N100
Fujitsu's networking arm unleashes network interface card bolstered by xRDMA technology
November 17, 2026
By
Giacomo "Jack" Lee
            Have your say

Fujitsu networking arm 1Finity released what it claimed to be the world’s first optical network interface card (NIC).
The 1Finity Ultra Optical System N100, also known as 1Finity NIC, is capable of delivering connectivity of up to 800 Gb/s over distances of approximately 62 miles (100 kilometers). With the release, 1FInity aims to address growing constraints on data center power, space, and computing capacity as AI adoption expands and AI workloads increasingly shift from training toward inference.
The PCI Express 5.0-compatible card incorporates two 400 Gb/s quad small form-factor pluggable 56 double density (QSFP56-DD) optical interfaces, providing an aggregate capacity of 800 Gb/s. Each interface uses eight lanes operating at approximately 50 Gb/s.
Central to the N100 is 1Finity’s proprietary Extended Remote Direct Memory Access (xRDMA) technology, developed to tackle the latency, packet loss, and throughput degradation seen with conventional Ethernet and RDMA communications over longer distances. Such protocols require repeated handshakes and other exchanges that can delay large data transfers and leave GPUs sitting idle. According to 1Finity, xRDMA reduces these exchanges through hardware acceleration, maintaining consistent throughput and low jitter regardless of changing transmission distances or traffic conditions.
In a comparison of AI inference data transfers across a 12-mile connection, the firm reported that its technology completed transfers in less than one-twentieth of the time required by conventional TCP/IP networking. Across workloads ranging from one to 64 concurrent executions, the company’s internal benchmarks showed transfer times of approximately 0.18 to 0.8 seconds using xRDMA, compared with up to 22.9 seconds over TCP/IP.
The N100 marks 1Finity’s first NIC release as well as the wider Fujitsu group from 
which it spun out of last year
. Its lineage dates back to 2022 research from Fujitsu working on a photonics-based NIC, held as part of the group’s wider research work into the 
all-photonics network (APN) concept.
 Both parent and subsidiary are part of the Innovative Optical and Wireless Network (IOWN) Global Forum.
In a press conference, Hideki Matsui, SVP and head of 1Finity's Photonics System Business Unit, claimed the N100 helps address “the growing compute demand of AI inference requires distributed data centers connected by (APN); 1Finity NIC enables this connectivity.”
