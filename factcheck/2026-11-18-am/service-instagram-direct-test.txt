Instagramのメッセージ専用アプリ「Direct」発表、アカウントなしでも使える

2026年11月17日 15時55分

　Metaは米国時間11月16日、「Instagram」のメッセージをやり取りする独立したアプリ「Direct」のテストを、タイとトルコで開始したと発表した。「Android」と「iOS」に対応し、Instagramのアカウントを持っていなくても、メールアドレスや電話番号で登録できる。
     提供：Meta
  ※クリックすると拡大画像が見られます
Direct
は、友人や家族と1対1やグループで会話するためのアプリだ。同社は、親しい相手との連絡に特化した場として開発したと説明している。Instagramの既存アカウントでもログインでき、その場合は両アプリ間で既存のチャットが同期される。
　Instagramのアカウントを使わずに登録する場合、連絡先の追加には相手の正確なユーザーネームを入力するか、対面でQRコードを読み取る必要がある。自分に新たな会話を持ちかけたり、グループに追加したりできるのは、登録済みの連絡先に限られる。
     連絡先を承認する画面。
提供：Meta
  ※クリックすると拡大画像が見られます
　会話を装飾する機能も備える。チャット内に絵を描く「Draw」、受信箱の上部で友人の近況を確認する「Notes」、独自のステッカーを作る機能などを用意する。音声の返信に笑い声や水中のような効果音を加えたり、チャットの背景をテーマで変更したりすることもできる。
     Directアプリでのやり取り。
提供：Meta
  ※クリックすると拡大画像が見られます
　安全策として、連絡先の通報やブロック、制限、削除に対応する。Metaは、若年層との不適切なやり取りの可能性を検知し、対処する技術も適用する。保護者は監督機能を通じて、子どもの会話相手を確認し、安全設定の調整や1日当たりの利用時間の制限ができる。
　Instagramのユーザーネームとパスワードでログインする10代の利用者には、Instagramの「ティーンアカウント」と同じメッセージ保護機能を適用する。利用対象は13歳以上で、13歳未満の可能性があるアカウントには年齢確認を求め、確認されなければ削除する。

===== https://about.fb.com/news/2026/11/introducing-direct-an-app-just-for-messaging/
(status 200)
Today, we’re introducing Direct, a new messaging app brought to you by Instagram for chatting one-on-one or in groups with friends and family. 
People rely on messaging to stay in touch with friends and family, and we built Direct to provide a dedicated space for these close connections. Direct has creative, customizable messaging tools made to make chats unique to you, including:
Draw:
 Draw and sketch directly inside your chat threads.
Notes:
 Catch quick updates from friends right at the top of your inbox.
Stickers:
 Create custom stickers to express yourself and share with friends and family.
Voice Effects:
 Add fun audio effects like crowd laughter or underwater sounds to your replies.
Themes:
 Personalize your chat backgrounds with custom visual themes.
How to Access Direct
Direct is now testing in Türkiye and Thailand, and is available with or without an Instagram account. You can sign up for Direct by using your email or phone number. If you have an existing Instagram account, you can log in to Direct with the same username and password, and your existing chats will sync across both apps. If you use Direct without an Instagram account, only your contacts can start a chat with you or add you to a group. To add a contact, enter their exact username or scan their QR code in person. 
Protections Built in From the Start
Direct has built-in protections from the start. Only your contacts and people you’re already connected to can start a chat with you or add you to a group on Direct. People can also report, block, restrict, or remove contacts at any time, and we’ll take action if someone breaks our rules.
We’ll use technology to detect and act on potentially inappropriate interactions with young people. And parents can use our supervision tools to see who their teen is messaging, adjust their safety settings, and set daily time limits.
Additionally, teens who use their Instagram 
username and password to access Direct receive all the same messaging protections they have in Teen Accounts.
Enforcing Our Age Limits
You must be at least 13 to use Direct. We’ll use technology to proactively identify accounts that may belong to someone under 13 and require them to verify their age. If their age is not verified, the account will be deleted. Additionally, we’re making it easier for our community to report underage accounts by simplifying our reporting process. This includes making it easier to submit a report on our 
Help Center
. 
We’re currently rolling out Direct in Türkiye and Thailand on Android and iOS.

===== https://www.engadget.com/2318452/meta-is-testing-direct-a-standalone-messaging-app-for-instagram/

Meta is testing Direct, a standalone messaging app for Instagram
It’s only available in Turkey and Thailand for now.
By
Karissa Bell
Nov. 16, 2026 8:00 pm EST
Meta
Add Engadget on Google:
Preferred Source
Google Discover
Meta is working on a dedicated messaging app for Instagram that could also expand the reach of the app's DMs. The company is starting to test Direct, a new standalone app that brings Instagram's inbox to a separate app, but doesn't require an existing Instagram account to use. Direct will only be available in Thailand and Turkey for now, Meta said in an update.
Direct looks pretty similar to Instagram's inbox at first glance. It has an almost identical layout, with existing chats carrying over from the main app if you log in with your Instagram credentials. It even has some of the same personalization features, like the ability to set custom backgrounds for individual threads and Instagram's 
status update feature
, Notes.
But while Instagram users can log in with their existing accounts, Direct doesn't actually require an Instagram login. People can also sign up for Direct with their email address or phone number, and they'll still be able to message friends in the app. However, it sounds like messaging without a linked Instagram account won't be quite as convenient. Meta notes that users without Instagram credentials will need to manually add contacts to the app via their Instagram username or a QR code in order to start conversations.
Meta
Meta says it's also bringing some of Instagram's safety features to Direct. The app won't allow people to start chats with accounts they aren't already connected with via their contacts. And parents will be able to monitor who their teen is chatting with and how much time they spend in the app if they use the company's parental control features. Additionally, Meta says it will "use technology to detect and act on potentially inappropriate interactions with young people," though it's vague about what exactly that will entail.
The test of Direct is an interesting move for Meta. Instagram head Adam Mosseri often notes that messaging is Instagram's most-used feature. But he was also 
reluctant
 to give Threads its own inbox because it seemed "redundant" to use multiple apps to reach the same people when Meta 
briefly considered
 bringing Instagram's inbox to Threads. He said 
at the time
 that mirroring the Instagram inbox could cause "notification routing weirdness" and generally seemed like a less than ideal experience for most people.
The app also raises questions about whether Direct would compete with WhatsApp in the countries where it's rolled out. It's unlikely an Instagram-centric app would replace WhatsApp for many people, but it could eat into that app's engagement if non-Instagram users can use Direct to message friends instead.
That may also be why Direct seems fairly limited for now. Meta describes it as a test that will only be available to people in Turkey and Thailand for the time being. But if the app is a success, it's likely the company will consider expanding it to more places.
