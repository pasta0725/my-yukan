 Directed by Jean-François Richet and starring Vincent Cassel in the title role, the film
 Quasimodo, the Soul of Paris 
will be available on Netflix on Friday, November 27, 2026. This historical drama revisits the literary myth amidst the revolutionary chaos of 1830.
In the Shadow of Victor Hugo: The Historical Context
The plot unfolds in 1830s Paris, as the capital is threatened by cholera under the reign of Charles X. The film departs from classic adaptations to explore a historical hypothesis: the existence of a hunchbacked stonemason working alone at Notre-Dame in the 1820s.
A reinvented plot: The story follows this sculptor, mistaken for a monster and targeted by the police, who potentially becomes the key to a medical cure. He tries to protect Agnès, a dancer he is in love with, turning the ruined cathedral into a sanctuary for their relationship.
The genesis of a literary figure: By shifting the action to the eve of the July Revolution, the screenplay bridges the worlds of The Hunchback of Notre-Dame and Les Misérables. The film draws on Tate Britain archives to imagine the encounter between the 28-year-old novelist and this "man of the people" who would fuel his work.
Character Incarnations
The narrative centers on characters navigating the social conflicts of the era.
Vincent Cassel (Quasimodo): The actor underwent a physical transformation requiring three and a half hours of makeup every day. Equipped with a single-use silicone mask painted to mimic skin and padding hidden under a linen smock, he brings to life a Quasimodo defined by restraint.
Daphné Patakia (Agnès): She plays a dancer who discovers herself through Quasimodo's gaze. Swept up by the momentum of history, she becomes an allegory of Liberty, wearing pale yellow in an echo of Delacroix's famous painting.
Supporting roles: Karim Leklou plays Coco-Lacour, head of the security brigade, dressed in a green reminiscent of the arsenic dye of the time. Benjamin Voisin plays Gaspard Tournier de Vaillac, an experimental doctor with a dark silhouette, surrounded by his students.
Immersion in 1830s Paris
Director Jean-François Richet has crafted an immersive dive into 19th-century Paris.
Notre-Dame at the center of the story: The cathedral is depicted as it was in 1830: an abandoned building threatened with dismantling. The interior scenes were shot at the Bourges Cathedral, which was completely emptied of its furniture for the film's needs.
The capital's neighborhoods: Production designer Philippe Chiffre and his team reconstructed the alleyways of Les Halles and La Grande Roquette, using the cobbled streets of Senlis and Compiègne as well as specifically built sets.
Art direction: The shoot mobilized a hundred technicians in the art department and required the creation of 117 costumes (and 800 rented) under the direction of Olivier Bériot. To recreate the catacombs of 1830, the team manufactured 9,000 skeletons and 8,000 skulls.
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
When will the film 
Quasimodo, the Soul of Paris
 be released?
 The film will be available on Netflix on Friday, November 27, 2026.
When exactly does the film's story take place?
 The plot is set in 1830, under the reign of Charles X, on the eve of the July Revolution and during a cholera epidemic. This is a departure from Victor Hugo's novel, which is set in the Middle Ages.
Who are the main actors?
 The cast includes Vincent Cassel (Quasimodo), Daphné Patakia (Agnès), Karim Leklou (Coco-Lacour), Benjamin Voisin (Gaspard Tournier de Vaillac), and Noémie Lvovsky.
Is the story of this Quasimodo based on true events?
 The screenplay is inspired by a historical discovery: the memoirs of English sculptor Henry Sibson mention a hunchbacked stonemason working on the restoration site of Notre-Dame in the 1820s. The hypothesis is that Victor Hugo might have crossed paths with him during his research.
How long did Vincent Cassel's makeup take?
 The actor's physical transformation required three and a half hours of makeup daily. The Atelier 69 team had to create about forty custom silicone masks (one per shooting day), with every single hair of the beard and eyebrows hand-punched.
How many costumes were used for the historical recreation?
 Costume designer Olivier Bériot's team crafted 117 costumes and rented 800 more. The carnival sequence alone required dressing 300 people.
Where was the film shot?
 Shot entirely in France, the production notably used the Bourges Cathedral to represent the interior of Notre-Dame, the Sorbonne University for the Hôtel-Dieu medical faculty, and military grounds south of Paris to recreate period streets with the help of digital effects.
