Appleサポートコミュニティ、11月20日に閉鎖へ　新規投稿は既に終了
公開
2026年11月12日 08時46分
[ITmedia]
印刷する
Googleで優先するソースとして追加
　米Appleは、「Appleサポートコミュニティ」を11月20日に閉鎖すると、コミュニティサイトで告知した。「Appleサポートコミュニティは、2026年11月20日（金）をもって閉鎖いたします。本日より新規投稿の受け付けを終了し、以降は閲覧のみ可能となります。引き続き、Appleサポートの公式サイトで、信頼できる情報やサポートをご利用いただけます。これまで貴重なお時間と専門知識を惜しみなく提供し、Apple製品をお使いの多くの方々を支えてくださったコミュニティメンバーの皆様に、心より感謝申し上げます。」と案内している。
サイトに表示された告知
　Appleサポートコミュニティは、ユーザー同士が質問や解決策、使い方のヒントを共有し合う公式オンラインフォーラムだ。1998年に「Apple Tech Exchange」という名称で開設された後、何度か改称され、2022年に現在の名称になった。
　本稿執筆現在は、既存のディスカッションを閲覧したり、過去の回答を検索したりすることは可能だが、新たな投稿はできなくなっている。閉鎖後、これまでの質問や回答のコンテンツをどうするのかについての説明はない。

'Apple Support Community' Permanently Closing on November 20
Tuesday November 10, 2026 7:50 pm PST
 by 
Joe Rossignol
Apple today announced that the long-running 
Apple Support Community
 discussion forums will be permanently closing on Friday, November 20. 
Effectively immediately, the Apple Support Community forums are in a read-only state, with new posts no longer being accepted. The 
final message
 posted to the site appears to have been submitted at 5:49 p.m. Pacific Time today.
There is still a sign-in button, but it leads to an internal portal for Apple's corporate employees.
Apple's full notice added to the site:
The Apple Support Community will permanently close on November 20, 2026. Starting today, the site will no longer accept new posts and is available in a read-only state.
You can continue to find trusted guidance and support on the official 
Apple Support site
. 
We want to thank the community members who have contributed their time and expertise to help others with their Apple products.
It is the end of an era. Apple offered an online discussion forum for user-to-user technical support since 1998, when it launched the Apple Tech Exchange. The forums were renamed to Apple Discussions in 2000, and then to the Apple Support Communities in 2011, before taking on their current Apple Support Community name in 2022.
Apple informed 
MacRumors
 that it decided to sunset the Apple Support Community after a careful evaluation of strategy and customer needs. 
Alternatives to the Apple Support Community include the 
MacRumors Forums
 and 
Reddit
, and customers can continue to 
contact Apple Support
.

Apple’s online community forum will close on November 20.
On Tuesday night, Apple posted 
a message
 to the Apple Support Community announcing the forum’s closure:
The Apple Support Community will permanently close on November 20, 2026. Starting today, the site will no longer accept new posts and is available in a read-only state.
You can continue to find trusted guidance and support on the official 
Apple Support site
.
We want to thank the community members who have contributed their time and expertise to help others with their Apple products.
For 28 years, the forum has provided Apple users with a way to get quick technical advice about their products. That advice usually came from fellow users, though Apple employees occasionally chimed in. As with any community forum, the helpfulness and accuracy of the advice varied.
Apple reportedly told 
MacRumors
 that it is shutting down the forum after evaluating its support strategy and customer needs.
The Apple Support Community dates to 1998, when the online forum was known as Apple Tech Exchange. Two years later, Apple renamed the community forum to Apple Discussions. 
In 2011
, Apple revamped the forum, adding dedicated communities for specific devices and the ability to track responses to posted questions, while renaming it Apple Support Communities. By 2022, the forum was called Apple Support Community, as MacRumors noted this week.
Based on yesterday’s announcement, it seems Apple now prefers that its customers rely on official Apple employees for technical support. The forum may also have become less popular as rival forums grow, including Reddit and the technical support communities on the 
MacRumors
 and 
AppleInsider
 forums.

It’s the end of the road for the Apple Support Community. The dedicated discussion forum, which gave Apple users a place to exchange advice, troubleshoot, and generally chat about Apple products, has been placed into read-only mode, with a message from the company announcing that the 
Support Community will shut down
 for good on November 20th.
Here’s the full banner notice that’s currently displayed on the website:
“The Apple Support Community will permanently close on November 20, 2026. Starting today, the site will no longer accept new posts and is available in a read-only state. The Apple Support Community has undergone several name changes over the years, but its history as an online forum for providing user-to-user tech support goes back to 1998, when it first launched as the Apple Tech Exchange. Apple hasn’t specified what will happen to the decades worth of Support Community posts when the platform shuts down next week. Presumably, they’ll all disappear with it.
