Netflix Releases 'Welcome to El Bulli' on November 20

09 November 2026
Global
Spain
Link copied to clipboard
Download images
Netflix today announced that 
Welcome to El Bulli,
 the series inspired by the true story that revolutionized world gastronomy, will premiere on 
November 20
.
Produced by 
Fishcorb
 and 
Minoría Absoluta
 and created and directed by 
David Pujol
, 
Welcome to El Bullil
 focuses on the decisive years of 
Ferran Adrià, Juli Soler and Albert Adrià
. Beyond the story of a legendary restaurant, the series explores friendship, ambition, personal sacrifice and the tensions that accompanied a constant pursuit of innovation, reflected in the impact it had on global food criticism.
Miquel Fernández
 plays Ferran Adrià, joined by 
Iván Massagué
 as Juli Soler and 
Carlos Cuevas
 as Albert Adrià. 
Mònica López
 plays Glòria Canabach, while 
Joana Vilapuig
 plays her niece Lola. To build their characters, the actors worked closely with the real people behind the story and those around them, incorporating testimonies and experiences that bring authenticity to the narrative.
The series unfolds across two eras. In the 1980s, a young Ferran Adrià arrives at the kitchens of el Bulli almost by chance. What begins as a temporary job becomes the origin of an unprecedented creative adventure. Together with Juli Soler and Albert Adrià, he will drive a transformation that puts a small restaurant on the Costa Brava at the center of world gastronomy.
Decades later, in 2025, Glòria Canabach, a prestigious food critic weighed down by deep guilt, returns from the United States after her father’s death. Reuniting with her niece, Lola, leads them to take on a new challenge: reopening the historic family restaurant, Can Canabach. As Ferran faces the end of an unparalleled era and Glòria seeks to reconcile with her past, both find in the kitchen a chance to heal old wounds and start over.
Filming took place at various locations across Catalonia, with the Costa Brava as the main setting. The spaces and atmosphere that witnessed the birth of one of the most influential periods in recent gastronomic history have been recreated.
Made up of six 45-minute episodes, “GÈNESIS” is supported by the ICEC and the Catalan Government’s Department of Agriculture, Livestock, Fisheries & Food and 
and the Catalan Tourist Board
. The series had its world premiere at the San Sebastián International Film Festival before its successful launch on 3Cat, where it racked up more than 2 million views on its platform and a 20% prime-time audience share.

Presented out of competition at the San Sebastian Film Festival under the title 
Genesis (Gènesi)
, the Catalan drama about the life of world-famous chef Ferran Adrià has been acquired by Netflix in a rare move for the streamer, and will now arrive as 
Welcome to El Bulli
 on November 20, 2026 in select regions including the US, UK and most of Western Europe (check here as to 
whether it’s coming where you live
). Here’s everything we know.
Over the past year, Netflix has gradually gotten back into the acquisition game for its series, after spending years focusing on productions of its own. After a couple of Australian shows and a few 
AMC coproductions
 in the US, the six-episode miniseries originally known as 
Genesis (Gènesi)
 is the latest to join the library. It will stream in a raft of territories, including the US, the UK, Western Europe and Latin America, and the streamer will also carry the show exclusively on SVOD in Spain. It’s a timely pick-up, too, with kitchen dramas like 
The Bear
 (FX/Hulu) enjoying global success.
What is 
Welcome to El Bulli
 about?
Netflix’s synopsis for the series reads:
×
The origins and rise of chef Ferran Adrià inspire this biopic series about how he transformed El Bulli into one of the best restaurants in the world.
The original logline from 3Cat goes into more detail, revealing the show is split across two timelines:
In the 1980s, a young Ferran Adrià arrives at El Bulli, sparking a gastronomic revolution alongside Juli Soler that would propel the restaurant to global success. Decades later, in 2025, food critic Glòria Canabach returns to Catalonia to reopen the family business with her niece, Lola. The two timelines intertwine in a story about obsession, resilience, and the power of cooking to heal the wounds of the past.
Netflix lists the series under its Spanish, Drama and Based on Real Life categories, describing it as inspiring and tagging it as a limited series, so don’t expect a second season.

(as Ferran Adrià),

Who is behind the show?
Greenlit in 2025 to coincide with Catalonia being named a global gastronomy destination, the six-episode show aired from mid-October on local channel and commissioner 3Cat, where it delivered the broadcaster’s biggest ratings for a series launch in three years, with 2 million viewers and 20% in ratings share. It was created by Eva Mor, David Pujol and Albert Val, with Pujol also serving as director.
What else do we know about the show?
The show was produced with the active involvement of 
Ferran Adrià
 himself, who recreated some 80 dishes over the (main) course of the series with the help of former elBulli cooks.
Speaking to 
Variety
 ahead of its screening in the Other Works section at San Sebastian, the director said the show would frame the eventual closure of elBulli, which came while the restaurant was at the height of its success, as a mystery.
Marco Arabit
, whose company New Regency holds the international rights to the show and sold it to Netflix, told 
trade outlet C21
 that the series also sets out to showcase the discipline of molecular gastronomy: “Some people think of gastronomy as ‘the more refined, the better,’ but with 
Genesis
 the connection with food is deeper and more meaningful. It shows the audience that food is also a form of expression – it brings people together, evokes memories and triggers emotions. It’s a story of legacy, family trauma, healing, creativity and a lot of heart.”
Finally, a 
Guardian
 piece following the San Sebastian premiere noted that while the real elBulli kitchen was calm and collected, the show ups the ante with a far more frantic portrayal: “Some cooking scenes are oddly frantic, given that Adrià was not a shouty chef à la Gordon Ramsay, nor even slightly in the rock’n’roll mode of Anthony Bourdain.”
When will 
Welcome to El Bulli
 be on Netflix?
All six episodes of 
Welcome to El Bulli
 will arrive on Netflix on 
Friday, November 20, 2026
, just weeks after the series wrapped its original run on 3Cat.
The acquisition isn’t quite global, but the series will be available in most regions, including the US and the UK (plus other international territories).
