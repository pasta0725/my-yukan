Starting today, GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol are available in ChatGPT Work and Codex for Plus, Pro, Enterprise, and Edu users.
Pro 500 subscribers can use Ultrafast with either model. For the first time, we’re also bringing Ultrafast to Pro 200 subscribers, starting with GPT-6.2 Sol.
Developers can access gpt-6.2-astra and gpt-6.2-sol in the OpenAI API, with Ultrafast available.
GPT-6.2 Astra
A frontier model built for your hardest projects. It sets new highs on evals across professional work and scientific research at less than a third of the cost per task of GPT‑6 Astra. You’ll see the difference in things like slideshows, websites, and 3D designs.
