IMAGES
After capturing hearts in Season 1 with its exploration of love, family and the search for belonging,
Tuiskoms
returns to the picturesque coastal town of Wilderness, where familiar faces, fresh drama and new beginnings await its residents.
Wedding bells are ringing for Fleur (
Amalia Uys
) and Werner (
Armand Aucamp
), but an unexpected pregnancy throws their carefully laid plans into question. As the couple considers what this means for their future, others are also confronting difficult choices and unresolved matters from the past.
Clint Brink
and
Kim Syster
join the cast as Brendon and Amy, two newcomers whose arrival brings a different dynamic to the close-knit community, sparking connections and complicating existing relationships.
Tuiskoms
Season 2 boasts an impressive ensemble of some of South Africa’s most recognisable acting talent, including newcomer
Adri-Jo Waterboer
,
Zane Meas
,
Pierre Van Pletzen
,
Melissa de Vries
,
Jane De Wet
and
Cantona James
. The second season of
Tuiskoms
premieres on Netflix on 8 December 2026 and runs for six episodes.
ENDS
Synopsis
When Fleur discovers unexpected news of a new arrival in her family, her carefully rebuilt life is upended, just as prodigal daughter Amy returns to Wilderness seeking redemption. A tight-knit community is forced to redefine what home, forgiveness and second chances truly mean.
