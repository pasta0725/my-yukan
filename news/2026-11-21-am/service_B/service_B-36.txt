‘Never Surrender’ Takes Indonesian Action Cinema Worldwide at No. 1 on Netflix
Entertainment
18 November 2026
Global
Indonesia
Link copied to clipboard
Netflix Indonesia’s latest original film,
Never Surrender
, has claimed the No. 1 spot on
Netflix’s Global Top 10 Non-English Movies
. Since its premiere on November 12, the film has garnered 11 million views.
Never Surrender
ranked No. 1 in 12 countries, and made the Top 10 in 83 countries, including Indonesia, the United States, Mexico, Brazil, Turkey, France, Germany, Saudi Arabia, Malaysia, Philippines, Thailand, Japan, and South Korea.
Never Surrender top 1
Set in a small village in Central Java,
Never Surrender
combines intense action sequences, tactical ingenuity and family dynamics that feel grounded in real life. The film follows an unlikely hero: a grandfather determined to reunite his family. When the unexpected arrival of his two grandchildren brings them into a dangerous situation, he draws on his experience as a special forces operative and puts his combat skills to the test to protect his family from a criminal syndicate.
Director
Sidharta Tata
said, “I poured my heart and soul into making
Never Surrender
, with a commitment to delivering a unique action film experience. Beyond its thrilling action sequences, the film also tells a heartwarming story about family. Thanks to the collaboration with Netflix and producer Kimo Stamboel and Edwin Nazir, I got the opportunity to explore various creative possibilities, including bringing to life a long-held dream of mine: staging an exciting car chase scene. I am also deeply grateful to audiences in Indonesia and around the world who have watched and warmly embraced
Never Surrender
. Your support means so much to me and motivates me to continue creating bold, fresh and entertaining films.”
Never Surrender
marks Sidharta Tata’s first collaboration with renowned producers
Kimo Stamboel
and
Edwin Nazir,
the duo behind the 2025 Netflix film,
The Elixir
. The movie stars
Lukman Sardi
,
Callista Arum
,
Sulthan Hamonangan
,
Derby Romero
and
Donny Alamsyah
.
