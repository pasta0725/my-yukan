TITLE: Walmart teams up with Meta to bring Black Friday deals to AI agent Muse
SITE: TechCrunch AI
PUBLISHED: 2026-11-25 06:32 (page: 2026-11-24T21:32:38+00:00)
OG_IMAGE: https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/muse-walmart.png?w=1200

TEXT:
Shopping with AI is becoming less of a futuristic idea and more of something companies expect us to do. Walmart is the latest retailer making that shift, teaming up with Meta to let customers shop its Black Friday deals through Muse, Meta’s AI agent.
The partnership,
announced
Tuesday, gives shoppers another way to find products, compare prices, and make purchases without browsing Walmart’s website themselves. Instead, customers can simply tell Muse what they are looking for and let the AI do the work.
The announcement highlights how differently Walmart and Amazon are approaching AI shopping. While
Amazon
has taken steps to stop third-party AI agents from making purchases for customers, Walmart is leaning into the idea.
Image Credits:
Walmart
To get started, users open the Muse app, sign in to their Walmart account, and return to the assistant to begin shopping. They can ask it to find specific products, compare options, or track down deals. Once a customer makes a request in Muse, the assistant sends it to Walmart, which returns relevant products and helps build a shopping cart.
Walmart says it has built privacy protections into the experience. For instance, Muse doesn’t get access to a customer’s Walmart shopping history or other personal information. Shoppers can also set spending limits, require approval before purchases, and review orders placed by the agent.
Plus, the company notes users can always delete their agent’s profile if they want to.
The experience is powered by Walmart’s Agent Gateway, a new system that allows outside AI assistants to connect directly with the retailer’s online store. This
launched
in October, and the company is planning to open it up to more developers and AI platforms, meaning Muse could eventually be just one of several assistants capable of shopping Walmart’s online store.
Walmart is not the only company teaming up with Meta’s Muse. Instacart also
recently joined forces
with the AI assistant.
Beyond Muse, Walmart has recently rolled out more AI-powered features to its own shopping assistant, Sparky.
Some of the
newer additions
include turning handwritten shopping lists into shopping carts, using photos and voice commands to find products, and helping customers get around their local store. Shoppers can search for an item and use a pin on the store map to see exactly which aisle it’s in. There’s also a “Flash Price Tag” button that makes the item’s shelf label light up.
