The film is a sweeping love story set in the world of Brazil's Air Force in the 1990s.
Duty, loyalty and love share the same runway in
Asas do Destino
, Netflix’s new Brazilian film, now in production. Starring André Lamoglia, Rayssa Bratillieri and Ronald Sotto, the romantic drama unfolds against a backdrop of military uniforms and dreams within the world of the country's Air Force, a setting never before explored in Brazilian fiction.
In the film, nursing technician Josi (Rayssa Bratillieri) and cadet Nelsinho (Ronald Sotto) are on the verge of building a life together. Then everything changes. With Matheus (André Lamoglia) by her side, Josi must decide how far honor, friendship and the courage to love can take her.
Asas do Destino
is a Glaz Entretenimento production, directed by Daniel Lieff (
Here the Whole Time)
. The screenplay was written by Marcos Bernstein and Mariana Torres, with Gustavo Bragança serving as a consultant, based on a story by Mayra Lucas, Marcos Bernstein and Hugo Kenzo. The cast also includes Eucir de Souza, Gabriel Kim, Jhenifer Delfino, João Fontenele, Sacha Bali and Sidney Santiago Kuanza, with a special appearance by Danton Mello.
