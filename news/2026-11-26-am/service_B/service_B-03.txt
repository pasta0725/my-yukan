‘Chira__jeevi’
made you look twice, you already know something is up. As for the missing ‘N’? It was a clue to where the Megastar was headed next.
The excitement is palpable as the fans wait to see Actor Chiranjeevi take the host’s chair in
Chiranjeevi’s Megaverse

Iddhi Na Adda
