São Paulo, November 9, 2026 – The documentary
The Unthinkable Daughter
premieres this Wednesday, 11 November, on Netflix. The production, whose trailer was released today, features never-before-seen interviews with Suzane, convicted of the deaths of her parents, Manfred and Marísia, in São Paulo, in 2002. More than 20 years after the crime, and more than 10 years after her last interview, the documentary investigates who Suzane became after serving part of her sentence and confronts her with what was investigated and established. Archive footage and interviews with experts and people connected to the case round out the narrative.
“More than two decades after the crime, the documentary allows us to discuss subjects that are very dear to our society, such as the media and its impact on this and other similar cases, resocialization, and a bit of the reality of the penal system in Brazil,” says Lygia M. Barbosa, who directs the documentary alongside Paulo Gambale Maká.
Produced by Kromaki, in co-production with Grama Filmes, the documentary has Rodrigo Letier as producer. Executive production is by Roberta Oliveira and Fernanda Abreu. The screenplay is written by Rafael Spínola, Thaís Falcão, Raphael Scire, Jordana Berg, EDT. and Victor Miaciro.
The Unthinkable Daughter
premieres on November 11, only on Netflix.
