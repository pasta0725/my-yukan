Top chip manufacturer TSMC is ready to invest $1 billion in Anthropic and OpenAI. That would give the Taiwanese chipmaker direct stakes in leading AI developers, even as its own contract chipmaking business has profited enormously from the AI data center buildout.
The chipmaker’s board approved plans to invest up to $500 million in OpenAI preferred stock reserved for large investors and another $500 million to purchase Anthropic common shares at the latter’s expected IPO price,
according to Reuters
. Such an investment may give markets “a shot of confidence as the artificial-intelligence trade shows signs of strain,”
The Wall Street Journal
reported.
But analysts also warned that such circular financing among investors, suppliers, and customers has made it difficult to evaluate real market demand for AI-related hardware and services, according to the Journal.
TSMC’s chip fabrication plants physically produce the vast majority of
Nvidia’s chips
designed for AI data center workloads and make most of Apple’s custom chips used in
iPhones
,
Mac laptops
and desktops, plus
Apple’s planned AI computing server
. Most of these fabs are located in Taiwan, but TSMC also operates fabs in Japan and China, and it has an expanding US manufacturing cluster in Arizona. It is also building a new fab in Germany.
Meanwhile, Anthropic and OpenAI are both purchasing huge quantities of AI hardware manufactured by TSMC for their data center projects. So TSMC’s announced investment certainly seems to fit into the broader trend of circular financing that has become common during the AI boom, even as tech stocks have gyrated recently as investors have grown increasingly jittery.
Still, TSMC has plenty of money to spare on such a bet. Ongoing demand for AI-related chips has enabled the Taiwanese chipmaker to report six consecutive quarters of record profit, with the latest example being the company earning more than $23 billion in profit during the July through September 2026 quarter,
according to Reuters
.
TSMC’s planned investment in Anthropic and OpenAI also pales in comparison with
circular financing deals
from major US tech companies. The most
notable example is Nvidia
, which has invested
$30 billion in OpenAI
and
$10 billion in Anthropic
so far, in addition to putting tens of billions of dollars into many other
AI and robotics companies
.
