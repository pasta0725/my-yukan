Meta is losing its longtime head of  infrastructure
A key figure in Meta’s growth into a social media and tech industry powerhouse is leaving the company.
Santosh Janardhan, the company’s longtime head of infrastructure and co-head of engineering, will be stepping down, Meta told TechCrunch. He will be replaced by Dave Brown, who joined the company as vice president of infrastructure earlier this year, the company added. Previous to coming aboard at Meta, Brown spent 19 years at Amazon, where he focused on compute, cloud, and AI,
his LinkedIn shows
.
It sounds like Janardhan will remain at Meta for a little bit, although he is expected to leave next year. “Santosh will stay on for transition and he is moving into an advisory role on Meta Compute,” said a Meta representative.
Janardhan, who originally joined the company in 2009, helped the firm grow into the massive internet presence that it is today. Bloomberg
originally reported
Janardhan’s departure, citing an internal memo that had recently been circulated. It’s unclear why Janardhan is stepping down from the role. The memo did not specify the reasons for his departure and Meta didn’t immediately respond to a request for clarity on the issue. However, Janardhan’s memo
apparently cited
the need to “pass the baton.”
In recent years, the company’s investment in infrastructure
has exploded
, as it seeks to keep up with its competitors in the AI race. Earlier this year, Janardhan was tasked
with leading Meta Compute
, the company initiative to scale its data center investments and strategy.
