As AI agents take on more work, they need capable open models and security that advances alongside them.
Hyundai Motor Group is exploring NVIDIA NemoClaw blueprints and the NVIDIA OpenShell runtime, while AIM Intelligence is integrating its Starfort platform with NVIDIA OpenShell to support secure AI agents for employee productivity and enterprise workflows
.
A key component of the NVIDIA Open Agent Safety Platform, OpenShell provides a secure runtime with sandboxed execution and policy-based controls to safeguard sensitive data, manage access to enterprise tools and govern autonomous agent actions.
AIM Intelligence
, a Korean AI security company, is integrating its Starfort agent control platform with NVIDIA OpenShell. The goal is to combine OpenShell’s runtime access controls with Starfort’s detection of malicious instructions and data leakage. The company is also considering a preliminary proof of concept for the public energy sector, with potential applications across enterprise, industrial and robotics environments.
On the model side,
LG AI Research
is opening application programming interface access for K-EXAONE-2.5, developed using NVIDIA Nemotron datasets, including
Nemotron-Personas-Korea
.
Nemotron-Personas-Korea is an open dataset of 7 million fully synthetic Korean personas, grounded in official Korean statistics, that model builders use to ground training data and evaluation in Korean contexts. NVIDIA Nemotron is a family of open models, datasets and techniques that help developers build specialized, efficient AI agents.

NVIDIA Nemotron
open models and get started building secure agents with
NVIDIA OpenShell
