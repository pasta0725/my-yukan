TITLE: Elon Musk says ousting Jensen Huang is like ‘kicking Michael Jordan off your team’
SITE: TechCrunch AI
PUBLISHED: 2026-11-19 08:25 (page: 2026-11-18T23:25:17+00:00)

Elon Musk has already been vocal about his disapproval of the Nvidia board’s decision to oust its founder and CEO Jensen Huang after
a series of extramarital affairs
were made public, including one with
an alleged Chinese spy
who is suspected of feeding sensitive information back to Beijing. At The New York Times DealBook Summit on Wednesday, Musk elaborated on his thoughts about Nvidia’s leadership change.
“Jensen is an old friend of mine,” Musk said. “I would call Jensen, you know, Michael Jordan at his peak level, in terms of what he means to a team.”
On X, the social media platform he owns, Musk even said he would hire Huang.
“He’s one of the most talented entrepreneur-CEOs in history,” Musk said when asked about his X post. “Who wouldn’t want to hire Jensen?”
Musk also signed a
petition
, alongside over 200,000 other people,
urging Nvidia
to reinstate its former CEO. Nvidia’s board, however, confirmed it is
not planning
to bring Huang back.
“[It] doesn’t seem like a smart decision to me…” Musk said of that decision. “There’s no one that can really replace Jensen.”
Huang “has long-term relationships that have been built up over decades,” Musk added. “He understands technology incredibly well. His team loves him… and many of them are there because of him, and have higher offers to go elsewhere, and have turned them down because of personal loyalty to Jensen.”
Anduril CEO Palmer Luckey also said that he thinks Nvidia is making the
wrong decision
by not reinstating Huang.
“China wants Jensen out, so I want him in,” Luckey said earlier in the day at the DealBook event. “They’re thrilled to have Jensen out… There’s a reason it’s knocking a trillion dollars off Nvidia’s market cap. They know it makes America weaker to knock him out.”
Musk also did not express concern over what Huang’s affair with an alleged Chinese spy could mean for U.S. national security.
“I don’t think he’s disloyal to the United States,” Musk said. “I think it’s like kicking Michael Jordan off your team when you’re on a winning streak, and it just doesn’t seem like a good thing for shareholders.”
Other peers in the industry, like
Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella
and
OpenAI CEO Sam Altman
, reaffirmed their faith in Nvidia to move forward without its founder and CEO of 33 years at the helm.
“It’s the Nvidia board’s decision, but I’ll just say this one thing,” Nadella said at DealBook. “Colette [Kress, interim CEO of Nvidia] worked at Microsoft for a long time. I’ve known her for a long time. She’s a tremendous person… I have a lot of confidence in the Nvidia team and their ability to continue to do the good work.”
