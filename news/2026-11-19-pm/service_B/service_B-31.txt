Netflix has unveiled a first look and super teaser trailer for
Summer Replay
, a new dating reality series following a group of men and women who put their everyday lives on hold to spend one summer building something new, together. The series starts streaming worldwide in 2027.
The series comes from the team behind
Offline Love
, which sent 10 men and women to a foreign country with no phones and nothing but a guidebook, in search of real love. Like
Offline Love
and other Netflix reality hits such as
The Boyfriend
,
Love Village
and
Badly in Love
,
Summer Replay
is similarly built on raw emotion and real moments.
In
Summer Replay
, a group of strangers who’ve “paused” their everyday lives spend the summer together in a beach house they build and operate — and will tear down again once the season ends. Along the way, they meet people from different walks of life, fall into romances, and face the inevitable goodbyes at the end of summer, which hit harder than they ever expected. Will one summer together be enough to help them figure out how to restart their lives?
Four first-look stills offer a glimpse of what’s ahead: the 11 cast members seen from behind as they face the ocean, pairs of matching beach sandals, the beach house in the evening, and T-shirts that say "The Summer We’ll Replay Forever." — each one quietly capturing the bittersweet pull of a summer you don’t want to end.
Summer Replay
captures the unpredictability of human relationships — as well as summer love, youthful romance and reluctant partings — in the way only an unscripted reality series can. This one special summer will go down as a "summer break from life" for each participant.
Summer Replay
streams worldwide in 2027, only on Netflix.
