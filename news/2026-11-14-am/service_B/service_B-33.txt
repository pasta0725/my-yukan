The Law of Beasts
is a four-episode drama directed by Kasper Bajon and starring Agnieszka Grochowska, Agnieszka Żulewska and Michał Pawlik. All episodes will be available November 18, 2026, only on Netflix.
Kasper Bajon, director and co-writer, talks about the story and its leading characters:
While researching and writing the script with Anna Kasińska, we noticed that many strong, capable women were involved in this case. The men talked at length about how to solve the problem, but the responsibility ultimately often fell to the women. Their presence inspired us to put two women at the center of the story. Anna, a government official, and Agnieszka, a psychiatrist, are fictional characters. They are not based on specific individuals, although, as is always the case in my scripts, they are a composite of traits drawn from the many people we met during our research. They represent two ways of looking at the world. Anna believes that evil can exist objectively and demands a decisive response. Agnieszka does not question people’s responsibility for their actions, but she sees the complex factors behind them. Anna takes a more unequivocal view; Agnieszka sees more nuance. Their relationship brings the conflict between the need for safety and the principles of the law down to a personal level.

What is
The Law of Beasts
about?
In 1988, Anna Bersz, a major in the Citizens’ Militia, leads the investigation into Mariusz Trynkiewicz. Her friend Agnieszka Grabska, a psychiatrist, is asked to assess the suspect’s mental capacity. Their professional duties and personal convictions lead them to decisions that end their friendship and shape the rest of their lives.
Twenty-five years later, the convicted man is due to be released. Anna is now a government official, while Agnieszka is a respected expert in psychiatry. Both are once again faced with the question of how to protect society while remaining true to the principles of the law. Returning to the case also means confronting their own choices from the past.
What kind of story can viewers expect?
The Law of Beasts
is a drama about the limits of friendship and justice. Told across two timelines, it shows how decisions made years earlier continue to affect the women’s lives.
The series is for viewers drawn to character-driven dramas about professional and personal dilemmas, as well as stories about how institutions work. It invites reflection on responsibility while leaving room for viewers to form their own opinions about the characters’ actions.
Who stars in the series?
The series stars Agnieszka Grochowska, Agnieszka Żulewska and Michał Pawlik.
The cast also includes Kamilla Baar, Adam Nawojczyk, Michalina Łabacz, Aleksander Talkowski, Jakub Sasak, Jan Jankowski, Andrzej Chyra, Artur Paczesny, Przemysław Kowalski, Małgorzata Gorol, Ewelina Starejki, Jacek Beler, Hanna Śleszyńska and Grzegorz Warchoł.
Who is behind the series?
The Law of Beasts
is directed by Kasper Bajon, who co-wrote the script with Anna Kasińska. Bartłomiej Kaczmarek is the director of photography, and Piotr Waglewski composed the music. The series is produced by Edyta Krajewska, with production by Entrego.
