Titu Mama ki life mein ek problem khatam hoti nahi ki doosri entry maar deti hai
(just when one problem in Titu Mama’s life ends, another one comes barging in)!
Netflix
has unveiled the trailer of
Dhindora
Season 2, premiering
November 20
, bringing back the madcap world of
BB Ki Vines
, where
Bhuvan Bam
, India’s first and biggest creator, plays an entire family of characters. Leading the chaos is the fan-favourite
Titu Mama
,  the ever-unlucky uncle who keeps landing in situations he never signed up for. He is joined by
Babloo ji
,
Janki
,
Sameer
and
Bancho
, all played by Bhuvan, and by a very important new addition to the family:
Sanjeeda Sheikh as Kavita Mami
, Titu Mama’s wife.
Season 2 finds Titu Mama nursing an ₹11 crore loss, with family equations hanging in the balance. His plan is to make peace with his sasural (in-laws), but it only stirs up a whole new storm. Will he find the treasure, or only a truckload of trouble? Directed by
Sneha Shetty Kohli
,
Dhindora
Season 2 is written by
Bhuvan Bam
,
Abbas Dalal
,
Hussain Dalal
, and
Chetan Dange
, and produced by
Rohit Raj
and
Bhuvan Bam
.
Bringing his world to Netflix, actor and creator
Bhuvan Bam
shares, “When I first created these characters, I wanted them to be outlandish yet familiar, and that remains at the heart of BB Ki Vines. Titu Mama has always had a talent for finding himself in situations he absolutely did not sign up for, and this time, he’s trying to make peace with his in-laws, which, as you can imagine, comes with its own set of complications! Partnering with Netflix has allowed us to take the BB Ki Vines universe to a much bigger canvas, where we could push our ideas further while staying true to the soul and humour of Dhindora. Season 2 takes the chaos up a notch, and I can’t wait for audiences to see Titu Mama back in action. After all, when Titu Mama is involved, peace is never really an option!”
On joining the
Dhindora
family as Kavita Mami,
Sanjeeda Sheikh
says
,
“What drew me to Kavita was that she can hold her own with Titu Mama. He’s always got an explanation ready, and she’s usually the one person in the room who can see right through it. I had a lot of fun playing that equation with Bhuvan. Audiences have known Titu Mama for years, and I’m excited for them to finally meet the woman who knows him best.”
Sneha Shetty Kohli
, who steered this season’s chaos from behind the camera, says,
"One of the most exciting aspects of directing this season was bringing a fresh scale, energy and vibrancy to an already much-loved world. Making my directorial debut with this project made the experience even more special. From bizarre action sequences to colourful song-and-dance moments, every day brought something exciting. Introducing new characters and exploring their dynamics with familiar favourites was equally rewarding. Above all, capturing Bhuvan’s inimitable humour while retaining the essence of this beloved universe was an absolute joy for me."
Having grown from a fan-favourite digital series into a Netflix title, the show keeps Bhuvan Bam’s signature mix of larger-than-life characters and
desi
humour. With Kavita Mami in the picture, Titu Mama had better buckle up because
sasural ki kahaani ab aur bhi interesting hone wali hai
(the in-laws’ story is about to get even more interesting)!
~ Dhindora Season 2 premieres November 20, only on Netflix ~
