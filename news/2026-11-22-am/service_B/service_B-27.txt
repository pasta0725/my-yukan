Netflix has officially announced production of a new season of the hit series
Tokyo Swindlers
, with
Go Ayano
and
Etsushi Toyokawa
reprising their lead roles, and
Hitoshi One
returning as director and screenwriter.
In 2024,
Tokyo Swindlers
became an extraordinary hit, achieving a feat never before seen since Netflix’s Weekly Top 10 began in Japan: it held the
No. 1 spot on Netflix Japan’s Weekly Top 10 (Series) for six consecutive weeks
, and remained in the Top 10 for
22 weeks
overall. It also ranked in the
Global Weekly Top 10 for Non-English Series for five consecutive weeks
, and entered the Top 10 in
15 countries
, making it one of the most successful Japanese live-action Netflix series to date.
The series also transcended the screen to become a true cultural phenomenon, with its quotable lines and unforgettable characters turning into viral memes across social media. The series was also selected for the
Top 10 of the 2024 U-Can New Words and Buzzwords Awards
, and received numerous other accolades, including:
Best Streaming Drama
at the
Filmarks Awards 2024
No. 1 Best Streaming Content
at the
Modelpress Best Entertainment Awards
Buzzworthy Person Award
at the
LINE NEWS AWARD 2024
Excellence Award
and the
AMD Chairman’s Award
at the
30th AMD Awards
The newly unveiled first-look image shows a dimly lit room reminiscent of Harrison’s lair. At the center of a desk lies a map overlooking a city, surrounded by stacks of cash. What lies ahead on that map — and what do they see beyond it? This evocative image says little, yet unmistakably heightens anticipation for the new series. More information will be revealed soon.
The new season of
Tokyo Swindlers
will exclusively stream on Netflix.
Go Ayano
, who plays
Takumi Tsujimoto
, shared:
“I am deeply grateful to be making Series 2 of
Tokyo Swindlers
. Building on the passion and fever you have nurtured, we will give everything to deliver an exquisite new season, full of swagger, that surpasses Series 1. I look forward to meeting you all as Takumi Tsujimoto. Now then, shall we begin?”
Talking about reprising his role as
Harrison Yamanaka, Etsushi Toyokawa revealed,
“I have been reunited with a terrifying man. He destroys my conscience, my sense of social decency, my love for my family. He destroys my life, my very humanity. I pray filming wraps before he consumes me. Imagine it: Harrison Yamanaka is always there in the mirror.”
Series director and screenwriter Hitoshi One
expressed his enthusiasm as well, saying,
“
Tokyo Swindlers
became such a massive hit that even I, the person who made it, was taken aback. Right after it started streaming, Netflix came to me and said, ‘Let’s do Season 2!’ I was terrified, thinking, ‘There’s no way we can top something that good…’ But as I worked on the scripts, I started to think, ‘Wait… isn’t this even better than Season 1?’ And that gradually turned into conviction. Then filming began, and when I saw Takumi and Harrison Yamanaka again for the first time in three years, that conviction became even ‘
more physical, more primitive, more sexually gratifying
…’ Please look forward to it!!”
Ko Shinjo, Author of the Original Novel
offered a message of his own, saying “Does a person who has pulled off one big con ever feel satisfied? Probably not. Human desire knows no limits. Searching for something beyond money alone, they will aim for an even bigger, even harder con. When I learned that a new season of
Tokyo Swindlers
series was being made, it struck me as oddly fitting. Once you’ve gotten a taste of it, you don’t walk away so easily. It seems that this time, too, an enormous plot of land (a target) is in play.”
