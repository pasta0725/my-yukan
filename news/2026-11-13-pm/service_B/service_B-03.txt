As one of today’s most popular global artists, the first music act in YouTube history to surpass 100 million subscribers, world renowned rapper, singer, dancer, and style icon
LISA
, who is best known as a member of BLACKPINK, has captivated millions with her incredible stage presence, distinct style, and chart-topping hits, influencing how fans consume K-Pop on YouTube.
Now, you can get a behind-the-scenes look at her journey to become a solo artist in
Always Lalisa,
a new documentary from Sony Music Vision, coming exclusively to
YouTube Premium
on November 18. LISA’s new single “Leave With Me” – a song made for the documentary – will also be released on November 18.
"YouTube has been so supportive in my music journey and my connection with fans around the world from the very beginning,” said LISA. “Bringing
Always Lalisa
to YouTube Premium gives us a global stage to share my story in a truly personal way. I can’t wait for everyone to see more behind the scenes of my journey so far.”

Behind the music and performance
Always Lalisa
offers an unscripted look at the artist beyond the stadium lights. Directed by Sue Kim, the film follows a pivotal year in LISA’s life, as she steps away from BLACKPINK for a year of independence for the first time since childhood. With the clock ticking before her return to the group, LISA confronts the fear and pressure of launching a solo career with her debut album
Alter Ego,
her solo
Coachella
debut, pursuing acting and landing her first role in The White Lotus, and her own company, LLOUD.
She gives fans an opportunity to see her in a vulnerable light, as she yearns to maintain a personal life beyond the spotlight. Whether you've followed her from the very beginning or are just discovering her work, the film offers an inspiring look at the artistry behind the star.
Celebrating LISA
In October, YouTube celebrated LISA’s
PRESS PLAY
release with an
event at Vinyl Room in Los Angeles
. Her top YouTube fans were invited to watch LISA chat about her new music with Quenlin Blackwell and premiere her "
Tarantino
" music video, while thousands of fans around the world tuned in via livestream.
Exclusive access for YouTube Premium members
YouTube has long served as a home for artists to delight and connect with their fans on a global stage.
YouTube Premium
continues to offer fans a unique ad-free experience with this exclusive story from one of the most pivotal artists in music today.
Mark your calendar for November 18 to stream the documentary and check out LISA’s exclusive song, "Leave With Me.” In the meantime, you can watch the
official trailer
on YouTube and
sign up for YouTube Premium
to prepare for the release.
The team behind the making of
Always Lalisa
At the creative helm is director Sue Kim, who also produces alongside Courtney Crockett for Salt Water Productions. Joining them are executive producers Morgan Neville and Caitrin Rogers for Tremolo Productions, Alice Kang and Joojong Joe for LLOUD CO, and Peter Edge, John Fleckenstein, and Camille Yorrick for RCA Records. Representing Sony Music Vision are Tom Mackay, Krista Wegener, and Abby Lynn Kang Davis.
Produced by Sony Music Vision in association with RCA Records and Tremolo Productions, in association with Salt Water Productions and LLOUD CO, this is a collaboration to share Lisa’s story with the world.
