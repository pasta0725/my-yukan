In 1929, the very first Academy Awards were held in the Blossom Room of the Hollywood Roosevelt Hotel. Tonight, in that exact same room, the next era of entertainment took the stage with our annual YouTube Creator Premiere.
Hosted by
House Guest
’s
Scott Evans
, the showcase offered sneak peeks, first-looks, and major programming announcements from the visionary storytellers shaping the future of the platform — including
Emma Chamberlain
,
Aryna Sabalenka
,
Nick DiGiovanni
,
Wesley Wang
, and
Grace Reiter
— proving that the most exciting, must-watch content is happening on YouTube.
The slate of announcements included:
Emma Chamberlain
| “What Are You Waiting For?”
Internet icon Emma Chamberlain doesn't like waiting in line - and wants to understand more about people who do. In this new ongoing series, what starts as an investigation of New York City's most frequented lines unspools into a thoughtful and expansive meditation on subcultures, fandoms, and what makes our modern world tick. At once whimsical, profound and provocative, and underpinned by Emma's singular POV,
What Are You Waiting For?
is a hilarious cultural commentary that not only asks what people are waiting for - but why. The series is executive produced by two-time Emmy and Golden Globe winner Joe Lewis, with Amplify Pictures (Fleabag, 100 Foot Wave) serving as the co-studio and production company.
Nick DiGiovanni
| “Chef”
Chef Nick DiGiovanni, the creator behind the world’s largest food YouTube channel, launches Chef (
@Chef
) - a new series from his production company, Knife Drop Studios. After reaching billions of viewers through his global culinary adventures (
@NickDiGiovanni
) and step-by-step recipes (
@NicksKitchen
), Nick is now turning the camera solely toward fellow professional chefs, offering an unfiltered look inside kitchens around the globe. From Michelin-starred restaurants and hidden street food gems showcased in full-length episodes, to POV recipes in short-form episodes,
Chef
puts viewers in the shoes of the people behind the scenes, offering a unique, captivating perspective for everyone from the culinary obsessed to the simply curious. The first full-length episode, “
Inside the World’s #1 Pizza Restaurant
” is available now, only on YouTube, along with the first short-form POV episode, “
Butter Chicken Shawarma
.” New short-form POV episodes will debut daily, with full-length episodes rolling out throughout 2026-27 exclusively on YouTube.
Aryna Sabalenka
| “Aryna’s Arena: Unfiltered”
She's the most dominant tennis player on the planet; and the world can't look away. Aryna Sabalenka has spent years letting us watch her win. Now cameras follow her everywhere press passes don't go. A Gucci ambassador designing her own $250K diamond necklace, a bride-to-be planning a wedding while living out of a suitcase for 300+ days a year, a daughter flying home to Belarus between slams, Aryna is building an empire in real time with the whole world watching. From UltraBoom Media, the creators of
OFF DAY
, a team led by Producers and Creatives from
Anthony Bourdain: Parts Unknown
,
Aryna's Arena: Unfiltered
is an evolution and expansion of Aryna Sabalenka's YouTube series (Aryna's Arena). It is a docu-reality series that lives inside her world, on the court, on the road, and everywhere in between, capturing the brand meetings and dress fittings, the late nights with her fiance, and the quiet hotel room moments where she finally gets to just be herself. The new docuseries will premiere on the official
Aryna Sabalenka YouTube channel
.
Wesley Wang
| “RESPONSE,” “Extract 14” and “Absolute Cinema”
Acclaimed director and filmmaker Wesley Wang debuted his latest horror short film,
RESPONSE
, in collaboration with director Jason Rodelo, where a routine welfare check at a remote desert preserve turns terrifying when park rangers discover an otherworldly presence. Wesley also teased “Extract 14,” a new six-episode, interactive murder-mystery series that puts viewers in the role of detective, with $100,000 on the line for the first person to crack the case. Additionally, Wesley discussed “Absolute Cinema,” another new series where ten aspiring filmmakers, chosen from thousands of internet strangers, are brought to New York for an immersive, seven-day filmmaking bootcamp where they’ll compete for $100,000 to bring their dream movie to life. All three projects
will premiere exclusively on YouTube
in early and spring 2027.
Lilly Singh
| “Eligible Aunties”
Eligible Aunties
is a warm, witty, and character-driven unscripted docu-follow series hosted by Lilly Singh that trades shallow reality-dating tropes for mature women with real emotional intelligence and life experience. With Singh serving as cheerleader and confidante, the show follows a diverse cast of older single women navigating modern dating apps, romance rules, and meddling family expectations — proving that while finding love doesn't get simpler with age, it certainly gets more interesting. The series, produced by Unicorn Island Productions and created and hosted by Lilly, will premiere on
Lilly's YouTube Channel
in 2027.
Grace Reiter
| “Twilight (but better)”
Comedian, writer, and
Saturday Night Live
cast member Grace Reiter is expanding her satirical universe with
Twilight (but better)
, her second unscripted parody feature. Following the breakout success of
The Hunger Games (but better)
— which has garnered over 18 million views on YouTube — Reiter brings her sharp improvisation and irreverent humor to one of the most defining pop culture franchises of a generation. The new parody film premieres February 18 on the
official Grace Reiter YouTube channel
.
Watch the teaser trailer here
.
Ina Garten
| Happy Hour with Ina Garten: “Thanksgiving Special”
Culinary legend Ina Garten gathers friends and fan favorites for a festive Thanksgiving edition of
Happy Hour
. Featuring Bobby Flay, Seth Meyers, Julia Louis-Dreyfus, Jennifer Garner, and Jeffrey, the holiday special tackles listener questions, shares cherished memories, and delivers expert entertaining tips ahead of the holiday. Produced in partnership with Vox Media Podcast Network, Happy Hour is part of the Vox Media Podcast Network. Ina Garten serves as host with Jenna Weiss Berman, Ali Jaffe Ramis and Deborah Davis as executive producers. Kate Tyler is contributing producer, with Jules Natt as producer and Mica Pollan Bahn as associate producer.
Watch the series trailer here
, and the new special premieres November 25 at 6:00 a.m. ET on the official
Happy Hour with Ina
YouTube channel.
Sam Eckholm
| Access Granted: “Flying with the Royal Air Force Red Arrows”
Access Granted
is a cinematic documentary series that takes cameras to the world’s most secretive places, putting audiences inside demanding missions that have never been seen before. In an upcoming episode, host Sam Eckholm joins the Royal Air Force Red Arrows as Britain’s legendary aerial display team crosses the Atlantic to celebrate America’s 250th birthday, going behind the scenes with the pilots, the engineers, and the crew. Sam Eckholm is the creator and host of Access Granted with JoJo Phillips serving as executive producer and Swayne Martin as producer. The new episode premieres November 27 on the
official Sam Eckholm YouTube Channel
.
Morgan Jay
| “Music Therapy with Morgan Jay”
Music Therapy is an original YouTube series developed and produced by viral comedian and musician Morgan Jay and Ty Stafford of Austin-based creative agency Hello Party, financed by Live Nation Studios. In each episode, Jay plays musical therapist to a celebrity guest, blending unscripted conversation and improv with live songwriting before the pair close with an original song they create together. The result lands somewhere between a talk show, a comedy special and a live concert. Brooke Schley serves as showrunner and co-writes the series with Jay. Executive producers are Graham Sicalowski, Robin Rapino, Stella Bulochnikov, Brooke Schley and Matt Sadeghian. The new series premieres November 18 on the
official Morgan Jay YouTube channel
, and you can
watch the series trailer here
.
Track Star*
| “Track Star Tonight”
Hosted by Jack Coyne, Track Star* is a music-driven digital show that brings artists and fans together. Expanding on its popular on-the-street game and Track Star Podcast,
Track Star Tonight
is a new, intimate live performance series featuring emerging and established artists in front of a small audience, blending live music, storytelling, and artist discovery. Following performances from artists including Remi Wolf, Kings of Leon, The Womack Sisters, Medium Build, and Florence Road, the series will continue to release new performances and collaborations through the end of 2026 and into 2027. Track Star* is produced by Public Opinion Corporation and created by Jack Coyne, Kieran Coyne and Henry Kornaros. New episodes are rolling out on the
official Track Star YouTube channel
.
Jon Youshaei
| “Created with Jon Youshaei”
Created with Jon Youshaei
is the award-winning docuseries where Jon goes behind the scenes with today’s biggest stars during their biggest moments. All access. No filters. In past episodes, These upcoming episodes feature Jon as he spends another month on-set with MrBeast for Beast Games season 3 (including filming in the Colosseum in Rome), goes behind-the-scenes with Mark Rober on his new streaming show, and hits the road with Dude Perfect for their multi-million dollar arena tour. Created is directed and hosted by Jon Youshaei with Amanda Marcovitch serving as producer. Episodes premiere this winter on the
official Jon Youshaei YouTube channel
.
Scott Evans
| "House Guest Vacay"
Scott Evans is packing his bags and taking
House Guest
on the road with
House Guest Vacay.
In this new series, he’s bringing the laughs, candid conversations and unexpected moments to celebrity getaways across the country and around the world. His first stop in Lagos, Nigeria premieres January 8 exclusively on YouTube.
House Guest Vacay
is produced by Summer Break Studios and created and hosted by Scott Evans.
Phil Rosenthal
| “Somebody Feed Phil”
Opa! Season 9 of Somebody Feed Phil kicks off in a destination long at the top of Phil’s wish list: Greece. On the island of Sifnos, globetrotting foodie Phil Rosenthal finally gets his first taste of the country, with sun-soaked tavernas, fresh-caught seafood and a warm welcome from new friends. The series won the 2022 Critics Choice Real TV Award for Best Travel/Adventure Show and earned a James Beard Award nomination. Rosenthal is the creator, executive producer and host of
Somebody Feed Phil
, a two-time New York Times bestselling author, and the creator of the Emmy-winning CBS comedy
Everybody Loves Raymond
.
Somebody Feed Phil
is produced by Lucky Bastards in partnership with Banijay Americas and Banijay Rights. Rich Rosenthal serves as executive producer and showrunner. Season 9 premieres spring 2027 on the
official Phil Rosenthal YouTube channel
.
