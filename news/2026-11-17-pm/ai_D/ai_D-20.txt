Nvidia’s board of directors “isn’t considering” having Jensen Huang return as CEO,
according to a report from CNBC
. A source tells the outlet the board “is focused on an investigation into Huang, and is not in discussions to bring him back.”
Since
Huang’s resignation last week
, tech executives, along with current and former Nvidia employees, have called for his reinstatement. An online petition to bring Huang back as CEO has
amassed more than 180,000 signatures
, including from major figures like Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney, SpaceX CEO Elon Musk, and Replit CEO Amjad Masad.

returning as CEO

.
