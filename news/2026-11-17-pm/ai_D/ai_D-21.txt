Audible is introducing an AI-assisted synopsis feature that’s designed to help users determine whether they’ll enjoy an audiobook before they jump in and start listening to it. The new audio discovery feature combines audiobook samples, AI overviews, and author commentary to give users more information before choosing their next story, in addition to recommended audiobook collections that are personalized to individual listening tastes.
Audio discovery provides a soundbite synopsis, with the aim of preventing Audible users from having to research titles on other platforms. The feature plays users a snippet of the audiobook and a brief AI-generated overview including its themes and listener reactions, drawing from reviews and other publicly available data. It can also include commentary from an author or Audible editor, which is pulled directly from past Audible interviews.
“Picking your next listen should feel like getting a tip from a friend. With audio discovery, you’ll get a quick audio take on why the title might be right for you, from our editors, fellow listeners, or even the author,” Audible chief product and analytics officer Andy Tsao said in the announcement. “Some of the best listening experiences are those rare finds or hidden gems, and we want to make those great discoveries happen more often and more easily.”
Audio discovery is rolling out on select English-language titles in the US, with plans to eventually expand to other regions.
Audible is also using AI to personalize audiobook collections around users’ listening tastes, in an effort to make titles that don’t appear on best-selling lists easier to discover. Like audio discovery, these personalized collections are rolling out to customers in the US, and also include commentary from Audible’s editors.
Audible users in the US will also start seeing AI-curated recommendation categories based on what they usually listen to.
