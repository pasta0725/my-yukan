Ten creators will train with Netflix’s Photo and AV Studio in Sydney this December, then get 12 months of ongoing support.
Netflix is looking for 10 independent Australian photographers, videographers and social creators for its Out of Frame Masterclass in Sydney on 9–10 December 2026. Applications are open now and close at 11.59pm on Monday 23 November.
Run in partnership with
Bursty
and
Media Mentors Australia
, the masterclass is led by Netflix’s Photo and AV Studio, the team behind Netflix’s key art and behind-the-scenes content. It shows creators how their skills translate to film and TV. No previous screen credits are required.
“If you know how to capture raw emotion in a split second at a high-stakes event, or craft a scroll-stopping reel right from your phone, you already possess the instincts that electrify a film set. Out of Frame is designed to supercharge those instincts and show you exactly where they can elevate your business,” said Abby Morgan, head of PAVS Netflix ANZ.
Participants will pitch and produce promotional content from a mock Netflix brief, working with actors and getting feedback from industry professionals and Netflix teams. They’ll also get guidance on finding freelance work, tour an active Netflix set (subject to production schedule) and attend an industry networking event on the night of 10 December.
After the masterclass, participants join a year-long alumni group with ongoing support to turn what they’ve learned into work.
“This is an incredible opportunity to build on your career and we’re thrilled to be supporting the successful applicants over the next 12 months. The combination of training, community and support is unprecedented and we cannot wait to see the creative talent that exists across Australia,” said Denise Eriksen from Media Mentors who will be working with the cohort for an extended period to help them turn their learning into business opportunities and success.
Who can apply
Applicants need at least two years of professional creative experience, full availability for both days and the evening event, and their own professional gear. Two of the 10 places are reserved for First Nations creatives and two for disabled, Deaf and/or neurodivergent creators. Flights and Sydney accommodation will be covered for selected interstate participants.
How to apply
Submit an expression of interest at
Out of Frame
by 11.59pm on Monday 23 November. Applicants will be screened for experience and invited to submit creative examples, and a shortlist will be interviewed.
