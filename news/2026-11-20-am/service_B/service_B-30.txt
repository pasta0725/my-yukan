Netflix’s
Physical: Asia
, the high-stakes competition where Asian nations clash for honor and pride, has been officially confirmed for a second season, coming in 2027.

Premiering in October 2025 as the franchise's first international team competition,
Physical: Asia
took the world by storm. Athletes pushed past human limits to complete monumental quests inspired by Asian cultures, and delivered non-stop adrenaline, moving sportsmanship, and unbreakable teamwork. Captivating global audiences, the
Physical
franchise cemented its Unscripted reign by charting in the Global Top 10 for three consecutive seasons.
The ‘
Physical
universe’ continues to expand worldwide. Local adaptations
Physical: 100: Mexico
and
Physical: 100: Italy
each spent two consecutive weeks in the Global Top 10 after release. With U.S., French, and Swedish versions also in the works, and
Physical: 100: Mexico
officially
renewed
for Season 2, the franchise continues to prove its unmatched global reach.
Producer Jang Ho-gi shared his gratitude to global fans awaiting the new season: “I want to sincerely thank our viewers for embracing the fierce passion and pride of athletes competing for their countries, as well as the incredible drama they created as teams pushing past individual limits.”
He added, “For Season 2, we have designed an entirely different world from previous installments and developed quests capable of becoming new signatures for the
Physical
series. Beyond simply expanding the scale, we aim to showcase fresh strategies, matchups, and unpredictable outcomes unique to a nation-versus-nation format. Please look forward to even more overwhelming battles that surpass Season 1, and the authentic stories of the formidable new competitors joining the arena.”
Physical: Asia Season 2 - Unbroken
is coming to Netflix in 2027.
