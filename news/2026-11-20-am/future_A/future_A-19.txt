In an effort to reach an elusive continuity of the self, some far-thinking technologists are working on ways to keep your brain going—even beyond death. Science editor Mary Beth Griggs and senior editor for biomedicine Antonio Regalado explore possible paths to keep your mind going, including gradual brain replacement and uploading our memories to the cloud.
