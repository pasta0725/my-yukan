TITLE: Google to keep Gemini chats for 2 months, up from 72 hours, even with history off
SITE: TechCrunch AI
PUBLISHED: 2026-11-18 03:48 (page: 2026-11-17T18:48:23+00:00)
OG_IMAGE: https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/google-gemini-app.jpg?resize=1200,800
IMAGES:

TEXT:
Google is extending how long it keeps temporary Gemini chats and conversations made with chat history turned off to around two months, saying the change aims to improve safety and security as AI systems become more capable.
The
change
, which will roll out globally in the Gemini app over the coming weeks, marks a significant increase from the previous 72-hour retention period mentioned in Google’s Gemini Apps Privacy Hub, according to an
archived version
from November 12.
OpenAI, by comparison, says it may
keep temporary chats in ChatGPT
for up to 30 days, whereas Anthropic
retains incognito chats
in Claude for 30 days by default. However, companies may keep some data longer for legal or safety reasons, or to investigate policy violations.
Google did not answer questions about what prompted the change and whether it would affect how conversations are accessed or reviewed, instead pointing to a
blog post
that says chats are kept longer to respond to users, process feedback, and protect its services and users. Its updated Gemini Apps Privacy Hub also cites potential system failures as a reason for retaining chats and says temporary conversations are not used to train AI models.
The longer retention period comes as Google introduces separate settings for Gemini’s chat history and AI training. Until now, both were tied to a single “Keep Activity” setting. Users will now be able to keep their conversations in their chat history without using them to train Google’s generative AI models.
Google says both new settings will be turned off by default for users who previously disabled Keep Activity. For those who had Keep Activity turned on, their chat history will remain enabled. They can, though, decide separately whether Google can use their conversations to train AI models.
