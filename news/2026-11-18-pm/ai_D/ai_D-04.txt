TITLE: Nvidia says its open source AI strategy remains intact after Jensen Huang’s exit
SITE: TechCrunch AI
PUBLISHED: 2026-11-18 08:04 (page: 2026-11-17T23:04:35+00:00)
OG_IMAGE: https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/GettyImages-2290097570.jpg?w=1024

TEXT:
Nvidia interim CEO Colette Kress reaffirmed the company’s commitment to open source AI during Tuesday’s earnings call with analysts — the first since CEO Jensen Huang’s resignation.
“At Nvidia, we are committed to fostering a vibrant open model ecosystem, so every country, company, and industry can build its own intelligence,” Kress said in her opening remarks. She also noted that open models were accelerating AI adoption, citing a 35-fold year-over-year surge in weekly token volume on OpenRouter, including a 3.5-fold increase over the past three months.
Kress also spoke about Nvidia’s plans for Hugging Face, a platform for open source AI models that the company agreed to
acquire for $12.9 billion in September,
in what would be the second-largest acquisition in Nvidia’s history.
The platform, which serves more than 18 million developers and 200,000 companies, is still on track to join Nvidia, despite Huang’s ouster. Huang had
heavily advocated
for open source AI technology. His departure
had raised questions
about the company’s future approach to open source AI models, with Hugging Face’s CEO
calling Huang’s departure
“a sad day,” and promising to “keep fighting for open-source AI, harder than ever.”
So far, Nvidia’s plans for Hugging Face appear unchanged. The acquisition was not directly addressed during the Q&A portion of the earnings call.
“Together, we will scale its platform and make intelligence more open, more capable, and more accessible,” said Kress of Nvidia’s plans for Hugging Face.
Last week, Nvidia founder Huang
resigned
from his position as CEO following news about his extramarital affairs, including one with Alice Huang, an executive at the Singapore-based company Megaspeed who is suspected of being a Chinese spy. He’s also accused of having
extramarital
affairs
with two direct reports and is now
set to testify
before Congress by Thanksgiving. Meanwhile, the
SEC is investigating Nvidia
over whether the company’s disclosures misled investors,
The WSJ reported
.
Within Nvidia, Huang pushed for the development of the
Nemotron family
of open-weight AI models built for long-running agentic AI systems and reasoning workloads. On today’s call, Kress noted that Nemotron is now the “most widely used U.S. open model on OpenRouter,” and it’s “where developers start to build their own intelligence.”
“That foundation is empowering the ecosystem,” she said.
Correction: Due to a typo, an earlier version of this article incorrectly stated the number of developers served by Hugging Face; it is 18 million, not 118 million.
