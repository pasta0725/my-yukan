Exclusive:

It’s confirmed that the Galaxy S27 Ultra will ditch the laser autofocus module, marking the first time Samsung has removed it since its introduction on the Galaxy S21 Ultra in 2021.

This also explains why the latest S27 Ultra design shows only the main camera, ultrawide camera, telephoto camera, and LED flash. The laser autofocus module has been axed as well.

In other words, compared to the Galaxy S26 Ultra, the S27 Ultra is losing both the 3x telephoto camera and the laser autofocus module.

レーザーオートフォーカスは、被写体に赤外線を照射することで、カメラのピント合わせをより速く、正確にする機能。特に暗い場所での撮影に役立ちますが、これがなくなればオートフォーカスの性能が低下する可能性があります。

こうした変更点を踏まえると、Galaxy S27 Ultraのカメラ性能は、現行のGalaxy S26 Ultraはもちろん、Galaxy S21 Ultra以降のSamsungのフラッグシップモデルと比べても低下しているように思えます。
価格を据え置きながら内容を変更する「シュリンクフレーション（ステルス値上げ）」は、現実のものとなっており、新製品が発売されるたびにその影響がますます目立つようになっています。

見た目はiPhoneぽいけど
Ice Universeは今月初めに、S27 Ultraの背面カメラデザインについて、iPhone 17 ProやiPhone 18 Proに似た横長に張り出したカメラデザインになると投稿していました。
Appleのカメラ部分にはチップセットが収められており、デバイスの他の部分に熱が伝わるのを抑える役割もあります。一方、サムスンの場合は単なる見た目重視で、張り出した部分は空っぽだと主張しています。
One of the biggest reasons people are unhappy with the Galaxy S27 Ultra's design is that Samsung seems to be abandoning a design philosophy it once stood for: “Entities should not be multiplied beyond necessity.”

Samsung's flagship designs used to be remarkably restrained. Every camera had its own place, and every design element had a clear reason to exist. Clean, minimal, and instantly recognizable. That was what made Samsung's design language so distinctive.

Now look at this camera island. The entire right side is almost completely empty. No cameras, no sensors, no obvious functional purpose. So why does it need to exist?

If the goal is simply to make the camera island look more like an iPhone, Samsung is sacrificing years of design identity just to follow its biggest competitor.

Good industrial design should be able to justify the existence of every element.

When a design needs a large area of functionally meaningless empty space just to maintain its appearance, I start to wonder: Is Samsung designing a smartphone, or designing a smartphone that looks like an iPhone?

Samsung used to have its own answers. Now, its design decisions are becoming increasingly difficult to understand.
