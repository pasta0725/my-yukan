After the leather-jacketed CEO, president, and co-founder of Nvidia, Jensen Huang,
acknowledged an affair
with Megaspeed exec Alice Huang, his company initially announced an investigation. However, after
The New York Times
reported there was evidence of another affair, this time with an Nvidia executive who reported directly to him, a board meeting
that night
ended with
Huang’s resignation
, and Nvidia CFO Colette Kress was installed as the new interim CEO.
And then… Huang was back.
In recent years, Nvidia has become a key part of the Trump administration’s AI push, a central figure in negotiations over potential Chinese access to cutting-edge AI hardware, and a company that has used its now $4 trillion-plus market cap to finance other AI companies. Saying it received input “from senior levels of the United States government,” Nvidia’s board announced his return as CEO on November 23rd, less than two weeks after his resignation.

The Jensen Huang era of Nvidia nearly came to an end this month. Huang, who three decades ago cofounded what’s come to be considered one of the most important companies in AI, resigned from his role on November 12th over mounting scrutiny around his alleged affairs. Huang admitted he had been “unfaithful” to his wife and

had enlisted
