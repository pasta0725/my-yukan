The elves don’t have to be the only ones busy in the workshop this holiday season. With the lights twinkling and the gift wrap out, it’s the perfect time to give someone — or yourself — a spark to take a hobby to the next level.
For some, that means diving into a new game. For others, it’s video editing or even building a robot.
NVIDIA technologies offer something for everyone on Santa’s list. Read on to unwrap the details.
Make Time for One More Game
With the holiday break here, everyone can be online to score bragging rights or explore a world that’s only been seen in video game trailers.
For some players, an upgrade means a desktop that brings out the detail in every scene. For others, it’s a laptop they can take along when visiting family, or a way to play in the cloud on a device they already own.
The right setup depends on where and what to play:
GeForce RTX 50 Series
laptops and desktops power the action. Built on the NVIDIA Blackwell architecture, they feature fifth-generation Tensor Cores and fourth-generation RT Cores for AI-enhanced graphics, detailed ray-traced worlds and high frames-per-second gaming.
NVIDIA RTX Spark PCs
combine RTX graphics with NVIDIA’s full-stack AI platform on a single superchip, bringing decades of NVIDIA innovation to slim, premium laptops and compact desktops on the familiar Windows platform. One PC can handle every workflow, from accelerated creation and immersive gaming to powerful local AI  — with a variety of designs and all-day battery life.
See games in a new light with NVIDIA DLSS 5 technology, which boosts performance and enhances image quality. The newest feature, DLSS 3D-Guided Neural Rendering, infuses scenes with lifelike lighting and materials in supported games.

G-SYNC displays

Make Something Worth Sharing
For creators with a project that’s been on the backburner, the right gift this season can be the push to finally make it happen. Maybe it’s holiday clips becoming a short film, a snowy scene coming together in 3D or a livestream that brings friends across time zones to the same virtual table. Creators can use NVIDIA systems — such as GeForce RTX 50 Series and RTX Spark devices — to keep up with the workflows, along with
NVIDIA Studio tools
and technologies for AI acceleration.
Studio Drivers support video editing, 3D design and livestreaming. In supported applications, faster processing gives more room to experiment and refine.
Build a Robot That Thinks
Every robot starts somewhere. This season, it could be under the tree.
Building a robot is an experiment in teaching a device to see or sense its surroundings. A first project might be as simple as using a camera to identify a candy cane or a gingerbread man, then moving on to an app that probes what the camera sees.
Testing brings new questions. Can the robot recognize the candy cane under twinkling lights? Can it tell a frosted gingerbread man from an unfrosted one? Each experiment leaves more to improve and explore.
The NVIDIA Jetson edge AI platform provides the hardware and software to build projects like these. It combines compact computers with the
NVIDIA JetPack
software development kit to help makers build robots, autonomous machines and intelligent devices.
A
Jetson Developer Kit
is a gift to explore computer vision, generative AI and multimodal robot control, with the option to add cameras and sensors suited to various projects. AI runs directly on the device, processing information from its surroundings. When ready to take a prototype further, production systems powered by Jetson modules provide a path to deployment.
