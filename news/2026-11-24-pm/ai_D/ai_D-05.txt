Nvidia employees greet Jensen Huang like a hero as he returns to company’s HQ
Jensen Huang returned to Nvidia’s offices on Monday to an applauding crowd of hundreds of employees, according to several videos circulating online.
The embattled Nvidia founder had stepped down earlier this month amid
a series of scandals
— including an affair with an alleged Chinese spy, which has triggered multiple Congressional hearings over national security, and two affairs with Nvidia employees — but was reinstated as CEO after pushback from the tech community and an
intervention
by President Donald Trump.
After basking in chants of his name, Huang briefly addressed the crowd.
“Thank you all for the second chance,” he said. “I’m going to be a better CEO the second time around.”
Over 200,000 people, including around 10,000 current Nvidia employees, had
signed an open letter
advocating for Huang’s return. Other tech leaders also
pushed
for Huang’s reinstatement, including Elon Musk, who said last week at the DealBook Summit that letting Huang go is like “kicking Michael Jordan off your team when you’re on a winning streak.”
Amid this increased scrutiny, however, other reports have depicted a different side to Nvidia’s top exec — one where Jensen is not seen as a beloved leader, but as a brutal bully. Based on decades of court documents and over a dozen interviews with current and former Nvidia employees, The New York Times
reported
that Huang “used humiliation as a tool” and verbally belittled staff at times.
