'Anaesthesia' Will Be Back With a Second Ceason. Netflix Renews Hit Medical Drama Starring Leszek Lichota
Entertainment
17 November 2026
Global

Link copied to clipboard
Following the success of Netflix's first Polish medical drama, it’s official:
Anaesthesia
is returning for a second season. Just 24 hours after its premiere, the series claimed the number one spot on the platform's Top 10 list in Poland and has held onto it ever since. The show's triumph almost immediately took on a global dimension.
Anaesthesia
is successfully climbing the charts in other countries around the world (including Hungary, Slovakia, the Czech Republic, Argentina, France, and Belgium) and currently ranks 5th on Netflix’s global Top 10 list of Non-English TV.
In the new episodes, Leszek Lichota will return as Dawid Łazarz. Will the consequences of his past decisions give his battle against rigid procedures a completely new context, forcing him to find his place in the medical world all over again?
About the series
Anaesthesia:
Anesthesia is the first Polish medical drama on Netflix, immersing the viewer in a tense world of hospital turmoil. Dr. Łazarz (played by Leszek Lichota) is a brilliant neurosurgeon whose career at a major hospital comes to an abrupt end following a bold, yet unsuccessful surgery. Forced to leave the large medical center, he takes a job at a provincial hospital. Getting to know the tight-knit local community and its unfamiliar rules, Łazarz discovers that his willingness to break the rules to save patients sparks both admiration and controversy. His confidence and lack of humility make him an exceptional doctor, but they also put him in conflict with the system and ethics, highlighting the weight of responsibility he bears for the lives of others. To ensure that
Znieczulenie
accurately reflects the procedures and realities of modern medical practice on screen, medical consultants oversaw the production. Creative input was also provided by Neal Baer — a physician and producer known for his work on the cult classic series
ER
— who shared his extensive storytelling and dramaturgical experience with both the creators and the cast.
