Netflix has today revealed the release date and first look from
The Palace of Secrets
. The series is based on the adaptation of the novel of the same name by
Estefanía Ruiz
, published by
Penguin Random House
, and will premiere globally on
Netflix on 22 January 2027.
Starring
Carla Campra
(
Holy Family
,
Dangerous Moms
) and
Carlos Cuevas
(
Smiley, The 47
), who will play Julia and Gonzalo respectively. Rounding out the cast are
Alberto Olmo
(
Bad Influence
),
Luís Bermejo
(
Magical Girl
),
María Morales
(
La bola negra
),
Roberto Álamo
(
May God Save Us
),
Bea Segura
(
Terra Alta
),
Helena Ezquerro
(
La Moderna
),
Nora Hernández
(
The Dinner
) and
Nico Romero
(
Cable Girls
),
Cristina Plazas
(
Locked Up
),
Karina Soro
(
UPA Next
),
Guillermo Toledo
(
The Last Night at Tremore Beach
),
Irene Montalà
(
City of Shadows
) and
Catalina del Rosario
(
Privilèges
), amongst others.
In the series, Julia Ponce (
Carla Campra
), daughter of the court painter, arrives at the royal palace and is captivated by its dazzling atmosphere. There she meets Gonzalo (
Carlos Cuevas
), the crown prince, who has returned to Spain, compelled by the king, to get married. Julia and Gonzalo’s impossible and forbidden relationship sparks intrigue, jealousy and passion in a court rife with ambition, deceit and revenge. Parties, clandestine trysts, a secret society of women plotting for power… And, in the midst of it all, Julia and Gonzalo struggle to live out their love.
Created by
Carlos de Pando
and
Sara Antuña
,
The Palace of Secrets
which
Zeta Studios
(
Olympo
,
Élite
) is producing for Netflix, is directed by
Paco Caballero
,
Jota Linares
and
Elena Trapé
.
Antonio Asensio
is the producer, and
Xavi Toll
and
Margarita Huguet
serve as executive producers of the series alongside Carlos and Sara.
About Zeta Studios
ZETA STUDIOS was founded with the aim of designing, creating and producing fiction content for the leading platforms and content distribution and exhibition markets. Among its projects, notable productions include the series
Olympo

When No One Sees Us
and
The Short Life

Mataleón
