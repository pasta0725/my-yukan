Gemini on Mac can now use your computer
With the new computer use feature, Google says Gemini can better help with things like filling in forms and creative workflows.

Google’s Gemini app for macOS now has computer use, meaning it can help you “get multi-step tasks done across your local files, desktop apps, and the web,”
the company says
. Google suggests the feature might be useful for things like speeding up workflows, filling in forms, making presentations, and listing items for sale.
Users will be able to decide which of your apps Gemini can access, and be able to follow along as Gemini works because it has its own background window that shows a live preview in your chat, Google says. The feature is rolling out now in English for users in the US.
Gemini is introducing the new feature ahead of Apple launching new restrictions for apps wanting “full disk access” on Mac due to potential risks from AI agents with computer use. A preview of the settings rolled out with
this week’s macOS 27.3 beta
.
