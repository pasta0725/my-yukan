Last night at the iconic Dolby Theater in downtown Los Angeles, the biggest brands, creators and leaders across the industry gathered to celebrate the inaugural
Clio Creator
awards. As the presenting sponsor of the awards and home to the creators shaping culture today, YouTube is proud to champion the craft and expertise that goes into creator partnerships and creator-led work.
YouTube creators were at the center of the celebration, taking home seven Grand Clio awards. With more than 3 million creators in the YouTube Partner Program and brand deals growing by double digits year over year, it’s clear brands and creators are continuing to find business success on YouTube.
Grand Clio winners for brand and creator collaborations included:
iShowSpeed
x
Expedia
- Grand Winner, Integrated Campaign:
Fans around the world are captivated by iShowSpeed’s unscripted travel streams, so Expedia teamed up with the creator for three epic 12-hour live streams, including a single-day sprint across five Caribbean countries. Expedia integrated directly into the streams to show how easy it was to book travel and the videos drove
9.6 million views in just the first 24 hours
alone.
Michelle Khare,
Challenge Accepted: I Tried Tom Cruise's Deadliest Stunt
- Grand Winner, Creator & Brand Collaborations:
In partnership with Adobe and Red Bull, Michelle Khare attempted to become the second person after Tom Cruise to perform the famous plane hang from
Mission: Impossible - Rogue Nation.
MrBeast
,
I Granted 100 Kids Their Biggest Wish
- Grand Winner, Public Relations:
In partnership with Disney and to raise funds for
Stand Up to Cancer
, the most-subscribed YouTube creator granted the wishes of 100 kids who had been impacted by childhood cancer, giving them the awesome days they all deserved.
Animated short of bruised characters like pinatas in a support group
Spaceskits
x
Morgan & Morgan
,
The Injury Club
- Grand Winner, Written Content:
Legal disclaimers don’t usually grab attention from viewers. Morgan & Morgan decided to change that by giving Cliff Benfield, the animator behind Spaceskits, the creative freedom to take inject humor into disclaimers and gather bruised everyday characters—from piñatas and punching bags to scarecrows and crash-test dummies—into a witty YouTube Short.
Jordan The Stallion
x
Blenders Eyewear
,
Jordan is the CCO
- Grand Winner, Creative Execution:
Blenders Eyewear took creator partnership a step further by naming Jordan The Stallion Chief Content Officer and embedding him directly into the business. Working out of Blenders HQ, shopping in stores, collaborating on product design, and training the in-house marketing team on how he crafts content, Jordan helped shape the brand’s creative vision from the inside out.
Grand Clio winners for creators included:
Kylie Kelce,
Not Gonna Lie
- Grand Winner, Podcasting:
Despite her best efforts to remain on the sidelines, being part of football’s favorite family means the world has found out about Kylie Kelce. Each week on
Not Gonna Lie,
Kylie shares her personal story beyond being just a “football wife,” setting the record straight on gossip and speaking her truths on topics like modern parenting, social media trends, women in sports and more.
fern
,
The Taliban Highway: From the Last Nomads to the New Silk Road
- Grand Winner, Video:
This groundbreaking documentary reported first-hand impressions from one of the most remote regions in the world, providing a human perspective to life and political conflict in Afghanistan.
A huge congratulations to every creator, brand, and agency honored last night. Spanning across Shorts, live streams, podcast and cinematic content for the biggest screens, these winners show that creators on YouTube are taking their content and brand partnerships to new heights.
