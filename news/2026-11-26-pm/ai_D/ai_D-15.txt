Nothing
is showing off a much more detailed look
at its upcoming AI earbuds ahead of their December 8th release. The Ear Note buds can record your calls, while their charging case can record, transcribe, and summarize your conversations.
Like the Ear 3
, the Ear Note’s microphone-equipped case comes with a “Talk” button that you can use to record your own voice. There’s also a new “Record” button that can record and transcribe your conversations.
In a post on X
, Nothing CEO Carl Pei says the button will allow you to record calls through the earbuds, and meetings through the case as a “first step toward a hardware context layer for AI.”
As previously reported by
Bloomberg
,
the buds will connect to an app
where you can manage recordings, transcriptions, and AI summaries. They’ll also “support interactions with various AI agents, including a more basic voice-control system developed by Nothing, letting users engage in back-and-forth conversations,” according to
Bloomberg
.
The Ear Note buds themselves look similar to the Ear 3 and Ear 3A, but come with Nothing’s “best in-ear audio to date.” The new device, which Nothing
teased
earlier this month
, will join some of the other AI-powered note-taking devices we’ve seen emerge recently,
such as the Amazon Bee
and the
Plaud Note Pro
. Apple also brought AI capabilities to its Watch Series 12, allowing Siri to
listen to your conversations
and generate recaps.
