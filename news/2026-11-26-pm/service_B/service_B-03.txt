The holidays just got a lot more explosive. Netflix announced that the action film sequel
Mission: Cross 2
premieres December 25, with its official teaser poster and trailer out now. Following the success of
Mission: Cross
, which won over viewers worldwide with its high-octane blend of action and comedy,
Mission: Cross 2
is an exhilarating action romp following husband-and-wife duo Kang-mu and Mi-seon. When a shadowy syndicate makes off with a national treasure, the couple launches into the mission of a lifetime, racing to keep it from being smuggled abroad.
The first-look teaser poster brings married action duo Kang-mu (Hwang Jung-min) and Mi-seon (Yum Jung-ah) back together, their chemistry as crackling as ever. In a playful touch, a bronze Pensive Bodhisattva statue flashes a finger heart between them — a wink at the electric dynamic driving their next mission to recover a national treasure.
The accompanying teaser trailer opens with a bold introduction to the two leads: Kang-mu, a skilled househusband and ex-special ops agent who rappels down a wire in his apron, and Mi-seon, an ace detective from the Violent Crimes Unit who takes down an opponent in a single move. When a band of unidentified assailants steals a national treasure in a raid, Kang-mu and Mi-seon receive a secret mission from the President (Cha In-pyo) to recover it. Set amid a vast collection of artifacts, the fight sequences feature an unconventional arsenal of shields and bows. It’s a glimpse of the rich visual spectacle and an even bigger action-adventure unfolding around Korea’s most prized treasures. Tension spikes as Cheon In-hak (Yoon Kyung-ho), head of the shadowy organization, delivers a chilling warning that the treasures will be smuggled overseas before the day is over. How will the couple navigate the situation on the brink of disaster?
The film brings cinematic flair to a cultural treasure heist, pairing an inventive plot with pulse-pounding action from Hwang Jung-min and Yum Jung-ah. Rounded out by a standout ensemble cast of Jung Man-sik, Yoon Kyung-ho, Im Seong-jae, Cha In-pyo, and Kim Kuk-hee — each bringing a distinct personality and seasoned acting chops —
Mission: Cross 2
is poised to captivate viewers worldwide.
Mission: Cross 2
premieres on December 25, only on Netflix.
