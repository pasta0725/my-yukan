Amazon has held talks about letting Meta’s Muse AI agent shop on its online marketplace,
according to a report from
Fortune
. The discussions, which could end
Amazon’s block on Muse
, reportedly center on the kinds of products the AI agent recommends and the potential impact on the ecommerce giant’s advertising revenue.
As reported by
Fortune
, Amazon is concerned that it could lose advertising revenue by allowing Muse on its site, since it depends on actual humans seeing ads across its online marketplace. It’s also reportedly worried about the quality of the product search results that Muse will present to users, as well as whether the AI agent will affect Amazon’s relationship with customers. Neither Amazon nor Meta immediately responded to
The Verge
’s request for comment.
