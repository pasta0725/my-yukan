New Horizons Association and Netflix continue to support women creators in Poland. Applications open for the 3rd edition of "Women Screenwriters on the Horizon"
Social Impact
26 November 2026
Global
Poland
Link copied to clipboard
Applications are now open for the 3rd edition of "Women Screenwriters on the Horizon" mentoring program by the New Horizons Association and Netflix. Until January 4th, aspiring screenwriters who want to gain the knowledge and experience needed to make their feature film debut can apply through the association's website. Over the course of its first two editions, the initiative received an overwhelming response - close to 500 applications were submitted in total, showing the scale of interest in the programme. Nearly forty women creators have participated in the first two editions of the initiative, and past experts and mentors have included Agnieszka Holland, Harlan Coben, Weronika Tofilska, Klara Kochańska-Bajon, Carla Simon and Agnieszka Smoczyńska among others.
The "Women Screenwriters on the Horizon" program was initiated in 2024 as a way to level the playing field in the creative industry and support women creators working on their first fiction feature film idea. Under the guidance of mentors and experts, participants not only develop their full-length debut film ideas, but also learn the ins and outs of the industry.
We are thrilled to announce the call for applications for the third edition of the program. For two years, we have been observing how much this program is needed and how quickly it brings spectacular results, both in terms of developing film ideas and the professional development of participants. We are also delighted by the successes of creators from previous editions that we follow. Sometimes this is finding a new job, securing a producer, initial project financing, and sometimes new, unconventional industry collaborations and new professional activities. This program certainly strengthens belief in one's own ideas and decisions, as participants of previous editions emphasize
 – says Weronika Czołnowska, program director and head of industry events at the TAURON New Horizons IFF.
Get to know the film industry from the inside out
Applications for the program are open – you do not need to be a film school graduate to apply. An idea for an original feature film and writing experience are enough. Selected participants take part in group sessions and individual consultations for half a year, during which they can, among other things, develop their ideas for a film, create an engaging synopsis, and write a "logline", which are essential components of a film project. As part of the workshops, women creators also learn how to pitch their ideas and how to handle feedback on their ideas and content. The program concludes during the 27th TAURON New Horizons International Film Festival in Wrocław, where participants will have the opportunity to deepen their connections with representatives of the film industry.
The two previous editions of the program are undeniable proof that Polish female creators have exceptionally interesting things to say. The diversity of topics, film genres, and conventions is captivating and provides even greater motivation to support female artists in making their voices heard. There is a place for them in the Polish film industry
 – notes Daria Drzewiecka, responsible for local original productions at Netflix. – 
We want the participants of "Women Screenwriters on the Horizon" to feel that they know how and to whom to present their projects, as well as what factors decisions may depend on. Thanks to this, our film community will be enriched by conscious artists whose projects will have a better chance of reaching audiences
.
Participation in the program is free of charge. Details on how to apply can be found on the TAURON New Horizons IFF website at: 
www.nowehoryzonty.pl/scenarzystki
.
