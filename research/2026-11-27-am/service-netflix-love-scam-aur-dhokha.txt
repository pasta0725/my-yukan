Netflix announces Love, Scam Aur Dhokha, a true-crime documentary on Sukesh Chandrashekhar's alleged multi-crore frauds, premiering December 18, 2026.
M Snehanjali
November 26, 2026
 / 15:29 IST  
The streaming platform announced on Thursday, sharing the first look at the true-crime documentary.
 Netflix has announced a new documentary based on conman Sukesh Chandrashekhar's multi-crore fraud case, titled Love, Scam Aur Dhokha: The Sukesh Chandrashekhar Story. The streaming platform announced on Thursday, sharing the first look at the true-crime documentary. The caption read, "The schemes. The scandals. The story that gripped the nation. Watch the new documentary, Love, Scam Aur Dhokha - The Sukesh Chandrashekhar Story, out 18 December, only on Netflix." 
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Netflix India (@netflix_in)
Directed by Moumita Sen and Niyantha Shekar, the documentary is produced by India Today Originals and is executively produced by Chandni Ahlawat Dabas, with Karan Rana serving as co-director. Munish Pandey and Shams Tahir Khan have come on board as crime consultants.
The documentary aims to follow Sukesh Chandrashekhar's journey from his early years in Bengaluru to becoming one of the most talked-about names in India's high-profile fraud cases. It will also examine his alleged connections with influential personalities, including politicians and Bollywood celebrities.
Beyond revisiting the allegations and controversies surrounding Chandrashekhar, the documentary will attempt to understand how he allegedly managed to gain people's trust through elaborate claims and multiple identities. It will also examine the psychology behind his schemes and why even influential individuals reportedly believed his stories.
Drawing on more than two years of investigation, the documentary promises to uncover lesser-known details about his background, methods and alleged network.
One of its key focus areas will be how Chandrashekhar allegedly continued orchestrating massive frauds while being lodged in Delhi's Tihar Jail. The film will look into the loopholes that reportedly allowed these operations to continue despite his imprisonment.

 With extravagant claims, alleged financial fraud running into hundreds of crores and links to several prominent personalities, Chandrashekhar's story has repeatedly attracted public attention. The upcoming Netflix documentary aims to go beyond the headlines and examine how the entire operation allegedly unfolded. Love, Scam Aur Dhokha: The Sukesh Chandrashekhar Story will begin streaming on Netflix on December 18, 2026.

~ Exploring the story behind one of India’s most audacious alleged frauds and the man accused of orchestrating it, 
Love Scam Aur Dhokha
 premieres December 18, 2026 ~
From a teenager in Bengaluru to the man who would dominate national headlines, Sukesh Chandrashekhar’s story raises one extraordinary question: how did ONE man allegedly con his way into some of India’s most influential circles? A story of multiple identities, audacious claims, alleged multi-crore fraud, and connections with glittering Bollywood stars and hushed acknowledgements in the corridors of powerful politicians, this is a saga that has gripped the nation, with twists that seem stranger than fiction. 
Netflix has announced 
Love Scam Aur Dhokha: The Sukesh Chandrashekhar Story
, an upcoming true-crime documentary from India Today Originals, directed by Moumita Sen and Niyantha Shekar & executive produced by Chandni Ahlawat Dabas, that delves into the extraordinary story of a man accused of orchestrating an elaborate web of deception. Premiering on December 18, the documentary comes from the makers of 
Curry & Cyanide: The Jolly Joseph Case
 and 
The Indrani Mukerjea Story: Buried Truth
, two of Netflix India’s popular documentaries.
Going beyond the headlines, the documentary draws on over two years of extensive research to trace Sukesh’s origin story that very few really know about. It also explores how he allegedly managed to operate on such a scale, even from inside Tihar jail, bringing to light the gaps in the system that allowed his operations to continue for so long. 
Love Scam Aur Dhokha: The Sukesh Chandrashekhar Story
 explores the real story of not just what he's accused of, but how the con kept working, and why people continued believing him.
Love Scam Aur Dhokha: The Sukesh Chandrashekhar Story
 premieres on Netflix on December 18, 2026.
