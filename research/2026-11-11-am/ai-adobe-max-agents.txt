Adobe Transforms its Creative Cloud Tools into Agentic Workflows Built with the Precision and Control Pros Expect, Giving Creatives More Time for Their Craft
Tuesday, November 10, 2026 9:00AM
Download the PDF
https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3508106
Adobe Creative Agent, now across Creative Cloud, orchestrates multi-step workflows so pros can delegate work and increase their creative velocity, with local MCP integrations that open Creative Cloud tools to third-party agents
New innovations across Creative Cloud flagships, from Relight and Generative Restyle in Photoshop to Color Mode in Premiere and Co-editing in InDesign, speed core workflows and expand what pros can achieve in the craft at the heart of the creative process
New Firefly Design in Adobe Firefly brings raster, vector, motion, 3D and print together on one canvas, giving pros a collaborative space to create, review and deliver with the control and precision they expect, all included with Creative Cloud
MIAMI BEACH, Fla. — November 10, 2026 —
 Today at Adobe MAX — the world's largest creativity conference — Adobe (Nasdaq: ADBE) — the global technology leader that unleashes creativity, productivity and customer experiences through innovative tools and platforms — announced innovations that transform 
Creative Cloud
 tools into agentic workflows, built with the precision and control pros expect, giving creatives more time for their craft. The 
Adobe Creative Agent
 is expanding across Creative Cloud, orchestrating multi-step workflows so creative professionals can delegate and direct work, increasing their creative velocity. Adobe also previewed the local MCP integrations that will allow pros to use Creative Cloud tools from their preferred third-party agents.
New Creative Cloud innovations, including Generative Restyle in 
Photoshop
, Color Mode in 
Premiere
 and Co-editing in 
InDesign
, help photographers, video editors and designers work faster and with greater precision. Adobe also introduced 
Firefly Design
 (beta), which brings raster, vector, motion, 3D and print together on one canvas so designers can create, review and deliver their work all in one place. Following Adobe’s acquisition, Topaz Labs is introducing two new image enhancement models, Wonder 4 and Image HDR delivering upscaling, denoising, deblurring and sharpening capabilities to transform images with richer detail.
Creative professionals are increasingly working across more mediums, apps and formats, while facing growing demand to deliver high-quality work faster. Adobe is advancing a new approach to creating that brings AI assistance and collaboration directly into the Adobe tools they rely on, helping them move from idea to execution faster and with greater flexibility, precision and control. Creative professionals remain in control throughout the process, choosing when to delegate tasks to AI, when to make hands-on adjustments and how to apply their perspective, taste and judgment to shape the final result.
“The ideas, taste, style and vision that creative professionals bring to their work are what make their work stand out,” said Deepa Subramaniam, vice president, product marketing, Creative Cloud at Adobe. “Our latest Creative Cloud innovations transform our trusted creative tools into agentic, intelligent workflows that give pros the precision, control and flexibility to explore their ideas, streamline their work and stay focused on the creative decisions that make their work uniquely theirs.”
New Agentic Capabilities Give Creative Professionals More Choice in How They Work
The Adobe Creative Agent is now available in flagship desktop tools including 
Premiere
, 
Illustrator
, 
InDesign
, 
After Effects
 and 
Frame.io
. This brings more creative professionals a creative partner that orchestrates multi-step workflows behind the scenes, putting them in the director’s chair so they can stay focused on their craft. The Creative Agent now supports new capabilities in Premiere including applying effects across every clip in a sequence, tracking masked subjects through a frame, and adjusting color and exposure across an entire sequence at once.
Adobe is also previewing 
local
MCP integrations for Creative Cloud tools
 that will let creative professionals direct their preferred third-party agents to work locally in Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere and After Effects, with the agent making changes directly in native project files on the user’s computer. Layers, vectors, bins and sequences remain editable, and creative professionals can step in at any moment to refine the work by hand. If the agent is also connected to other MCP-capable tools such as Google Drive or Slack, it can bring them into the same workflow, pulling in a brief from Google Docs, working on the project with Adobe’s tools and sharing the result with colleagues in Slack.
Firefly Brings Creative Professionals One Place to Create, with the Precision and Control They Expect
Creative work increasingly spans 
image
, 
video
, 
audio
 and 
design
 to bring projects to life. 
Adobe Firefly
, the AI creative studio, brings Adobe’s industry-leading creative tools, 
top industry AI models
 and the Adobe Creative Agent together in one place, with the power and precision creative professionals need. When pros want to go deeper, they can seamlessly move into Creative Cloud tools to further refine their work.
New capabilities in Adobe Firefly include:
Firefly Design
 (beta): A new design editing space that brings together tools for illustration, image editing, animation, 3D and print on a single canvas where creators can take a brand identity or social campaign from first idea to final review.
Firefly Graph
 (beta): Helps creative professionals build custom, repeatable workflows that bring together leading AI models and Adobe’s production tools, from character studies and shot lists to scaling brand guidelines across campaign assets. A built-in AI Assistant helps create and optimize each workflow.
Firefly Builder
 (private beta): A preview of design-to-code prototyping capabilities, helps creative professionals transform ideas into interactive, on-brand prototypes, like a website, presentation or app, using natural language, with no code required for the initial prototype and then edit any element directly on the canvas, while a design system keeps the project consistent.
New Creative Cloud Innovations Help Pros Create, Refine and Deliver their Best Work
Adobe is introducing new editing tools and performance enhancements across Creative Cloud to help creative professionals tackle complex tasks faster, refine their work with greater precision and advance their craft. New innovations include:
Photoshop
 is introducing new ways to reimagine, edit and adjust images, including 
Relight
 for adding and adjusting light, 
Generative Restyle
 for turning prompt-based edits into adjustable sliders, 
Remove Tool (related elements)
 for more seamless object removal, 
Quick Compare
 for easily reviewing before-and-after edits and 
AI Assisted Editor
 for a simplified, AI-first editing experience.
Premiere
 is delivering faster, more intuitive ways to edit video, color and audio. 
Color Mode
,
 a first-of-its-kind color grading system, gives editors confidence and control to craft brilliant color. 
Generative Media Tool
 generates video, sound effects, soundscapes and music directly in the timeline. AI-powered audio tools including 
Enhance Audio
, 
Separate Crosstalk
 and 
Separate Sound Source
 quickly clean up, separate and refine audio. 
Generative Remove
 (beta) lets editors easily remove unwanted objects from a video and seamlessly fill the space with realistic detail.
Lightroom
 is unveiling new ways to enhance and refine photos, including 
Glow
 for adding soft, cinematic light with five style options, 
Blemish Removal
 for one-tap retouching with fine-tuning controls, 
Open Eyes
 (beta) for correcting untimely blinks with natural-looking results based on a reference photo. and improved 
Camera Tethering
 across Desktop and iPad for live review, organization and collaboration.
Illustrator
 gives pros 
greater flexibility to refine their designs
 with refreshed content libraries and new on-canvas Blend controls.
InDesign
 streamlines collaboration and layout workflows with real-time 
Co-editing
 (beta), 
Flex Layout
 that dynamically resizes text frames, and new 
Check Document
 and 
Rewrite
 capabilities to catch common issues.
Topaz Labs
Following Adobe’s 
acquisition of Topaz Labs
, a leader in AI-powered image and video enhancement technology, the company is introducing two new image enhancement models, 
Wonder 4
 and 
Image HDR
, giving creative professionals even more power to enhance and refine their images. Powered by a first-of-its-kind Mixture of Experts architecture, Wonder 4 is an image enhancement model that combines upscaling, denoising, deblurring, sharpening, and more, to transform images with a single click. Image HDR uses AI to transforms standard images into HDR, enhancing brightness and creating richer details. The latest capabilities empower photographers, retouchers and designers to recover images that might otherwise have been set aside and deliver more polished visuals in less time, freeing them to spend more time capturing new images or bringing their photos into new creative projects.
Pricing and availability
New capabilities in Creative Cloud are generally available today. Co-editing in InDesign, Open Eyes in Lightroom and Firefly Design, Firefly Graph are available in beta. Firefly Builder is currently in private beta.
AI Assistants powered by the Adobe Creative Agent are available in beta in Adobe’s flagship desktop apps, except Photoshop where the AI Assistant is available in the Photoshop beta app. Topaz models are available to purchase 
here
. Adobe’s local MCP integrations for Creative Cloud tools will be available in private beta in early 2027.

Adobe is pouring more AI into its products, including Photoshop, Premiere, Illustrator, and InDesign, in a major product feature announcement at its MAX event in Miami. The company is also expanding AI capabilities in its creative tool, Firefly, and releasing a Model Context Protocol (MCP) that lets other chatbots like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini use Adobe features.
Adobe’s Firefly app is moving into Figma’s territory with a new prototyping tool in private beta called Builder, which lets users create designs to show off ideas. There is also a new tool to create on-brand graphs, along with an image-editing tool that lets you change part of an image without changing other objects.
A new AI-powered video tool in Firefly will allow users to assemble a video from clips and other elements like images and sound effects. The company is making its agent proactive and always on so that it can get more context about work and be present for any action. Adobe is also making it easier for people to take quick action, like removing the background or generating a thumbnail for a video.
Image Credits:
Adobe
The company has already added a ton of AI features across its apps like Photoshop, Express, 
Premiere, and Illustrator
. At the MAX event, Adobe is continuing the trend. Photoshop is getting a new relighting feature and sliders for prompt-based generative styles; Premiere will have a new color grading system for professionals and an object removal feature for video; and Lightroom has a way to correct blinking eyes and a blemish removal tool.
Adobe already offers a lot of third-party models from companies like Google, Runway, ElevenLabs, Black Forest Labs, OpenAI, Kling, and ByteDance. 
One of its newer acquisitions, Topaz Labs
, is releasing two image models, called Wonder 4 for upscaling and denoising, and ImageHDR, to turn standard images into HDR. 
Image Credits:
Adobe
The company already has ChatGPT and Claude integrations to let users access some of its product functions. Now, Adobe is releasing a local MCP to expose more features of its Creative Cloud apps to users.
Adobe vice president Deepa Subramaniam told TechCrunch that the company is still figuring out what capabilities to expose and will rely on user feedback.
“We are still figuring out where and when and how to draw the line. What we’re putting out there is a number of endpoints and capabilities across Illustrator, Photoshop, After Effects, and Premiere. We’re just going to see how people use it, what they’re trying to do, and keep showing more. We don’t have any need or desire to sort of limit what the MCP endpoint can expose. What we really want to do is to support that creative workflow, how you choose to do it,” said Subramaniam.
Last month, Adobe rival Figma co-founder Dylan Field said at an event in India that 
MCP is not a perfect standard and benefits frontier labs more

Adobe (NASDAQ: ADBE) opened its Adobe MAX 2026 conference in Miami Beach on Nov. 10 by spreading AI assistants across more of its creative apps, adding third party AI models to Firefly and letting outside AI agents work directly inside Photoshop and Premiere project files. The company outlined the changes in its 
MAX announcement
, which also named new partnerships with Google, Microsoft and The Walt Disney Company.
The conference runs through Nov. 12. Adobe did not list prices, plan changes or credit costs for the new tools, and many of the features carry a beta or preview label.
If you edit video, design layouts or draw
The biggest change for people who already pay for Creative Cloud is a set of AI Assistants, in beta, inside Illustrator, Premiere, InDesign, After Effects and Frame.io. Adobe groups them under its Creative Agent, the assistant that already runs in Firefly.
App by app, Adobe listed these updates:
Photoshop:
 Relight and Generative Restyle tools, plus versions tuned for Arm based Windows PCs
Premiere:
 Color Mode, which Adobe describes as a new approach to color grading
Lightroom:
 Open Eyes, in beta
InDesign:
 Co-editing, in beta
Substance 3D Painter:
 an Arm based Windows version, in beta
Adobe is also bringing two Topaz Labs models, Wonder 4 and Image HDR, into Creative Cloud for upscaling, sharpening and noise cleanup. The release does not give a date for those.
Using your own AI agent on Adobe files
A preview feature called local MCP integrations lets a creator point a third party agent at native Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere and After Effects files. Adobe said the layers, sequences and compositions stay editable after the agent works on them.
Adobe said its earlier connectors for Anthropic and OpenAI chat apps, along with Slack, are the base it is building on. For context on how OpenAI is selling its business tools, see our report on 
ChatGPT Enterprise in AWS Marketplace
.
What changes in Firefly
Firefly, Adobe's AI creative app, is getting the widest set of additions:
More model choice.
 Users can pick models from Adobe, Topaz Labs, ByteDance, Google, Kling, OpenAI and ElevenLabs, among others.
Firefly AI Assistant (beta).
 The Firefly version of the Creative Agent gains a Plan Mode, a Model Selector and new customization settings.
Firefly Design (beta).
 A new editing space for illustration, image edits, animation, 3D and print work.
Firefly Graph (beta).
 A tool for building workflows that can be rerun.
Firefly Builder (private beta).
 It turns a plain language idea into an interactive website, presentation or app prototype without code.
Firefly Video gets a combined workflow for building a story and assembling clips, and Firefly Image adds Edit Object, Relight and Retouch. A Quick Actions gallery offers ready made jobs such as removing an object or making a thumbnail.
For marketing teams and businesses
Adobe also announced agents aimed at companies. Always on agents, in preview, can run recurring jobs in the background, such as sorting assets or checking quality. Adobe Brand Intelligence learns a company's brand rules and is coming to Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign, while CX Enterprise Coworker coordinates campaign agents inside Adobe GenStudio. Workfront gets a shared AI canvas called Ideation Space.
The partner deals
Google:
 Acrobat in Google Workspace, Adobe connectors for Gemini and Gemini Enterprise, and Google image and video models inside Firefly, Photoshop, Express and Premiere. Adobe and Google DeepMind also plan to explore new AI editing in Photoshop.
Microsoft:
 Photoshop, Premiere and After Effects optimized for Arm based Windows PCs, including the new Surface Laptop Ultra, and Adobe Express built into the Microsoft Photos app.
Disney:
 a multiyear alliance that makes Adobe Disney's 
Official AI Innovation Partner
, with custom AI tools for concept work in its innovation labs and its parks and cruise pipelines.
