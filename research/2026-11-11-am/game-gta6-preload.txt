GTA 6 is now available for preload if you have a spare 212GB
Beat the rush so you’ll be ready to play when the game unlocks on November 19.
By
Steve Dent
Nov. 10, 2026 5:01 am EST
Rockstar Games

With its release just 
nine days away
, 
Grand Theft Auto VI
 is now available for preloading on Xbox Series X|S and PlayStation 5 in most regions around the world. This means that once you pre-order the game, you'll be able to fully install it to your console's drive ahead of the November 19th release. Given 
GTA 6
's huge 212GB file size and reported pre-release sales already around 
8 million copies
, pre-loading would certainly help you avoid any rush. 
Publisher Rockstar Games staggered the game's digital preloading schedule, starting yesterday at 7 PM EST for Xbox Series X and S owners. If you have a PS5 or PS5 Pro console, preloading opened at midnight today local time (November 10) across all regions. For those with a physical copy of 
GTA 6
, it will start shipping and arrive at retail stores on November 12th. A box in the code will allow you to preload the game as soon as it arrives. 
GTA 6
's reported 212GB file size could pose an issue for many users. Even if you own a 2TB PS5 Pro, it's going to use a lot of your space. And if you have a standard 512GB Xbox Series S console, the game will take up more than half of the approximately 362GB of usable storage. Preloading will thus allow users to make sure their storage is organized and ready for the download, something that will be doubly important if there's a day-one patch. 
If you're on Xbox and pre-ordered 
GTA 6
, you can start preloading by going to the Microsoft Store, searching for Gran Theft Auto VI and selecting install. You can also pre-download by enabling "Keep My Games & Apps Up to Date" in the 
Settings
 > 
System
 > 
Updates
 section. On PS5, find and select the preload option on the PlayStation Store's 
GTA 6
 page. You can set it to automatically download the game by going to your Game Library, selecting the 
GTA 6
 pre-order and toggling the Auto-Download setting on. 
Give yourself plenty of time, as a 212GB could take hours to load depending on your connection speed. To get started, you'll first need to order the game: 
Grand Theft Auto VI
 costs $80 for the Standard Edition and $100 for the Ultimate Edition, on the 
Xbox
 or 
PlayStation
 stores, along with retailers including 
Amazon
 and 
Walmart
.

Rockstar has moved GTA 6 preloads up two days, from November 12 to November 10. The game still unlocks at midnight on November 19, so digital buyers now get a longer window to get the download out of the way before launch.
PS5 preload starts on November 10 at midnight local time, so it begins at 12:00 AM wherever you are. Xbox preload starts at midnight GMT, which is 7:00 PM Eastern on November 9. Xbox players on the US East Coast can start the evening before PS5 players do.
The download is up to 212GB. That is a big install, so clear space now rather than on preload night. Delete old games you are not playing, and check your storage before the start time, not after the download stalls. If you are on a smaller internal drive, an external or expansion drive may be worth sorting out this weekend.
To be ready when preload goes live: pre-order the digital edition, turn on automatic downloads, and leave your console in Rest Mode. With those three things set, your console should be able to start pulling the game on its own the moment it becomes available. For the full walkthrough, see our 
GTA 6 pre-order and preload guide
.
How long will it take? These are best-case estimates for a 212GB download at your full advertised speed. Real-world times will be longer, especially on launch-week servers and shared Wi-Fi.
1 Gbps: about 28 minutes
500 Mbps: about 56 minutes
300 Mbps: about 1.5 hours
100 Mbps: about 4.7 hours (call it 5)
50 Mbps: about 9.4 hours
25 Mbps: about 19 hours
If you are on 100 Mbps or slower, start the moment preload opens and let it run overnight. Wired ethernet beats Wi-Fi for a download this size.
Preloading does not let you play early. GTA 6 unlocks at midnight on November 19. In the US, store listings point to midnight Eastern, which is 9:00 PM Pacific on November 18. Because the unlock is midnight local in many regions, players in places like New Zealand will get in first, and the Americas last.
Buying the physical edition? The disc version is just a download code, and it comes out November 12. That means boxed buyers cannot start preloading on November 10 unless they also hold a digital pre-order. Once you have the code, you redeem it and download the game the same way.
Quick reference:
PS5 preload: November 10, 12:00 AM local time
Xbox preload: November 9, 7:00 PM ET (midnight GMT November 10)
Download size: up to 212GB
Boxed edition (download code): November 12
Launch unlock: November 19 at midnight (12:00 AM ET / 9:00 PM PT on November 18)
We will update this article if Rockstar adjusts any of these times.
