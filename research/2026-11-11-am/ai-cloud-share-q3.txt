Global Cloud Market Reaches 
$167.7 Billion in Q3 2026
 as Growth Hits 55%
The global cloud market reached a new high in Q3 2026. Enterprise spending on cloud infrastructure services rose 55% year over year to an estimated $167.7 billion, according to Synergy Research Group, now part of TechInsights. The market’s growth rate increased for the twelfth consecutive quarter.

Key Takeaways
1. Global cloud infrastructure spending reached an estimated $167.7 billion in Q3 2026, up 55% year over year.
2. AI has helped the market grow 185% since ChatGPT launched; AI-specific cloud services now account for about one-third of the total market.
3. In Q3 2026, Amazon led with 26% market share; Oracle ranked among the fastest-growing tier-two providers.
AI continues to accelerate global cloud market growth
The market doubled in size in just eight quarters. Before ChatGPT launched in late 2022, cloud market growth had been declining. Since then, AI has helped the market grow by 185%, and year-over-year growth rates have tripled. AI-specific cloud services now account for about one-third of the total market.
Public infrastructure-as-a-service (IaaS) and platform-as-a-service (PaaS) services make up most of the market. They grew 59% in Q3. Synergy estimates that trailing 12-month cloud infrastructure service revenues, including IaaS, PaaS, and hosted private cloud services, reached $558 billion.
Provider shares shift as neoclouds segment grows
Amazon retained the largest share of the Q3 worldwide market at 26%. Microsoft and Google continued to post substantially higher growth rates than Amazon, while holding a 19% and 16% market share, respectively.
Among tier-two providers, Oracle and several neoclouds recorded the highest growth rates, including CoreWeave, Crusoe, Nebius, Fluidstack, Together AI, and Nscale. Anthropic and OpenAI ranked strongly through the platform portions of their operations. SpaceX/SI entered the top 10 after launching cloud services this year.
Key Takeaway
“AI has totally transformed the cloud market in terms of service capabilities, customer demand, and growth opportunities. In the 10 years before the launch of ChatGPT, cloud providers drove a rapid shift in workloads and investment from on-premises data centers towards public cloud services. What we now see is AI technology propelling cloud markets in a way that was unimaginable just four years ago. As the growth rate jumps to well over 50%, the leading cloud providers are benefiting from surging revenues, but they are also facing a much-changed market landscape, with AI labs and neoclouds emerging as both partners and competitors.”
 - John Dinsdale, chief analyst at Synergy Research Group.
The global cloud market continued to grow strongly across regions
The United States is the largest market and had the highest growth rate among major countries, accounting for 44% of the world market.
Measured in local currencies, India, Indonesia, Ireland, Canada, and Taiwan were among the other countries with the strongest growth. The U.K. and Germany remained Europe’s largest cloud markets, while Ireland, Norway, Denmark, Finland, and the Netherlands were the region’s fastest-growing markets.

Enterprise spending on cloud infrastructure services in Q3 jumped 55 percent year-over-year, the 12th consecutive quarter of growth, according to Synergy Research Group.
Synergy’s latest cloud market growth data revealed that global cloud revenue reached $168 billion in Q3 2026, more than double the revenue in the same period in 2024.
– Synergy Research Group
Growth was seen in all regions globally. The largest market remains the US, at 44 percent market share; it also has the highest growth rate among major countries.

Cloud Market Growth Rate Surges to Well Over 50% per Year; Q3 Revenues Reach $168 Billion
Reno, NV, October 29, 2026 – 
Synergy Research Group
, now part of 
TechInsights
, today announced that Q3 enterprise spending on cloud infrastructure services yet again broke all previous records. The growth rate increased for the twelfth successive quarter, with Q3 spending on cloud services jumping by an unprecedented 55% year over year to reach $168 billion.
Despite the already massive scale of the market, it has taken just eight quarters to double in size. Before the launch of ChatGPT in late 2022, the cloud market growth rate had steadily declined, as might be expected for a large, decade-old market. Since then, AI has helped the cloud market to grow by 185%, with year-on-year growth rates tripling. AI-specific cloud services now account for around a third of the total market.
Among the major cloud providers, Amazon maintains a strong lead in the market, while Microsoft and Google continue to achieve substantially higher growth rates. Q3’s worldwide market share for these companies was 26%, 19%, and 16%, respectively. Among the tier-two cloud providers, those with the highest growth rates included Oracle and a group of neoclouds: CoreWeave, Crusoe, Nebius, Fluidstack, Together AI and Nscale. Anthropic and OpenAI also now rank strongly through the platform portions of their operations. After launching cloud services this year, SpaceX/SI has already jumped into the top 10 based on cloud infrastructure service revenues. The rapid growth of neoclouds is now causing the market share of many more established cloud providers to erode.
“AI has totally transformed the cloud market in terms of service capabilities, customer demand, and growth opportunities,” said John Dinsdale, Chief Analyst at Synergy Research Group. “In the 10 years before the launch of ChatGPT, cloud providers drove a rapid shift in workloads and investment from on-premises data centers towards public cloud services. What we now see is AI technology propelling cloud markets in a way that was unimaginable just four years ago. As the growth rate jumps to well over 50%, the leading cloud providers are benefiting from surging revenues, but they are also facing a much-changed market landscape, with AI labs and neoclouds emerging as both partners and competitors.”
With most major cloud providers reporting their Q3 earnings, Synergy estimates that quarterly cloud infrastructure service revenues (including IaaS, PaaS and hosted private cloud services) were $167.7 billion, with trailing 12-month revenues reaching $558 billion. Public IaaS and PaaS services account for the bulk of this market, and those grew by 59% in Q3.
Geographically, the cloud market continues to grow strongly in all regions of the world. Notably, the US, which is by far the largest market, also had the highest growth rate among the major countries. It now accounts for 44% of the world market. When measured in local currencies, other countries with the strongest growth included India, Indonesia, Ireland, Canada and Taiwan. In Europe, the largest cloud markets are the U.K. and Germany, but the fastest-growing markets were Ireland, Norway, Denmark, Finland and the Netherlands.
