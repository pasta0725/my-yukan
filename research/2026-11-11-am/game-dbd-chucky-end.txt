Announced on the official 
Dead by Daylight Twitter account
, Chucky and all his associated cosmetics will be leaving Dead by Daylight on November 24th following the loss of the license to promote the character. Anyone who currently owns Chucky will still be able to play him, and he is currently on sale until his departure, but after November 24th, this Good Guy is as good as gone.
 Breaking Up the Band 
This departure makes Chucky the third horror icon to be 
added to Dead by Daylight
 before jumping ship, coming after Michael Myers and Pinhead. Michael Myers was the game's very first crossover killer, helping to put Dead by Daylight on the Map for many, while both Chucky and Pinhead provided fun, flavorful ways of playing the game before being revoked by their license holders.
Chucky being removed is very surprising, as Behaviour had plenty of leeway to create multiple unique designs for him, including legendary cosmetics that swap him out for Tiffany, complete with new voice acting. Chucky was clearly a labor of love from Behaviour, so the choice to remove him has minds racing.
Personally, I'm guessing this is the preamble to some kind of Child's Play video game, as the same thing happened to both Michael Myers and Pinhead. With Behaviour holding the license, no one else can make a game starring Chucky, and after the excitement of Halloween and wild success of the 
new Hellraiser game
, it seems like the perfect time to strike while the iron's hot.
Anyone who currently owns Chucky will still be able to play him, and he is currently on sale until his departure, but after November 24th, this Good Guy is as good as gone.
Of course, this means the existing Perks Chucky grants players are also getting renamed, as well as joining Pinhead's perks in the general killer pool. A small victory for budget killer players, but a genuine loss for newcomers to Dead by Daylight. Chucky completes the game's balance of genuine fear and black comedy, and his loss is a real blow to the game.
Again, he will still be usable for current owners, but his absence will mean new players have no chance to pick up the Good Guy, unless they buy a code from resellers online, which is its own massive can of worms.
 Digital Scalping 
Gaming is no stranger to 
predatory resellers
, more commonly known as scalpers. These dregs plague everything from ticket sales to merch events, and Dead by Daylight is a unique spot to fall victim to them. The practice saw a rise when Pinhead was removed from the game, leading scalpers to buy keys for the chapter and hold on to them, selling them for much higher prices.
This practice is still being done today, with the cheapest key I found selling for a frankly comedic $2,270. That isn't a typo, and I won't link the seller on principle, but the expectation that killers can fully just leave Dead by Daylight adds a very gross feeling of FOMO to every chapter, putting these awesome inclusions on a time limit before the guillotine falls.
It isn't impossible for killers to stick around. The Xenomorph is notably still in DBD despite the announcement of a second Alien Isolation, but many studios want to avoid confusing consumers when they're ready to sell new horror titles, leading to the scalper's perfect opportunity to jump in.

Behaviour Interactive has confirmed that the Chucky Chapter is leaving 
Dead by Daylight
 stores permanently on 
November 24, 2026 at 11am Eastern Time
. The chapter has been in the game for roughly three years, but the window to buy it is closing fast. This guide covers the exact cutoff, every platform's sale window, what happens to Chucky's three perks after the removal, and what you actually need to do before the deadline.
important
The removal affects the ability to 
purchase
 Chucky content. Players who already own him keep full access, including in 2v8 matches. Nothing is being deleted from your account.

When exactly does Chucky leave DBD stores?
The cutoff is 
11am Eastern Time on Tuesday, November 24, 2026
. That is not midnight, and it is not the end of the business day. Behaviour's official FAQ is specific about the morning deadline, so completing any intended purchase before the final day is the safer move.

Can you still play Chucky after November 24?
Yes, if you own him before the deadline. Behaviour's FAQ is explicit: any content unlocked before November 24 remains fully playable after the store removal. That includes:
Playing Chucky in standard matches and 2v8 modes
Continuing to level him up in the Bloodweb
Obtaining his Add-Ons with Bloodpoints
Earning all three 
Prestige Cosmetics
 and three 
Perk Charms
 through progression
Completing Character Quests
The mistake most players make here is treating the removal like a content wipe. It is not. The store closes; your owned content does not disappear. There is also no emergency grind required before the deadline. Prestige rewards remain earnable for owners after November 24.
One item worth checking separately is the 
Good Guys Box Charm
, which the official Chucky Chapter page describes as exclusive to the DLC add-on purchase. If that charm is your reason for buying, verify the exact add-on listing before assuming any purchase route includes it.
What happens to Chucky's perks when he leaves?
Here's the short answer: Chucky's three perks are not disappearing. Behaviour is renaming them and moving them into the 
General perk pool
, where any Killer can unlock them regardless of owning the Chucky Chapter.

Owners of Chucky keep the original licensed names and flavour text in his Bloodweb. Non-owners will see the renamed versions in the General pool.

What is Chucky's kit in DBD?
Chucky entered the game as the 
44th Killer
, voiced by Brad Dourif who has played the character across eight films and a three-season television series. He is notable for being the first Killer in Dead by Daylight to use a 
third-person camera
.
His power, 
Playtime's Over
, centers on 
Hidey-Ho Mode
: Chucky becomes Undetectable and generates map-wide Illusionary Footfalls to disorient Survivors, with a cooldown after use. While in Hidey-Ho Mode, he has access to two additional abilities:
Scamper
: slips under a downed pallet or through a window to close distance quickly
Slice & Dice
: a sprint and lunge that can chain directly into Scamper if a Survivor tries to vault near him
His small stature makes him genuinely difficult to spot, though his slower movement speed is the trade-off. He received nerfs shortly after launch, then further tuning to compensate.
tip
Behaviour's FAQ confirms it will continue supporting Chucky with bug fixes after the store removal. The removal is a licensing change, not a signal that the character is being abandoned mechanically.
What about achievements, refunds, and cross-progression?
Behaviour's FAQ addresses three common questions:
Achievements
: Chucky-related achievements become generic rather than character-specific after the removal. Completed achievements stay recognised and current progress is retained.
Refunds
: No refunds will be issued because of this removal, since purchased Chucky content remains playable. This is the developer's stated position; individual platform refund policies are separate.
Cross-progression
: Previously unlocked content stays unlocked when you cross-progress accounts. This applies to content you already own, not content you never purchased.
Why is Chucky leaving Dead by Daylight?
Behaviour's published FAQ gives no reason. No contract length, rights dispute, or negotiation outcome is confirmed. Community theories exist, but none have been verified by Behaviour. The confirmed facts are the store removal date and the continued access for existing owners. A future return is not announced.

What should you do before November 24?
The right action depends entirely on your situation:
Already own Chucky and his cosmetics
: No action needed. Progression, Prestige rewards, and Add-Ons remain fully available.
Want to play Chucky but do not own him
: Use your platform's sale window above and complete the purchase before 11am ET on November 24.
Only interested in his perks
: Wait. The General pool route means non-owners are not permanently locked out of Hex: Eye For An Eye, One Track Mind, or Gaining Steam.
Own Chucky but want a specific cosmetic
: Treat that as a separate store decision with the same November 24 cutoff. Owning the Killer does not preserve an indefinite shopping window for his outfits.
Want the Good Guys Box Charm
: Check the exact add-on listing before purchasing, as the Charm is described as exclusive to the DLC.

FAQ
Is Chucky being deleted from the game?
No. Behaviour confirmed that content unlocked before the November 24 deadline remains playable. The change removes the purchase option, not existing owners' access.
What time does Chucky leave the stores?
11am Eastern Time on November 24, 2026, which is 4pm UTC. The cutoff is not midnight.
Will Chucky's perks become unavailable to non-owners?
No. Behaviour is moving renamed versions (Hex: Eye For An Eye, One Track Mind, Gaining Steam) into the General perk pool after the removal.
Can I still Prestige Chucky after November 24?
Yes, if you own him before the deadline. Behaviour confirms continued levelling, Add-Ons, three Prestige Cosmetics, and three Perk Charms for owners.
Has BHVR explained why Chucky is leaving?
No. The official FAQ does not disclose a reason. Any claims about specific causes remain unverified.
