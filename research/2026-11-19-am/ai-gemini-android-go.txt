Google announced today that it has optimized its Gemini app for Android Go devices with lower memory. Android Go is a lightweight version of Android for entry-level smartphones and tablets with a minimum of 2GB of memory, and until now, these devices didn’t have access to the Gemini app.
“For hundreds of millions of people globally, a lower-memory Android device is their everyday connection to the internet, education, and business. Until recently, hardware limits meant those phones only had basic voice commands,” 
Google said today
. “We re-architected the app so the core Gemini experience runs smoothly on hardware with less memory.”
With this Android Go support, Google said that its chatbot is now available for “hundreds of millions more people.” On existing Android Go smartphones and tablets, the Gemini app can be downloaded from the Play Store, but Google is also working with hardware partners like Transsion to preinstall it on upcoming devices.
Yesterday, Google also announced that 
66 new regional voices are now available
 in Gemini, including India English, Hindi, Brazilian Portuguese, Indonesian, and Korean. Each of these new voices is “tuned for natural cadences and everyday clarity,” and custom settings are also available.
