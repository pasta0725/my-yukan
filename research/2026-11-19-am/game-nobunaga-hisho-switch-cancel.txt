We recheck by Nov. 25, 2026
Nobunaga's Ambition: Hishou will no longer come to the original Switch. Its PS5, Switch 2 and PC versions remain planned, and a new release date is due at the Nov. 25 stream.
Koei Tecmo said on Nov. 2 that it was reviewing Hishou’s release date and supported systems, according to AUTOMATON. It delayed a livestream planned for Nov. 4. The release date set to appear in the Nov. 19 issue of Weekly Famitsu would no longer match the publisher’s plans. The first clear result of that review is one fewer platform: the original Switch.
The revised platform list is PS5, Switch 2 and PC via Steam. The first official Hishou stream is now set for Nov. 25, when Koei Tecmo plans to show a new trailer and announce the release date, Nintendo-Town says. The game will have the series’ first story mode, centered on Oda Nobunaga. Its rebuilt castle-town system lets players develop a town; AUTOMATON says placed facilities affect resource benefits and income. Original Switch players will not get to build that town on their system.
What we don’t know
Koei Tecmo has not given a specific reason for dropping the original Switch. It has not announced the new release date or confirmed a release outside Japan and Asia.
That’s the whole story. Back to the main quest.
