: A clipboard manager for keeping track of copied content.

Ulysses
: A writing app with tools for formatting documents.
Mac Game of the Year
Big Walk
: A multiplayer adventure focused on solving puzzles with friends.
Blue Prince
: A mystery game set inside an unusual mansion.
Crimson Desert
: A fantasy adventure set in a world shaped by war.
Apple Arcade Game of the Year
Perchang World
: A physics puzzle game about guiding marbles.
PowerWash Simulator
: A cleaning simulation that puts players behind a pressure washer.
Pusheen's Place
: A cat-themed game featuring Pusheen.

Adobe Premiere
: A video editor with a workspace designed for spatial computing.
MLB
: A baseball streaming app offering a view behind home plate.

Cueva de las Manos
: A puzzle game built around manipulating light and shadows.
Ping Pong Club

Apple Watch App of the Year
Bevel
: A fitness app that interprets health data to offer guidance.

: A tracker for moon phases and lunar events.
StressWatch
: A health app for monitoring estimated stress levels.
Apple TV App of the Year
Case Solved: The London Files
: A murder mystery game featuring logic puzzles and noir themes.
