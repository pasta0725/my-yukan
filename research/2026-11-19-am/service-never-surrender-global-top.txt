Netflix
‘s most-watched film of the past week did not come from Hollywood, and it did not need its own country to get there. 
Never Surrender
, an Indonesian action thriller about a retired soldier defending two estranged grandchildren from a gang in a Central Java village, drew more viewers than every English-language film on the service, every series and every Christmas movie Netflix has stacked up for the holidays. It did that in a short first week.
The reviews did not do this. Only a handful of critics have weighed in, and they have been polite at best. What carried the film is a premise that needs no translation. The map of where it landed tells Netflix more about where Indonesian action sells now than any review could.

The figures come from Netflix’s own Top 10 data for the week ending Sunday, Nov. 15. 
Never Surrender
 was No. 1 on the global list of non-English films with 11.0 million views and 25.5 million hours viewed. It ranked No. 1 in 12 countries and reached the top 10 in 83. No title on any of Netflix’s four global lists drew more views that week. The leading English-language film, 
Heartland
, had 7.3 million, and the top English series, 
Taken: Natalee Holloway
, had 7.2 million.
It got there in four days. The film premiered on Thursday, Nov. 12, and Netflix’s tracking week closes on Sunday. The views figure also works against it: Netflix divides hours viewed by running time, and at two hours and 17 minutes this is a long film. Measured in hours, its lead is wider still. Its 25.5 million hours were nearly double the 13.9 million logged by 
Heartland
.
Bigger abroad than at home
The country list explains the result. 
Never Surrender
 was No. 1 in Turkey, Nigeria, Kenya, the Philippines, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Jordan and the Dominican Republic, and in Guadeloupe, Martinique, Réunion and New Caledonia. It was No. 2 across much of Latin America, including Argentina, Chile, Colombia and Peru, and in Greece, Romania and South Africa. In Indonesia, the country that made it, it was also No. 2, behind 
402 Rumah Sakit Angker Korea
, which led neighbouring Malaysia as well.
In the large Western markets it charted without dominating. It was No. 3 in Germany and Mexico, No. 4 in Italy, Brazil, Poland and South Korea, and No. 5 in Spain and Portugal. Japan had it at No. 6; France, Sweden and Taiwan at No. 7; the United States at No. 8; and Norway at No. 10. It missed the top 10 in Denmark and India.
That is the profile of an export. Film Combat Syndicate reported that Netflix announced the film as part of its Indonesia slate, and a slate like that pays off when it travels. A genre film that its home audience ranked second, yet still drew the largest audience on the platform, is the result such a strategy is built for. The premise crosses borders without help. In Netflix’s synopsis, a grandfather takes in two grandchildren he has not seen in a long time, “a major problem from Jakarta follows them,” and the choice comes down to “surrender or fight back.” MovieZine reached for 
John Wick
, the 
Rambo
 films and Finland’s 
Sisu
 to describe it. Flickonclick read it as a tribute to 1990s American revenge and survival films. Those reference points work in every market, which is the advantage.
What the No. 1 does not show
The win needs some context. A week earlier, the non-English No. 1, 
Collision Course
, drew 17.2 million views. 
Never Surrender
 took the top spot in a softer week, when the English film list was led by 
Heartland
 and filled out with seasonal titles such as 
A Dog’s Perfect Christmas
 and 
The Gospel of Christmas
.
The better yardstick is Indonesian. 
The Shadow Strays
, 
Timo Tjahjanto
‘s action thriller, also arrived on a Thursday, on Oct. 17, 2024, and opened with 10.6 million views in its first four days. That was good for No. 2 on the same non-English film list. 
Never Surrender
 beat that number, and it took the No. 1 spot 
The Shadow Strays
 missed.
What the critics said
The reviews are few and divided. Rotten Tomatoes lists three critics and no Tomatometer yet. Flickonclick gave the film two stars out of five. It called the fight scenes “the best thing about Never Surrender” but warned that “those same fights go on for too long” and that “you can guess most of what happens long before it does.” Heaven of Horror also gave it two out of five, praising the fights and the villains and faulting the predictable story and the running time. But Why Tho? scored it 6 out of 10, found the family bonding “surprisingly believable” and concluded that “it never manages to excel, but it rarely truly fails either.” Casey’s Movie Mania said it “packs a visceral punch despite its overlong runtime.”
Viewers did not wait for the critics to agree. That makes the second week worth more than the first: a four-day debut measures curiosity, and the hold measures word of mouth.
Who made Never Surrender
Sidharta Tata directed the film and wrote the screenplay with Khalid Kashogi. According to What’s on Netflix, it is his first collaboration with producers Kimo Stamboel and Edwin Nazir. 
Lukman Sardi
 leads the cast as the grandfather, Eyang Hari. Callista Arum and Sulthan Hamonangan play the grandchildren, Putri and Dimas, with Derby Romero as Bastian and Donny Alamsyah, from 
The Raid 2
, as Ulo. MCM covered the film’s premise and creative team before its release.
Never Surrender: runtime, sequel and where to watch
How long is 
Never Surrender
? It runs two hours and 17 minutes. That length is the most common complaint in the reviews, and the reason its views figure trails its hours.
Where can you watch it? It is an Indonesian-language Netflix original, released on Nov. 12, 2026, under the original title 
Serangan Balik
, and it streams only on Netflix.
Is there a 
Never Surrender 2
? Netflix has announced nothing. Flickonclick reports a scene early in the end credits that points to a sequel. If that tease becomes a film, viewers in Turkey, Nigeria and the Philippines will have asked for it louder than Indonesia did.

Global chart success
Never Surrender has jumped straight to the top of Netflix’s Global Top 10 Non-English Movies list. The film has already pulled in 11 million views and reached the No.1 spot in 12 countries while landing in the Top 10 in 83 territories, from the US and Mexico to Germany, Japan and South Korea. For UK viewers this shows how quickly a regional title can cross over when it lands on the platform.
What the story delivers
Set in a Central Java village, the film mixes high-stakes action with family drama. An ex-special-forces grandfather must protect his two grandchildren after they stumble into trouble with a criminal syndicate. The cast includes Lukman Sardi, Callista Arum, Sulthan Hamonangan, Derby Romero and Donny Alamsyah. It is the first collaboration between director Sidharta Tata and producers Kimo Stamboel and Edwin Nazir.
Director’s take
Director Sidharta Tata said, “I poured my heart and soul into making Never Surrender, with a commitment to delivering a unique action film experience. Beyond its thrilling action sequences, the film also tells a heartwarming story about family. Thanks to the collaboration with Netflix and producer Kimo Stamboel and Edwin Nazir, I got the opportunity to explore various creative possibilities, including bringing to life a long-held dream of mine: staging an exciting car chase scene. I am also deeply grateful to audiences in Indonesia and around the world who have watched and warmly embraced Never Surrender. Your support means so much to me and motivates me to continue creating bold, fresh and entertaining films.”
