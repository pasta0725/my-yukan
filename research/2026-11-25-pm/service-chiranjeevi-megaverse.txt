      Chiranjeevi's Netflix talk show Megaverse’s makers share new promo; Telugu superstar says 'This is my territory': Watch
Chiranjeevi is set to make his OTT debut as a host with Netflix's Megaverse. The streaming platform has unveiled a new promo as filming gets underway.
M Snehanjali
November 24, 2026
 / 17:19 IST  
Netflix India shared the latest promo of Megaverse on Tuesday, November 24, on their official social media pages.
Telugu veteran superstar Chiranjeevi is set to make an OTT debut as a host with his talk show titled Megaverse on Netflix, and the makers have unveiled a new promo clip on Tuesday, garnering further excitement.
Netflix India shared the latest promo of Megaverse on Tuesday, November 24, on their official social media pages. The video begins with everyone on the streets searching for the ‘N’ in 
Chiranjeevi
.
From billboards and newspaper advertisements to posters on autorickshaws, T-shirt designs, murals and even tattoos on fans' arms, the missing letter becomes the talk of the town.
The unusual mystery leaves everyone puzzled, including a coconut seller, an autorickshaw driver and even a girl out on a date. Just when the search seems to be going nowhere, Chiranjeevi makes an appearance on a smartphone screen.
Seated comfortably in a red leather chair, the superstar signals the letter 'N', which resembles Netflix's iconic logo, to complete the missing part of his name. With his trademark swag, he declares, “This is my territory,” before breaking into a laugh that adds a playful, gangster-style touch to the promo.
