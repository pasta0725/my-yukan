===== https://tellybrief.co.uk/asas-do-destino-netflix-brazilian-romantic-drama/
(status 200)
Netflix has announced that production is underway on Asas do Destino, a new Brazilian romantic drama film. The project stars André Lamoglia, Rayssa Bratillieri and Ronald Sotto. Set against the backdrop of Brazil’s Air Force during the 1990s, the story explores a military environment that Netflix notes has never previously featured in Brazilian fictional screen storytelling.
The plot centres on nursing technician Josi, played by Bratillieri, and cadet Nelsinho, played by Sotto, who are on the verge of building a shared life before unexpected events alter their trajectory. Alongside Matheus, portrayed by Lamoglia, Josi faces difficult choices concerning honour, loyalty and romantic devotion.
Directed by Daniel Lieff, whose directorial credits include Here the Whole Time, the film is produced by Glaz Entretenimento, an audiovisual production company with two decades of experience in Brazil. The screenplay was written by Marcos Bernstein and Mariana Torres, with Gustavo Bragança acting as consultant, based on an original story created by Mayra Lucas, Marcos Bernstein and Hugo Kenzo.
