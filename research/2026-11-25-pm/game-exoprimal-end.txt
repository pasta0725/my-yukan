Capcom to End Service for Mech Suit vs. Dinosaur Shooter Exoprimal in September 2027

©CAPCOM
Capcom announced today (25th) via its official website and social media channels that service for its team-based TPS Exoprimal will be coming to an end.
Set in the year 2043, Exoprimal is a third-person shooter where humanity fights back against dinosaurs that suddenly appear in the future. In the game, players equip exosuits to cooperatively battle dinosaur swarms. After completing missions, players customize various suits to take on even larger hordes of powerful dinosaurs or push through interference from rival teams.
Upon its release in July 2023, the game drew attention for its impressive graphics powered by the RE Engine, a unique PvEvP mode where two teams compete to accomplish given missions, Capcom's signature exhilarating action, and its premise combining mech suits with dinosaurs. However, new content updates ceased after Season 4 in July 2024, one year after launch, and today's announcement confirms that service will end on September 30 next year.
Alongside today's announcement, Capcom ceased store sales of Exoprimal Survival Pass: Premium Tier starting at 10:00 AM. Additionally, store sales of the main game, DLC, and all other products will end on March 31 at 10:00 AM. Even after store sales end, players can re-download the game prior to the end of service, but it will no longer be playable once service officially terminates.
Further details regarding the end of service and inquiries for Exoprimal can be found on the official website and support page.
