Summary:
Mega Man X Legacy Collection 2 is finally getting a proper physical release for Nintendo Switch via Limited Run Games, giving collectors and longtime fans a chance to own Mega Man X5 through X8 on cartridge. The release includes a Standard Edition, a Double Pack that pairs both Legacy Collection volumes across two cartridges, and a premium Maverick Hunter Edition with extra physical goodies aimed at collectors. Pre-orders open November 12, 2026 and run through January 3, 2027, letting you secure whichever edition suits your setup. This release fixes a longtime gripe from the original Switch retail bundle, where the second collection was only distributed as a download code rather than on cart. Whether you missed the download-only era or want a boxed version for the shelf, this Limited Run launch brings a full physical option that celebrates both classic and later-era Mega Man X entries, and it’s worth considering if you care about cartridges, extras, or simply having the complete saga in your hands.
What Limited Run announced for Mega Man X Legacy Collection 2
Limited Run Games announced that Mega Man X Legacy Collection 2 will receive a standalone physical release on Nintendo Switch, bringing the later entries of the X series to cartridge collectors for the first time in most regions. The announcement covers three editions: a Standard Edition, a Double Pack pairing both Legacy Collection volumes, and a special Maverick Hunter Edition packed with physical extras. This move is notable because the previous retail bundle for Switch included only the first collection on cartridge while the second collection was delivered via a download code. Limited Run’s edition promises both convenience and collectible value, giving players the option to buy just the second collection or to own both collections together in a themed set. For NintendoReporters readers who follow Switch releases and collector runs, this is the kind of announcement that matters – it restores a physical-presence to games that were previously partly digital-only, and it’s exactly the sort of Nintendo-focused release our audience expects to hear about.
Which games are included and what each collection covers
Mega Man X Legacy Collection 2 collects Mega Man X5, X6, X7, and X8, representing the franchise’s turn into later-generation hardware and design experiments. The original Mega Man X Legacy Collection compiles Mega Man X, X2, X3, and X4, covering the classic 16-bit and early 32-bit era entries. Together, the two collections span the core X saga from its origins through the early 2000s, letting you play as X and, in many entries, Zero. The Limited Run physical offerings put these games on cartridge for the Switch, with the Double Pack delivering both volumes across two physical cartridges inside a themed slipcase. That means no extra download codes and a shelf-friendly package for fans of boxed games. If you’ve been curious about the series’ evolution from tight 2D action to the more varied experiments in the later titles, this set gives you the whole picture in one collectible package.
Editions explained – Standard, Double Pack and Maverick Hunter
The Standard Edition offers Mega Man X Legacy Collection 2 on cart for players who only want the later four X titles without extras. The Double Pack is aimed at collectors or newcomers who want the entire X series on cartridge, pairing Legacy Collection 1 and 2 across two carts with a slipcase – a tidy solution if you missed the initial retail that included a download code for the second set. The Maverick Hunter Edition is the premium option, bundling both collections plus physical extras designed to appeal to longtime fans – think items like a hologram canister light, a Metool keychain, iron-on patches, and a themed collector’s box. Pricing varies by edition and Limited Run typically lists MSRP on release pages, so compare editions and extras before choosing. Each edition is designed to give a different balance of value, convenience, and collectible flair depending on whether you just want to play or you want a display piece for your shelf.
Why this physical release matters for collectors and players
This physical release matters because it resolves a common frustration among collectors: the second half of the Mega Man X Legacy Collection was only available via download code in earlier retail releases. For players who prefer cartridges, dislike relying on download vouchers, or want a sealed box for their collection, Limited Run’s decision to press X Legacy Collection 2 to cart is a welcome reversal. Physical editions are more than nostalgia – they give you a durable, transportable copy that doesn’t depend on an eShop code that could expire or be lost. Beyond collector concerns, a boxed release offers extras and packaging that digital storefronts don’t, and the Double Pack and Maverick Hunter Edition especially aim to recreate that premium box-feel. For those who follow Nintendo hardware and the culture around it, having a fully physical Mega Man X saga on Switch fills a noticeable gap on many shelves.
How this release differs from the original Switch retail bundle
The original Switch retail bundle for Mega Man X Legacy Collection included the first collection on cartridge but provided the second collection via a download voucher, which left a digital-only experience for those later titles. Limited Run’s new releases change that by putting the second collection on physical cart, meaning the whole saga can now be owned without relying on a code. The Double Pack goes one step further by packaging both carts together in a slipcase, mimicking classic boxed sets where each volume had its own disc or cart. That difference is practical – no more code redemption – and symbolic, because it signals respect for the full scope of the series. If you’re nostalgic for boxed releases or you collect boxed Switch games, this edition effectively rewrites the earlier distribution choices and gives the Mega Man X series a proper, fully physical treatment on Switch.

What to expect from the Maverick Hunter Edition and extras
The Maverick Hunter Edition packages both Legacy Collection volumes with a collection of physical extras meant to appeal to fans. Typical Limited Run collector editions include items like a themed box, a small light or display piece, enamel or embroidered patches, and a novelty keychain – in this case a Metool keychain and a hologram canister light are listed. These extras are useful for display or casual cosplay, and they add value for collectors who want more than the carts.
