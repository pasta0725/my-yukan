Klipsch
 is celebrating its 80th birthday with its new Stratos Series of wired headphones.
Currently consisting of the closed-back Stratos HP-2 and semi-open-backed Stratos HP-3, the new duo are built to offer outstanding sound and enhanced user comfort in celebration of Klipsch's 80 years in the industry.
Each pair deploy a two-way coaxial driver array. The semi-open-backed HP-3 feature a 50mm bio-cellulose mid/bass driver and a 10mm dynamic tweeter paired to deliver "warm, punchy bass" and "crisp, sparkly highs".

Klipsch has spent decades building its reputation on loudspeakers, but headphones have never occupied the same prominent position in its product lineup. That could be changing. After teasing three new headphone models at 
CES 2026
 and previewing the wired 
HP-2 and HP-3
 under the Atlas name at High End Vienna, Klipsch is returning to 
CanJam Dallas 2026
 on November 14–15 with production-ready versions of both headphones, now officially called 
Stratos
.
The name change isn’t the only development. Unlike the earlier prototypes, the Stratos HP-2 and HP-3 are moving toward commercial availability, with attendees getting an opportunity to hear the production models before their release. The wireless HP-1 remains on the sidelines until early 2027, when Klipsch expects to reveal its final specifications and pricing. For a company that has been relatively quiet in wired headphones, this is a meaningful return to a category where it has something to prove.

The Stratos Series brings together the best of everything we’ve learned, setting new standards in acoustic performance and materials design for Klipsch headphones

Klipsch Stratos HP-3 Headphones
Materials & Comfort
The HP-2 and HP-3 incorporate premium materials, including machined aluminum yokes and contact points, real wood earcups, copper ring accents, Klipsch Heritage Twitchell grille cloth, and protein leather headbands.
The cushioned headbands are lined with open-cell foam and feature a cutout designed to reduce pressure points and prevent discomfort at the crown of the head. The earpads are wrapped in ultrasoft microsuede to create a comfortable seal around the ears and support a more immersive listening experience. Magnetic attachments allow the earpads to be easily removed and reattached.
The Stratos headphones follow Klipsch’s longstanding design principles of high efficiency, low distortion, and full-range sound reproduction. Each model features a two-way coaxial driver array.
HP-3
The semi-open-back HP-3 is the flagship model, featuring a 50mm biocellulose midrange/bass driver and a 10mm dynamic tweeter designed to deliver warm, punchy bass and crisp, extended highs with low distortion and exceptional clarity.
The semi-open-back design is intended to optimize two-channel listening by emphasizing spaciousness, air, and a more speaker-like presentation. The HP-3 also prioritizes comfort and premium construction, with a wider cushioned headband for improved weight distribution, Alcantara finishes, and perforated earcups for better ventilation during extended listening sessions. Klipsch positions the HP-3 as its most ambitious headphone design to date.
HP-2
Although the HP-2 sits below the flagship HP-3, it has its own strengths. The closed-back model combines a 40mm biocellulose midrange/bass driver with a 10mm dynamic tweeter, with a tuning intended to deliver deep, controlled bass. Its closed-back construction also provides passive noise isolation, making it potentially suitable for studio monitoring and listening in environments where outside noise is a concern.
The HP-2 targets listeners who prefer a more physical, visceral low-frequency presentation without excessive boom or loss of control. Its design emphasizes bass impact and isolation rather than the more open, spacious presentation of the HP-3.
Both models are designed to work with a wide range of listening setups from smartphones to high-end headphone amplifiers such as the 
Onkyo HA-70
 (coming soon).

The Bottom Line 
After nearly a year of trade show previews, Klipsch is finally ready to introduce its Stratos HP-2 and HP-3 wired headphones. Their unusual two-way coaxial driver designs, premium materials, and distinct closed-back and semi-open-back configurations help separate them from a crowded field dominated by conventional dynamic and planar magnetic headphones.
The Atlas-to-Stratos name change is interesting, but performance matters more than branding. Focal, Meze, HiFiMAN, Sennheiser, and Audeze have set a high bar, and Klipsch still needs to demonstrate that its new headphones can compete on sound quality, comfort, and value. Eighty years of loudspeaker heritage earns some attention, but not a free pass.

Klipsch Returns to Premium Headphones with the Stratos Series
Klipsch
November 13, 2026
Two wired models make their world premiere at CanJam Dallas, Nov. 14–15
INDIANAPOLIS, IN (November 13, 2026) 
— Klipsch continues its yearlong celebration of 80 legendary years, announcing its return to premium over-ear headphones with the new Stratos Series, which makes its world premiere at CanJam Dallas this weekend. The new Stratos HP-2 (closed-back) and Stratos HP-3 (semi-open back) headphones feature an innovative coaxial array driver design, premium materials, and enhanced comfort for longer listening sessions. The Stratos models represent Klipsch’s commitment to performance, craftsmanship, and exploration, inviting listeners to discover just how far the music can take them. 
“The Stratos Series brings together the best of everything we’ve learned, setting new standards in acoustic performance and materials design for Klipsch headphones,” said Ryan Hansen, Product Manager for Klipsch. “Refined styling and exceptional materials give the series a look and feel that fits naturally with our storied loudspeakers, while inviting new listeners to discover the Klipsch experience. The Stratos Series carries forward the philosophies that have guided us for 80 years, propelled by the same ambition that has always driven us: To bring you closer to the music you love, wherever you listen.”
The sound of Stratos headphones is based on Klipsch’s legendary principles of high efficiency, low distortion, and full-range sound. Each model features an innovative two-way coaxial driver array. The semi-open back design of the HP-3 features a 50mm bio-cellulose mid/bass driver and a 10mm dynamic tweeter that are paired to deliver warm, punchy bass and crisp, sparkly highs with low distortion and remarkable clarity. The HP-2 marries a 40mm bio-cellulose mid/bass driver to a 10mm dynamic tweeter to deliver deep bass, and its closed-back design offers excellent noise isolation for studio monitoring. Both models are designed to work with a wide range of listening setups from smartphones to high-end headphone amplifiers such as the new Onkyo HA-70.
The nostalgic yet contemporary style of the new Stratos Series embodies durability and luxury. The HP-2 and HP-3 are crafted from premium materials including machined aluminum for the yokes and contact points, real wood earcups (stained sapele), copper-ring accents, Klipsch Heritage Twitchell grille cloth, and a protein-leather headbands. Comfort is paramount—the Stratos headphones are designed for extended listening. The cushioned headbands are lined with open-cell foam and feature a cutout to prevent hotspots and relieve pressure on the crown of the head. The earpads are also wrapped in ultrasoft microsuede, creating an immersive seal around the ears that deeply envelops the listener in pure, clear sound. Convenient magnets also allow the earpads to be easily removed and reattached. 
Like previous Klipsch headphones, the Stratos Series was inspired by the earliest acoustic experiment of founder Paul W. Klipsch. In 1919, when he was just 15 years old, PWK conducted his first acoustic experiment using a small speaker housed in a pair of primitive headphones. This test created a lifelong passion for efficiency and dynamics that carries through every Klipsch product made today. More than a century later, Klipsch still has those very first headphones. They serve as a reminder of the fundamental scientific foundation and spirit of exploration by which Klipsch loudspeakers and headphones are designed and developed.
The Stratos HP-2 and HP-3 will be available in a rich Auburn finish. Both models are packaged with two detachable braided nylon cables: one 2.5m (3.5 mm unbalanced to dual mono) and one 1.37m (4.4mm balanced to dual mono). A 3.5mm female to 1/4" male adapter is also included. 
Both models will be available in the US in December with suggested retail prices of $999.99 (HP-2) and $1,199.99 (HP-3) from
 authorized Klipsch retailers

The optional new Klipsch Headphone Stand features a stunning Walnut veneer with copper accent and will also be available in December with a suggested retail price of $149.99.
