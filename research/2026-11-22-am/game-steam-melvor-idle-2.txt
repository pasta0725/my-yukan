Melvor Idle 2: A RuneScape Adventure is Available Now on PC in Steam Early Access 
New skills, a higher level cap and refreshed gameplay systems expand the million-selling idle RPG.
17th November, 2026, Cambridge, UK 
—
Jagex: the RuneScape company, and developer Games by Malcs today launched 
Melvor Idle 2: A RuneScape Adventure
 in Steam Early Access for PC. The sequel to Melvor Idle, which has sold over 1 million units, adds new skills, a higher level cap of 120 and refreshed gameplay systems
. 
Combining the satisfaction of incremental games with the familiar RPG systems inspired by 
RuneScape
 in a single-player experience, this accessible adventure invites players to skill up as they journey through the lands of Melvor. They’ll master and max interconnected skills from woodcutting, smithing, cooking, and farming, to combat and magic at a relaxing and engaging pace. They’ll explore dangerous dungeons and put their melee, ranged, and magic skills to the test as they engage in fierce monster and ferocious boss encounters. And with offline progression, the adventure continues even without playing.   
Melvor Idle 2
 brings a fresh, new look with updated graphics, audio, animations and more, along with plenty of expanded content. With the introduction of five new skills, bringing the skill count up to 29, players can uncover synergies and explore the skill Mastery system. Players can test their combat prowess and experiment with mechanics through the new Quest system as they uncover Melvor’s rich history. Additional game modes allow players, for the first time, to choose the level of challenge they can experience, allowing their stories to unfold with as much ease or adversity as they choose.   
Anna Mostyn-Williams, Senior Director of Business Development & Partnerships at Jagex said:   
"Melvor Idle struck a chord with players who love the satisfaction of steady progress, and it’s been brilliant to see its community grow. Brendan is a lifelong RuneScape fan, and as we celebrate 25 years of RuneScape, backing him to build Melvor Idle 2 feels like a perfect fit. He’s created something that feels unmistakably like an adventure in the RuneScape world, while still being very much its own game. We can’t wait for players to dive in and tell us what they think during Early Access.” 
Brendan Malcom, Founder, Games by Malcs said: 
“We made Melvor Idle for people who love planning, optimizing and watching their progress add up, and the community’s support has shaped everything in Melvor Idle 2. With five new skills, quests and new game modes, there’s more to discover than ever. Early Access is about building the rest of the adventure alongside the players, and we’re excited to get started.” 
Melvor Idle 2: A RuneScape Adventure 
is now available in Early Access for PC via 
Steam
 for £12.99 / $14.99 / €14.49. Players can save 10% for a limited time during launch week, until 24 November, 2026.
About Jagex
Headquartered in Cambridge, United Kingdom, Jagex: The RuneScape Company, is a leader in creating deep and engaging forever games that empower communities across PC and mobile. Our MMORPG franchise, RuneScape, is played around the world and has welcomed over 300 million player accounts.
As RuneScape enjoys its 25th year in 2026, we are accelerating our strategy to expand RuneScape into a broader ecosystem of forever games and connected experiences, grounded in a renewed commitment to integrity and fair play. This includes the reduction of monetisation mechanics, as well as the launch of RuneScape: Dragonwilds in 2026, representing a major step in building a multi-genre future for the RuneScape universe. Jagex: The RuneScape Company remains focused on building enduring worlds, empowering player-led innovation, and laying the foundations for the boldest era in RuneScape’s history.
For more information, visit 
www.jagex.com

Melvor Idle 2: A RuneScape Adventure launches in Steam Early Access with five new skills
Inven Global Team
desk@invenglobal.com
Nov 17, 2026
Add us on
Jagex and developer Games by Malcs have launched Melvor Idle 2: A RuneScape Adventure, a single-player idle role-playing game (RPG) sequel, in Early Access on PC via 
Steam
 today, November 17th.
According to a release, the game follows Melvor Idle, which the companies said has sold over 1m units, and adds new skills, a higher level cap of 120 and refreshed gameplay systems. The launch comes after the two companies 
announced their publishing partnership
 last month.
Idle games, also called incremental games, let characters keep gaining resources and experience with little direct input, often progressing while the game is closed. Melvor Idle 2 pairs that loop with RPG systems inspired by RuneScape, including offline progression.
The game is priced at £12.99, $14.99 and €14.49 in Early Access, with a 10% launch-week discount until November 24th. A new trailer accompanied the launch.
What is new in Melvor Idle 2
Players skill up across interconnected disciplines from woodcutting, smithing, cooking and farming to combat and magic, then take their melee, ranged and magic skills into dungeons and boss fights. The release says the sequel has updated graphics, audio and animations.
Five new skills bring the total to 29, and a Mastery system lets players look for synergies between them. A new Quest system covers combat challenges and Melvor’s history, while new game modes let players choose their level of challenge for the first time in the series.
Quotes from Jagex and Games by Malcs
“Melvor Idle struck a chord with players who love the satisfaction of steady progress, and it’s been brilliant to see its community grow,” said Anna Mostyn-Williams, Senior Director of Business Development & Partnerships at Jagex. She called Games by Malcs founder Brendan Malcom a lifelong RuneScape fan and said the sequel feels ‘unmistakably like an adventure in the RuneScape world’ while remaining ‘very much its own game’.
“Early Access is about building the rest of the adventure alongside the players, and we’re excited to get started,” said Malcom, Founder of Games by Malcs. He said the community’s support has shaped everything in Melvor Idle 2.
Jagex is headquartered in Cambridge, UK. According to the release, its RuneScape franchise has welcomed over 300 million player accounts, and the studio is marking the game’s 25th year in 2026. Jagex said it is expanding RuneScape into a broader ecosystem of ‘forever games’, including the launch of RuneScape: Dragonwilds in 2026, alongside a reduction of monetisation mechanics.

What the developers have to say:
Why Early Access?
					“Early Access is vital to the development of Melvor Idle 2. Built alongside the community, the game will be shaped alongside community feedback and in line with our public roadmap. It is an amazing gift to be able to incorporate community feedback during the development of a title like this.”
Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access?
					“It may take up a total of 12-18 months to complete all planned content for the full version game.”
How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version?
					“We plan for the full version to contain many new Skills, content and mechanics. Plus any extra features, changes or quality of life enhancements based on community feedback during the Early Access period.”
What is the current state of the Early Access version?
					“The game includes many of the planned & outlined Skills, mechanics and features, providing an experience that closely resembles the desired look, feel and design we are striving for. Most of the content listed in the About Us section is implemented, with the exception of some Skills, Combat content, and quality of life features yet to be released. We plan to release these during the early access period.”
Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access?
					“The price will remain the same upon full release.”
How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process?
					“The community is one of the most important aspects in developing a title for us. We will use feedback provided on all possible social platforms, as well as user-testing and surveys to gauge  what needs to be added, changed, fixed or yeeted from the game.”

About This Game
Embark on a brand new adventure in Melvor Idle 2!
Melvor Idle 2
 expands upon the beloved foundations of its predecessor and the wider world, to bring a deeper, richer, and more refined idle gaming experience. This 
single-player
 adventure lets you explore an ever-growing world of skills, combat, and strategy at your own pace.
Whether you're a veteran of idle games, a fan of the original Melvor or a maxxed out RuneScape completionist, 
Melvor Idle 2
 offers a fresh adventure that balances depth with accessibility. It's the ultimate idle RPG for both busy adventurers and those ready to immerse themselves in a constantly evolving world
Master Melvor’s many unique skills with just a click or a tap. Melvor Idle 2 is a feature-rich, idle/incremental game combining a distinctly RuneScape feel with a fresh gameplay experience. Maxing your skills has never been more zen! If you are looking for a deep but accessible adventure that easily fits around a busy lifestyle, then Melvor Idle 2 is an idle experience unlike any other.
Every skill in this game serves a purpose, interacting with the others in interesting ways. This means all the hard work you put into one skill will in turn benefit others. What strategy will you conjure to achieve maximum skillage?
It doesn’t just end with Woodcutting, Smithing, Cooking and Farming either – take your finely honed tapping abilities into battle and face off against monsters using your Melee, Ranged and Magic skills. Conquering brutal dungeons and toppling boisterous bosses has never been quite like this before…
In addition to the skills to level up, there’s Quests, offline progression, cloud save compatibility, an integrated Bank/Inventory system and more! And thanks to its regular updates, the adventure continues to grow all the time.
Do you have what it takes to conquer Melvor?
A Brand-New Look and Feel:
 Experience Melvor like never before with 
updated graphics
, enhanced visuals, animations, audio, and a modernized user interface, all designed to make your adventure even more immersive and engaging.
New Skills:
 Build upon the legacy Skills from the original game with 
newly introduced Skills
, adding greater variety and synergies to your journey.
Quests:
 Embark on engaging story-driven quests that uncover the rich history of Melvor that guide you through your journey. You will face powerful Quest Bosses, train unique Skilling content, and help save Melvor from devastation along the way.
Day/Night Cycle & Weather:
 Enjoy a more immersive experience with a customisable 
dynamic day/night cycle & weather system
 that adds visual flavour and atmosphere to your adventure.
Brand New Combat Mechanics: 
A familiar Combat system, now updated with new mechanics that increase strategic decision making.
Mastery Guilds - 
Dive into the 
Master Guilds
, a completely reimagined Mastery system for all skills, including Combat.
Current Action Tracking:
 See all information about your hourly rates based on your current action. This includes Skill XP/hr, items used & gained/hr, Combat stats/hr and everything in between.
Event Log:
 Tired of missing notifications for milestones, items, offline progression breakdown etc? You’ll now be able to view all notable events that occurred during your session.
Updated Bank & Equipment Management:
 Placeholders, keyboard shortcuts, item tagging, Bank Tab names, Equipment set names, updated equipping menu from Combat itself, and more is being introduced to streamline this overwhelming area of the game.

In-Depth Skill Progression:

Dynamic Combat System:
 Take on monsters, formidable bosses, and epic dungeons with new & refined Combat mechanics.
Robust Inventory System:

Cross-Platform Cloud Saving:

Regular Expansions:
