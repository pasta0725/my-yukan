Is Apple’s Black Friday offer worth waiting for?
Apple gives gift cards with selected purchases rather than lowering prices. It is worth waiting for Apple’s Black Friday if you are likely to want to spend more money at Apple because in some cases the value of the gift cards could be better than the discount you see elsewhere. 
However, the Black Friday period is one of the strongest periods for Apple deals, alongside Prime Day and other sales. We therefore recommend that you consider an early offer if it is close to that model’s previous low; stock and popular configurations can run out, and the biggest reductions often apply to older or clearance models.
In short: Compare retailer discounts with the value of Apple’s gift card before deciding. Treat the card as its full face value only if it covers spending you already planned, and compare the exact same model and configuration.
Apple’s Black Friday deal 2026 vs 2025
Apple’s Black Friday 2026 promotion is slightly more generous than last year’s, with gift cards worth up to $300/£240, a 20% increase on the maximum of $250/£200 offered in 2025. The biggest improvement is for the 16-inch MacBook Pro, while the 13-inch MacBook Air also qualifies for a larger gift card.
Apple has expanded the promotion to include the iPad Pro and Apple Watch Ultra 4, which were excluded last year. However, several products eligible in 2025, including the standard iPad, iPad mini and iMac, are missing from this year’s offer.
As in 2025, Apple has included some relatively recent releases. Last year’s promotion covered the Apple Watch Series 11, Apple Watch SE 3 and AirPods Pro 3, while this year’s includes the Apple Watch Series 12, Ultra 4 and AirPods 5. However, many of Apple’s newest products remain excluded.
For most comparable products, including the iPad Air, Apple Watch Series and AirPods Pro, gift-card values remain unchanged. Overall, Apple’s 2026 promotion offers better rewards on selected premium devices, but shoppers looking for other products may find that the deals are no better than last year.

 FAQ 
1.
Does Apple do a Black Friday sale?
Apple doesn’t typically reduce prices for Black Friday. We wish it would, but instead, Apple typically runs a Black Friday-to-Cyber Monday shopping event during which eligible purchases come with an Apple gift card.
2.
When do the Black Friday sales begin? 
Black Friday officially begins on the day after Thanksgiving, which in 2026 is Friday, November 27. However, the sales begin well before that–we saw deals starting at the beginning of November. The main event runs through the weekend and concludes on Cyber Monday, which is November 30.
3.
Should I wait for Cyber Monday to purchase?
While it’s always possible that a better deal is waiting around the corner, if you see a good deal, don’t wait. While Cyber Monday promises impressive deals there’s always the risk that the Apple device you want sells out and you miss out on the great deal everyone else got.
4.
Are Apple’s Black Friday gift cards worth it?
Apple’s gift-card offers give you extra value on an eligible purchase rather than an immediate price cut.
A key benefit is flexibility: you can put the card toward another Apple product or services and digital purchases, such as subscriptions and App Store purchases.
That flexibility is most valuable if you expect to spend more with Apple. A gift card doesn’t lower the price you pay for the qualifying item, and it may be less appealing if you don’t plan to buy anything else from Apple; competing retailers may offer direct discounts instead.
5.
Can I use the gift card toward the product I’m buying?
No. The gift card can only be used for future purchases. You can’t use the gift card to reduce the overall cost of the qualifying purchase.
6.
What can I buy with an Apple Gift Card?
An Apple Gift Card is remarkably versatile – it can be used to buy almost anything Apple sells, including hardware, services, subscriptions, and digital content.
This means you can use the balance toward:
Physical Apple products
: iPhones, iPads, Macs, Apple Watches, AirPods, HomePods, Apple TV, and accessories like the Apple Pencil or Magic Keyboard.
Apple services and subscriptions
: Apple Music, Apple TV+, iCloud storage, Apple Arcade, and Apple News+.
Digital content
: movies, music, games, apps, and in-app purchases from the App Store, iTunes Store, and Apple Books
The card can be redeemed to your Apple Account, and the balance never expires. It’s accepted at the Apple Store (online and in retail locations), the App Store, and across Apple’s digital storefronts.
7.
What happens to my Apple Black Friday gift card if I return the product?
If you return a product purchased during Apple’s Black Friday promotion, you’ll also need to return the accompanying gift card to receive a full refund. If you’ve already redeemed the gift card, or don’t return it, Apple will deduct its value from your refund.
For example, if you buy an eligible MacBook Pro with a $300 gift card and subsequently return the MacBook without returning the gift card, Apple will deduct $300 from the refund.
8.
