First of all, never mind how this thing works, get a load of how it 
looks!
 That said, the Borslon is in fact claimed to be an improvement on traditional gaming mice, with a radical articulated finger-worn design.
Currently the subject of a Kickstarter campaign, the Borslon is made by gaming electronics company Komodo Smart Technology. Along with being worn on the right-hand index finger (sorry, lefties), it can also be curled up and used handheld-style like a traditional mouse.
On the outside/back-end of the device are five programmable buttons that users can remap to create macros for commonly used commands.
Those buttons are pressed by the thumb, although some potential backers have expressed doubts as to how quick and easy that will be to do while gaming. It's also worth noting that one of the buttons has to be assigned to left-clicking, as that task is 
not
 performed by the fingertip (the user's finger pad sits above a motion sensor, which tracks the mouse's position).
The Borslon is made mainly of aluminum, and is claimed to tip the scales at approximately 36 g (1.27 oz) 
Komodo Smart Technology
Scrolling is handled by a thumb-operated optical scroll wheel, located on the next segment forward from the buttons. And yes, the mouse is hard-wired only, although the cable is routed over the top of the hand in an effort to keep it out of the way.
Specs-wise, the Borslon offers up to 26,000 DPI, an 8,000-Hz polling rate, a 300-IPS tracking speed, and 80G maximum acceleration. These parameters can be adjusted in a Windows, macOS or Android browser.
The various components of the mouse
Komodo Smart Technology
According to Komodo, the whole design makes for increased precision and control, by adapting to the user's finger movements as they play. At this point in time, the device is still a functional engineering prototype.
Should you be interested in getting a Borslon mouse when (and if) it reaches production, you can do so for a 
pledge of US$99
. The planned retail price is $158.

===== https://www.notebookcheck.net/New-unusual-finger-gaming-mouse-packs-8K-polling-26-000-DPI-and-weighs-just-36-g.1455174.0.html
(status 200)
Borslon is a new 
gaming mouse
 that straps onto the index finger, so the cursor follows that one finger rather than the whole hand. 
It’s on Kickstarter
 right now — the unique peripheral has already raised $37,935 from 385 backers against a $5,000 goal. The campaign ends on December 13.
Borslon wraps around the right index finger through a set of adjustable, articulated joints, while the sensor sits under the fingertip. Hence, moving that finger moves the cursor. Clicking works differently though. The fingertip doesn’t click by itself; one of the five programmable buttons has to be mapped to left-click. Those buttons are present along the back of the mouse for the thumb to press, alongside a scroll wheel with a TTC optical encoder. Interestingly, Borslon can also be curled up and held like a regular mouse.
ⓘ  Kickstarter
ⓘ  Kickstarter
While it might not look like a conventional gaming mouse, the specs are closer to a high-end one — on paper, at least. The campaign lists up to 26,000 DPI, an 8,000 Hz polling rate, 300 IPS tracking and 80G acceleration. It weighs 36 g and measures 10 x 3.2 cm. Borslon is wired, and the USB-C cable goes over the back of the hand, which might take some getting used to. DPI, polling rate and button mapping are changed in a browser, with 
Windows
, 
macOS
 and Android supported. The body is mainly plastic and CNC aluminum. Also, bad news for left-handed users — the Borslon is right-handed only.
Pledges start at $99 for the Super Early Bird tier, against a planned $158 retail price. An earlier $89 launch-day tier has already sold out. Shipping is charged separately after the campaign.
The unveil comes with your standard crowdfunding caveats. The team says it has a functional engineering prototype; mass production is slated for December 2026 to February 2027 and global shipping planned for February 2027. The campaign further admits that some of its lifestyle visuals were made with AI image tools.

===== https://www.eteknix.com/borslon-is-a-gaming-mouse-you-wear-on-your-index-finger/
(status 200)
There are some unusual projects out there, but BORSLON is one of the ones that has caught our attention the most recently. It is a 
gaming mouse
, but one that does not have the shape of a mouse. Instead, it is an articulated structure that you place your index finger into. It also includes a panel with buttons on one side.
The project has just arrived on 
Kickstarter
 and has already far exceeded its funding goal. Its creators, based in Louisville, Kentucky, were asking for 5,000 dollars and have already raised 40,968 dollars. So far, 415 people have backed it. The campaign remains open until December 13. To support it, the minimum contribution is 99 dollars.
As for its specifications, the sensor reaches 26,000 DPI, with tracking of 300 IPS and acceleration of 80G. Its polling rate, meaning how many times per second it reports its position to the computer, is 8,000 Hz. It also weighs 36 grams and measures 10 x 3.2 centimeters.
It Can Be Worn on Your Finger or Held in Your Hand
The BORSLON is not only designed to be worn on the index finger. It can also be held in the hand, with a grip that is more similar to that of a regular mouse. In addition, as shown in the images, it has five buttons. These are programmable and allow users to reassign functions or create macros. The scroll wheel, meanwhile, uses a TTC optical encoder.

Meanwhile, configuration is handled through a web browser, without installing any software. From there, users can adjust the DPI, polling rate and buttons on Windows, macOS and Android.
According to the project’s page, a working prototype already exists. Mass production is planned between December and February, with shipments scheduled for February 2027.
