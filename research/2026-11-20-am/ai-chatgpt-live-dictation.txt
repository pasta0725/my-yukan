ChatGPT app gains enhanced Live Dictation streaming without 10-minute limit
Zac Hall
 | Nov 18 2026 - 10:25 am PT																
								0 Comments							
ChatGPT dictation is getting a boost today. OpenAI has updated the speech-to-text feature with Live Dictation that improves both speed and reliability. The enhanced dictation update also removes the 10-minute limit.
“Dictation in ChatGPT is getting faster and more reliable,” OpenAI announced on X.
“With Live Dictation, your speech turns into text as you talk, so you spend less time waiting for your message to finish transcribing,” the company continued. 
Atty Eleti, who works on voice features at OpenAI, 
added
 that the 10-minute dictation limit has also been removed as part of the dictation streaming upgrade.
More details from today’s app update 
release notes
 below:
Dictation in ChatGPT is getting faster and more reliable.
With Live Dictation, your speech turns into text as you talk, so you spend less time waiting for your message to finish transcribing. Tap or click on the waveform to show or hide text as you go. We’ve also removed the 10-minute recording limit.
Live Dictation is rolling out globally to all ChatGPT plans on iOS, Android, web, and the desktop app.

OpenAI has rolled out an upgraded Live Dictation feature in ChatGPT, delivering faster, more reliable 
speech-to-text transcription
 and removing the previous 10-minute recording limit. The update is rolling out globally across all ChatGPT plans on iOS, Android, the web, and desktop apps.
Previously, users dictating longer prompts or notes in 
ChatGPT
 had to speak without seeing real-time text appear until processing finished, while running up against a hard 10-minute ceiling. Live Dictation changes that workflow by showing words on the screen as the user speaks. Seeing live transcription unfold makes it easier to catch misheard words on the fly, adjust phrasing mid-thought, or speak uninterrupted during extended brainstorming sessions, drafting, or multi-step instructions. 
The freedom to speak your mind is here
While built-in operating system dictation tools offer basic voice entry, Live Dictation works directly inside the ChatGPT interface, tightening the loop between speaking, reviewing text, and sending prompts.
Hatice Baran / Pexels
For writers, developers, and everyday users who prefer talking through complex ideas rather than typing them out, Live Dictation turns voice input into a continuous, real-time editing experience. By pairing faster transcription with unlimited session length, 
OpenAI
 makes hands-free interaction with ChatGPT noticeably smoother across every supported device.
Is this what cool kids call yapping?
The update also introduces a subtle interface control for those who prefer less visual distraction while speaking. Users can tap or click the audio waveform during dictation to toggle the live transcript visibility on or off on the fly.

OpenAI is rolling out ChatGPT Live Dictation, which turns your speech into text as you talk instead of waiting until you stop recording.
Tap or click the audio waveform to show or hide the transcript as you speak, then edit the text in the prompt box before sending. OpenAI has also removed the old 10-minute recording limit and hasn’t published a new maximum. OpenAI also says the feature is getting more reliable.
The rollout began on 18 November 2026 and covers every ChatGPT plan, including free, worldwide. OpenAI announced the update on X and in its release notes. It’s rolling out on the web, desktop app, iOS and Android, according to 
The Verge
, which reported it first.

Dictation isn’t Voice mode
Live Dictation replaces typing in the prompt box. You speak, review the text, and decide whether to send it. Voice mode is for a back-and-forth spoken conversation with ChatGPT.
There’s also a separate record mode for transcribing meetings and voice notes. It’s still in beta and limited to Plus, Enterprise, Edu, Business, and Pro workspaces.
