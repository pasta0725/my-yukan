A tense reunion drives the story
Netflix has unveiled its latest Indonesian original, the psychological thriller Secrets. The film follows Parni, who fled an abusive relationship sixteen years earlier, only for her former partner Jamal to reappear with a plan for revenge. The story unfolds from Parni's perspective as she fights to protect the life she has rebuilt for herself and her daughter.
Shifting power and trauma
Sha Ine Febriyanti and Abimana Aryasatya star as the central pair. Abimana said: "For Jamal, ownership is everything. Throughout the film, we see how he regards his partners as objects that belong to him." Sha Ine added: "I had to convey how trauma can remain embedded in a person’s body, even years after the experience. After experiencing trauma, Parni doesn’t move much because she is in a state of freeze. It’s as though everything is turned inward."
Director moves into thriller territory
Ravi Bharwani directs after his earlier films 27 Steps of May and Jermal. The project draws from Dewi Kharisma Michellia's short story Rahasia dari Kramat Tunggak and marks his first psychological thriller. A main trailer is now live, and the cast also includes Shenina Cinnamon and Ruth Marini.
Global drop on 3 December
Secrets arrives worldwide on Netflix on 3 December 2026. It joins a growing slate of Indonesian titles on the platform, including the action film that recently topped charts in multiple countries.
