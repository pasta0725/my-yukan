===== https://automaton-media.com/en/news/muv-luv-girls-garden-mellow-days-a-companion-game-where-you-unlock-interactions-with-meru-momozono-out-on-steam-with-10-launch-discount/
(status 200)
MUV-LUV GIRLS GARDEN Mellow Days
, a productivity companion and adventure game based on the Muv-Luv Girls Garden RPG, has been released for Windows (
Steam
). 
The game revolves around completing your daily tasks alongside Meru Momozono. Your bond with Meru grows deeper as your productivity increases, unlocking new conversations and story episodes to experience, including ASMR stories. 
MUV-LUV GIRLS GARDEN Mellow Days features a Pomodoro timer to keep track of your work and break sessions, as well as other helpful tools. You can choose from several pieces of music, backgrounds, and ambient sounds to set the mood, as well as change the time of day outside Meru’s room. Some items in her room can also be recolored, including her chair, night light, and bunny plushie. 
Those who purchase Mellow Days will receive a serial code that unlocks access to the exclusive “Peaceful Days” memory in the Muv-Luv Girls Garden mobile RPG. Though there was an issue that prevented serial codes from being issued to players, Desk Garden Project has since 
resolved the problem
 (those who encountered the problem are encouraged to generate another serial code from within Mellow Days). 
MUV-LUV GIRLS GARDEN Mellow Days is out now for Windows (
Steam
) with a 10% launch discount available until December 3. A free demo is also available.
