Netflix's 'Physical: Asia' to return for second season next year
Yonhap News Agency 
  /  2026-11-19 11:36:33
Add to Preferred Sources
This poster for Netflix's original reality show "Physical: Asia: Unbroken" is provided by Netflix. (PHOTO NOT FOR SALE)(Yonhap)
SEOUL, Nov. 19 (Yonhap) -- Netflix's original reality show "Physical: Asia" will return for a second season next year, the streaming service said Thursday.
The upcoming season, subtitled "Unbroken," is scheduled for release sometime next year, according to Netflix.
The show is a physical competition series in which contestants from across Asia compete in a nation-versus-nation format, representing their respective countries.
Producer Jang Ho-gi, who directed the first season, will return to helm the new installment.
The second season will feature a new setting and newly developed challenges, aiming to showcase the strategic elements and matchups unique to international team competitions, Netflix said.
"We plan to go beyond simply scaling up the production and deliver unpredictable contests," Jang said. "Viewers can look forward to the genuine stories of the athletes joining the competition for the first time in Season 2."
Meanwhile, the "Physical" franchise has expanded with previously released editions in Mexico and Italy. U.S., French and Swedish versions, as well as a second season of "Physical: Mexico," have also been confirmed.

Netflix's physical survival reality show "Physical: Asia" (2025) is set to return for a second season in 2027, the streaming platform said Thursday.
Titled "Physical: Asia Season 2 - Unbroken," the new installment will once again be produced by Jang Ho-gi, who also helmed the first season, alongside Kim Bo-hyun.
"Physical: Asia" is a spin-off of Netflix's hit "Physical: 100" franchise that introduced a nation-versus-nation competition format. The original "Physical: 100" features 100 contestants competing individually in a series of grueling physical challenges for the top prize.

“I want to sincerely thank our viewers for embracing the fierce passion and pride of athletes competing for their countries, as well as the incredible drama they created as teams pushing past individual limits,” Jang said in a press release.
The producer also teased that the new season will feature a larger scale and more challenging missions.
“For Season 2, we have designed an entirely different world from previous installments and developed quests capable of becoming new signatures for the 'Physical' series," 
Jang said. "Beyond simply expanding the scale, we aim to showcase fresh strategies, matchups, and unpredictable outcomes unique to a nation-versus-nation format. Please look forward to even more overwhelming battles that surpass Season 1, and the authentic stories of the formidable new competitors joining the arena.”
The participating countries have yet to be announced.
The first season of "Physical: Asia" premiered in October 2025 and featured contestants representing eight countries — Australia, the Philippines, Thailand, Korea, Mongolia, Japan, Indonesia and Turkey.
The "Physical" franchise has continued to expand internationally through local adaptations, including "Physical: 100 Mexico" and "Physical: 100 Italy." A second season of "Physical: 100 Mexico" has also been greenlit.
Additional adaptations have been confirmed for production in the United States, France and Sweden.
