Embededded Power Platform (EPP)
The Architecture Behind the Future of Power Density
Discover onsemi's Embedded Power Platform, which reimagines power delivery from the die to the system level. By bringing electrical, thermal, and mechanical optimization together within a single architecture. Designed for AI infrastructure, electric vehicles, charging infrastructure, renewable energy, and industrial automation applications.
EPP supports development of solutions for AI infrastructure, software-defined vehicles, and industrial automation, allowing engineers to integrate multiple semiconductor devices, power, control, and isolation functions within a common architecture, reducing development complexity and system footprint.
