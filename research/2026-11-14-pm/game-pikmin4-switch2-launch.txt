新モード“だれでもチャレンジ！ ダンドリ検定”とは？
　このモードでは、ステージ内のオタカラなどをいかに早く運びきれるかに挑戦することで、あなたの“ダンドリ力（りょく）”がわかります。
　効率よくオシゴトを進めるために上手にピクミンに指示しましょう。
練習して、本番にチャレンジ
　スコアが記録される検定本番が開催されるのは、毎週金、土、日となります。
　ステージの練習はいつでも何度でもできるので、練習を重ねてより早くオタカラを回収できるルートを発見しておくことが、高いスコアの獲得につながります。
検定結果に応じて、メダルを獲得
　検定本番のスコアに応じてメダルを獲得できます。
　メダルはステージごとに記録されていき、これを積み重ねていくことで、ユーザーアイコンなどのごホウビがもらえます。
全72ステージを順次配信
　ダンドリ検定のステージは全72ステージです。1年間、毎月6ステージずつの配信を予定しています。
　はじめてでも安心なやさしいステージもあるので、 だれでも気軽にチャレンジしてみてください。
※受検と検定ステージの受け取りには、インターネット接続が必要です。

Launch-day exam.
 Nintendo Japan’s 2 November demo post says that on 
12 November, launch day
, Dandori Academy exams are specially open even though it is a Thursday (normal exams run Friday to Sunday). That post is from Nintendo Japan; we have not seen the same note on US or UK pages.
Hardware and online.
 Nintendo Japan says this is Nintendo Switch 2 software and needs a Nintendo Switch 2 console. It also says an 
internet connection is required to sit Dandori Academy exams and to receive new exam stages
. Online play requires a paid Nintendo Switch Online membership.
Demo.
 Nintendo Japan’s launch post says the free demo is still available. It includes the opening story area, Sun-Speckled Terrace, plus Dandori Academy’s introductory stage and some exam stages, and you can sit the weekend exams in the demo too. Nintendo Japan’s 2 November demo post says demo save data carries over to the full game, and that Oatchi voice commands are not usable in the demo.
Pikmin Bloom tie-in.
 The launch post says sitting a Dandori Academy exam in the demo gives you a postcard in the Pikmin Bloom app. The same postcard can also be received in the full game by viewing the story’s exploration results from Day 2 onward for the first time; you cannot receive it twice.
Coming later.
 Nintendo Japan says that from December it plans to show example routes for earning Dandori Academy’s top “Master Medal” rating in the Nintendo Today! app.

Friday, Saturday, and Sunday

once per week

GameShare: the UK store says you can compete in Dandori Battles against another player using GameShare via local wireless or online via GameChat. It lists players as Single System (1–2), Local Wireless (1–2), and Online (1–2).

FAQ
Is Pikmin 4 Switch 2 Edition out?
Yes. Nintendo Japan’s official news post dated 12 November 2026 says it launched that day. The official Nintendo US store lists the game as in stock, and Nintendo’s official UK eShop price data lists it as on sale, both for 12 November 2026.
When is the Pikmin 4 Switch 2 release date?
Nintendo Japan, the official Nintendo US store, and the official Nintendo UK store all list 12 November 2026.
How much is Pikmin 4 Switch 2 Edition?
On our 12 November 2026 check, the official Nintendo US store listed $69.99 for the full game and $9.99 for the upgrade pack. Nintendo’s official UK eShop listed £58.99 and £7.99 for the upgrade pack. In Japan, Nintendo lists ¥7,500 (download) and ¥7,578 (package), including tax, and ¥1,000 for the upgrade pack.
Can I upgrade if I already own Pikmin 4 on Switch?
Yes. Official Nintendo pages say owners of Pikmin 4 for Nintendo Switch can purchase an upgrade pack to play the Switch 2 Edition.
What is Dandori Academy?
It is a new mode in the Switch 2 Edition. Nintendo says you collect treasure against the clock, sit exams on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday (once per week), and that 72 stages will release over time, six each month.
Does the Switch 2 Edition work on the original Switch?
The Switch 2 Edition features are playable only on Switch 2. Nintendo UK says that if the game card is used in a Nintendo Switch console, you play the original Switch version without the Switch 2-exclusive features.
What time does Pikmin 4 Switch 2 Edition unlock?
Official Nintendo Japan, US, and UK pages confirm the 12 November 2026 launch. The storefront pages we checked list the date, not a single global launch hour. As of this check (America/Denver), the US store already shows the game in stock and the UK eShop lists it as on sale.
Do I need Nintendo Switch Online for Dandori Academy?
Nintendo Japan says you need an internet connection to sit the weekly exams and receive new stages, and that practice works offline. A paid Nintendo Switch Online membership is needed for the scoreboard and some rewards, such as user icons. The Nintendo Japan pages we checked do not say a membership is needed to sit the exams themselves.
Can you play Dandori Battles single-player?
Yes. Nintendo’s Pikmin 4 support page says Dandori Battle Mode offers P1 vs CPU, as well as P1 vs P2 and P1 & P2 vs CPU.
How many Dandori Academy stages are there, and when do new ones arrive?
Nintendo Japan plans 72 stages over one year (6 stages × 12 drops), with new stages arriving on the first Monday of each month.
