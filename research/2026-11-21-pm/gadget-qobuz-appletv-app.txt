Independent music streaming service 
Qobuz
 has officially launched on 
Apple
 TV, and it’s bringing more than just hi-res music streams to the big screen.
Qobuz
 has reportedly designed this app for "living-room listening", with everything from its full catalogue of lossless quality tracks to its editorial picks and personalised recommendations based on your listening habits.
Qobuz curated playlists (which focus on human-led curation rather than algorithms) are also featured, as is the service's Qobuzissimes feature, which highlights new, up-and-coming songs and artists.

Thanks to expanded real estate that comes with viewing the service on a TV or projector, Qobuz presents album covers, tracklists, and credits side-by-side, meaning everything is now viewable at a glance.
Lyrics are presented on-screen and in sync with the track, meaning singing along is certainly possible (though we should stress, very much optional), and your Qobuz libraries (including tracks, albums, artists and playlists) will sync across devices, meaning any changes you make on the Apple TV app will reflect on your smartphone, tablet or computer.
Last, but by no means least, the app supports Qobuz Connect, meaning you can start playing music on a mobile device and then beam it to your Apple TV provided you're on the same wi-fi network.
What’s more, you can continue to control the music on your phone while the Apple TV handles playback.

The Qobuz app is available to download from the App Store now, although we should note that playback capabilities vary by model.
It's available on all Apple TVs that can run tvOS 26, with the 2022 and 2026 Apple TV 4K supporting playback up to 24-bit/192 kHz. The 2022 model is limited to 24-bit/48 kHz, which is a shame. Still, we're always pleased to see a new service join the Apple TV roster, especially when it focuses on high-quality music streaming.

Qobuz Finally Arrives on Apple TV After Hitting Android TV Last Year
Ashley King
November 18, 2026
Photo Credit: Qobuz
Independent French music streaming platform Qobuz announces the launch of its app on Apple TV, bringing its lossless quality audio and editorial selections to the big screen, designed for “living-room listening.”
On Wednesday, Qobuz, the independent French music streaming and download platform, announced the launch of its app on Apple TV, available on the App Store for compatible models. Designed for living-room listening, the app showcases Qobuz’s music for streaming and editorial content. The homepage has been created to bring together editorial picks, new releases, Albums of the Week, 
Qobuzissimes
, Ideal Discography selections, label pages, playlists, and radio, as well as personalized recommendations and mixes based on listening habits.
Album pages display credits and tracklists side by side, so users can see everything at a glance. Track lyrics appear during playback, scrolling line by line in time with the music, complete with translations. Subscribers’ libraries (albums, artists, playlists, and favorites) are synced across devices. With Qobuz Connect, users can control playback from their smartphone or tablet while Apple TV streams the music.
“Our subscribers have been regularly asking us for a Qobuz app on Apple TV. Now, they can enjoy their library, our team’s editorial selections, and Qobuz audio quality in their living room, within an interface designed for the big screen,” said Axel Destagnol, Chief Product Officer at Qobuz.
The app is rolling out across all countries where Qobuz is available and is compatible with Apple TV models from the fourth generation onward, running tvOS 26 or later. It supports audio playback up to 24-bit/192 kHz, with a 48 kHz limit on the second-generation Apple TV 4K. Qobuz’s catalog is entirely lossless, with audio quality ranging from CD to Hi-Res.
Founded in 2007, Qobuz is an independently owned music platform and a pioneer in high-quality sound. Available in 27 countries across Europe, the Americas, Oceania, and Japan, it is aimed at all those seeking a musical experience that remains true to the artists and the music they create. With more than 100 million tracks in lossless quality, Qobuz offers the richest Hi-Res catalog on the market.
In 2025, Qobuz became the 
first music platform to publish its average per-stream payout rate
 of $0.01873, officially verified. Since September 2026, it has used 
AI tagging to identify AI-generated content
 and continues to champion a fairer streaming model built on transparency regarding shared revenue and where music comes from. The platform was named one of TIME’s Best Inventions of 2026.

The continued expansion of Qobuz Connect, now integrated by more than 100 audio partners, lets listeners stream directly to compatible audio equipment from the Qobuz apps, while its Android TV app brings the catalog to the big screen. 
Designed for living-room listening, the app showcases Qobuz’s music for streaming and editorial content. With Qobuz Connect, users can control playback from their smartphone or tablet while Apple TV streams the music.
"Our subscribers have been regularly asking us for a Qobuz app on Apple TV. Now, they can enjoy their library, our team's editorial selections, and Qobuz audio quality in their living room, within an interface designed for the big screen,"

Compatibility and audio quality
