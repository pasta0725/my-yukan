Samsung’s upgraded sleep apnea detection is rolling out to Galaxy Watch and Galaxy Ring
Newer watches are first in line, but older watches aren't completely left behind either.

TL;DR
Samsung is rolling out an upgraded sleep apnea detection algorithm to the Galaxy Watch 9, Galaxy Watch Ultra 2, and the Galaxy Ring across the US and South Korea.
The feature tracks blood oxygen drops and breathing disruptions over two sleep sessions in a 10-day period, categorizing severity rather than giving a simple binary result.
The Galaxy Ring becomes the first commercial smart ring with regulatory clearance for sleep apnea tracking. Support for Galaxy Watch 7 and 8 will be coming later.
Samsung has begun rolling out 
upgraded sleep apnea detection
 to its wearable lineup, leading with its flagship smartwatches on your wrist and expanding down to your finger. The capability is now reaching users on the Galaxy Watch 9 and Galaxy Watch Ultra 2, alongside an expansion to the Galaxy Ring. This improved sleep apnea function refines how Samsung monitors overnight breathing disturbances, transforming passive tracking into more actionable health data.
The rollout follows regulatory clearances from the US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and South Korea’s Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS), as 
announced
 by Samsung. The approvals allow Samsung to push upgraded sleep apnea detection across both key markets via updates to the Samsung Health Monitor app.
Rather than offering only a binary assessment, the upgraded sleep apnea detection algorithm tracks overnight vitals over two sleep sessions within a 10-day evaluation window. It monitors relative blood oxygen drops and breathing disruptions to identify signs of moderate to severe obstructive sleep apnea, categorizing the severity of disruptions so users can then take all of this data to a physician.
While the Galaxy Watch 9 and Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 are first in line, older hardware will not be left behind. Samsung plans to bring the upgraded detection to earlier models, including the Galaxy Watch 7 and Galaxy Watch 8 families, in future firmware releases.
For Galaxy Ring owners, this rollout marks a notable milestone: it is the first commercial smart ring to gain regulatory authorization for sleep apnea tracking. Some people do not like to wear their smartwatches to bed, so having this feature available on a relatively lightweight and discreet ring makes long-term sleep tracking a lot more practical for everyday users.

Samsung revealed during Unpacked this summer that Sleep Apnea was headed to the Galaxy Ring, making it the "first and only" smart ring to feature it.
Samsung has been working to improve its Sleep Apnea detection while navigating the necessary government procedures. Today (Nov. 3), Samsung says the feature has been cleared.
In a 
Newsroom post
, Samsung announced that its "upgraded Sleep Apnea" detection software has cleared the FDA in the U.S. and the MFDS in South Korea. This is crucial because Samsung's Sleep Apnea software was cleared for the Galaxy Ring. According to the post, the company has become "the first company in the world to bring sleep apnea risk detection on a smart ring."
Regarding what's changed, Samsung says it's increased the level of depth of Sleep Apnea insights provided to users. In the Health app, users will see the level of breathing disruption experienced the night before. The company intends for this to help users better identify how severe their underlying condition could be before seeking medical attention.

TL;DR
Samsung has started rolling out its upgraded Sleep Apnea feature to the Galaxy Ring, Galaxy Watch 9, and Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 through the Samsung Health Monitor app.
The update adds deeper analysis of breathing disruptions, giving users more detailed sleep apnea risk insights than the earlier version.
It is FDA and MFDS approved, works with select Galaxy Watches and the Galaxy Ring, and needs two nights of sleep data within 10 days for users 18 and older.
Samsung has started rolling out its 
upgraded Sleep Apnea feature
 to the 
Galaxy Ring
, 
Galaxy Watch 9
, and 
Galaxy Watch Ultra 2
. Announced a few days ago, the update is arriving via the Samsung Health Monitor app, and several older Galaxy Watches will also get the feature in the near future.
This rollout makes Samsung the world's first company to bring a sleep apnea risk detection feature to a smart ring.
What is new with the upgraded Sleep Apnea feature?
Samsung has already received approval from the US FDA and South Korea’s Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS) for the upgraded feature. It builds upon Samsung's 
original Sleep Apnea feature
, which was the first of its kind to receive US FDA De Novo authorization.
It analyzes a user's sleep vitals in greater depth than the previous version. Instead of just providing a basic risk assessment, it offers insights into the levels of breathing disruptions detected. This allows users to better understand their condition and have more informed conversations with a healthcare professional.
Users who own both a Galaxy Ring and an eligible Galaxy Watch can choose between the two devices for sleep apnea detection, depending on which one they find more comfortable to wear while sleeping.
Which devices are eligible to get this feature?
The feature is currently available on Galaxy Watches running Wear OS 7.0 and One UI 9.0 Watch. It will expand to the Galaxy Watch 7, Galaxy Watch Ultra (2024 and 2025 versions), Galaxy Watch 8, and Galaxy Watch 8 Classic via a future software update.

Upgraded Sleep Apnea Feature Now Available on Galaxy Ring Following Regulatory Approvals From US FDA and Korea MFDS
                        Korea on November 3, 2026

Building on its US FDA clearance received in July 2026, the upgraded feature earns approval from Korea's MFDS — paving the way for expansion to more countries
Samsung Electronics today announced that its updated Sleep Apnea feature
1
 is now available on Galaxy Watch and Ring.
2
 Following United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA) clearance in July, the updated feature has also received approval from Korea’s Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS). With this update, Samsung becomes the first company in the world to bring sleep apnea risk detection on a smart ring.
The updated Sleep Apnea feature, available through the Samsung Health Monitor app, marks a significant leap forward from its predecessor. The enhanced algorithm analyzes a user’s vitals during sleep in greater depth and now provides insight into the level of breathing disruptions detected — going beyond the previous sleep apnea risk assessment to deliver more comprehensive, meaningful information that helps users better understand their condition, which can lead to productive conversations with healthcare professionals.
Most notably, Samsung is extending sleep apnea risk detection to a smart ring for the first time. Galaxy Ring’s comfortable, lightweight design makes it easy to wear through the night and convenient for sleep measurement, encouraging nightly wear. With the feature’s low data requirement, users can gain meaningful insight into their sleep health from as little as two nights of monitoring within a 10-day period. By offering the feature on both Galaxy Watch and Galaxy Ring, Samsung lets users monitor their sleep health in the way that best fits their lifestyle.
Samsung’s original Sleep Apnea feature previously received groundbreaking U.S. FDA De Novo authorization in February, 2024 — the first of its kind for a wearable device designed to detect signs of moderate to severe sleep apnea. Building on that clinically validated reliability, the updated feature has now also been approved by Korea’s MFDS last October. Samsung will continue to expand the feature’s availability so more people around the world can detect possible signs of sleep apnea and take proactive steps toward better sleep health. This underscores Samsung’s dedication to accessible preventive care, with sleep health serving as a fundamental pillar.
“Samsung has long worked to make sleep health a simple and seamless part of everyday life,” said Jongmin Choi, Head of Health R&D Group, Mobile eXperience (MX) Business, Samsung Electronics. “This approval from Korea’s MFDS, along with our introduction of the world’s first support for a smart ring, reflects our commitment to accessible innovation. We are giving people the tools to help spot possible early signs of sleep apnea and take charge of their wellbeing.”
The Sleep Apnea feature is an over-the-counter (OTC) software-only, mobile medical application operating on a compatible Samsung Galaxy Wearable and Phone. This feature is intended to detect signs of moderate to severe sleep apnea in the form of significant breathing disruptions in users 18 years and older, over a two-night monitoring period. It is intended for on-demand use and requires two nights of valid data within a 10-day period. This feature is not intended for users who have previously been diagnosed with sleep apnea. Users should not use this feature to replace traditional methods of diagnosis and treatment by a qualified clinician.
