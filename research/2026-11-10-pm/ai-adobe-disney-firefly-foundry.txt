Disney and Adobe have launched a multi-year strategic partnership that will see the two companies advance opportunities for creativity and storytelling through emerging technologies, including responsibly developed AI.
Under the partnership, the studio will collaborate with Adobe’s Firefly Foundry on “human-centered and artist-led” generative AI models tailored to its properties. These proprietary models are trained on unique world knowledge, such as character attributes, environments and visual styles, to generate commercially safe and production-ready outputs, while accelerating work and enabling the development of more creative projects. 
Walt Disney Imagineering Research & Development is already using Firefly Foundry to bring new ideas into the theme park development pipeline with greater speed, including a sketch-to-image model that transforms hand-drawn concepts into fully rendered 2D concept art, as well as a custom image model that generates on-brand, franchise-accurate creative assets.
“Adobe and The Walt Disney Company have had a decades-long collaboration, where Adobe’s creativity, productivity and customer experience orchestration tools have supported everything from Disney’s content creation pipelines and workflows to personalized marketing campaigns and digital experiences that come to life in the parks,” Adobe’s vice president of GenAI New Business Ventures Hannah Elsakr said in a statement. “Today’s announcement marks the next chapter of the relationship, where the two companies will build a new creative foundation that thoughtfully deploys AI as an accelerant for artistic vision, creativity and guest engagement.”
“Disney has always embraced innovation, and this alliance with Adobe builds on a longstanding relationship between two companies that share a deep commitment to creativity,” Disney Corporate Alliances SVP Becca Vodnoy added. “By bringing together Disney’s world-class storytellers and beloved brands with Adobe’s technology, we have an exciting opportunity to give our creative teams new ways to explore and develop their ideas and bring Disney stories and experiences to life for guests and fans around the world.”

In addition to early access to Adobe Firefly and Firefly Foundry, Disney will be given Adobe Premiere for video editing and Photoshop for digital imaging, as well as dedicated engineering and research resources, to support its production pipeline. 
The alliance will also see Adobe increase its presence across Disney’s media platforms to connect with audiences across entertainment and sports and work with Walt Disney World Resort, Disneyland Resort, Disney Cruise Line, ESPN Edge and StudioLAB through global marketing activations, industry events, thought leadership, customer storytelling and other strategic engagement opportunities.
Adobe will collaborate with Disney creatives and technologists to explore and expand internal creative workflows through StudioLab and work with the ESPN Edge Innovation Center to identify ways for AI to increase engagement with sports fans, shape how the network delivers content and bring its archive to life.   
The partnership will come to life at Adobe Summit, Adobe MAX and other major industry conferences. Co-innovation projects with StudioLAB and ESPN Edge are already underway, and additional activations and experiences will roll out over the alliance’s multi-year term.

アドビとディズニーが提携。自社IP学習の独自生成AIモデル構築へ
編集部：吹越友一
2026年11月10日 12:13
 　アドビとウォルト・ディズニー・カンパニーは11月10日、クリエイティビティカンファレンス「Adobe MAX 2026」において、複数年にわたる戦略的提携を発表した。 
 　今回の提携は、新たなテクノロジーと体験を通じて、クリエイティビティ、イノベーション、ストーリーテリングのさらなる発展を目指すもの。ディズニーランド・リゾート、ウォルト・ディズニー・ワールド・リゾート、ディズニー・クルーズ・ライン、ESPN Edge、StudioLABとの取り組みにおいて、グローバルなマーケティング活動や業界イベント、事例紹介などを通じ、両社のイノベーションを広く発信していく。 
 　アドビは公式AIイノベーションパートナーとして、クリエイター向け生成AI「Adobe Firefly」、企業向けカスタムモデル構築基盤「Adobe Firefly Foundry」、動画編集「Adobe Premiere」、画像処理「Adobe Photoshop」などの最新ツール群への早期アクセスを提供。専任のエンジニアリングおよび研究リソースを投入し、ディズニーの制作パイプラインを支援する。 
 　ディズニーはアドビと直接連携し、Adobe Firefly Foundryを活用して自社IPに合わせた人間中心かつアーティスト主導の生成AIモデルを構築する。キャラクターの属性や環境、ビジュアルスタイルといった固有の世界観を学習させ、商用利用に安全で制作現場でそのまま使える品質のコンテンツ生成と、クリエイティブ制作の高速化を図る。 
 　すでにウォルト・ディズニー・イマジニアリングの研究開発部門では導入が進んでおり、手描きのスケッチを完成度の高い2Dコンセプトアートへ変換する「スケッチから画像生成」機能などをテーマパーク開発パイプラインに取り入れ、初期アイデアの具現化を大幅に加速させているという。 
 　また、映画制作スタジオのイノベーションハブ「StudioLAB」での制作ワークフローの検証・拡充や、スポーツ専門チャンネル「ESPN Edge」におけるファンエンゲージメント強化およびアーカイブ映像の活用手法の探求など、共同イノベーションプロジェクトもすでに進行している。 
 　アドビはこの提携を通じ、ディズニーのメディアプラットフォーム全体におけるプレゼンスを高め、エンターテインメントとスポーツの両分野にわたるファンとのつながりを強化していく方針だ。

Adobe and The Walt Disney Company Announce Multi-Year Strategic Alliance to Support Disney Innovation with New AI-Powered Tools
Monday, November 9, 2026 6:00AM
Download the PDF
Adobe technology will support concept exploration in Disney’s innovation labs as well as parks and cruise development pipelines, using custom AI models and tools to help teams develop ideas more quickly, while protecting creators and their works. Building on the companies’ longstanding relationship, the alliance will also include global marketing activations, industry event collaborations, customer storytelling and more.
MIAMI BEACH, Fla. and BURBANK, Calif. — November 9, 2026
—Today at Adobe MAX—the world’s largest creativity conference— Adobe (Nasdaq: ADBE)—a global technology leader that unleashes creativity, productivity and customer experiences through innovative tools and platforms—and The Walt Disney Company have announced a multi-year strategic alliance to advance creativity, innovation and the art of storytelling through new technologies and experiences. Building on the companies’ longstanding relationship, the alliance enables Adobe to amplify their innovation stories with Walt Disney World® Resort, Disneyland® Resort, Disney Cruise Line, ESPN Edge and StudioLAB through global marketing activations, industry events, thought leadership, customer storytelling and other strategic engagement opportunities. In addition, this deeper collaboration will advance opportunities to explore emerging technologies, including responsibly developed AI, in support of Disney’s creative teams.
“Adobe and The Walt Disney Company have had a decades-long collaboration, where Adobe’s creativity, productivity and customer experience orchestration tools have supported everything from Disney’s content creation pipelines and workflows to personalized marketing campaigns and digital experiences that come to life in the parks,” said Hannah Elsakr, vice president, GenAI New Business Ventures, Adobe. “Today’s announcement marks the next chapter of the relationship, where the two companies will build a new creative foundation that thoughtfully deploys AI as an accelerant for artistic vision, creativity and guest engagement.”
“Disney has always embraced innovation, and this alliance with Adobe builds on a longstanding relationship between two companies that share a deep commitment to creativity,” said Becca Vodnoy, senior vice president, Corporate Alliances, The Walt Disney Company. “By bringing together Disney’s world-class storytellers and beloved brands with Adobe’s technology, we have an exciting opportunity to give our creative teams new ways to explore and develop their ideas and bring Disney stories and experiences to life for guests and fans around the world.”
For decades, Adobe has stood at the heart of creativity in media and entertainment, with technology innovations that have earned multiple Academy Awards for scientific and technical achievement. As an Official AI Innovation Partner of The Walt Disney Company, Adobe will provide early access to the latest AI, creative and media technologies. This includes 
Adobe Firefly
 (generative AI for creatives), 
Adobe Firefly Foundry
 (proprietary AI models), 
Adobe Premiere
 (video editing) and 
Adobe Photoshop
 (digital imaging)—along with dedicated engineering and research resources—to support the Disney production pipeline.
With Adobe Firefly Foundry, Disney will work directly with Adobe on generative AI models tailored to Disney’s properties that are human-centered and artist-led. Customized on Disney properties, these proprietary models are trained on unique world knowledge (character attributes, environments, visual styles) to generate commercially safe and production-ready outputs, while accelerating work and enabling the development of more creative projects.
Walt Disney Imagineering Research & Development is 
already using
 Adobe Firefly Foundry to bring new ideas into the theme park development pipeline with greater speed, including a sketch-to-image model that transforms hand-drawn concepts into fully rendered 2D concept art, as well as a custom image model that generates on-brand, franchise-accurate creative assets.
The Adobe and Disney alliance will include collaboration on emerging technology development across The Walt Disney Studios’ StudioLAB and ESPN Edge, expanding the deep history both companies have had around creativity and media:
StudioLAB
: As a collaborative innovation hub for The Walt Disney Studios, StudioLAB brings together creative and technical talent to prototype ideas and turn experiments into proven tools and new storytelling opportunities. Adobe joins the program as a StudioLAB Innovation Partner of The Walt Disney Studios, collaborating with Disney creatives and technologists to explore and expand internal creative workflows.
ESPN Edge
: The ESPN Edge Innovation Center was created to explore, test and implement emerging technologies that impact the art of sports storytelling. ESPN and Adobe will identify ways for AI to enhance the engagement with sports fans and shape how ESPN delivers content as well as new ways to bring ESPN’s archive to life.
Adobe will increase its presence across Disney’s media platforms as part of the alliance, to connect with audiences across entertainment and sports. The relationship is also expected to come to life at Adobe Summit, Adobe MAX and other major industry conferences. Co-innovation projects with StudioLAB and ESPN Edge are already underway, and additional activations and experiences will roll out over the alliance’s multi-year term.
