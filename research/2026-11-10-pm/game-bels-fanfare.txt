Bel’s Fanfare
An RPG inspired by the Zelda classics
Bel’s Fanfare 
is an 
immersive
RPG
 with exploration and combat inspired by the Zelda 3D classics
. You play as Bel, the new aura cleaner of the once-luxurious vessel 
The Witch of the Sea
.
Purify the rooms of this cruise ship inhabited by otherworldly beings and find a resolution to their spiritual traumas.
Inspired by the first Zelda 3D classics
, we present our most atmospheric adventure to date, brimming with exciting and fast-paced combat and promising narrative excellence at every moment of this heartful story.

GAMEPLAY FEATURES
Secrets within secrets
Explore the many (hidden) rooms of the Witch of the Sea, bask in its glorious past and uncover its (unsettling) past history. Every area is carefully crafted with attention to detail and a 
nostalgic touch
.
Meet friends, foes and anything in between
Immerse yourself into the lives of the magical beings that inhabit the 
Witch of the Sea
. Many will ask for your help, whether they deserve it or not.
Your choices matter
Push characters into a resolution of your choice. Experience its consequences as their stories unfold.
Battle encounteres

expressive battle arenas
 with surprises at every stage. Culminate the experience with
 boss battles
 set inside the spirit of passengers.
