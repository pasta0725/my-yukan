Fitbit Sense & Versa losing 3rd-party watch faces this month, existing downloads remain
Abner Li
 | Nov 9 2026 - 1:21 pm PT																
								1 Comment							
Google 
announced today
 that Fitbit Sense and Versa smartwatches will soon lose the ability to “install or update third-party apps and clocks.”
This change to the Fitbit App Gallery applies to the Versa, Versa 2, Versa 3, Versa 4, Sense, and Sense 2.
Existing “third-party apps or clock faces installed on your Fitbit device” will remain available, but you can no longer download new ones (or update them) starting November 28. As such, “if you choose to delete an app or clock face, or factory reset your device, you will no longer be able to re-install it.”
Instead, the Fitbit App Gallery will “exclusively feature apps and clock faces created by Fitbit and Google.”

The November 28 deadline also applies to third-party developers. Google will “no longer support uploading new apps and clock faces or updating existing ones.”
“Before then, you can download your existing projects or request their removal from the Fitbit App Gallery.”
“After this date, the Gallery Admin tools will be retired, and your content will remain accessible only to users who have already installed it.”
Google winding down Fitbit OS for smartwatches is not particularly surprising as the company has been all-in on Wear OS and the Pixel Watch. That said, the Sense 2 and Versa 4 should probably 
no longer be on sale
.

Google said
 it is closing the third-party Fitbit smartwatch ecosystem on November 28. Sense and Versa owners will still be able to use apps and clock faces they already have installed, but after that date they cannot download new ones or reinstall anything they remove.
This has been coming for a while, but Google has now put an actual end date on it. The Fitbit App Gallery will switch to apps and clock faces made only by Fitbit and Google, bringing an end to the third-party software ecosystem that was once a fairly important part of owning a Fitbit smartwatch.
Don’t delete anything you want to keep
The change applies to Fitbit Sense, Sense 2, Versa, Versa 2, Versa 3, Versa 4 and Versa Lite. Google also lists the recalled Fitbit Ionic, although there should not be many of those still in normal use.
Anything already installed can continue working after November 28. The important catch is what happens if you delete it. Once a third-party app or clock face is removed, there will be no way to download it again. The same problem applies after a factory reset, because the watch will no longer be able to retrieve that software from the gallery.
That makes the next couple of weeks fairly important for anyone still using an older Versa or Sense. If there is a third-party clock face or app you regularly use, this is the time to make sure it is installed and leave it alone.
Developers are being cut off at the same time. From November 28 they will no longer be able to upload new apps and clock faces or push updates to existing ones.
Sense 2 and Versa 4 were already halfway there
For Sense 2 and Versa 4 owners, this is slightly different because Google had already stripped out third-party app support when those watches launched. We covered that back in 2022 when 
Fitbit confirmed third-party apps were not coming to Sense 2 and Versa 4
.
Those two watches did retain third-party clock faces, though, and that door is now closing as well. Google still lets Sense and Versa owners keep up to five clock faces saved on the watch, so anyone using a third-party design they like would be wise not to remove it.
Older Versa and Sense models lose more because they supported proper third-party apps. Fitbit’s gallery once included things such as Strava, Starbucks, Uber, barcode tools and various utilities. Our old 
guide to the best Fitbit smartwatch apps
 gives a pretty good idea of how much broader the platform once was.
The App Gallery was never on the scale of Apple’s App Store or Wear OS, but it did make Fitbit’s smartwatches feel more expandable. That distinction has been fading for years as Google shifted its proper smartwatch ambitions toward Pixel Watch and Wear OS.
Another piece of Fitbit OS disappears
The timing is not especially surprising. Google has spent the past few years steadily moving away from Fitbit OS as a smartwatch platform, while Fitbit itself has moved back toward simpler health and fitness hardware.
There is also some history here. Google already removed third-party Fitbit apps and clock faces for users in the European Economic Area in 2024 because of regulatory requirements. What happens on November 28 takes that much further, effectively ending new third-party Fitbit smartwatch software everywhere.
For owners, nothing suddenly stops working on November 28. That is worth stressing. Existing third-party apps and clock faces remain on the watch and can continue to function. But there is now an expiry mechanism built into them. Delete one, reset the watch or eventually replace the device, and it is gone.

From 28 November 2026, 
Fitbit
 smartwatch owners can no longer install or update third-party apps and clock faces, and anything they delete after that date cannot be installed again.
Google says in a 
notice on its Google Health community forum
 that the Fitbit App Gallery will “exclusively feature apps and clock faces created by Fitbit and Google” from that date.
Third-party apps and clock faces already on a watch will keep working, according to 
Google’s help page
.
Deleting a third-party app or clock face, or factory resetting the watch, removes it for good because it cannot be reinstalled.
The help page lists Fitbit Sense, 
Sense 2
, Versa, Versa Lite, Versa 2, Versa 3, 
Versa 4
 and Ionic.
Developers lose the ability to upload or update apps and clock faces on the same date, when Google retires its Gallery Admin tools.
Google asks owners to update to version 5.12 of the Google Health app so that Fitbit and Google apps keep working as expected.
What it means for Google-Fitbit owners
Anyone who wants a particular third-party clock face or app should install it before 28 November, because it cannot be fetched afterwards. Owners in the European Economic Area lost third-party installs in June 2024, so this deadline only helps owners elsewhere. A factory reset after that date wipes every third-party item permanently, and a bug in one will remain unfixed because developers can no longer publish updates.
Versa 4 and Sense 2 launched without third-party apps, so owners of those two lose access to third-party clock faces only. Sense and Versa have had no new model since 2022. Owners who depend on third-party apps or clock faces will need to leave Fitbit: Pixel Watch on Android, Apple Watch on iPhone, or a Garmin with Connect IQ.
Fitbit Air and Fitbit Edge have no third-party apps, and Pixel Watch gets its apps from Google Play. I expect Fitbit’s watch software to stay in maintenance mode, which leaves Google Play as the only home for third-party apps on Google’s wearables. Fitbit developers would have to rebuild their apps for Wear OS to get there.
Quick answers
Will my third-party Fitbit clock faces stop working?
No. Apps and clock faces already installed keep working, but once you delete one or factory reset the watch you cannot install it again.
Which Fitbit watches are affected?
Google lists Fitbit Sense, Sense 2, Versa, Versa Lite, Versa 2, Versa 3, Versa 4 and Ionic. Fitbit trackers, including Edge and Air, are not on the list, and Pixel Watch uses Wear OS apps from Google Play.

Googleは2026年11月9日（米国時間）、
Fitbit Sense 2・Versa 4
などのスマートウォッチ向けアプリギャラリーを変更すると発表しました。
11月28日からは、Fitbit以外の開発者が作った文字盤とアプリを新しく入れることも、更新することもできなくなります。
すでに入れている文字盤とアプリは、11月28日以降もそのまま使えます。ただし、一度削除したり本体を初期化したりすると、入れ直すことはできません。お気に入りの文字盤を使っている人は、何が変わるのかを先に押さえておくと安心です。
11月28日からギャラリーはFitbit製とGoogle製だけに
Googleがサポートコミュニティに掲載した告知によると、変更の対象はFitbit Sense、Sense 2、Versa、Versa 2、Versa 3、Versa 4です。Googleのヘルプページでは、Versa LiteとIonicも対象の機種として挙げられています。
11月28日以降、ギャラリーに並ぶのはFitbitとGoogleが作ったアプリと文字盤だけになります。上の画面の「By Fitbit」にあるようなFitbit製の文字盤は、これまでどおり選んで入れられます。
なお、2022年に発売されたSense 2とVersa 4は、もともと他社製のアプリには対応していません。
入れ済みの文字盤は使えるが削除と初期化で戻せない
Googleのヘルプページでは、11月28日の時点で入っている他社製のアプリと文字盤は引き続き動作すると説明しています。一方で、アプリや文字盤を削除した場合と、本体を初期化した場合は再インストールできないとしています。
更新もできなくなるため、今後は開発者による不具合の修正や機能の追加も届きません。ギャラリーには有料の文字盤もありますが、有料で購入したものの扱いについて、告知に個別の説明はありません。
気に入っている他社製の文字盤があるなら、11月28日以降は削除や初期化を避けるのが確実です。ギャラリーで気になっていた他社製の文字盤があれば、期限の前に入れておく必要があります。
開発者は11月28日までにプロジェクトの保存を
アプリや文字盤を公開している開発者も、11月28日で新しい作品のアップロードと既存の作品の更新ができなくなります。それまでに、自分のプロジェクトをダウンロードして保存するか、ギャラリーからの削除を申請できます。
期限を過ぎると、ギャラリーを管理するツール（Gallery Admin）は終了します。公開していた作品は、すでにインストールした人だけが引き続き使える形になります。
欧州では2024年6月に先行して終了
他社製のアプリと文字盤の配信は、欧州経済領域（EEA）のユーザーに向けては2024年6月にすでに止まっていました。今回の変更で、それ以外の地域も同じ扱いになります。
