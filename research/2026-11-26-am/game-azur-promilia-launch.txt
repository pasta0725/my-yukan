アズールプロミリア よくあるご質問
『アズールプロミリア』は、2026年11月24日（火）16:00より事前ダウンロードを開始し、11月26日（木）11:00より正式サービスを開始する予定です。
対応プラットフォームはPC（Windows）、Android、iOSです。
正式サービス開始に先立ち、星臨者の皆さまが疑問に思われる点を、Q&A形式でまとめました。
なお、事前ダウンロードおよび正式サービス開始の日時は変更となる場合があります。最新情報は公式サイトのお知らせをご確認ください。
1．サービス開始について
Q：正式サービスはいつ開始されますか？
A：2026年11月26日（木）11:00（日本時間）より開始予定です。PC（Windows）、Android、iOSの各プラットフォームでお楽しみいただけます。
Q：PCとスマートフォンでプレイデータを共有できますか？
A：はい。PC、Android、iOS間でプレイデータを共有できます。同じアカウントでログインすることで、異なる端末でも続きからプレイできます。
Q：プレイに必要な動作環境を教えてください。
A：各プラットフォームの動作環境は以下のとおりです。
PC（Windows）
・OS：Windows 10（64bit）以降
・DirectX：11.1以降
・CPU：推奨 Intel Core i7-10700Kまたは同等以上の性能を持つCPU／最低 Intel Core i5-9400Fまたは同等以上の性能を持つCPU
・GPU：推奨 NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060または同等以上の性能を持つGPU／最低 NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060または同等以上の性能を持つGPU
・メモリ：推奨 16GB以上／最低 16GB
・ストレージの空き容量：120GB以上を推奨
Android
・OS：Android 10.0以降
・SoC：
推奨：Snapdragon 8 Gen 1、Dimensity 9000+、またはこれらと同等以上の性能を持つSoC
最低：Snapdragon 855、Dimensity 1000+、Kirin 990、またはこれらと同等以上の性能を持つSoC
・メモリ：推奨 8GB以上／最低 6GB
・ストレージの空き容量：55GB以上を推奨
iOS
・OS：iOS 15.0以降
・対応端末：推奨 iPhone 13 Pro以降／最低 iPhone 12 Pro
・ストレージの空き容量：55GB以上を推奨
※ PC版はSSDへのインストールを推奨します。
※ 最低動作環境を満たしていない端末では、動作の遅延やフレームレートの低下、発熱などが発生する場合があります。快適にプレイしていただくため、推奨動作環境を満たす端末でのプレイをおすすめします。
2．事前ダウンロードについて
Q：事前ダウンロードはいつ始まりますか？
A：2026年11月24日（火）16:00より、PC（Windows）、Android、iOSの各プラットフォームで開始予定です。ゲーム本体およびプレイに必要な追加データを事前にダウンロードできます。
Q：どこからダウンロードできますか？
A：PC版は『アズールプロミリア』公式サイトのダウンロードページからYostar公式ランチャーをダウンロード・インストールし、ランチャーを通じてゲーム本体をダウンロードしてください。
スマートフォン版は、Google Play（Android）またはApp Store（iOS）からダウンロードできます。
Q：ゲーム本体のインストール後、追加データのダウンロードは必要ですか？
A：はい。ゲーム本体のインストール完了後、ゲームを起動し、画面の案内に従って追加データをダウンロードしてください。
追加データの容量は、ゲーム内の表示をご確認ください。正式サービス開始後、スムーズにプレイできるよう、事前にダウンロードを完了しておくことをおすすめします。
Q：事前ダウンロードが完了すれば、すぐにプレイできますか？
A：いいえ。正式サービス開始前はゲームにログインできません。2026年11月26日（木）11:00に予定している正式サービス開始後に、プレイできます。
事前にダウンロードしたデータを再度ダウンロードする必要はありません。ただし、追加データが配信された場合は、画面の案内に従ってダウンロードしてください。

Q：事前登録特典はいつ受け取れますか？
A：正式サービス開始後、ゲーム内メールを通じて受け取れます。

改善しない場合は、通信環境をご確認のうえ、再度お試しください。それでも解決しない場合は、エラーの内容を添えてサポート窓口までお問い合わせください。

Official Launch on November 26, First Special Featured Banners, and New Costumes
Tushan Xiaoyu, a Wind and Light attribute Assault character, joins as the first special featured banner. ©Nexon
Azur Promilia will officially launch on November 26. Early in the stream, the world's theme song 'Starlight Step' played, followed by the reveal of the companion character lineup coming in the launch version. Tushan Xiaoyu was introduced as the first special featured character. She is an Assault character wielding Wind and Light attributes, dealing up to 5 stages of Wind attribute damage with her normal attacks. Her skills and sigils can be chained into combos, and using her ultimate triggers an explosion while renewing her empowered state.
Minamoto Chiyo, a Dark attribute character revealed as the second special featured banner ©Nexon
Minamoto Chiyo was then unveiled as the second special featured character. Having left her hometown to travel alone, Chiyo decides to accompany the Starborn after recognizing similar experiences in them. In-game, she is a Dark attribute character who swings a tachi while summoning demonic arms for co-op attacks. Using active skills or her ultimate builds up Night Blossom stacks, which are consumed on the 4th and 5th stages of her normal attack to continue the demonic arm combo. Dodging mid-normal attack will not interrupt the combo, and linking a heavy attack after the 5th stage or a skill speeds up charging while reducing incoming damage during the charge.
New costumes for four characters were also shown. Tushan Xiaoyu's 'Bunny Festa,' Minamoto Chiyo's 'Red Night Stealth,' Abbie's 'Feather Hymn,' and Chalet's 'Starry Night Pilgrimage' were presented in order. Chalet's outfit was prepared specifically for the Starlight Festival. Alongside these, the Starborn outfit 'Moonlit Dance' will also be released—a familiar look for players who participated in previous tests.
Appearance customization for the Starborn has also been enhanced. Saved color presets can be swapped freely, and more dyeable slots have been added to initial outfits. Players can adjust the color and material transparency of specific parts, as well as customize hair color, eyes, ears, and skin.
A photo mode system will also open. With Kibo and character placement features, players can take photos combining the Starborn, acquired characters, and Kibo. The outline rendering of character models has also been refined to produce more natural-looking photos.
©Nexon
Minamoto Chiyo's new costume 'Red Night Stealth' ©Nexon
Mystical Creatures to Accompany Your Adventure: Kibo
©Nexon
Kibo are mystical creatures found throughout the continent of Promilia. Even within the same species, they exhibit different forms depending on growth stage, environment, and attribute, which are categorized as Subspecies. Special individuals like Starlight Kibo also exist within the same species; however, Starlight variations are mostly visual and do not change their attribute or innate abilities. Separately, Shiny Kibo carrying a Shiny Factor were introduced. Certain characters have Exclusive Kibo, which can be acquired through related quests or character progression and display special interactions with that specific character.
The world of Kibo incorporates ecological behaviors tailored to each species' traits. From Kibo playing around to exploring surroundings or reacting to the environment, players can discover even more ecology details in the launch build.
A main method of acquiring Kibo is bonding with those encountered during exploration. Players can attempt to bond while moving or mounted, adjusted so the process does not interrupt exploration. Card quality affects success rates, and using special cards makes it easier to bond with desired Kibo or convert them into Starlight Kibo. Additionally, hatching Kibo eggs is another key acquisition path. Eggs can be earned through activity rewards or overworld exploration. Higher-quality eggs yield higher chances of hatching Kibo with superior stats, while specific eggs may hatch Starlight or Shiny variants.
Players can leap from a ground mount and transition into a flying mount mid-air. ©Nexon
In combat, Kibo also assist the Starborn. Different Kibo fulfill roles such as area-of-effect attacks, crowd control, or domain expansion. Players can also leap while riding a ground mount and transition into a flying mount in mid-air. The launch version will feature Kibo that lift and fly with the Starborn, as well as powerful flying Kibo that can be mounted once earned.
Furthermore, Kibo progression takes place through the Potential Training system. Base stats can be enhanced by assigning high-stat Kibo of the same name as partners, and raising a Kibo's growth tier also boosts the paired character's stats. Various Kibo costume styles have been prepared, and unique color variant Kibo will display their distinct palette even when wearing costumes.
Divine Beast Kibo Chrysha, Combat, and Social Content
©Nexon
Combat requires coordinating Spirits (Lingzi), equipment, and Kibo together. A gameplay demonstration was presented showing Chalet, Abbie, and Luruka challenging the Divine Beast Kibo Chrysha of the 'Golden Realm of Abundance.' Chrysha pressures the battlefield with attacks symbolizing gold and abundance, unleashing wider area attacks upon entering Phase 2. As certain crowd control statuses can incapacitate action, players counter by swapping characters to dodge.
For combat content, Daily Commissions for collecting materials lead the list. Daily Commissions can be tackled in multi-player parties, and clearing them with 3 stars unlocks auto-combat to lessen repetitive grind. Strong foes encountered during the journey are cataloged under separate Remnant content, playable solo or through matchmaking with other Starborn. Rotating high-difficulty content runs via permanent stages and rotating stages, with rotating stages available to challenge again upon reset. Additionally, the co-op raid content Crisis Suppression returns in the launch build with refined combat and social features. Damage meters are more precise, and joint cutscenes have been added where multiple teams launch coordinated combo attacks when a boss breaks posture. After battle, players can commend teammates or add friends on the results screen.
Kibo Battles are played by building a deck of 8 Kibo and deploying up to 3 simultaneously. ©Nexon
For social content, Fleece City opens first as a multiplayer area. Meeting the unlock criteria allows players to explore the zone solo or in groups, interact with other Starborn, or initiate Kibo Battles. Kibo Battles feature a streamlined process. Both sides construct a squad of 8 Kibo, select their starter, and begin; deployment points accumulate over time up to a maximum of 3 points. Up to 3 Kibo can be deployed simultaneously, requiring switches when deploying more. Defeated Kibo cannot be redeployed and deduct points, and depleting the opponent's total points wins the match. Friend invites, practice matches, and point-based competitive modes are available, with the Wood-attribute themed Fleece Gym opening as the first gym in the launch version.
From Housing to Exploration and the Observation System
©Nexon
Housing serves as both a resting place for the Starborn and a space for resource production. Efficiency can be boosted by placing production facilities, dispatching Kibo tailored to specific traits, and activating relevant nodes, while harvested crops can be traded for profit. Fulfilling regional orders increases local reputation and rewards product recipes. Weapon and armor crafting have been consolidated into one location, and clearing gear dungeons of a certain difficulty allows players to craft equipment dropped by that dungeon directly at the craft bench. Raising housing level unlocks higher-grade crops and increases production-related caps such as dispatchable Kibo, farm fields, and dining table limits.
After expanding housing, players can construct dormitories to invite companions. Exclusive dormitories are available for select characters, with those for Tushan Xiaoyu, Misha, and Minamoto Chiyo opening first in the launch version. In dormitories, casual attire and hidden interactions can be viewed, with more character-exclusive dorms and interactions planned for future updates.
In region exploration, Kibo act as puzzle-solving helpers. Utilizing Kibo of matching attributes can unlock blocked clues, and bonding with a Kibo increases the exploration progress of the area where it appears. Collecting other regional collectibles also raises exploration progress; reaching higher zone exploration tiers grants item rewards alongside permanent buffs such as increased bonding success rates and special Kibo appearance rates. Waypoint nodes have been added to simplify navigating large maps, unlocking automatically just by approaching them. Climbing mechanics that let players summon flying mounts mid-air after jumping make traversing obstacles easier, and new vehicles for touring the continent of Promilia have been introduced. The pause menu can be customized beyond the default screen to showcase owned Spirits (Lingzi), characters, costumes, or unlocked story CGs.
Tushan Xiaoyu's Destiny Observation 'Floral Bond' and Minamoto Chiyo's 'Moonlit Beauty' ©Nexon
The Observation system for acquiring characters and Spirits (Lingzi) was also revealed. Destiny Observation offers featured 5-star characters per version. The first-half banner features Tushan Xiaoyu's 'Floral Bond,' running from November 26 to December 17, while Minamoto Chiyo's 'Moonlit Beauty' will follow after the December 17 update through January 5, 2027. Destiny Observations share pity counts. If a 5-star character is not obtained in 70 consecutive pulls, the 5-star drop rate increases per pull thereafter: by +2% per pull from pull 71 to 80, and by +6% per pull from pull 81 to 89, guaranteeing the featured 5-star character on the 90th pull.
Bond Observation provides featured Spirits (Lingzi). During Tushan Xiaoyu's period, the Spirit 'Red Silhouette' is featured, while 'Iaido Echoes' is featured during Minamoto Chiyo's period. If a 5-star Spirit is not obtained in 60 consecutive pulls in Bond Observation, rates increase per pull: +2% per pull from pull 61 to 70, +6% per pull from pull 71 to 79, and a guaranteed 5-star Spirit on the 80th pull. Accumulated pity count carries over to future Bond Observations.
Standard banners Order Observation and Eternity Observation will open on launch day, sharing the currency 'Star Memories.' Order Observation contains 10 standard 5-star characters; if a 5-star character is not obtained in 70 consecutive pulls, rates increase per pull, guaranteeing a 5-star on the 90th pull. Reaching a cumulative total of 50 pulls for the first time allows players to select one standard 5-star character, with additional selector opportunities granted for every 200 cumulative pulls thereafter. Eternity Observation guarantees a standard 5-star Spirit within 80 pulls, and selecting a desired standard 5-star Spirit ensures that chosen Spirit appears whenever a 5-star Spirit is pulled.
Meanwhile, Leap Observation was introduced for new Starborn. Leap Observation uses Star Memories but is available only in 10-pull bundles, costing just 8 Star Memories per 10-pull. It guarantees a 5-star character within 30 pulls, after which Leap Observation ends. Pull counts and pity counters for Leap Observation are calculated separately and are not shared with other Observations.
