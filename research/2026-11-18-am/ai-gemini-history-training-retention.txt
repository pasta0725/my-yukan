For years, AI chat history came as a package deal. You got the convenience, and the big AI companies got the data. Well, it looks like 
Google
 is finally unbundling that.
Google has announced that the 
Gemini app
 is splitting its Keep Activity setting into two controls, one for chat history and one for AI training (via 
Google
).
Google
What changes in the Gemini app?
Until now, the old Keep Activity setting was a single switch that was doing two jobs. First, it decided how long Gemini saved your chats. Second, whether Google could use them to train its 
generative AI models
. 
Recommended Videos
It wasn’t just prompts or responses; it also included photos, files, audio, location info, and data collected from connected Workspace apps. 
Those who asked for their history had to agree to let Google train its AI models on their data with no other option. That compensation is ending now. The new “History” section now controls how long Google saves your chats. 
Then there’s the second setting that decides whether your chat content, text and attachments included, can train those models. Basically, you can keep history enabled (chats are deleted after 18 months by default) and skip training. 
So you can keep your history and skip training
.
If Keep Activity is already off, both new settings start off as well.

What’s the catch if I turn History off?
There’s a big one. If you turn History off, Google now keeps your chats for around two months instead of up to 72 hours. You see? It’s 60 days versus three days. During that window, Google uses the information to personalize the responses and process feedback.
There’s another wrinkle, too. Even with History off, sending feedback on a response makes Google collect the last 24 hours of your chats, along with any attachments. 
The rollout is global over the coming weeks. Google is already emailing users. In my experience, menus rarely match the email. I’d suggest reopening your Gemini activity page when yours lands and setting History and training separately.
