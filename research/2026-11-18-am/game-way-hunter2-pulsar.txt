　THQ Nordic Japanは，オープンワールド狩猟ゲーム
「Way of the Hunter 2」
（
PC
 / 
PS5
 / 
XBOX Series X|S
）の
無料DLC「Pulsar Pack」
の配信を開始した。あわせて，追加される装備を紹介するトレイラーも公開されている。
　本作は，北米とアフリカの広大な自然を舞台に，野生動物の痕跡をたどりながら狩猟を楽しむ作品だ。猟犬と協力して獲物を追跡したり，銃弾カメラを使って射撃の結果を分析したりできる。
　今回のDLCでは，
サーマルイメージング
（物体の熱を映像化する技術）や
ナイトビジョン
（暗視機能）に対応した
5種類の光学機器
が登場する。
　収録されるのは，レーザー距離計を搭載したサーマルライフルスコープ
「Thermion 3 LRF XT60」
，ゲーム序盤から使用できる
「Thermion 2 XP50 PRO」
，通常のスコープに装着する
「Krypton 2 FXG50」
などだ。
　さらに，サーマル映像，暗視映像，昼間のカラー映像を切り替えられる双眼鏡
「Symbion LRF DXT50」
と，サーマル単眼鏡
「Telos XP50」
も追加される。これらの装備を使うことで，暗い場所でも野生動物を発見・観察しやすくなる。
　なお，
PS5パッケージ版は11月26日に発売予定
で，価格は5170円（税込）。現在，全国のゲーム取扱店やオンラインショップで予約を受け付けている。

November	15, 2026
Five premium optics. One free DLC. Pulsar has arrived in Way of the Hunter 2. Update 1.5 is out on all platforms.
Update 1.5 and the Pulsar Pack are out now on PC (Steam), Xbox Series X|S and PS5. Track wildlife more effectively, scan vast landscapes with greater confidence, and extend your awareness from first light to deep night with equipment inspired by one of the world’s leading manufacturers of thermal and night vision optics.
The Pulsar Pack is available now and for free in Way of the Hunter 2.
The Pulsar Pack includes:
Thermion 3 LRF XT60: A flagship thermal riflescope combining high-definition imaging, a wide field of view, and integrated rangefinding capabilities
Thermion 2 XP50 PRO: A high-end thermal riflescope featuring multiple image modes and up to 16x magnification, available early in the game
Krypton 2 FXG50: A compact thermal clip-on attachment that transforms daytime optics into thermal imaging riflescopes
Symbion LRF DXT50: Multispectral binoculars combining thermal imaging, night vision, full-color daytime imaging, and an integrated laser rangefinder
Telos XP50: A premium thermal imaging monocular offering multiple image modes and excellent thermal sensitivity for challenging conditions
The Pulsar Pack is part of the Season One Season Pass, which includes a total of 8 DLC packs. Two of them are free and available to the entire community, and the Pulsar Pack is one of them.
Update 1.5 is out now
Update 1.5 and the Pulsar Pack are out now on PC (Steam), Xbox Series X|S and PS5.
Along with the Pulsar Pack, Update 1.5 is also out for all players on all platforms. It focuses on tweaks and fixes, and we wanted to get what we have into your hands as soon as possible. Some of the highlights:
Auto-walk now works when using a controller (double-click the right analog stick)
Trophy scores at maximum age were adjusted to match fitness, so you should no longer see high fitness animals that never reach 5 stars
Leaves on bushes and trees no longer shake or flicker unnaturally
Improved rock collision and movement on steep terrain, so you are less likely to get stuck
Hunting map progress now carries over to multiplayer sessions correctly (discovered lodges, cabins, animal zones)
Improved shotgun pellet spread and further increased .45-70 ammo penetration, based on your feedback
Crash fixes, improved performance while driving in vehicles, and more
We see your reports about performance, and we’re not done yet. We plan to tweak it even further with upcoming updates. Thank you to everyone who has reported it! For more info about version 1.5, please check the patch notes below.

 says both are out “on all platforms,” which for this game means PC, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
How to Get the Free Pulsar Pack
The pack is one of eight in the Season One Season Pass and one of two that Nine Rocks says are free for everyone, so you do not need the pass or the Deluxe Edition.
Steam:
 open the 
Pulsar Pack store page
, which lists it as free, add it to your account, then let Steam download it.
PS5 and Xbox Series X|S:
 look for it with the game’s add-ons in the PlayStation Store or the Microsoft Store.
What Is in the Pulsar Pack
Five pieces of gear inspired by real Pulsar optics, shown in THQ Nordic’s 
Pulsar Pack trailer
:
Thermion 3 LRF XT60:
 the flagship thermal riflescope, with a wide field of view and a built-in rangefinder.
Thermion 2 XP50 Pro:
 a thermal riflescope with several image modes and up to 16x magnification, which THQ Nordic says is available early in the game.
Krypton 2 FXG50:
 a thermal clip-on that turns daytime optics into thermal riflescopes.
Symbion LRF DXT50:
 binoculars with thermal, night vision and daytime imaging plus a laser rangefinder.
Telos XP50:
 a thermal monocular with several image modes.
What Update 1.5 Changes
Nine Rocks calls 1.5 a tweaks-and-fixes update. The highlights:
Auto-walk now works on a controller: double-click the right analog stick.
Trophy scores at maximum age now match fitness, so high-fitness animals should no longer fall short of 5 stars.
The .45-70 gets another “significant” penetration buff, shotgun pellet spread improves, and expandable and fixed-blade broadheads are rebalanced.
Suppressors’ recoil scale goes from 3 to 5 options, and non-typical Mule Deer spawn less often.
Hunting map progress (lodges, cabins and animal zones) now carries over to multiplayer, and several crashes that hit players in someone else’s session are fixed.
Better movement on steep, rocky ground and smoother driving.
The studio says it is “not done yet” with performance, and its known issues list is on 
Nine Rocks Games’ site
.
