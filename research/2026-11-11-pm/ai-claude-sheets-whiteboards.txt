Claude Dashboards

 becomes generally available as Claude Data. Whiteboards is a shared board where your team brainstorms with Claude, Sheets is a spreadsheet Claude builds and edits with you, and Claude Data adds data stories, certified queries, and live sharing. These are available on all paid plans.
Working with Claude on a project often meant moving the output over to another tool or program, where Claude didn’t have live access to follow progress and changes. Now, your documents, boards, sheets, and dashboards can stay in Claude, with access for your team to continue editing together. When ready, you can bring the outputs directly into tools from our partners and continue working there.
Claude Whiteboards
Many people use Claude as a thought partner, but brainstorming with it has typically meant copy-pasting the results elsewhere so you can then start brainstorming or collaborating with your coworkers. Now, you can talk through a messy plan out loud with Claude, and Claude can turn it into a board with a timeline with owners and dates, so you and your team can collaborate in real time. In a retro meeting, for example, everyone can add their notes, and Claude can group them by theme and draft the action items for each person. You can focus on any part of the board by dragging a box around it and asking Claude questions. When you’re done brainstorming, Claude can turn the board into a doc or deck, or bring it into tools like Lucid, Miro, or Vani by Zoho, where you can continue working on it with your team.
Claude Sheets
Claude can now generate spreadsheets with tabs, formulas, formatting, and drop-downs, often from just a prompt like asking for a campaign tracker or a hiring budget. It can edit in the cells, outlining each new edit so you can track changes. You can share sheets with your team, so you and Claude can work on them together.
For now, sheets have simple spreadsheet functionality, but we are working on adding more. When you need the full functionality of Excel, you can export your file to .xlsx or CSV, or use 
Claude for Excel
 (opens in new tab)
. You can also continue working in partner tools including Airtable, Asana, monday.com, and Smartsheet.
Claude Data
Claude Dashboards
 (opens in new tab)
 launched in beta in October, enabling teams to ask quick questions of their company’s data in tools like Amazon Redshift, BigQuery, and ClickHouse. Starting today, it’s generally available as Claude Data. We changed the name because the way companies use their data is broader than dashboards, and the product now covers how they work more broadly. Certified queries and live sharing are in beta. Here are some ways teams can use Claude Data:
Create certified definitions of metrics. 
Your data team can certify the queries everyone should use, so you don’t end up building or working on dashboards with conflicting data. Charts built on these metrics carry a "certified" badge showing they’re certified.
Craft stories
from data.
 You can work with Claude in Claude Data to draft data stories or narrated videos from your metrics, for you to review before sharing.
Share current numbers with
everyone.
 With live sharing, your colleagues can see up-to-date numbers when they open a shared dashboard, even if they don’t have access to the connectors that keep that data current. Viewers see the results of the queries you’ve approved, and can’t run others with your access.
Match your look-and-feel. 
Design systems are now supported in Claude Data.
Get faster loading with caching. 
Previously, every dashboard would reload on every load. Now, dashboards support caching, and refresh cadences can be set on a per-dashboard basis.
Track changes over time. 
Claude Data now has tracked changes, so you can see who has made edits and roll back to a specific version if needed.
Our partners for Claude Data include Amplitude, Mixpanel, Omni, PostHog, and Sigma.
Work in your other tools, too

interactive connectors

Claude Whiteboards, Sheets, and Data work on web, desktop, and mobile. Claude Data needs a data connector, such as a warehouse or CRM.

Anthropic announced new workplace features on Tuesday, including Claude Meetings, which brings Claude into video calls to begin working on tasks while participants are still talking. The company also unveiled Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets.
The launch of Anthropic’s new productivity-focused features comes just a few weeks after 
OpenAI unveiled a series of workplace tools
 that allow users to collaborate with ChatGPT to do things like visualize company information and create presentations, as both companies compete to turn their products into office suites of sorts to take on Microsoft and Google. Meta is also joining the competition with its Muse AI agent, which can 
build mini-apps on the fly
 to help users complete specific tasks.
With Claude Meetings, teams can add Claude to a video call as a visible participant. Claude then follows the conversation and gets to work on tasks in real time. As participants talk, it can draft the plan being discussed, map out a timeline on a whiteboard, answer questions in the meeting chat, and access connected work apps to retrieve information, such as last year’s figures. After the call, the meeting notes, action items, and other materials Claude created are available in claude.ai.
The work that comes out of a meeting usually starts after everyone hangs up, but with this new feature, teams can turn discussions into actions in real time, Anthropic says.
Claude identifies itself as a meeting participant and posts a notice in the meeting chat. It won’t join calls with external guests unless authorized by an admin, and will automatically leave if an external guest joins. 
Claude Meetings is launching in beta on Team and Enterprise plans. At launch, it works with Google Meet, with support for additional plans expected to follow.
Image Credits:
Anthropic
As for Claude Whiteboards, Anthropic describes the feature as a collaborative board where teams can talk through a plan and have Claude turn it into an organized visual workspace with timelines and deadlines.
The company notes that many people already brainstorm with Claude, but that in order to share their results with others, they would have to copy-paste them somewhere else. Now, a team can work directly with Claude on the same board in real time.
When the brainstorm is done, Claude can turn the board into a doc or a slide deck. Additionally, Whiteboards also work inside Claude Meetings, so the board builds while people talk, Anthropic says. 
Claude Whiteboards is available in beta on paid plans, on web, desktop, and mobile.
The company also launched Claude Sheets, which lets teams create and collaboratively edit spreadsheets with Claude. A team can ask it to build a campaign tracker or hiring budget, and Claude will then generate a working spreadsheet.

Anthropic says Claude handles the initial setup of trackers and budgets, while allowing teams to continue refining and updating the same spreadsheet. Each spreadsheet can include multiple tabs, formulas, formatting, and dropdown menus. Claude also makes edits directly in cells alongside the team, and outlines each change so users can see what it did.
Claude Sheets is available in beta on paid plans, on web, desktop and mobile.
Anthropic also announced that Claude Data, formerly known as Claude Dashboards, is now generally available. The feature connects Claude to systems that companies use to keep their numbers, like a data warehouse or CRM. Users can ask questions, and Claude responds with dashboards, written data stories, or narrated videos.
Additionally, the company announced interactive connectors that open the full version of apps like Adobe, Atlassian, and HubSpot inside Claude. Anthropic notes that teams can now open these apps directly alongside their Claude conversations and ask the assistant to work within them, essentially bringing existing workflows into the same workspace.

Anthropic is significantly expanding Claude's 
suite of productivity tools
. At an event in San Francisco today, the company announced Claude Sheets. As you can probably guess, Sheets allows Claude to generate and edit spreadsheets for you. Each file can include its own set of tabs and formulas. You'll also find the usual assortment of formatting options.
Anthropic is billing Sheets as collaborative tool you can use alongside both Claude and your coworkers. Everyone with editing access can make changes to the file, and any tweaks Claude makes will be noted for you and your coworkers to track. You can export any files Claude generates to 
Excel
, and there's even support for Anthropic's Google Workspace plugin. Sheets is currently available in beta to Claude subscribers across the web, desktop and mobile.
Alongside Claude Sheets, Anthropic announced two other new products: Claude Meetings and Claude Whiteboards. The first of those is Anthropic's take on a meeting agent like the kind 
offered by Otter.ai
 and other companies. Available in Google Meet to start (which, incidentally, offers its own meeting agent through Gemini), Claude can join your video calls and start taking notes as you and your colleagues talk. You can later chat with Claude about any plans or action items that came up during your call. "Claude marks itself as a participant and posts a notice in the meeting's chat," says Anthropic. "It won't join a call with outside guests unless an admin allows it, and it leaves if one joins anyway." At the moment, Claude Meetings is only available to Team and Enterprise customers.
Moving to Claude Whiteboards, it's a pretty cutesy take on a collaborative brainstorming app. As your team works together, Claude will group everyone's ideas together by theme and draft action items for each individual. In a nifty bit of integration, you can use Claude Meetings and Whiteboards at the same time. Anthropic is billing the tool as a natural extension of how people already use Claude. "Many people already brainstorm with Claude, but sharing the results with coworkers has meant copy-pasting them somewhere else," the company says. Claude Whiteboards is available in beta to all paid users across the web, desktop and mobile.
Separately from those announcements, Anthropic is renaming Claude Dashboards to Claude Data and starting to roll out the tool to all paid users. Anthropic says it decided to rename the app because "companies use their data for more than dashboards." In any case, you can connect Claude Data to your company's CRM so that the chatbot can then answer specific questions you might have about what's inside.
Lastly, Anthropic is upgrading Claude's plugin functionality, which allows you to connect apps like 
Canva
 and Notion to the chatbot so it can pull information from those services. As Anthropic notes, there are already more than 9,000 connectors offered through the Claude Marketplace, but now you can open full apps inside of Claude, allowing the chatbot to act on what it sees in the window. Anthropic is rolling out this update starting with a small group of partners that includes Adobe, Atlassian and HubSpot.
