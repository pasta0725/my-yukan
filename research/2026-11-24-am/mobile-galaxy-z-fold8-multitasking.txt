Galaxy Z Fold 8 features: What you can do on a foldable
Sponsored Post
 | Nov 21 2026 - 9:00 am PT

Folding phones have come in numerous variations over the years as the concept has evolved. The Galaxy Z Fold 8 is the latest evolution that introduces a form factor that fits every user. Its unique design makes it a foldable most people can actually use – which makes it a perfect gift. You might be surprised by what it can do.
This post is sponsored by Samsung.
Unfold for a bigger canvas
It’s in the name. The 
Galaxy Z Fold 8
 folds and unfolds like other foldable devices – that much you know. But what the Galaxy Z Fold 8’s form factor provides is something different to what most might be familiar with.
Instead of a tall device that unfolds into a near-uniform square display, you get a wider screen more like a tablet. That makes it 
perfect
 for watching videos or movies. Content that naturally works well on TVs or your computer will look great on the Galaxy Z Fold 8.

What about content that’s best viewed in portrait mode? Because it opens up to a tablet-like display, you can turn the Fold 8 to watch YouTube Shorts or Instagram Reels without a problem, so if you or someone you know likes to watch content on a bigger display, the Z Fold 8 makes that much easier. It really doesn’t matter what content you come across – the wider inner display gives you that large screen feeling.
Fold for a minimal display
Like a well-designed tool, the Galaxy Z Fold 8 has more purposes than the tasks best suited for the inner display. That shorter, wider profile makes the outer display smaller, but not as thin as something on other foldables, like the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra.
In reality, the aspect ratio is similar enough across both the inner and outer screens that most content scales well on the shorter front panel. Social apps like X and Instagram look great because videos and images are able to fill the screen, rather than squeeze. Samsung’s One UI software also supports app optimization across both screens, although the experience may vary by app.
Saying the Galaxy Z Fold 8’s outer display is “minimal” isn’t a dig, either. Apps can be refreshing to use when you don’t need to open them on a large scale. Take Messages, for instance. It’s not a complex app, and there isn’t a ton to see – just the last couple of messages exchanged. The same goes for plenty of apps available on the Google Play Store.
And guess what? If you don’t like using the app on a smaller display, just unfold the phone. The Galaxy Z Fold 8 is such a versatile phone, whether it’s a gift during the holidays or one for yourself.
Run three apps (or more) at once
There are instances where you need the large real estate. One UI 9 gives the Galaxy Z Fold 8 the option to open and run multiple apps on your screen. There are several ways you can do it, too.
The main approach is to split two apps on the wide inner display. But if you want to see more from one app, you can simply drag the split over in one direction. One UI 9 leaves room to add one more app to that split screen, giving you a three-way Split View. All it takes is dragging an app from the taskbar and choosing the spot.
If you truly need a fourth app open, you have the option to open another in a floating window. Having four apps open at once is definitely a niche instance, but the Galaxy Z Fold 8 handles it like it’s nothing. You can have YouTube up in one corner while you check Messages for gift ideas and shop through the Amazon app. It might be helpful to keep a list of what you got and for whom in a floating Sheets window.
The point is not only that it’s technically possible, but it’s 
usable.
 So often devices let users open apps in split screen, but are in no way comfortable enough for the task. The Galaxy Z Fold 8 is perfect for it, and the experience actually makes you more productive, not less.
Built-in tripod
Most phones don’t let you get the best angle without any sort of third-party accessory. The Galaxy Z Fold 8, though, acts like its own tripod in a way.
Let’s say you’re taking photos, and you need to get a shot of yourself, but no one is around to take the photo for you. The Galaxy Z Fold 8’s hinge lets it prop itself up, so you can do things like set a timer and run to strike a pose.
This comes in handy for videos, too. Think of how many times you wanted to get a good angle on a plane or somewhere where there’s this flat surface just asking for a tiny tripod or kickstand. Well, the Galaxy Z Fold 8 is its own kickstand and can be positioned at just the right angle.
Where to buy the Galaxy Z Fold 8
You can get the Galaxy Z Fold 8 from your favorite carriers and retailers, but Best Buy is one of the leading places to find deals during the holiday season. You’ll find hefty discounts, higher trade-in values for your old devices, and more.

Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8: Eight Generations of Engineering – One Fantastic Foldable Experience
                    28-10-2026

Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold8 blends cutting-edge engineering with real-world usability to redefine what a foldable smartphone should be. At just 201g, it is the lightest foldable ever made, posting a significant reduction from the 253g of the Galaxy Z Fold5. This weight reduction is not just a number on a spec sheet; it translates into a device that feels more natural in the hand and easier to carry throughout the day.
The Fold8 is a mere 4.5mm thin when unfolded and 9.7mm folded, with a symmetrical design that enhances its portability and aesthetic appeal. Samsung achieved this by meticulously re-evaluating approximately 270 components and over 180 auxiliary parts to ensure that every element contributes to a lighter, thinner, and more refined device without compromising on performance.
Why Flex Titanium Matters in a Foldable
The engineering behind the Galaxy Z Fold8 goes beyond mere weight reduction. Samsung introduced the Flex Titanium display support plate with the Galaxy Z Fold Special Edition in 2024, but with the Fold8, this technology has evolved into a two-layer system.
The Flex Titanium now consists of an ultra-thin titanium-alloy film beneath the OLED panel and an enhanced titanium plate beneath it. This dual-layer approach allows each component to serve a distinct purpose: the titanium-alloy film provides strength and flexibility close to the panel, while the titanium plate offers structural support and impact absorption. This innovation has resulted in a 10% thinner display module, improved surface flatness, and reduced crease visibility, all of which contribute to a more durable and visually appealing device.
Redefining Durability in Foldables
Durability is another area where the Galaxy Z Fold8 excels. Samsung subjected the device to over 100 reliability tests to ensure it can withstand the rigors of everyday use.
The Fold8 features an Advanced Armor Aluminum frame, a Flex Titanium display backplate, and an Easy Open structure for smoother operation. It also boasts IP48 dust- and water-resistance, making it resilient in various environments.
The inner display is equipped with an anti-reflective finish, while the front and back are protected by Corning® Gorilla® Glass Ceramic3 Armor2 and Corning® Gorilla® Glass Victus® 2, respectively. These materials, combined with a precision hinge made from over 100 components, ensure that the Fold8 remains dependable over years of use.
The Best Camera Set up on a Foldable
The camera system on the Galaxy Z Fold8 is equally impressive. It features a 50MP Wide and 50MP Ultra-Wide setup, powered by the ProVisual Engine for AI-enhanced capture and editing.
The device supports Dual Preview/Dual Recording, allowing users to capture scenes from both front and rear camera perspectives simultaneously. Photo Assist provides a side-by-side view of original and edited images on the unfolded display, making editing more intuitive.
The Definitive Foldable Display
The display on the Galaxy Z Fold8 is another highlight. It boasts a 5.5” cover display and a 7.6” main display, both with up to 3000 nits peak brightness. The Vision Booster technology ensures excellent visibility even in bright sunlight, while the reduced crease visibility enhances the overall viewing experience.
Software-wise, the Galaxy Z Fold8 runs on Android 17 with One UI 9 that offers a seamless multitasking experience. Users can run up to three apps simultaneously with Multi Window, and AI features like Gemini Notebook and Now Nudge enhance productivity.
The device also supports Photo Assist, which uses the unfolded display to show original and edited images side by side, making editing more efficient.
Foldable Battery That Goes On and On
Battery life is another area where the Galaxy Z Fold8 shines. It features a 4,800mAh battery with 45W fast charging, ensuring that users can stay powered throughout the day. Samsung’s first silicon-carbon anode improves energy density, while higher-performance graphite enhances heat dissipation. The Independent Charging Architecture manages power efficiently, reducing heat and stress on the battery cells.
The Galaxy Z Fold8 is not just a technological marvel; it is a device designed with real-world users in mind. It represents eight generations of innovation and learning. It is a device that not only pushes the boundaries of what is possible with foldable technology but also delivers tangible benefits to users in their everyday lives. From its lightweight design and durable construction to its advanced camera system and powerful software, the Galaxy Z Fold8 is a testament to Samsung’s commitment to creating devices that truly enhance how we live, work, and create.
