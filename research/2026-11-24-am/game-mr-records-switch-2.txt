Mr. Records Brings a Turntable Controller to WePlay Expo 2026
Glee-Cheese Studio's rhythm adventure Mr. Records was playable at WePlay Expo 2026 in Shanghai.
1 source
1h ago
November 23, 2026, 1:39 PM WIB
Reporting from 1 source:
 4Gamer.net.
Why it matters
The turntable and the record-store framing turn a rhythm game into something readable from across an aisle, and the publishers are deliberately tuning difficulty down so the novelty is not gated behind expert play.
Key Facts
Mr. Records is developed by Glee-Cheese Studio and was playable at WePlay Expo 2026 in Shanghai.
The protagonist George runs a record store by day and plays rhythm stages at night.
Rhythm stages use three actions: jump, attack, and slide, with sliding controlled by the turntable.
Wired Productions and Gamesky Games handle publishing for Mr. Records.
The publishers said all songs in the game are original.
By day, protagonist George runs a record store and matches customers with records; by night he plays rhythm stages with jump, attack, and slide actions, sliding handled by a turntable. Publishers Wired Productions and Gamesky Games said the rhythm stages aim for a lower difficulty so all players can clear them, and all songs are original.
The demo station at WePlay Expo 2026 was built like a stage, with the player's turntable play mirrored on a display behind them. Sliding is the turntable's job: the rotation direction has to match the slide direction, and a finger can slip off before the player adjusts. Jump and attack are ordinary buttons. A gamepad swaps the turntable for the up and down direction keys.
All songs are original, and Glee-Cheese Studio is putting effort into the stage editor so players can place objects against their own favorite tracks and keep playing longer.

Follow Mr. Records Socials:
A Record Shop With A Heart
Step into MR. RECORDS, a cosy rhythm adventure where you run a tiny vinyl store by day and dive into surreal musical journeys by night. You play as George, an ageing dreamer finally opening the record shop he has carried in his head for decades, with his loyal dog Buzz always by his side.
Rhythm Gameplay Inside Every Record
Every vinyl in the shop is more than just a sleeve on a shelf. Drop the needle and you are pulled into a fully playable rhythm level, running, jumping, sliding, and dodging in time with the beat. Each track becomes a handcrafted dream world built from memories, emotions, and the stories behind the music.
Music That Tells A Story
MR. RECORDS is powered by an original soundtrack that spans funk, progressive rock, hip hop, electro, world music, and 80s pop. Every song has a purpose, from joyous anthems that lift George up to bittersweet tracks that surface long buried regrets, turning the soundtrack into an emotional spine for the whole adventure.
Meet Customers, Change Their Lives
Running the shop is about more than just ringing up sales. A colourful cast of customers come through the door, each with their own tastes, problems, and hidden histories. Learning which record fits which person lets you recommend albums that become part of their lives, and part of Georges own journey.
A Bedroom Above The Shop
Between musical journeys, you can unwind in Georges small bedroom above the store. It is a quiet space to review memories, check in on your progress, take calls, and reflect on how the music is reshaping his life. It is a reminder that even in a game about rhythm, it is okay to pause, breathe, and just listen.
A Rhythm Adventure About Second Chances
MR. RECORDS is ultimately a story about late blooming, memory, and finding the courage to finally start living on your own terms. It blends cosy shopkeeping, expressive rhythm gameplay, and heartfelt storytelling into a single experience where every record tells a story, including Georges own.
