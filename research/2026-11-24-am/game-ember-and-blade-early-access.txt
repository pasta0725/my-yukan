LINE Games Showcases Upcoming Lineup at WePlay Expo 2026... 3,000 Attendees Experience Demos

ⓒ LINE Games
LINE Games Corporation announced on November 23 that it had successfully concluded its first-time participation in WePlay Expo 2026. Approximately 3,000 visitors visited the booth during the event to test its upcoming titles.
LINE Games operated its own dedicated booth at the event, which was held for two days starting November 21 at the National Exhibition and Convention Center in Shanghai, China. The company featured hands-on demo zones centered around its internally developed titles Ember and Blade and QUIET, alongside interactive booth activities tied to game playthroughs.
The two main featured titles fall into the premium survivors-like and co-op comedy horror genres, respectively. Ember and Blade is scheduled to launch in Early Access on January 8, 2027, on PC and consoles (PS5, Xbox). Meanwhile, QUIET, coming to PC, targets a release in the first half of 2027.
Visitors also had the opportunity to play other upcoming releases on site. The playable lineup included sci-fi horror title CODE EXIT, roguelike survival game Amazing Slot Survivor, and dark comedy visual novel COME TO MY PARTY!
A LINE Games representative stated, “It was a meaningful event where we could immersively convey the appeal of our diverse new lineup to global players by pairing hands-on gameplay with interactive on-site activities.” They added, “We ask for your continued interest in LINE Games’ new PC titles scheduled for release next year.”

About
In Ember and Blade, death is only the beginning. Players take control of Fenrix, a cursed immortal trapped in an endless cycle of death and rebirth, and descend into the depths of oblivion to prevent the rise of Asmodeus. Each run offers new opportunities to transform Fenrix’s curse into overwhelming power, wielding an overhauled arsenal of weapons, linking angelic Blessings to attacks, and utilizing Runes and Arcana to create increasingly devastating builds.
This Early Access release date announcement follows the major updates introduced in Demo 2.0, which overhauled Ember and Blade’s combat and gave players even more ways to wreak havoc. Chain light and heavy attacks into powerful combos, link Blessings directly to attacks and dashes, experiment with weapons ranging from the Sword and Hammer to the recently introduced Shotgun, and stack abilities to transform Fenrix into an increasingly unstoppable demon-slaying force.
Key Features of Recent Demo 2.0 Update:
Chain the Carnage
 – String light and heavy attacks into devastating combos with a reworked action and stamina system built to keep the fight flowing.
Lock, Load, and Let Loose
 – Rediscover the fully reworked Sword and Hammer, or blast through demon hordes with the all-new Shotgun in Fenrix’s arsenal.
Divine Power, Your Way
 – Link Blessing powers directly to light attacks, heavy attacks, and dashes to unleash their effects in combat without consuming stamina. Experiment with different combinations and complete Blessing Link Sets to unlock additional bonuses.
Build Beyond the Limit
 – Discover Arcana cards and open up even more possibilities for powerful builds.
Give ‘Em Hell
 – Face improved monster reactions and interactions with the environment, a new Break state, and redesigned Elite combat.
Stay in the Fight
 – Take advantage of expanded control options, improved visibility, and an overhauled Elevator.
Even Immortals Need a Break
 – Step away from the demon-slaying to meet adorable marimos and watch the Sanctuary change dynamically with the time of day.
