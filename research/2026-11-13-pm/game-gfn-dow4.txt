NVIDIA confirmed on Thursday, November 12 that Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War IV is coming to GeForce NOW. Dragon’s Dogma 2: Dark Arisen and 10 more games also join the library this week. If your PC can’t handle a demanding new release, the cloud is how you play it anyway: downloads and updates happen there, not on your drive. 
On November 6 we told you
 that GeForce NOW was adding 24 games in November, Cyberpunk 2077 among them. Here is what NVIDIA announced today.
Dawn of War IV: what NVIDIA confirmed
Dawn of War IV is developed by KING Art Games and published by Deep Silver. It launches on Steam on Thursday, December 3. It is a real-time strategy game that returns to the series’ roots, with large-scale battles, base building and a renewed fight for planet Kronus.
Factions:
 Space Marines, Orks, Necrons and the Adeptus Mechanicus. The Adeptus Mechanicus make their series debut.
Campaign:
 more than 70 missions, playable solo or in co-op.
Other modes:
 The Last Stand, Skirmish and multiplayer.
GeForce NOW will bring it to supported PCs, Macs, handhelds and other devices. NVIDIA did not give an exact cloud date. It says to stay tuned to GFN Thursday for launch.
What arrives this week
Dragon’s Dogma 2
 gets Dark Arisen, which is streaming now. It adds the long-abandoned region of Norgan, the enigmatic Eir, the undying Fallen Dragon and the 12 dungeon challenges of Lost Rites. It also includes a collaboration with Delicious in Dungeon.

Cyberpunk 2077 was already in our November 6 post. What changes this week is how it arrives: NVIDIA lists it with Xbox Play Anywhere and as a new release on Battle.net. The 8800 GTX entry is a playable demo for that GPU’s 20th anniversary, with DLSS 4.5 and path tracing in the cloud. NVIDIA notes that GeForce NOW availability may vary, because games are added throughout the week.
DLSS 5 for War Thunder and Where Winds Meet
War Thunder adds DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering in its Eagle’s Shadow major update, arriving in mid-November. Where Winds Meet gets support on Monday, November 23. According to NVIDIA, the October model doubles performance over the previous version, and model performance has improved tenfold since the March announcement. That brings the technology to more gamers, including Ultimate members streaming on GeForce RTX 5080 cloud rigs.
Which ones are heaviest for your PC
By our recommended GPU score, Ashes of the Singularity II (19,850) and Main Sequence (19,810) are the most demanding of the batch. Dragon’s Dogma 2 and Cyberpunk 2077 come next. At the other end is Worst Class Ever (5,450), which is far lighter. On a modest rig, the heavy ones are the best candidates for the cloud. For what you can run locally, check
