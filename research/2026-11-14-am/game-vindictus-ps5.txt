'Vindictus: Defying Fate' Confirmed for PS5... First Original Character 'Noctia' Revealed

Nexon's upcoming action RPG 'Vindictus: Defying Fate' has been confirmed for a PS5 release. Alongside the announcement, the first look at original character Noctia was revealed, as well as a teaser for a new cinematic trailer.
On November 12, Nexon Lead Director Dongseok Oh announced via the official PlayStation Blog that Vindictus: Defying Fate will launch on PS5. The post also detailed the game's world, core combat systems, and distinct character traits.
Vindictus: Defying Fate is an action RPG developed based on the world and characters of Vindictus. Set in a Celtic mythology-inspired medieval fantasy world, the game revolves around the longstanding war between humans and Fomors and the story hidden behind it. Players become a new recruit of the Kalbram Mercenaries, exploring various regions and confronting powerful foes.
Combat centers around distinct weapons and actions for each character. Lann specializes in consecutive evasions and rapid strikes, while Fiona focuses on defense and counterattacks using her shield. Delia builds up power for a heavy strike, while Karok withstands incoming attacks and engages in contests of strength.
Director Dongseok Oh explained that combat is the core element of the game experience, noting that character design begins with the choice of weapon. The goal is to implement unique combat styles for each character, offering a distinct experience based on the player's choice. The main story will unfold through a single-player focus, while separate multiplayer content is also being prepared to challenge formidable enemies alongside other players.
First original character 'Noctia' © Nexon
A look at the new character Noctia was also revealed. Director Dongseok Oh introduced Noctia, the first original character of Vindictus: Defying Fate, via an X post on November 10. Noctia is a brand-new character who did not appear in the original Vindictus, unveiled wielding a massive scythe alongside the phrase "The face behind the scythe."
Introducing Noctia, Director Dongseok Oh stated, "Back in April, we revealed a little secret about a new character, and now we are ready to show everyone." However, specific details regarding her combat style and in-game role have not yet been disclosed.
Meanwhile, Vindictus: Defying Fate will participate in TwitchCon 2026, held in San Diego, US, from November 13 to 15. Nexon will set up an on-site booth and provide a new playable demo build for attendees. In addition to the four previously revealed characters, visitors will be able to try a newly added fifth character in this demo, though it has not been confirmed whether that character is Noctia. The booth will also feature a photo zone utilizing the game's universe and giveaway events for souvenirs.
A teaser video for a new cinematic trailer was also released. The clip serves as a teaser ahead of the full cinematic trailer, which is expected to premiere in conjunction with TwitchCon.
Vindictus: Defying Fate is currently in development for PC and console platforms, with its PS5 release confirmed through this announcement. The PlayStation Store page has also gone live, allowing players to add the game to their wishlists. Further details about the game can be found on the official website.

Vindictus: Defying Fate is coming to PS5 in 2027, Nexon says
Nexon Korea confirmed a PS5 version of its action RPG, with no date or price yet, and showed its four playable characters and a multiplayer mode.

Nexon Korea's Vindictus: Defying Fate is coming to PS5 "next year," which means 2027. The studio announced it Thursday on 
the PlayStation Blog
 without a release date or a price, and 
@PlayStation shared the news on X
 the same day.
Game director Dongseok Oh wrote the post. The action RPG takes place in a medieval fantasy world with Celtic roots, where humans and Fomors go to war over a prophecy that promises to open "the gates of paradise" once one race wipes out the other. Players join the Kalbram mercenaries, under the Rocheste Order, and travel through places such as Colhen, the Northern Ruins, Hoarfrost Hollow and Ainle.
Four characters are playable, each with a different combat style:
Lann
 dodges instead of blocking and counterattacks after landing a hit.
Fiona
 blocks with a shield and strikes back.
Delia
 builds up power for one devastating blow.
Karok
 soaks up hits and returns them as a pulse of force.
The main journey is built for one player. Nexon is also preparing multiplayer content in which players team up against enormous threats, but it did not describe the modes.
The post mentions only PS5. It says nothing about PC or Xbox, or about whether the game will launch on other platforms at the same time. The PlayStation Store page already lets players add the game to a wishlist, and the studio has yet to set a release date.

美しく戦う
手応えのある、引き込まれるような戦闘が、『Vindictus: Defying Fate』の体験の核となる要素です。そのため、英雄のコンセプトを考える際も、まず武器から着想を得ます。プレイヤーが期待する馴染みのある武器から、独自の魅力的な戦闘の可能性を生み出す意外性のある武器まで、幅広い選択肢を慎重に検討し、そのなかからひとつを土台として選びます。
物語、アクション、外見を担当する各チームには、全体の方向性が定まる前に、それぞれのデザインを自由に模索し、創造性を発揮する余地が与えられています。こうして、独自の戦闘スタイルを持つ英雄を作り上げていきます。どの英雄を選ぶかによって、プレイヤーの体験は全く別のものになります。お気に入りの英雄を極めることも、複数の英雄を育て、状況に合わせて選択することも可能です。
リシタ
ガードを使わず、回避を軸に戦います。連続回避で攻撃をかわし、攻撃を命中させると、すぐさま踏み込んで一撃を繰り出します。
フィオナ
盾で防ぎ、反撃する安定した攻防が特徴です。敵の攻撃を正確なタイミングで受け流すと、自分の攻撃を途切れさせずに続けられます。
この過酷な世界の戦闘を生き抜くには、高度な集中力と精密なコントロールが求められます。生き残るためには、敵の動きを学ぶことが欠かせません。
選んだ英雄の特性に合わせて、強烈な一撃を打ち返したり、激しい連続攻撃を防いだり、正確なタイミングで回避したりすることができます。隙を作り出し、重い反撃を叩き込むことが鍵となります。
デリア
力を溜め、一気に放つ強烈な一撃が持ち味です。敵を貫くように通り抜けながら、攻撃をかわす動きも備えています。
カロック
衝撃を吸収して敵の攻撃に耐え、立ち向かいます。強敵の攻撃にタイミングを合わせると、互いの力を競い合う展開へと繋がります。
また、自分だけの武器を製作し、衣装や外見をカスタマイズして、自分だけの英雄を作り上げることができます
