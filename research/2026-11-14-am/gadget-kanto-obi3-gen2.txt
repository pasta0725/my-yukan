Kanto Audio、入門価格帯レコードプレーヤー「OBI3 Gen2」。Bluetooth対応＆フォノイコ内蔵
編集部：小野佳希
完実電気は、同社が取り扱うカナダ・Kanto Audioブランドより、エントリー価格帯ながらBluetooth接続機能なども備えるレコードプレーヤー「OBI3 Gen2」を11月27日に発売する。オープン価格だが、税込35,750円前後での実売が予想される。
なお、蔦屋家電二子玉川店では、本日11月13日から先行展示および先行販売を行う。
OBI3 Gen2は、ベルトドライブ方式のレコードプレーヤー。カラーバリエーションとしてブラック、グリーン、アイスブルー、ホワイト、ウォールナットの全5色を用意している。
高密度MDF製のウッドキャビネットとアルミニウム製プラッターを採用し、不要な振動を抑えることで安定したレコード再生を実現できるよう配慮。駆動部にはスピードセンサーを搭載したベルトドライブ方式を採用し、本体前面のボタンから33 1/3回転／45回転の切り替えが行える。
また、±10％のピッチコントロール機能を搭載し、楽曲に合わせて再生速度を微調整することが可能。トーンアームにはアルミニウム製J字型アームを採用し、カウンターウェイトやアンチスケート機構を備えている。
カートリッジにはオーディオテクニカ製「AT3600L」を標準搭載。カートリッジ交換にも対応している。また、再生終了時にプラッターの回転を自動停止するオートストップ機能を搭載している。
接続面ではBluetooth 5.3によるワイヤレス音声出力に対応し、Bluetoothスピーカーやワイヤレスヘッドホンと組み合わせたレコード再生が可能。また、フォノイコライザーも内蔵している。
背面にはRCA出力端子を装備し、フォノ入力を備えたアンプへの接続はもちろん、フォノイコライザーを内蔵しないアクティブスピーカーなどにも直接接続できる。
ワウ・フラッターは0.15％以下（WRMS）。外形寸法は432W×363D×124H mm、質量は5.5kg。専用電源アダプター、ダストカバー、フェルト製スリップマット、45回転アダプター、カウンターウェイト、RCAケーブル、アース線などが付属する。

Kanto Audio Launches 2nd Gen Obi3 Turntable
September 9, 2026
A solidly built manual turntable with a weighted aluminum platter, sensor-controlled drive, engineered-wood plinth, adjustable counterweight and anti-skate — with ±10% pitch control and Bluetooth 5.3. Launching alongside a full range of Kanto turntable accessories.
Canadian brand Kanto Audio today announces the 2nd Generation of
 Obi3,
 a belt-drive turntable that brings real build quality and hands-on control not commonly found in entry-level units. With a weighted aluminum platter and solid engineered-wood plinth, a sensor-controlled belt drive, adjustable counterweight and anti-skate, ±10% pitch control, a pre-installed Audio-Technica AT3600L cartridge, a built-in phono preamp and Bluetooth® 5.3, Obi3 2nd Gen pairs high-end turntable control with the convenience modern listeners expect
Much of the vinyl market at this price point features fully automatic decks with fixed, sealed tonearms — easy to use, but little to adjust. Obi3 2nd Gen takes a different approach: a proper manual turntable you can set up and tune yourself, without stepping up to a specialist unit.
“We wanted Obi3 2nd Gen to feel like a high-end turntable at an entry-level price, one you can set up, adjust and grow into. Paired with our new range of accessories, it’s everything you need to start a record collection and look after it, designed as a cohesive collection.”
— Brett Smalley, Director of Marketing, Kanto Audio.
Built to feel a class above
Where many budget turntables lean on plastic, Obi3 2nd Gen is built around a weighted aluminum platter and a solid, engineered-wood plinth. That mass and rigidity settle vibration and give the deck a planted, quality feel – construction that’s easy to overlook for entry-level turntables. Driving Obi3 2nd Gen is a sensor-controlled belt drive motor with electronic speed selection: switch between 33-1/3 and 45 RPM from the front without moving the belt by hand, and auto-stop halts the platter at the end of a side to help protect the stylus. The pre-installed Audio-Technica AT3600L cartridge delivers smooth, balanced sound straight out of the box.
A turntable you can set up your way
An aluminum J-shaped tonearm carries an adjustable counterweight and anti-skate, so you can balance the arm and set tracking force yourself, guided by clear reference marks, for clean, even playback and less wear on your stylus and records. That’s hands-on control that the fixed, automatic decks dominating this price simply don’t offer. A manual cue lever lowers the stylus gently and precisely onto the groove, and with ±10% pitch control, there’s room to play. Nudge a record a little faster or slower and hear it in a new, fun way as you get to know your collection.
Connect any way you listen
Obi3 2nd Gen fits into any setup. An RCA output with a selectable built-in phono preamp means it works with everything from a full hi-fi separates system to a pair of powered speakers — flip the phono/line switch to match your gear. For wireless listening, Bluetooth 5.3 streams to speakers or headphones anywhere in the room. It’s a natural partner for any of Kanto Audio’s powered speakers.
Designed to be seen
True to Kanto’s design language, Obi3 2nd Gen keeps its form clean and its details considered, from flush-mounted hinges to a polished aluminum platter. It’s offered in five finishes — Black, White, Green, Ice Blue and Walnut — and its compact 43.2 x 36.3 cm (17″ x 14.3″) footprint sits comfortably on a desk, shelf or sideboard. Everything needed to get set up is in the box — dust cover, slip mat, 45 adapter, counterweight, and RCA and ground cables.

Pricing & Availability
Obi3 2nd Gen
is priced at $249.99 USD / CA$269.99 / £179.99 / €199.99 / $349.99 AUD and goes on sale in North America on August 10, 2026, in Black, White, Green, Ice Blue and Walnut. The full range of Kanto turntable accessories is available now.
