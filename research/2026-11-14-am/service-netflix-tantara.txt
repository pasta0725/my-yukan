Watch: Song Hye Kyo, Gong Yoo, Seolhyun, And Cha Seung Won Strive Toward Success In New Drama "Tantara" Teasers
Drama Preview

by

Netflix’s upcoming drama “Tantara” has unveiled new posters and a teaser!
Set in the reckless and merciless Korean entertainment industry of the ‘60s and ‘80s, “Tantara” chronicles the rise of those who had almost nothing, yet bet everything on a dream of dazzling success.
The posters feature Min Ja (
Song Hye Kyo
), Dong Gu (
Gong Yoo
), Min Hee (
Seolhyun
), and Seok Gil Yeo (
Cha Seung Won
). Through facial expressions and quotes that reveal each character’s unique personality, the posters showcase their different desires and determination toward success.
First of all, Min Ja reveals her strong greed for success, stating, “You know I can smell money a mile away. There’s money here.”
Dong Gu also displays a fearless attitude, saying, “We’ve had it rough. What have we got to fear?”
Min Hee hints at a complex relationship intertwined with Min Ja through the line, “I have to make it so you won’t abandon me.” 
Seok Gil Yeo captures attention with the line, “You think someone uneducated like me got this far by chance?” which hints at a life hidden behind the glamour of the stage.
The teaser that was released along with the posters captures the stories of four people struggling to achieve their dreams. Min Ja and Dong Gu, who have faced hardships together since childhood, live by relying on each other. Later, they meet Min Hee, who possesses a talent for singing, and jump into the music industry to begin a new challenge. Despite the successive difficulties they face, they do not give up and move forward toward the success they desire. 
In particular, the tagline, “A reckless beginning. Countless challenges. The intense journey of entertainers,” encapsulates the process that these individuals, who started with nothing, will go through to realize their dreams.

That Winter, The Wind Blows
” and “
Coffee Prince
” and director Lee Yoon Jung, will release Part 1 on December 11, followed by the subsequent parts on December 18 and 25.

A teaser and a release schedule

The three weekly drops mean the full story will be available by Christmas Day. Anyone planning to follow the series from the start should note the Friday, December 11 opening date given in the official post.

Daring to Dream Big!  Teaser Poster & Trailer for Upcoming  1960s-80s Korean Showbiz Saga ‘Tantara’

Netflix has unveiled the teaser trailer and poster for the upcoming Korean period drama series 
Tantara
, premiering December 11 across three weekly installments. Part 1 (Episodes 1 ~ 8) will launch on December 11, followed by Part 2 (Episodes 9 ~ 15) on December 18, and Part 3 (Episodes 16 ~ 22) on December 25. Set against the gritty, cutthroat backdrop of Korea's entertainment industry from the 1960s to the 1980s, the series chronicles the journey of resilient individuals from humble beginnings who risk everything in pursuit of glittering success.
The accompanying teaser poster captures Min-ja (Song Hye-kyo), Dong-gu (GONG YOO), Min-hui (Kim Seol-Hyun), and Seok Gil-yeo (Cha Seung won), each driven by fierce ambition. The poster highlights the quartet's relentless hunger for success while hinting at the uneasy alliances and rivalries that complicate their rise, especially the simmering tension between Min-ja and Min-hui, as conflicting ambitions put their fragile bond to the test.
The teaser trailer immediately hooks viewers with its dynamic visuals and rhythmic beat, painting a vivid portrait of characters willing to throw their whole lives into the ruthless world of showbiz. Having weathered life's harshest trials together since childhood, Min-ja and Dong-gu dive into the record industry after discovering Min-hui, a raw vocal talent. Their refusal to back down in the face of relentless setbacks invites audiences to root for their gritty survival and hard-earned rise.
Uniting master writer Noh Hee-gyoung and director Lee Yoon-jung with a stellar powerhouse cast, 
Tantara
 delivers an inspiring story of tenacity and burning ambition. 
Tantara
 premieres Friday, December 11, exclusively on Netflix.
