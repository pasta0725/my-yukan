For nearly two decades, Firefight has been a Halo favorite, challenging Spartans to team up, hold the line, and survive wave after wave of enemies. The tradition continues this winter as we add the four-player cooperative PvE mode to 
Halo: Campaign Evolved
 in a free update.
We're kicking things off tonight at XBOX FanFest, where attendees will be the first to jump in and experience Firefight across new maps and armed with plenty of weapons to help even the odds. 
And there's more on the way. We'll be sharing additional details about this free update at 
Halo Fest
, happening December 12-13 in Seattle, WA, where fans attending the event will also have a chance to play Firefight throughout the weekend. 
We can’t wait to see you all at Halo Fest and to celebrate 25 years of Halo and XBOX together!

Halo: Campaign Evolved came and went, didn’t it? It’s a fairly solid remake, but plenty of fans, myself included, felt 
it was missing that special something
. And that special something was multiplayer.
I was okay with the decision to skip a full multiplayer component, given that I wanted the campaign to be the absolute best it could be. However, adding a mode like 
Firefight 
would have definitely gone a long way toward boosting replayability.
In fact, 
I covered news recently suggesting that Halo: Campaign Evolved might get one final update
, despite the franchise being handed off to Activision. In that piece, I detailed reports that the game would receive both Firefight and Theater mode. While I can’t speak to the status of Theater mode, 
Xbox announced
 that 
Firefight is definitely heading to Halo: Campaign Evolved
.
When can we play Firefight?
Halo: Campaign Evolved 
(Image credit: Xbox Game Studios)
Fans attending Xbox FanFest in Seattle today
 (November 12, 2026) will be the first to play, with additional details set to be revealed 
next month at Halo Fest
, taking place 
between December 12 and December 13, 2026
. It's all part of an ongoing celebration of 
Xbox's 25th anniversary
, which includes Halo as its launch title.
Not much is known yet, but we have been told that 
Firefight will arrive as a free update this winter
. It will feature new maps, new weapons, and four-player co-op. For those unfamiliar, Firefight is a wave-based PvE mode that pits you against escalating waves of enemies, usually with modifiers coming into play.
I think this is pretty great. I was absolutely gutted that we didn’t get any real form of replayability for Campaign Evolved at launch, so it’s nice to see what remains of Halo Studios pull together and get something for fans to enjoy out the door.
I will definitely be jumping in to try Firefight when it releases. Part of me is hoping we’ll see a new batch of achievements, too, but I think that’s just wishful thinking on my part since I’ve already 100%’d the game. Until then, we can look back on our gaming history with 
Microsoft's Spotify Wrapped-style nostalgia feature: Xbox Playback
.
