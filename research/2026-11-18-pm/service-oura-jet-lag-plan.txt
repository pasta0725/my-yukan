Oura is starting to roll out Jet Lag Plan today, giving travelers a schedule for light exposure, sleep and caffeine based on their 
flights and usual sleep habits
. The feature is designed for trips spanning at least three time zones and offers guidance ahead of departure, in transit and at the destination. Members worldwide are included in the rollout, with eligibility requiring Oura Ring Gen3 or newer and English selected for the app.
To create a plan, members enter their flights, including connections, and specify why they are traveling. Oura says a vacation can produce different recommendations from a work trip, while sleep history and chronotype also shape the schedule. Travelers sharing a flight can therefore receive different guidance, even with the same departure and destination.

The schedule includes periods for getting bright light or limiting exposure, generally lasting about three hours each and shifting as the body clock adjusts. Oura says it estimates the point when a member's body temperature reaches its minimum and sleep is most likely, then considers travel direction and the number of time zones crossed. According to Oura, poorly timed light can move the body clock away from the destination's schedule.
Members can check off recommended actions, receive push reminders and look ahead through their schedule. They can also follow Readiness during the trip and compare recovery against similar journeys, using a reference drawn from 10 million completed member trips. If travel arrangements change, Advisor can suggest revisions in response to a plain-language request, but members must approve those changes; trip details can also be edited directly.
The rollout follows Oura's personalized wellness plans through Oura Advisor, which support goals using sleep, recovery and activity information 
alongside details about daily routines
. Those plans last no longer than seven days, and members can keep a Jet Lag Plan running alongside their single active Advisor plan.
Members can begin through the app's Today screen, its menu or a conversation with Advisor. A detected phone clock shift of at least three hours may also trigger an invitation to create a plan. After returning home, members receive a report comparing Sleep Score and Readiness Score against their baseline before travel, along with completed actions and a review of their trip goal.
