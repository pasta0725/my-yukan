 Netflix Launches ‘Out of Frame’ Masterclass for Australia’s Emerging Screen Creatives
 By
 Lauren McNamara
Netflix
Netflix is opening the door to Australia’s next generation of visual storytellers, launching a new masterclass designed to help photographers, videographers and digital content creators break into the film and television industry.
The streaming giant is seeking 10 independent Australian creatives to take part in its Out of Frame Masterclass, a two-day program taking place in Sydney on December 9th-10th, 2026. Developed in partnership with Bursty and Media Mentors Australia, the initiative will be led by Netflix’s Photo and AV Studio (PAVS), the team responsible for producing promotional imagery, key art and behind-the-scenes content for the streaming platform’s productions.
The program aims to demonstrate how skills developed in photography, videography and social media content creation can translate into opportunities across the screen industry, with applicants not required to have any previous film or television credits.
“If you know how to capture raw emotion in a split second at a high-stakes event, or craft a scroll-stopping reel right from your phone, you already possess the instincts that electrify a film set. Out of Frame is designed to supercharge those instincts and show you exactly where they can elevate your business,” said Abby Morgan, head of PAVS Netflix ANZ.
Across the two days, selected participants will gain hands-on experience developing promotional material from a simulated Netflix creative brief. Working alongside actors, they will pitch and produce content before receiving feedback from Netflix teams and other industry professionals. The masterclass will also offer practical advice on securing freelance opportunities within the entertainment industry, alongside a potential visit to an active Netflix production set, subject to filming schedules. The program will conclude with an industry networking event on December 10th.
Beyond the two-day workshop, participants will receive 12 months of additional professional development and support through an alumni network aimed at helping them translate their new skills into paid opportunities.
“This is an incredible opportunity to build on your career and we’re thrilled to be supporting the successful applicants over the next 12 months. The combination of training, community and support is unprecedented and we cannot wait to see the creative talent that exists across Australia,” said Denise Eriksen from Media Mentors Australia, which will work with the cohort throughout the following year.

Of the 10 available positions, two have been reserved for First Nations creatives, while another two are designated for disabled, deaf and/or neurodivergent applicants.
To be eligible, applicants must have at least two years of professional creative experience, access to their own professional equipment and availability for both days of the program, including the evening networking event. Netflix will cover flights and accommodation for successful applicants travelling from interstate.
The selection process will begin with an expression of interest, followed by a review of applicants’ professional experience and creative portfolios. Shortlisted candidates will then be invited to interview.
Applications are now open, and close at 11:59pm on Monday, November 23rd. See

Who the program is for
Netflix is seeking 10 independent Australian photographers, videographers and social creators for its Out of Frame Masterclass, run with Bursty and Media Mentors Australia. It takes place in Sydney on December 9 and 10, with a networking event on the second evening.
The announcement asks for at least two years of professional creative experience, availability for both days and the evening event, and participants' own professional equipment. Prior screen credits are not required. The application declaration limits eligibility to Australian residents aged 18 or over.
The confirmed deadline and application route
The declaration says applications close at 11:59pm AEDT on Monday, November 23, equivalent to 12:59 UTC that day. Use the official expression-of-interest form linked below. No application was filled in or submitted for this report.
Netflix says applicants are screened for experience, asked for creative examples and shortlisted for interviews. Two places are reserved for First Nations creatives and two for disabled, Deaf and/or neurodivergent creators. Meeting the criteria does not guarantee selection.
What support covers
The form says selected participants living outside Sydney receive return economy flights from their closest capital city or airport and three-star accommodation for the program. Meals, transport, incidentals and insurance are participants' responsibility. A laptop is preferred but not mandatory.
The workshop includes a mock promotional brief, feedback and a year of alumni support. A set visit depends on the production schedule. These are training and support arrangements, not a guaranteed Netflix job or freelance contract.
