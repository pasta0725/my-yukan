Nick DiGiovanni brings viewers behind-the-scenes into some of the world’s most acclaimed kitchens with his new YouTube series, “Chef,” which offers a first-person look at the people and craft behind some of the best food in the world.
The series will air full-length episodes on YouTube rolling out throughout 2026-27, TheWrap has exclusively learned. The premiere episode “Inside the World’s #1 Pizza Restaurant” showcased pizza maestro Anthony Mangieri of the New York-based Una Pizza Napoletana, taking viewers through an unfiltered, unedited glimpse into how he makes his award-winning pizza pies.
In addition to long form episodes, DiGiovanni will air a short-form POV companion episodes. The first one shows viewers how to make Butter Chicken Shawarma.

Creator Cooking Shows Are Heating Up Online — and Netflix Is Buying
“We’re starting at world’s #1 ranked pizzeria, through the lens of a pizza chef who has spent over 40 years perfecting his craft,” DiGiovanni told TheWrap. “From there we go everywhere: a busy street cart in Japan, a Michelin-starred kitchen in London, even your favorite fast food restaurant. The viewer will be able to experience everything as if you’re standing right there in the kitchen.”
The short-form videos will feature individual recipes that viewers can recreate at home, filmed start to finish from the chef’s point of view. DiGiovanni describes those videos as the appetizer, with the longer episodes serving as the entrée, giving audiences an opportunity to explore the professional kitchens and techniques behind the food.
“Many viewers will find ‘Chef’ through one of our short-form videos, and the full episodes are there when they’re hungry for more,” he said.
The project also drew on DiGiovanni’s own experience in the culinary world, which predates his career as a digital creator. After competing on “MasterChef,” he continued building his food-focused online presence, but his background includes working in restaurants and training in a three-Michelin-star kitchen.
“I’ve been in front of the camera ever since ‘MasterChef.’ Before that, I worked in restaurants and even trained in a three-Michelin-star kitchen,” DiGiovanni said. “That’s where I watched the best cooking of my life, and almost nobody outside this industry ever gets to see it. I’ve wanted to make this channel for years. The chefs are the story, and they deserve far more credit than they get.”
The launch comes as YouTube continues investing in original creator programming. The platform announced new projects from creators including Emma Chamberlain, DiGiovanni, Aryna Sabalenka, Morgan Jay, Grace Reiter, Jon Youshaei and Wesley Wang during its second annual Creator Premieres showcase Tuesday at The Hollywood Roosevelt.
DiGiovanni has expanded his YouTube viewership to other platforms including Netflix. The content creator signed a licensing deal with Netflix in July to stream his catalog and simultaneously release fresh content day-and-date with YouTube.

Hannah Sterling
 (U.S. Media & Entertainment Business Editor)

NEW YORK (AmeriLedger Newsroom)
 — Sean Downey of Google sounded a lot like a network TV executive on Tuesday evening as he opened the second annual YouTube Creator Premieres content showcase in Hollywood. The event, held at the Hollywood Roosevelt hotel, showed off upcoming titles from such disparate stars as influencer Emma Chamberlain, director Wesley Wang, tennis superstar Aryna Sabalenka, [...]
Sean Downey of Google sounded a lot like a network TV executive on Tuesday evening as he opened the second annual YouTube Creator Premieres content showcase in Hollywood.
The event, held at the Hollywood Roosevelt hotel, showed off upcoming titles from such disparate stars as influencer Emma Chamberlain, director Wesley Wang, tennis superstar Aryna Sabalenka, musician and comedian Morgan Jay, chefs Nick DiGiovanni and Ina Garten and comedians Lilly Singh and Grace Reiter. Downey opened the hourlong presentation by stating the obvious — the definition of television has changed. He noted that the first Academy Awards was held in the same ballroom where a few hundred creators, influencers and journalists gathered. The studio system that defined who got on the air and into the multiplexes is under reconstruction.
“Today, the studio are the creators who are in this room. They own the studio. The creators decide what gets to be shown and they have an unbreakable connection with the fans and people that want to watch their content,” Downey, who is president of Americas and global partners for Google.
Of course, the sales pitch wasn't far behind. The event was designed to warm up deals for big stars such as Chamberlain, Sabalenka and Wang as well as rising stars. “Brands and creators were built to be together,” Downey said. “They speak a language of trust and resonance.”
Sean Downey, president of Google, touted a “new era” of entertainment unfolding on YouTube, which energizes the world of the creator economy. The streaming pipeline controlled by YouTube also facilitates all the new dimensions of the digital economy, from second screen activity and community building to supporting vertical media, microdramas, e-commerce and livestream events.
The event was hosted by Scott Evans, the former “Access Hollywood” co-host who has seen his YouTube business blossom with the success of his “House Guest” lifestyle series. YouTube unleashed a slew of trailers and teasers for shows rolling out through the end of the year and into the first quarter. Evans is getting a “House Guest” spinoff series that will find him traveling to visit homes in exotic locations, starting in January with a trek to Lagos, Nigeria.
The list of projects includes a filmmaking competition series “Absolute Cinema,” which puts contestants through a seven-day filmmaking boot camp with Wang in the hopes of winning $100,000 to fund their films. Multihyphenate comedian Grace Reiter has a new movie parody, “Twilight (but better),” a follow on the success of her “Hunger Games (but better)” parody released in 2025.
Here is a rundown of all of the projects unveiled and content descriptions provided by YouTube:
Chef Nick DiGiovanni, the creator behind the world’s largest food YouTube channel, launches Chef (@Chef) — a new series from his production company, Knife Drop Studios. After reaching billions of viewers through his global culinary adventures (@NickDiGiovanni) and step-by-step recipes (@NicksKitchen), DiGiovanni is now turning the camera solely toward fellow professional chefs, offering an unfiltered look inside kitchens around the globe. From Michelin-starred restaurants and hidden street food gems showcased in full-length episodes, to POV recipes in short-form episodes, Chef puts viewers in the shoes of the people behind the scenes, offering a unique, captivating perspective for everyone from the culinary obsessed to the simply curious. You can view the first full-length episode, “Inside the World’s #1 Pizza Restaurant” now, only on YouTube, along with the first short-form POV episode, “Butter Chicken Shawarma.” New short-form POV episodes will debut daily across Nick’s newly-launched @Chef channel with full-length episodes rolling out throughout 2026-27 exclusively on YouTube.
Morgan Jay | “Music Therapy with Morgan Jay” | New series premieres November 18
Music Therapy is an original YouTube series developed and produced by viral comedian and musician Morgan Jay and Ty Stafford of Austin-based creative agency Hello Party, financed by Live Nation Studios. In each episode, Jay plays musical therapist to a celebrity guest, blending unscripted conversation and improv with live songwriting before the pair close with an original song they create together. The result lands somewhere between a talk show, a comedy special and a live concert. The first season features Kam Patterson, Ron Funches, Mara Wilson, Brennan Lee Mulligan, Yung Gravy, Matt Friend, Abbey Romeo, Mark Smalls, Tony Jefferson, ASHBY, Josh Johnson and Mandal Mann. Music Therapy was created by Morgan Jay and Ty Stafford (Hello Party) and is directed by Stafford. Brooke Schley serves as showrunner and co-writes the series with Jay. Executive producers are Graham Sicalowski, Robin Rapino, Stella Bulochnikov, Brooke Schley and Matt Sadeghian. Watch the series trailer here.
Emma Chamberlain | “What Are You Waiting For?” | New series premieres early 2027
Emma Chamberlain doesn't like waiting in line — and wants to understand more about people who do. In this new ongoing series, what starts as an investigation of New York City's most frequented lines unspools into a thoughtful and expansive meditation on subcultures, fandoms, and what makes our modern world tick. At once whimsical, profound and provocative, and underpinned by Emma's singular POV, What Are You Waiting For? is a hilarious cultural commentary that not only asks what people are waiting for — but why. The series is executive produced by two-time Emmy and Golden Globe winner Joe Lewis, with Amplify Pictures (Fleabag, 100 Foot Wave) serving as the co-studio and production company.
Grace Reiter | “Twilight (but better)” | New parody film premieres February 18
Comedian, writer, and actor Grace Reiter (Saturday Night Live) is expanding her comedic universe with Twilight (but better), her second unscripted parody film. Reiter first made waves with her viral sketches and The Hunger Games (but better), which launched on YouTube in November 2025 and has garnered more than 18 million views. Now, alongside her work on SNL and her Netflix series Minimum Wage, Reiter brings her signature blend of spontaneous comedy, sharp satire, and irreverent humor to one of the most iconic franchises of a generation. Twilight (but better) is produced by But Better Productions. Grace Reiter serves as director with Andie Schlenker and Anderson Appleby as producers, and Cindy Schlenker as co-producer. Watch the teaser trailer here.
“Response” | New short film premieres early 2027 in collaboration across the Wesley Wang and Jason RodeloYouTube channels
In this new sci-fi horror short film, a routine welfare check at a remote desert preserve turns terrifying when four park rangers discover an otherworldly presence inside a woman’s tent, leaving the youngest ranger separated from his own body. Response is produced by Wesley Wang Media with Jason Rodelo serving as director and writer.
“Extract 14” | New interactive series premieres spring 2027
A new six-episode, interactive murder-mystery series that puts viewers in the role of detective, with $100,000 on the line for the first person to crack the case. Told through found footage, digital breadcrumbs and hidden clues woven throughout each episode, the mystery unfolds over six weeks on YouTube, challenging viewers to pause, rewind, decode and collaborate to uncover the truth. The first viewer to correctly solve the case wins $100,000! Extract 14 is directed and written by Wesley Wang and produced by Wesley Wang Media.
“Absolute Cinema” | New competition series premieres spring 2027
A new series where ten aspiring filmmakers, chosen from thousands of internet strangers, are brought to New York for an immersive, seven-day filmmaking bootcamp where they’ll compete for $100,000 to bring their dream movie to life. Guided by filmmaker Wesley Wang and a panel of Hollywood industry experts, contestants will take on every stage of the filmmaking process, culminating in a red carpet premiere where the viewers get the final say in choosing the grand prize winner. Absolute Cinema is produced by Wesley Wang Media.
Aryna Sabalenka | “Aryna’s Arena: Unfiltered” | New series premiering spring 2027
She's the most dominant tennis player on the planet; and the world can't look away. Aryna Sabalenka has spent years letting us watch her win. Now cameras follow her everywhere press passes don't go. A Gucci ambassador designing her own $250K diamond necklace, a bride-to-be planning a wedding while living out of a suitcase for 300+ days a year, a daughter flying home to Belarus between slams, Aryna is building an empire in real time with the whole world watching. From UltraBoom Media, the creators of OFF DAY, a team led by Producers and Creatives from Anthony Bourdain: Parts Unknown, Aryna's Arena: Unfiltered is an evolution and expansion of Aryna Sabalenka's YouTube series (Aryna's Arena). It is a docu-reality series that lives inside her world, on the court, on the road, and everywhere in between, capturing the brand meetings and dress fittings, the late nights with her fiance, and the quiet hotel room moments where she finally gets to just be herself. Raw and glamorous with the playfulness that is uniquely Aryna, this is an unfiltered voyeuristic portrait of power, identity, chaos, and the chosen family around her that makes it all possible.
Track Star* | “Track Star Tonight” | New series now streaming, with new episodes rolling out this winter
Track Star* is a music-driven digital show hosted by Jack Coyne that brings artists and fans together. Expanding on its popular on-the-street game and Track Star Podcast, Track Star Tonight is a new, intimate live performance series featuring emerging and established artists in front of a small audience, blending live music, storytelling, and artist discovery. Following performances from artists including Remi Wolf, Kings of Leon, The Womack Sisters, Medium Build, and Florence Road, the series will continue to release new performances and collaborations through the end of 2026 and into 2027. Track Star* is produced by Public Opinion Corporation and created by Jack Coyne, Kieran Coyne and Henry Kornaros.
Ina Garten | Happy Hour with Ina Garten: “Thanksgiving Special” | New special premieres November 25 at 6:00am ET
On November 25, Happy Hour will air a special Thanksgiving episode featuring past guests and friends of Ina, including Bobby Flay, Seth Meyers, Julia Louis-Dreyfus, Jennifer Garner and, of course, Jeffrey. Ina and her friends will answer listener questions, share Thanksgiving memories, and offer hosting tips to get you inspired before the big day. Happy Hour is part of the Vox Media Podcast Network. Ina Garten serves as host with Jenna Weiss Berman, Ali Jaffe Ramis and Deborah Davis as executive producers. YouTube Unveils New Slate of Creator-Led Shows and Films
Wednesday, 18 November 2026, 06:05
Photo source: Deadline
The lineup spans projects from Nick DiGiovanni, Emma Chamberlain, Aryna Sabalenka and Phil Rosenthal, alongside three new projects from director Wesley Wang.
YouTube announced the slate at its second annual Creator Premieres event at The Hollywood Roosevelt in Los Angeles, as competitors including Netflix and Disney move further into the creator space.
This is reported by 
Deadline
.
Nick DiGiovanni, behind the world’s largest food YouTube channel, is launching Chef, a series from his production company, Knife Drop Studios. It follows professional chefs and offers a look inside kitchens around the world. Its first full-length episode, Inside the World’s #1 Pizza Restaurant, has been released.
Emma Chamberlain’s What Are You Waiting For? is due next year. Produced by Amplify Pictures with executive producer Joe Lewis, the series follows Chamberlain as she explores why people wait in line, particularly in New York.
Grace Reiter, a Saturday Night Live star who also appears in Netflix’s Minimum Wage, launched The Hunger Games (but better) parody last year. She is following it with Twilight (but better), her second unscripted parody film. Reiter directs; Andie Schlenker and Anderson Appleby are producers, and Cindy Schlenker is co-producer. The film is produced by But Better Productions.
Tennis star Aryna Sabalenka will launch the reality series Aryna’s Arena: Unfiltered next year. It will follow her as a Gucci ambassador designing her own $250K diamond necklace, a bride-to-be planning a wedding while living out of a suitcase for 300+ days a year, and a daughter flying home to Belarus between slams. UltraBoom Media is producing the series.
Former late-night host Lilly Singh is returning to YouTube with Eligible Aunties, an unscripted docu-follow series about mature women with emotional intelligence and emotional baggage, in place of shallow villa romances. It is produced by Singh’s Unicorn Island Productions.
Deadline reported in March that, after Netflix ended his food show Somebody Feed Phil, Phil Rosenthal was taking it to YouTube. Season nine is set to premiere in spring 2017 and will take the Everybody Loves Raymond creator to Greece to eat at tavernas. Lucky Bastards is producing with Banijay Americas and Banijay Rights. Rich Rosenthal is showrunner and executive producer.
YouTube is also featuring director Wesley Wang, whom the company hopes can replicate the success of Backrooms director Kane Parsons. Wang will premiere three projects on the platform, including the sci-fi horror short Response. In the film, a routine welfare check at a remote desert preserve turns terrifying when four park rangers discover an otherworldly presence in a woman’s tent, leaving the youngest ranger separated from his own body. Wang writes and directs; Wesley Wang Media produces, with Jason Rodelo serving as director and writer.
Wang’s two other projects, Extract 14 and Absolute Cinema, are launching in the spring. Extract 14 is a six-part interactive murder-mystery series that casts viewers as detectives. The first person to solve the case wins $100,000. Told through found footage, digital breadcrumbs and clues hidden throughout each episode, the mystery unfolds over six weeks on YouTube, inviting viewers to pause, rewind, decode and collaborate. Wang writes and directs the series, which is produced by Wesley Wang Media.
Absolute Cinema is a competition series featuring ten aspiring filmmakers selected from thousands of internet strangers. They will travel to New York for an immersive, seven-day filmmaking bootcamp and compete for $100,000 to make their dream movie. Wesley Wang Media is also producing the series.
YouTube does not directly fund most of its slate, but it offers promotional opportunities and a direct route to advertisers, an approach it cemented at the Upfronts in May. CEO Neal Mohan told Deadline at the time:
We fundamentally do two things: one is we help amazing creators like this build an audience, no matter where they are in the world, and that takes an enormous amount of investment in the technology and the capabilities to do that. Then, the second thing we do is we help them build businesses.
 - Neal Mohan
Sean Downey, Google President of Americas and Global Partners, said at the event:
YouTube creators are not just shaping modern culture, they are redefining the entire entertainment landscape. This slate of shows demonstrates how creators are commanding primetime attention on the big screen and building unmatched fan loyalty. For brands, this isn’t about buying thirty seconds of ad space; it’s about moving past standard commercial breaks and partnering directly with the storytellers who are defining modern culture.
 - Sean Downey
