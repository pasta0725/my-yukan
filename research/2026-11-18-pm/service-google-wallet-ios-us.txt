Google Wallet is now available on iOS in the U.S.
P.J. Linarducci
VP, Consumer Payments
Nov 17, 2026
Today, we’re bringing 
Google Wallet to iOS
 users across the U.S., making it faster to get what you need and easier to organize purchases, tickets, boarding passes, and more in one easy place. As we look toward the future of shopping, Google Wallet will also be a secure hub for managing agentic purchases across Google, and today’s update expands access to more people, no matter what type of device they use.
By automatically syncing with your Gmail and other Google apps, Google Wallet keeps your package tracking, digital boarding passes, concert tickets, and receipts anywhere easily across your devices. Here’s what else you can do with Google Wallet on iOS:
Digitize your physical cards. Quickly scan loyalty cards and gym memberships using your phone's camera to import them into Google Wallet.
Securely manage payment cards to check out faster online across Google and anywhere you use Google Pay.
Stay in control of agentic purchases made across Google. Easily view and manage order confirmations, receipts, and delivery updates all in one secure place.
Please note that tap-to-pay functionality isn't supported on iOS at this time.
Whether you're on iOS, Android, or on your desktop, Google Wallet makes managing your everyday credentials and passes simpler than ever.

Google and Apple have a long history of not launching first-party software for their rival's hardware, so it's usually a bit of a surprise when these companies manage to make nice. The latest instance is Google Wallet, which is 
available
 starting today on iOS for US users.
It's an odd integration considering Apple has its own Wallet app that's 
very similar
 to Google's offering. Both can handle digital payment tools, they store tickets and travel information and they safely store IDs and credentials.
The notable standout in today's announcement is the positioning of Google Wallet as a hub for agentic purchases made across Google tools. That seems like the only area where this could be a markedly different app experience, since Apple has been 
more
cautious
 about agentic AI than its competitors to date. The iOS version of Google Wallet also will not support tap-to-pay, but the blog post didn't specify whether that feature might be added in the future.

TL;DR
Google Wallet is now available on iOS devices.
The app is available to iOS users located in the US.
Tap-to-pay functionality is not supported on iOS yet.
If you’re an iOS device owner, then you may be aware that 
multiple Apple services went down
 today. According to the company, the outage was caused by an internal update. If you use Apple Pay, then you were probably scrambling for an alternative payment option. Well, if the outage returns, you can now turn to Google Wallet.
Today, Google 
announced
 the rollout of Wallet to iOS. With the rollout, your iOS device can now use Google’s solution for organizing purchases, tickets, boarding passes, and more. If you’re unfamiliar with Wallet, here are a few other features the app offers:
Card digitization:
 Quickly scan loyalty cards and gym memberships using your phone’s camera to import them into Google Wallet.
Card management:
 Securely manage payment cards to check out faster online across Google and anywhere you use Google Pay.
Manage purchases:
 Easily view and manage order confirmations, receipts, and delivery updates.
The iOS version of Google Wallet is missing one crucial feature, however. According to the tech giant, tap-to-pay does not currently work on iOS yet. Google did not say when this feature will become available.
Google Wallet’s rollout on iOS is a limited one, as it is only be available for iOS users in the US. The company did not say if or when it would expand the rollout to users outside of its home country.
