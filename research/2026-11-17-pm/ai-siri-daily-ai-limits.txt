iOS 27.3 Beta Adds Daily Siri AI Limits and iCloud+ Upgrades
Monday November 16, 2026 10:14 am PST
 by 
Juli Clover
With the latest iOS 27.3, iPadOS 27.3, and macOS 27.3 betas, Apple is starting to 
test limits and paid upgrades for Siri AI
 and 
Apple Intelligence
 features.
Every Apple device gets a free daily access allowance for 
Siri
 AI and ‌Apple Intelligence‌ features that rely on server-side AI processing, such as 
Image Playground
 and Clean Up in 
Photos
. When that limit is hit, there will be an option to extend usage with an 
iCloud
+ plan. Here's what will be limited after the daily allowance has been reached:
‌Siri‌ AI for multi-step, personal context requests that rely on server-side processing, such as asking ‌Siri‌ what time you need to leave from home to make it to a location on time.
Image generation and editing tools, including ‌Image Playground‌ and the AI options in ‌Photos‌ like Clean Up, Extend, and Spatial Reframing.
Workflows and automations in Shortcuts that rely on AFM Cloud and AFM Cloud Pro.
Third-party apps that use Apple Foundation Models in Private Cloud Compute for AI features.
While more complex, multi-step requests will be restricted, basic ‌Siri‌ commands will continue to be available even after a device limit is reached. Examples of basic ‌Siri‌ commands include those with a single ask, such as "set a timer for 30 minutes" or "text Eric I'm on my way." 
Apple's existing ‌iCloud‌+ plans will include higher daily usage for ‌Siri‌ AI and ‌Apple Intelligence‌, with no change in pricing. If you already subscribe to an eligible ‌iCloud‌+ plan, you will have higher usage limits. Free limits are per device, not per account, and do not roll over. 
More access starts with the 200GB ‌iCloud‌+ plan, which is priced at $2.99 per month. Users who subscribe to this plan have up to a 2x usage limit. Users who subscribe to the $9.99/month 2TB plan get up to 5x usage. The $29.99/month 6TB plan comes with up to 10x usage, and the $59.99/month 12TB plan offers up to 20x usage. Family members who share a plan will have a shared usage pool.
50GB - No limit increase
200GB - Up to 2x limit
2TB - Up to 5x limit
6TB - Up to 10x limit
12TB - Up to 20x limit
Every Apple device will use the free daily limit first, and when that's reached, ‌Siri‌ AI and ‌Apple Intelligence‌ will use the ‌iCloud‌+ paid access. In a family group, a device that hits the free limit will start drawing from the shared pool on a first-come, first-served basis. Limits will reset at around 12:00 a.m. in the user's current time zone, and in families, the reset is based on the plan purchaser's time zone.
Each of the four categories has a different limit, and Apple will likely use the beta testing period to refine those limits and make sure people aren't hitting their caps too quickly. Limits depend on feature category, request complexity, and system demand, and they could change over time. 
Apple plans to give warnings when people are approaching their limits, and a notification when the limit has been hit. From there, users will be able to purchase additional access through an ‌iCloud‌+ plan, or wait for their limit to reset.
Apple is not planning to offer additional usage beyond what's available with the 12TB ‌iCloud‌+ plan at this time.

Learn about iCloud+ with Apple Intelligence and Siri AI
Some Apple Intelligence and Siri AI features rely on powerful server-side models and have daily usage limits to ensure a high-quality, reliable experience for everyone. To increase your limits, you can upgrade your iCloud+ plan.
Apple Intelligence and Siri AI feature categories
Starting with the 2027 software releases, Siri AI and Apple Intelligence features are available for free for users with a supported device set to a supported language. Some features rely on powerful server models and have limits where increased access is available with eligible iCloud+ plans, starting with the 27.3 release. These include, but aren't limited to, features in these categories:
Siri AI
Images: Intelligent Photo Editing Tools including Clean Up, Extend, and Spatial Reframing, and Image Playground
Workflows and automation: AFM Cloud and Cloud Pro models in Shortcuts
Third-party apps using Apple Foundation Models in Private Cloud Compute
Apple implements daily usage limits on these feature categories and other features to ensure a high-quality, reliable experience for everyone. Limits may vary by feature category, request complexity, system demand, system policies, and other factors.
Some Apple Intelligence features in the Home app, such as video summaries, are available for a limited number of cameras: up to 1 camera in the 2 TB plan, up to 2 cameras in the 6 TB plan, and up to 5 cameras in the 12 TB plan.
About the Siri AI limit
Siri AI in the 2027 software releases is more capable, conversational, and personal, so you can get more done.
Siri AI includes a generous daily usage limit. If you reach it, you will be notified, and you can still make everyday Siri requests like "Set a timer for 3 minutes" or "Text Mark that Annika's party is at 123 Main Street.”
If you reach your limit, advanced requests are paused until your limit resets or you upgrade to an eligible iCloud+ plan. Advanced requests are more complex, like "Text Mark the address for Annika's party, and tell him what time we need to leave from home to make it on time" or "Add a summary of my San Francisco trip note to this email draft."
What happens when you hit your daily usage limit
When you hit your daily usage limit for a particular feature category, you’ll be notified. Each device has its own daily usage limit per feature category. Once you reach a limit, you can wait until your daily usage limit resets on that device or immediately increase your limit by upgrading to an eligible iCloud+ plan.
When your daily usage limit resets
All feature categories reset daily at the same time, and limits do not roll over to subsequent days. Your limit resets around midnight in your current time zone except:
If you’re traveling internationally, your limit resets around midnight in the central time zone of the country or region of your Apple Account.
If you share an iCloud+ plan with a Family Sharing group, your limit resets based on the time zone of the iCloud+ plan purchaser.
If you’re not signed in to your Apple Account on your device, your limit resets around 24 hours after you started using that feature category.
How to increase your Apple Intelligence and Siri AI daily usage limit
For more access, upgrade to an iCloud+ plan with at least 200 GB of storage. After you reach your daily usage limit on a device, you gain access to an increased usage limit that is shared by all devices signed in to your Apple Account and across any Family members sharing your plan, on a first-come, first-served basis.
