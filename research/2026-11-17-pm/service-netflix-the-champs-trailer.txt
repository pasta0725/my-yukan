Produced by Groenlandia, a Banijay group company, 
The Champs
 is a comedy that uses the world of sport to tell a story of encounters, human connection and personal growth.
Marco is a restless, immature basketball coach whose terrible attitude, both on the court and in his private life, costs him his job and lands him in trouble with the police. To avoid prison, Marco is forced to do community service: coaching a basketball team made up of young neurodivergent people at a parish in the province of Bologna. Initially reluctant and unprepared for this new challenge, he gradually discovers a different way of experiencing sport and human relationships. Through daily contact with the team – between practices, games, clashes and new bonds – Marco and his players get to know one another and find a new balance, on and off the court.
The Champs
 is a remake of 
Campeones
, the Spanish film that won two Goya Awards in 2019 and was a major box-office hit, grossing over €23 million.
