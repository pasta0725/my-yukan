About This Game
About This Game
In a world of beautiful silence, a killer named NULL lurks among you.
『Who's NULL?』 is an online social deduction game for 4–8 players, set in a liminal space.
In this world, you can't see the other players.
Your only clue to their identities is their unique footsteps.
Listen to the footsteps echoing through the silence and figure out who's nearby.
Roles
■ VISITOR
As a VISITOR, your goal is to escape from the strange world you've wandered into.
If even one of you escapes, you can destroy this strange world and rescue everyone else.
Search the map for scattered shape pieces and fit them into the walls to complete each shape.
Once all the shapes are completed, an exit will appear.
If even one VISITOR escapes before time runs out, the VISITORS win.
But beware: a killer named NULL is hiding among you.
■ NULL
As NULL, your goal is to kill the VISITORS and prevent anyone from escaping.
Only NULL can run, allowing you to approach other players quickly.
However, if someone hears you running, they might figure out who you are.
You can also carry pieces alongside the VISITORS and pretend to help them.
If no one escapes before time runs out, NULL wins.
■ After Being Killed or Eliminated
You can see nearby players as glitchy silhouettes.
Close the shutters at room entrances to block the way and help your side.
Nearby players can hear the shutters. That sound might help your teammates figure out what happened.
Meetings
Meetings are held at regular intervals during exploration, bringing all players together.
If someone is killed during exploration, all you're told is "Someone has died."
Who was killed, when, and where is only revealed once a meeting begins.
"Whose footsteps did you hear, and where?"
"Who was the last person in that room?"
"Who placed this piece?"
During meetings, you can listen to every player's footsteps as many times as you like.
Player 1 has the lowest footsteps, and player 8 the highest. Comparing them with your own may help you tell who's who.
Think back to the sounds you heard while exploring and compare them with what the other players say.
Vote to eliminate players you suspect of being NULL.
But eliminating an innocent teammate will make escaping that much harder.
Modes
Werewolf: A deduction-focused mode where no one knows who the NULL is.
Tag: A mode focused on escaping, where everyone knows who the NULL is from the start. There are no meetings.
Room Settings
The host can choose the following options in the room.
Recommended settings for your player count are ready from the start.
Shorten the exploration time or add a second NULL to adjust the difficulty and play style however you like.
Map: Small, Medium, Large
NULLs: 1 or 2
Time: Exploration per round (60–130 sec), number of rounds (2–5), meeting length (60–240 sec)
*2 NULLs are only available with 6 or more players.
What You Need to Play
Players: 4–8
Online multiplayer only
An external voice chat app such as Discord
There is no random matchmaking or built-in voice chat.
Create a room with your friends and enjoy the game while talking over an external voice chat app.
