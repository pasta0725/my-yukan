Imtiaz Ali's O Saathi Re with Aditi Rao Hydari, Avinash Tiwary gets release date
Imtiaz Ali's O Saathi Re, starring Aditi Rao Hydari, Avinash Tiwary and Arjun Rampal, is set to release next month. The series examines affection, inequality and separation through a divorcing couple's relationship.

Filmmaker Imtiaz Ali's upcoming romantic series, 
O Saathi Re
, starring Aditi Rao Hydari, Avinash Tiwary and Arjun Rampal, will premiere on Netflix on December 4. Created and written by Ali, the series will be directed by Arif Ali and explore the complexities of a relationship between a couple going through a divorce.
The series follows two people as they navigate love, separation and the unequal expectations within a relationship. It will look at themes such as personal identity, individual desires, power dynamics and the process of letting go.

The official Instagram handle of Netflix shared the poster of the series along with the release date. "O Saathi Re..the love story of a separating couple. Watch O Saathi Re, out 4 December, only on Netflix (sic)," read the caption. 
Speaking about the project, 
Imtiaz Ali
 described it as a modern romance with a vintage heart. He said the story explores how affection can grow even as a couple moves towards separation.
"Sometimes, love surprises you by showing itself in the most unexpected corners. This is the story of the growth of affection in a divorcing couple. Affection has a throbbing heart, love is beautiful, but a relationship can be unequal," he said.

Ali also praised the cast, saying the characters were layered and required actors who could handle their emotional complexities. The filmmaker has previously worked with Netflix on projects such as She and Amar Singh Chamkila.
About O Saathi Re
O Saathi Re
 is produced by Imtiaz Ali, Mohit Choudhary and Shibasish Sarkar under the banner of Window Seat Films. Ali is the creator and showrunner, while Arif Ali has directed the series. The writing team includes Imtiaz Ali, Amogh Kanaskar and Nidhi Sethia.
Known for films such as 
Jab We Met, Rockstar, Tamasha 
and 
Love Aaj Kal,
 Imtiaz Ali has frequently explored complicated relationships and love in his work. With
 O Saathi Re
, he returns to the romance genre in a series format.
The show will premiere on Netflix on December 4, 2026.

'O Saathi Re'
Imtiaz Ali’s next series, “O Saathi Re”, is set to premiere on Netflix on December 4, 2026. Starring Aditi Rao Hydari, Avinash Tiwary and Arjun Rampal, the romantic drama explores the changing dynamics of a relationship as a couple moves towards divorce.
Created and written by Ali, the series follows two people negotiating love, separation and the unequal expectations within a relationship. It examines questions of personal identity, individual desires and power dynamics, while looking at how affection can evolve even as two people prepare to part ways.
Ali described “O Saathi Re” as a modern romance with a vintage heart, centred on the unexpected ways in which affection can emerge. Speaking about the story, he said it explores the growth of affection between a divorcing couple, while acknowledging that love and equality do not always coexist within a relationship.

~ A love story of a separating couple finding their way through the complexities of their relationship, and a love that was never equal~  
This December, Netflix brings to its audiences  
O Saathi Re, 
a series 
premiering 
December 4
. Created, written and showrun by 
Imtiaz Ali
, and directed by 
Arif Ali,
 this Window Seat Films production stars
 Aditi Rao Hydari, Avinash Tiwary 
and
 Arjun Rampal 
in an exploration of complex emotions and a relationship in which love was never equal. 
Produced by 
Imtiaz Ali, Mohit Choudhary
 and 
Shibasish
Sarkar
, 
O Saathi Re
 follows layered characters as they navigate the imbalances that can exist within love. From finding identity and personal desires to power dynamics within relationships and the delicate balance between understanding and letting go, the series presents a mature take on love that goes beyond conventional romance.
Speaking about the series,
 Imtiaz Ali 
shares,
 “O Saathi Re is a modern romantic show with a vintage heart. Sometimes, love surprises you by showing itself in the most unexpected corners. This is the story of the growth of affection in a divorcing couple. Affection has a throbbing heart, love is beautiful, but a relationship can be unequal. To be a matched couple can often be a burden. In O Saathi Re, we see that while love is yearning, dreaming, and getting together, it can also be the process of letting go. The characters are multi-layered and lifelike, which is why we went for the highest grade of actors. The cast, headlined by Aditi Rao Hydari, Avinash Tiwary, and Arjun Rampal, negotiates steep curves and sensitive moments in the captainship of Arif Ali, the director. We’ve had a wonderful run of partnership with Netflix with the experiences of She and Amar Singh Chamkila. We couldn’t have conceived this series without them. We present O Saathi Re with love and hope people like it.”
Tanya Bami, Series Head, Netflix India
, said “
Romance is such a loved genre and yet so under served in the series format. After Musafir Cafe, we are extremely excited to bring our next offering in the romance genre - Imtiaz Ali's O Saathi Re. The complexity of this romance is what sets it apart as it takes something as familiar as love and sees it through the lens of a dynamic that always exists in a love story, raising a never asked question - is all love truly ever equal? 
Imtiaz has always had a wonderful understanding of people and the emotions that drive them and this love story through his lens of finding the deeper meaning in the everyday is something we can't wait for the audience to discover. After our wonderful partnership with him for Amar Singh Chamkila, there is something very special about seeing him bring his distinct storytelling sensibility to his next long-form series. With an incredible cast comprising Aditi Rao Hydari, Avinash Tiwary and Arjun Rampal, Imtiaz has created a world where every character brings a unique perspective to the story
."
Known for creating some of Indian cinema's most memorable love stories through films such as 
Main Vaapas Aaunga, Jab We Met, Rockstar, Tamasha
 and 
Love Aaj Kal
, Imtiaz brings a new dimension to the genre with 
O Saathi Re
. With its exploration of contemporary relationships through his  distinctive storytelling lens and Arif Ali’s deft direction , 
O Saathi Re
 promises an evocative addition to Netflix's diverse slate of Indian stories. 
O Saathi Re 
premieres December 4, 2026, only on Netflix
.
