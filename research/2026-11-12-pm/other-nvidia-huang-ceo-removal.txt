エヌビディアの取締役会がジェンスン・フアンCEOの去就を検討しているという/Sean Gallup/Getty Images
（CNN）
 米半導体大手エヌビディアの株価が、11日の時間外取引で急落した。同社のジェンスン・フアン最高経営責任者（CEO）が社内の幹部と不適切な関係にあったという報道に加え、取締役会がフアン氏の去就について協議するために会合を開いていると報じられたことを受けたものだ。
米東部時間午後6時時点で、エヌビディアの株価は8.4%下落した。
世界で2番目に時価総額が大きい企業である同社の株価は、米紙ニューヨーク・タイムズがフアン氏と幹部との不適切な関係を報じたことをきっかけに下落した。さらに米紙ウォールストリート・ジャーナルが、フアン氏解任の準備を取締役会が進めていると報じたことで下落に拍車がかかった。同氏は30年前にエヌビディアを共同設立した。
今回の新たな報道に関してエヌビディアにコメントを求めたが、現時点で回答は得られていない。
この数日前、エヌビディアの取締役会はフアン氏が「外国の諜報（ちょうほう）活動の標的」となっていたかどうかを調査すると発表していた。これに先駆け、フアン氏については前出の幹部とは別の女性と関係を持っていたという報道が浮上。米当局者はその女性を中国のスパイだと見ている。フアン氏は8日に不適切な関係があったことを認めて謝罪したが、相手の氏名は明らかにしていない。
取締役会がフアン氏の解任を決定した場合でも、同氏の後継者となるべき明確な「ナンバー2」は存在しない。フアン氏自身、以前に「後継者計画（サクセッション・プランニング）」という考え方には懐疑的だと述べている。
人工知能（AI）ブームの中心にあるこの半導体メーカーについて、投資家たちはフアン氏がトップの地位から追われた場合の先行きを懸念している。フアン氏は、AIの最も熱心な推進者かつ出資者の一人であり、投資家たちは同氏の描くハイテク業界隆盛のビジョンに惹（ひ）かれてエヌビディア株に殺到してきた。
週末にフアン氏が自身の不倫を認め、さらにエヌビディアの取締役会が調査に乗り出したというニュースが伝わると、同社の株価は9日から10日にかけて9.5%下落した。
エヌビディア株の下落はより広範な市場にも波及し、11日午後6時30分時点で、ナスダック100先物は1.1%、S&P 500先物は0.5%それぞれ下落した。

Nvidia's stock price tumbled after the close of trading on Wednesday on news that the chipmaker's 
board of directors is preparing to oust
 longtime 
CEO Jensen Huang
. 
Nvidia this week launched an investigation into Huang, who admitted to 
having an extramarital affair
. U.S. intelligence agencies have linked the CEO to a Chinese business executive, Alice Huang (no relation), who is suspected of being an asset of China's Ministry of State Security.
The Trump administration is aware of the Nvidia board's plans, sources told CBS News. The Wall Street Journal was the first to report that board members were preparing to remove Jensen Huang. 
Nvidia shares fell more than 8%, to $159.51, in after-hours trading. The drop continued a steep decline in the company's market value, which has sunk more than $450 billion since Nvidia announced the probe into Jensen Huang's conduct on Nov. 8.
Jensen Huang, who co-founded Nvidia in 1993, is seen in many circles as the face of the burgeoning artificial intelligence sector, with the company's advanced chips providing the critical computing resources that power frontier AI models.
His departure would be a major blow to Nvidia, which lacks any obvious successor to fill his shoes, according to Wall Street analysts.
"The news is obviously negative, as Jensen is (arguably) the most prominent executive at the most prominent company sitting at the center of the world's most important industry — someone who foresaw the rise of AI years before ChatGPT arrived and made sure the firm he founded would be prepared to capitalize on it," Adam Crisafulli of Vital Knowledge said in a report.
Nvidia's code of conduct
Corporate governance experts said Jensen Huang's alleged relationship with Alice Huang appears to violate Nvidia's 
code of conduct
. That policy highlights that personal relationships could create a conflict of interest for Nvidia, while Huang himself specifies in the code of conduct that the company's workers have a responsibility "to maintain high ethical standards."
Nvidia's code also instructs employees to ask themselves whether they could defend an action if it were made public on social media or known to colleagues. If not, the policy states, "do not proceed." 
In a message to company employees on Sunday, Huang admitted to having an affair, and he apologized to his wife and their two children, both of whom also work at Nvidia. "There is no excuse for what I did. I am deeply sorry," he wrote.
Nvidia's 11-person 
board
 includes Jensen Huang, although the other board members are designated as independent, according to a regulatory 
filing
. 
President Trump said this week the FBI is also investigating romantic and business ties between Jensen Huang and Alice Huang, who was a senior executive at 
Megaspeed International
. The Singapore-based company has been one of Nvidia's largest foreign customers, purchasing nearly 
$5 billion in AI chips
 and other advanced hardware.
The turmoil at Nvidia is also drawing scrutiny in Congress, with lawmakers expected to question company executives about whether its chips are being funneled to China through other countries. 
Dan Ives, a tech analyst with investment adviser Yorkville Ives, said investors will now consider who might succeed Huang, what impact that could have on the company's short-term strategy and what any government investigations might reveal.
"Jensen is not replaceable by another CEO or group of interim executives, but Nvidia likely now has to move on without Jensen at the helm, which seemed like a Twilight Zone episode narrative before Sunday afternoon when the news broke," Ives said in a report.
