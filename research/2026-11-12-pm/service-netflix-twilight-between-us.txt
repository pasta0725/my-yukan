 SEOUL, Nov. 12 (Yonhap) -- Actors Kim Go-eun and Gang Dong-won have been cast in an upcoming historical romance drama, Netflix said Thursday, in what marks their first collaboration.
 Titled "The Twilight Between Us," the action-packed series follows a man and a woman from two families that have been locked in a long feud, who are forced to marry by royal decree, according to the streaming platform.
 Gang portrays Mu-yeong, an illegitimate son of the Kang family, who lives an isolated life despite his excellent martial arts skills and noble mind.
 Kim plays Su-ryeon, the only daughter of the Han family, who is compelled to marry Mu-yeong after failing to become crown princess.
 Park Ji-eun, who penned hit dramas such as "Queen of Tears" (2024) and "Crash Landing on You" (2019-2020), wrote the upcoming drama.
 It is directed by hit producer Kim Won-suk, known for "When Life Gives You Tangerines" (2025), "My Mister" (2018) and "Signal" (2016).

Actors Gang Dongwon and Kim Goeun are teaming up for the new Netflix series "The Twilight Between Us." 
 On November 12, Netflix confirmed the production of the series "The Twilight Between Us," which has already drawn buzz for uniting director Kim Won-seok and writer Park Ji-eun, and officially unveiled its starring lineup featuring lead actors Gang Dongwon and Kim Goeun. 
 "The Twilight Between Us" is a romance-action period drama depicting the story that unfolds when Muyoung and Sooryeon, a man and a woman from two families locked in a century-long feud, are wed by royal command. The Korean title's "Hon" carries a double meaning, signifying both marriage (婚) and the "hour of twilight" (昏), the time just after sunset when the boundary between day and night blurs together. 
 Gang Dongwon takes on the role of Muyoung, an illegitimate son of the Gang family known as the "demon of the north." Possessing exceptional martial arts prowess and an honorable character, he has lived like a shadow in the north, but after his marriage, he encounters a new destiny and is set to showcase intense action and a dramatic emotional journey. 
 Kim Goeun portrays Sooryeon, the only daughter of the Han clan. Having been bound by a royal marriage ban after falling short in the selection for Crown Princess, Sooryeon is wed to Muyoung from the rival house, yet she is a multi-dimensional character endowed with inner strength that refuses to surrender to fate, alongside a bright personality. 
 Above all, the collaboration between this trusted creative team captures keen attention. Director Kim Won-seok, who proved his delicate directing in the Netflix series "When Life Gives You Tangerines" and acclaimed dramas such as "Misaeng," "My Mister," and "Signal," takes the helm. Writer Park Ji-eun, who penned mega-hits like "Queen of Tears" and "Crash Landing on You," writes the script, heralding the birth of a well-crafted historical drama spanning romance, action, and fantasy.

Netflix Announces ‘The Twilight Between Us,’ a New Period Romance Epic From the Makers of ‘When Life Gives You Tangerines’ and ‘Queen of Tears’

Netflix has greenlit production of 
The Twilight Between Us
, a romance-action period drama from director Kim Won-suk (
When Life Gives You Tangerines
, 
Misaeng: Incomplete Life
, 
Signal
, 
My Mister
) and writer Park Ji-eun (
Queen of Tears
, 
Crash Landing on You
, 
Legends of the Blue Sea
), and unveiled its lead cast: Gang Dong-won and Kim Go-eun.
The Twilight Between Us
 follows Mu-yeong and Su-ryeon, heirs to two houses locked in a century-long feud, who are forced into marriage by royal decree — a union intended by the king to end generations of conflict, but one that soon spirals in unforeseen directions.
The word 
hon
 in the original Korean title carries a dual meaning: it evokes marriage (
gyeol-hon
), but also the twilight hour of 
hon
, the fleeting moment just after sunset when the boundary between day and night dissolves. 
The Twilight Between Us
 begins at the moment Mu-yeong and Su-ryeon's fates become irrevocably intertwined, in that blurred space between light and shadow, self and other.
Gang Dong-won takes on the role of Mu-yeong, the illegitimate son of the House of Kang, who carries the ominous title “Demon of the North” and lives in obscurity despite his exceptional martial skill and noble heart. Gang, whose past roles span action, melodrama, thriller and fantasy, brings both Mu-yeong’s combat prowess and his emotional evolution to life as he enters a marriage that will alter the course of his destiny.
Kim Go-eun stars as Su-ryeon, the only daughter of the House of Han. After failing to become Crown Princess, Su-ryeon finds herself bound by the royal marriage ban — her union with Mu-yeong an unforeseen twist of fate. Kim brings her own vitality to Su-ryeon, a woman who refuses to be subdued even as she marries into her family's sworn enemy.
Director Kim Won-suk takes the helm, bringing his signature emotional depth to each character. The series is written by Park Ji-eun, known for expanding the boundaries of romance, fantasy and period drama with bold storytelling and unforgettable characters.
Two rival houses are bound by royal decree. In the hour of 
hon
, when the vow is made and light surrenders to dusk, destiny takes hold. 
The Twilight Between Us
 is coming soon, only on Netflix.
