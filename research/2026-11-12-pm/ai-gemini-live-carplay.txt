For millions of people, the daily commute averages about an hour behind the wheel. While voice assistants have long been great for quick tasks like playing a playlist or getting directions home, all that quiet time on the road is also an opportunity to catch up, prepare for what’s ahead, or simply think out loud.
Following our rollout on 
Android Auto and cars with Google built-in
, today we’re bringing the Gemini app directly to Apple CarPlay. Now, iOS users can have natural, free-flowing conversations with Gemini Live right from their car’s display — hands-free, so you can get more done while keeping your hands on the wheel and your eyes on the road.
Pick up where you left off — or start a fresh conversation
We designed Gemini on CarPlay to work like a conversational driving companion that minimizes distraction. When you tap the Gemini app icon on your CarPlay screen or side rail, you can easily pick up where you left off with a recent chat or start a fresh conversation.
You can talk back and forth with Gemini at your own pace — interrupting to add a detail, changing the subject, or pausing when you need to focus on an exit.
Make the most of your drive
Whether you’re commuting or setting out on a road trip, Gemini on CarPlay helps you be more productive during your ride. Here are a few ways to make the most of your drive:
Get ready for the day ahead:
 Use your morning commute to see what's coming up and walk into your first meeting prepared. Ask Gemini what's on your calendar, when you need to leave for your next appointment, or talk through what you want to say before you arrive.
Try saying:
 "What does my day look like?" or "Help me prep for my 10am with the design team."
Talk through your inbox:
 Because Gemini connects with your Google Workspace apps like Gmail and Google Calendar, you can ask for your daily brief
1
, catch up on unread emails, get quick summaries of long email threads, and talk through a quick reply using just your voice.
Try saying:
 “Give me my daily brief for today,” or “Catch me up on my unread emails and summarize Sarah’s Q4 budget thread.”
Explore local spots and landmarks:
 Ask Gemini for recommendations in the area you’re heading to, from a quick place to grab lunch to the backstory on a local landmark.
Try saying:
 “What’s a quick lunch spot in downtown Austin that’s easy to get in and out of?”
Brainstorm, rehearse, and learn out loud:
 A quiet car cabin is one of the best places to think out loud. Use Gemini Live as a sounding board to practice an upcoming presentation, prepare for a tough conversation, or break down a complex topic you’ve been curious about.
Try saying:
 “Let’s roleplay my Q4 pitch — act as a skeptical VP and challenge my strategy,” or “Break down how solid-state EV batteries work in simple terms.”
And because your CarPlay conversations sync automatically with the Gemini app on your iPhone, every summary, note, and brainstorm is waiting in your chat history the moment you park.
Get started today
Gemini on Apple CarPlay is rolling out globally starting today to all Gemini users on iOS across every supported Gemini Live language.
To get started, make sure you’ve updated to the latest version of the Gemini app on your iPhone, enabled microphone permissions, and used Gemini Live on your phone at least once. The next time you connect your phone to your car, tap the Gemini icon on your CarPlay display, then start a new conversation or pick up a recent one.

Google Says Gemini App Rolling Out on Apple CarPlay Starting Today
Wednesday November 11, 2026 11:50 am PST
 by 
Joe Rossignol
Google has 
announced
 that a dedicated Gemini app on Apple CarPlay is rolling out around the world in several languages starting today, allowing you to have conversations with the AI assistant right from your vehicle's dashboard.
Gemini conversations on your iPhone automatically sync to CarPlay, so you can continue a previous conversation or start a new one.
For example, you can ask Gemini to "Give me my daily brief for today" and it can give you a personalized answer based on your information in apps such as Gmail and Google Calendar. Or if you are driving around and looking for a restaurant, you could ask "What's a quick lunch spot in downtown Austin that's easy to get in and out of?"
To get started, make sure you have the latest version of the Gemini app on your iPhone. Then, tap on the Gemini icon on CarPlay to start a conversation.
CarPlay gained support for voice-based conversational AI apps starting with iOS 26.4. AI apps that have already rolled out CarPlay support include OpenAI's ChatGPT, Anthropic's Claude, X's Grok, Microsoft's Copilot, Perplexity, and others. iOS 27 also added Siri AI support to CarPlay, complete with a Siri app.
Gemini was already available within the Google Maps app on CarPlay.

Google、Gemini アプリを Apple CarPlay でも展開。Gemini Live も利用可能に
2026
11/12
2026年11月12日
Masahide Omura
当サイトは Google Adsense、Amazon アソシエイト等 アフィリエイト広告を利用して収益を得ています.
Google は 2026 年 11 月 11 日 (現地時間)、Gemini アプリを Apple CarPlay に対応させ、iPhone と接続した車のディスプレイから Gemini Live で会話できるようにしたことを
発表
しました。
現在、Gemini Live に対応するすべての言語で、iOS ユーザー向けにグローバルで展開が始まっています。
目次
CarPlay の画面から Gemini Live を起動
これまで、CarPlay で Gemini を呼び出せるのは、2026 年 10 月に展開が始まった
Google マップのナビゲーション中
に限られていましたが、今回の対応で、CarPlay でも Gemini アプリ単体で会話できるようになっています。
CarPlay の画面で Gemini アプリのアイコンをタップすると、直近のチャットを再開することも、新しい会話を始めることもできます。また、話の途中で割り込んで補足したり、話題を変えたりできるほか、運転に集中したいときは会話を一時停止することもできます。
画面には波形だけが表示され、Gemini が聞き取り中か、考え中か、応答中かがわかります。左上には、会話を終了するボタンとミュートするボタンがあります。
CarPlay での会話は iPhone の Gemini アプリにも自動で同期され、あとからチャット履歴から確認できます。
運転中に使える主な用途
Google は、運転中の使い方として次のような例を挙げています。
1 日の準備:
 予定の確認、次の予定に合わせた出発時間の確認、会議前に話す内容の整理
受信トレイの確認:
 今日のまとめ、未読メールの確認、長いスレッドの要約、返信内容の相談
周辺の検索:
 目的地周辺で昼食をとれる店探し、ランドマークの歴史の説明
話しながらの練習や学習:
 プレゼンの練習、難しい会話の準備、気になるテーマの解説
Gmail や Google カレンダーなどの Google Workspace アプリと連携しているため、「今日の予定を教えて」、「午前 10 時のデザインチームとの会議の準備を手伝って」のように話しかけるだけで、予定やメールの内容を確認できます。
なお、
今日のまとめ (Daily Brief)
 を含む一部のパーソナライズ機能は、事前に有効にしておく必要があります。
利用の条件
CarPlay で使うには、iPhone の Gemini アプリを最新版に更新し、マイクの使用を許可したうえで、iPhone で Gemini Live を 1 回以上使っておく必要があります。
条件を満たした状態で iPhone を車に接続すると、CarPlay の画面に Gemini のアイコンが表示されます。
