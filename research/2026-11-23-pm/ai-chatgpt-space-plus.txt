OpenAI brings its collaborative Space hubs to Plus users
You can upload documents, files and more to the hubs for easy sharing.
By
Igor Bonifacic
Nov. 20, 2026 1:00 pm EST
OpenAI
Add Engadget on Google:
Preferred Source
Google Discover
Back at its Dev Day event in September, OpenAI 
debuted ChatGPT Space
. It's a hub that allows you, your coworkers or friends and any 
Dot agents you've set up
 to share information and collaborate on documents and other files. On release, ChatGPT Space was available to users on the company's Pro, Business and Enterprise plans. Now, OpenAI is expanding availability to those paying for its $20 per month Plus subscription. If you're one those people, you can create your own Spaces starting today.
You can create your first one by selecting "Space" from the left-hand navigation bar. It's where the Library section used to live. From there, click or tap the plus icon to make your first Space, and select a template or start uploading pages, slides, spreadsheets, images or links to websites. "Pages are a great place to start: they're a new type of document built specifically for the work you do with agents, like writing, visualizing, researching and coding," OpenAI explains. "Pages can stay automatically updated from your connected tools, too."
Spaces work with 
ChatGPT's recently revamped Scheduled Tasks
 feature, and you can invite others to collaborate on your projects with you. As a Plus user, you can't use a Dot with your Spaces (or at all, for that matter), but that's likely to change soon, with OpenAI 
starting to test the agents with a select group of Plus subscribers as of yesterday
. As for use cases, the company suggests you can use a Space to create a city guide for a friend that plans to visit your home soon. As you find new spots, you can add them to the guide for ChatGPT to organize. For students, you can use the feature to create a study guide with 3D visuals and questions to quiz yourself. As with most of these kind of tools, a little bit of creativity goes a long way.

Introducing ChatGPT Wallet
ChatGPT Wallet helps your dot go from finding what you need to making a purchase on your behalf. Whether you’re shopping for a new sweater or booking a hotel, you can ask your dot to help and use Wallet when it’s time to check out. OpenAI has started rolling out 
ChatGPT Space
 to subscribers on the 
Plus
 plan. According to the ChatGPT release notes entry dated November 20, 2026, Space is now reaching Plus users aged 18 and older globally, and it takes over the place that Library used to occupy in the ChatGPT sidebar as the home for pages, slides, images and uploaded files.
Until this week the feature was limited to paid tiers above Plus. OpenAI introduced Space on September 29, 2026 for eligible Pro, Business and Enterprise users in the ChatGPT desktop app and on the web, and it added ChatGPT Slides to the same group of plans on October 16. The November 20 update is the first time the individual Plus subscription receives the workspace, which brings document-style pages, collaborative presentations, comments that can call on ChatGPT, and pages that refresh themselves from connected apps and tools.
The rollout is gradual. OpenAI describes it as "rolling out", so some Plus accounts will see the new Space entry in the sidebar before others, and as of November 21 the plan comparison table on the ChatGPT pricing page still showed Space only in the Pro column.

WHAT CHATGPT SPACE GIVES PLUS SUBSCRIBERS.
Space gathers the output that ChatGPT produces into one place instead of leaving it scattered across conversations. OpenAI's documentation lists several kinds of content that live there: pages, which are editable documents combining text, images, charts and interactive elements; ChatGPT Slides presentations; spreadsheets for tables and calculations; Sites, the websites and small tools built with ChatGPT; and the images and files a user has uploaded or saved.
A page can start from a template, from an existing conversation, or from a blank document where the user types or pastes notes. ChatGPT can be opened alongside the page to revise it, and the user can also edit the text directly. Pages support child pages, and related pages can be grouped into a space dedicated to a topic or a team, with collaborators invited at the level of the whole space or of a single page.
The feature that separates Space most clearly from the old Library is automatic refresh. The November 20 entry says Plus users can set pages to update from their connected apps and tools, and OpenAI's feature page describes recurring automations built from instructions, sources and a frequency. A page that tracks to-dos, for example, can pull items from Slack, email and calendar and keep due dates and next steps current. The sources available depend on which apps the user has connected, such as Google Drive or Slack.
ChatGPT Slides builds a deck from a conversation, an outline, source files or a reference template. Text, images, shapes, tables and charts can be edited directly, or the user can select an element and choose "Request edits" to have ChatGPT change it. Decks can be presented from ChatGPT with speaker notes, and on the web they export to PowerPoint, PDF or Google Slides.

SHARING RULES FOR CHATGPT SPACE PAGES AND DECKS.
Collaboration is the part of the rollout with the most restrictions for Plus users. People with edit access can work on the same page or deck at the same time, each with their own ChatGPT, and comments can tag ChatGPT to request a change. Pages offer view, comment and edit roles, while slides support only view and edit.
The November 20 release notes set two limits on who can see shared material. Opening a shared page or presentation requires view permission and a Plus, Pro, Business or Enterprise account with Space access, which means Free users cannot open them. Public links are also not offered. A Plus subscriber who wants to show a page to a client or a friend on the free tier therefore has to export the content or ask that person to use a paid account.

Projects are untouched: when Space launched for Pro and business plans, OpenAI stated that existing Projects remain separate, and the Plus announcement does not change that.
Users who relied on Library to find generated images and uploaded files will now find them under Space, which offers views for all saved work, items the user created and items shared with them, plus search by name. Anyone planning to share pages outside their own account should check whether the recipients pay for ChatGPT before choosing Space as the delivery format, since free-tier recipients cannot open the content.

CHATGPT SPACE ON PLUS: CONFIRMED DETAILS AND GAPS.
The release notes confirm the essentials: Space is rolling out globally to Plus users aged 18 and older from November 20, 2026, it replaces Library, it includes interactive pages and presentations, it supports collaborators and pages that update from connected apps, and shared content can be opened only by paid accounts with Space access, with no public links.
Several points are not documented. OpenAI has not published storage quotas, limits on the number of pages or collaborators, or the frequency limits for automatic page refreshes on Plus, and it has not said whether those differ from Pro. The plan comparison on the pricing page had not been updated when this report was written, and OpenAI has given no completion date for the gradual rollout. Plus subscribers who do not yet see Space in the sidebar are still inside that rollout window, and the absence of public links remains the main practical limit for anyone who wants to publish pages to a wider audience.
