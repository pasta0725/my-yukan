Dungeon Baller Turns the Hero Into the Pinball and Adds a Backpack Build
New Zealand studio Lazy Bones brought Dungeon Baller to WePlay Expo 2026 in Shanghai on November 21 and 22. The pinball roguelike is scheduled for March 2027.

Why it matters
Making the hero the ball lets the project turn pinball's lost-ball penalty into roguelike damage and loot, which is why the run-based structure holds together at all.
Key Facts
Lazy Bones is a New Zealand indie studio.
Dungeon Baller was exhibited at WePlay Expo 2026 in Shanghai on November 21 and 22.
Dungeon Baller is scheduled for release in March 2027.
The game has 10 heroes, each with a different fighting style.
Dungeon Baller includes over 250 types of items.
Programmer Jeremy Teal said the hero becomes the ball, and falling between the flippers costs health instead of a ball. Ten heroes are planned, and the game lists over 250 item types.
Ten heroes are planned, each with a different fighting style, and a preview plays before you pick one. Items fit into a backpack grid on the right of the screen, so what you pack becomes the build; chests drop bags that offer three items, one to keep. Along the dungeon, minigames based on retro arcade machines pit the player against a goblin merchant named Grabfoot. Vending machines replace a shop, and gold comes from the dungeon. Nudging the table becomes a dash ability, and overusing it triggers a tilt lock curse that cuts control for several seconds.
