Gemini アプリ、約 3 秒の GIF アニメーション作成に対応。日本でも無料で利用可能に
2026
11/21
2026年11月21日
Masahide Omura
当サイトは Google Adsense、Amazon アソシエイト等 アフィリエイト広告を利用して収益を得ています.
Google は 2026 年 11 月 21 日、Gemini アプリで約 3 秒でループする GIF アニメーションを作成できるようになったことを
発表
しました。
この GIF は Google の最新の動画生成モデル「
Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash
」で生成され、日本を含む 8 か国で、Google AI プランユーザーだけでなく無料のユーザーも利用できるとしています。
目次
GIF の作成方法と使い方
Gemini アプリのチャットから、画像または動画のツールを選び、形式の選択肢から GIF を選んで作りたい内容を入力すると、GIF が生成されます。
写真にループの効果を加えたものや、ミーム風の GIF を作れるほか、画像を参照としてアップロードすることもできます。
筆者の 
Google Workspace
 アカウントでも利用できるようになっており、実際に 
Gemini 4 Hydrogen
 で試してみました。
作成した GIF は、ドキュメント、メール、メッセージ、SNS などのアプリにコピーして貼り付けられるほか、.gif 形式でデバイスにダウンロードして保存できます。
対象のユーザー
Google によると、Google AI プランに加入しているユーザーだけでなく、オーストラリア、カナダ、フランス、ドイツ、日本、韓国、英国、米国では無料のユーザーでも利用できるとしています。
ただし、Google によると段階的に提供されているため、まだ利用できない場合があることに注意してください。使用量の上限については明記されていませんが、画像または動画の上限に準拠すると思われます。

Generate videos with Gemini Apps
You can create videos or animated GIFs in minutes in Gemini Apps with Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, our latest AI video generation model. Gemini Omni makes creating and editing videos as easy as having a conversation. Blend text, images, and video to bring your ideas to life – whether you're creating for fun, sharing with friends, or visualizing a concept.
What you need
To use this feature with a personal account, you must have a Google AI plan. 
Learn how to upgrade
.
To use this feature with a work or school account, you must have a qualifying Workspace license. 
Learn more about feature availability for Workspace plans.
For now, this feature isn’t available to users under 18.
To use this feature, you must be signed in to Gemini Apps. 
Learn how to sign in to Gemini Apps
.
To create GIFs you must be in Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Japan, Korea, the UK, or the US.
Tip:
  The ability to create GIFs is rolling out gradually and may not be available to you yet.
Important:
 When you generate videos with Gemini Apps, remember that you agreed to Google's 
Terms of Service
, including the 
Prohibited Use Policy
. You must have rights to any images that you upload. Be sure not to violate others' rights, including copyright or privacy rights. The violation of the Prohibited Use Policy or the rights of others could result in the termination of your account. Use your discretion before you rely on, publish, or use videos that Gemini Apps generate. Do not generate or share videos to deceive, harass, or harm others.
  Learn about Gemini Omni
Omni, our advanced video generator, offers the following capabilities over prior models:
Video to Video Edit:
 Upload a video and instruct the model to apply edits*.
Video length:
 Videos can be from 3 seconds to up to 10 seconds long or up to 15 seconds for users with an AI Ultra subscription.
Multi-turn editing:
 Continually edit a video within a single conversation. Each turn can include image or video references.
GIF creation:
  Use text, images, and/or videos to create short looping GIFs (~ 3 seconds long) that can be easily copied, downloaded, and shared.
Avatar creation and sharing
: Record your face and voice to create a likeness that can be used in generated videos
Aspect ratio control
: Choose the  aspect ratio of the video before it’s created.
For text prompts, the default is landscape. If you upload a reference image or video, the aspect ratio will match your uploaded content.
Templates: 
Get instant inspiration with a premade template. Apply a curated style to see what’s possible in a single tap. 
* Uploading videos to use for video edits and avatar creation and sharing aren’t available in the EEA, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, and some US states.
Generate a video or GIF
Important: 
Only upload photos you have the right to use.
On your computer, go to 
gemini.google.com
.
On the top left, click Open Sidebar 
Create video
.
(Optional) Select a template for your video or GIF.
In the text box, enter a prompt of the video or GIF you want to generate.
To create a video or GIF from images or videos, click Add image 
 and/or Add video 
 and select the files you would like to upload.
You can upload one video and up to 5 images.
To use your avatar in a Gemini Omni video or GIF, type 
@
[your Google username] in your prompt.
If someone has shared their avatar with you, type  @[their Google username] to include them in your video or GIF.  
Learn more about avatars
.
Click Submit 
.
Tips:
You can brainstorm and refine your prompts within your main chat with Gemini before generating the video or GIF. If so, you won’t need to tap Video to create it.
It may take a few minutes for the video or GIF to be generated.
While your video or GIF is being generated, you can’t interact with the same chat. You can start a new chat and come back to view your video or GIF later.
For videos, you can also ask Gemini to generate audio along with your video.
Edit a generated video or GIF
After generating a video or GIF, you can make edits across the full video with a prompt to do things like:
Remove or replace objects and characters.
Change camera angles.
Modify the scene.
Extend the video length up to another 10 seconds.
Export or download a generated video
To export or download a video:
Under the video, click Share 
Click Share on YouTube 
 or  Download video 
.
Share or download a GIF
You can copy & paste your GIF and it will appear as an animated GIF in many applications including docs, emails, messages, and social media.  
You can also download it to your device in 
.gif
 format.
Why Gemini Apps might remove or not generate a video
Gemini Apps may remove videos when our systems detect a possible violation of Google's 
Terms of Service
, including the 
Prohibited Use Policy
.
