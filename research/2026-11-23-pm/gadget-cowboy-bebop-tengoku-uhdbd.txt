Cowboy Bebop: The Movie Gets 4K Blu-ray Release February 26
The 25th anniversary edition adds a new commentary by Koichi Yamadera, Megumi Hayashibara, and Aoi Tada.
By
Anime.com Newsroom
·
November 20, 2026

Official source
View original
Bandai Namco Filmworks announced on Friday that it will release 
Cowboy Bebop: The Movie
 on 4K Ultra HD Blu-ray in Japan on February 26, 2027, for the film's 25th theatrical anniversary.
The 4K Remaster Memorial Edition pairs a 4K Ultra HD Blu-ray with a Blu-ray Disc and costs 19,800 yen including tax. It carries a new audio commentary by 
Koichi Yamadera
, 
Megumi Hayashibara
, and 
Aoi Tada
. The video extras include the theatrical teaser and trailer, the textless opening and ending, and footage of the October 31, 2026 stage greeting for the 4K remaster. A storyboard collection and a booklet with new interviews with director 
Shinichiro Watanabe
 and composer 
Yoko Kanno
 come in a box with new art by character designer 
Toshihiro Kawamoto
. Both discs carry Japanese and English subtitles.
A Blu-ray edition in the EMOTION Big Jacket Collection, with a 32-centimeter square jacket that uses the same art, releases the same day for 8,580 yen including tax. It holds the same Blu-ray Disc and extras.
The 4K remaster returned to Japanese theaters on October 30. Bones animated the film, which opened in Japan in 2001.
