The Elder Scrolls 5: Skyrim
 is officially 15 years old today, having originally launched on the 11th November, 2011.
And no, Bethesda doesn't have a new re-release lined up or anything like that, so the memes will have to be put on hold for now.
But that doesn't mean the developer's forgotten about its most successful RPG. To celebrate the occasion, it's reduced 
Skyrim: Special Edition's
 price by 80% on PS5 and PS4, cutting it all the way down to $7.99 / £6.99.
Buyers can then upgrade to the Anniversary Edition, which, as far as I can tell, still costs $19.99 / £15.99. Anniversary Edition is the one that bundles all of the 
Creation Club stuff
 into the deal.
If you somehow haven't played Skyrim before, or you've just never got around to it on PS5, it's a decent discount — although it's also worth pointing out that the Special Edition is currently available through a 
PS Plus Extra
 subscription as well.
Next up, Bethesda's curated a Creations (premium mods verified by the developer) bundle for the anniversary. It throws together five of the most highly rated Creations available at a heavily discounted price.
The bundle includes:
Arquebus (a unique Dwemer weapon)
Custom-Voiced Follower - Kaitryn Fair-Shadows (a new follower)
Legacy of Orsinium (a full series of quests with new locations, enemies, and equipment)
Legendary Dungeons: Dwarven Delves (a selection of new dungeons with new enemies and treasures)
Shadetree Lodge (a new player home that can be customised)
If you were to buy all of these Creations separately, they'd set you back 3,000 credits — roughly $28 / £27, since you have to buy separate 'Creation Credits' packs.
This bundle is going for 1,500 credits, which amounts to buying 500 and 1,000 Creation Credits at $15 / £14.
Obviously Skyrim's fan-crafted DLCs aren't everyone's cup of tea, but some of them really do fall in line with what you'd expect of an official release. Legacy of Orsinium in particular is a borderline expansion, and it's got a lot of fans.
Beyond all of this, 
The Elder Scrolls Online
 is unsurprisingly getting in on the action. You can unlock Skyrim-themed rewards by taking part in the MMO's current 'Golden Pursuit' campaign.
"Fans can also expect a week full of giveaways, community spotlights, and social content," Bethesda's press release adds.

Today marks the 
15th anniversary
 since 
Bethesda Game Studios
 released 
The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim
. The game had a lot of hype following the acclaimed releases of Morrowind and Oblivion, but it went on to become even more popular than both combined, at least in terms of sales. 
Last we heard this past July, the game had sold 
over 65 million units
 across all platforms, which puts it in the 
All-Time Top 10 best-selling premium games chart
 (roughly tied with another stellar open world action RPG, CD Projekt RED's The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt).

To this day, it's Bethesda's most played game, primarily thanks to one of the most vibrant modding communities in all of gaming. On Nexus Mods, one of the biggest modding websites, there are 
144K mods
 for The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition, more than any other PC game listed there. Perhaps the most impressive figure is the total downloads: SSE's mods have been downloaded 
13.7 billion times
, far exceeding the combined downloads of the other Top 20 most downloaded games. Actually, the third game on the list is the original version of Skyrim, which has 1.9 billion downloads of its own.
The developer did not prepare a big update 
as they did for the 10th anniversary in 2021
. However, perhaps fittingly given how critical modding was and still is to Skyrim's continued success, they have 
released
 a new 
Anniversary Creations Bundle
 that includes five premium mods:
Legacy of Orsinium
 by Veelpot - A professionally voiced orc-themed faction storyline with multiple endings, new dungeons, armor, and artifacts.
Kaitryn Fair-Shadows
 by anbeegod - A young companion, a lorekeeper of the Reach who specializes in magic and healing. Voiced by 
Chloe Elmore
, Kaitryn responds to Skyrim's main quest, DLC storylines, every guild questline, and Daedric quests, plus select side quests and world details. She also has her own approval system and can be romanced and married.
Legendary Dungeons: Dwarven Delves
 by Trainwiz - Stone Garden and Frostvault, two sprawling dungeons from The Elder Scrolls Online, reimagined in Skyrim with new factions, enemies, items, and secrets.
Arquebus
 by Shoeburglar - The new Dwemer dungeon of Thuncharnthumz, north of Cradlecrush Rock, features powerful automatons and the titular weapon, a hand cannon powered by the Archery skill tree, which can be earned upon completion of the quest.
Shadetree Lodge
 by TheRealElianora - A new player home by one of the most famous housing modders within the community, located just outside of Riften.
These Creations would normally be available for 
3,000 credits
, or around $28, but the bundle cuts that down in half (
1,500 credits
, or around $15).

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Strategy Guide
, signed by Todd Howard and members of the Bethesda Game Studios team. The grand prize winner will also receive 
exclusive 15th-anniversary commemorative posters
 and a custom Displate metal print; the five winners will be randomly selected in a week, on November 18. You can read the official giveaway rules 
in this PDF
.

The Elder Scrolls Online: Season Two —Echoes of Skyrim
, adding the Greymoor Chapter to the base game at no cost to all players. Greymoor brings iconic Skyrim destinations such as Solitude and Blackreach, along with the Antiquities system and the Dark Heart of Skyrim saga. The Markarth DLC also became free as part of the ESO base game on November 9, adding the Reach zone and the epic finale to Greymoor's main story. To celebrate ESO's 15th anniversary, the game is also hosting a 
free, limited-time Golden Pursuit campaign
. Players can earn Skyrim-themed rewards by exploring Western Skyrim through December 2.
Besides the official activities, modders 
continue to impress by creating brand-new improvements for the game
 and updating older mods. One of the best-rated and most downloaded ones, 
Beyond Skyrim: Bruma
, just received a massive 2.0 update for the 15th anniversary. Beyond to various additions and bug fixes, this new version also makes the mod compatible with PlayStation consoles.
In other Elder Scrolls news, Bethesda 
confirmed
 earlier this month the full title of Skyrim's sequel: 
The Elder Scrolls VI Sentinel
. Unfortunately, credible sources have since shared that 
the game is at least two more years away
, thus tentatively placing its release between late 2028 and 2029. Thankfully, modders appear to be nowhere near finished with Skyrim.
