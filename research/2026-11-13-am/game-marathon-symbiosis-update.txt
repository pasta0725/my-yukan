First look: 
Marathon
 Symbiosis gameplay trailer reveals what’s coming December 8
Available to all players, Symbiosis features new PvE and PvP modes, areas to explore, weapons, and more
Open Play Week December 8-15, with limited-time Crew Up rewards available through December 22
Marathon
 is evolving. Symbiosis marks the first step in that journey, expanding the ways Runners can explore, survive, compete, and crew up on Tau Ceti IV. Today, Bungie revealed a new gameplay trailer showcasing the update’s new PvE and PvP modes, updated Perimeter zone, and more ahead of its December 8 launch.
The Symbiosis update is available to all players and features:
Perimeter zone update:
 Fungal growth and alien life have transformed Perimeter, with new playable spaces including the underground Burrows and the submerged Reservoir. The Tick Queen also lurks deep beneath the surface…
Expedition:
 Phase 1 of the new PvE-only mode built around a three-run series. Play solo or with a crew, take on new combat and environmental challenges without PvP threats, and use Flash Boosts to shape your build during a run.
Deathmatch [Beta]:
 An experimental PvP mode featuring bite-sized 3v3 Team Deathmatch matches in which two crews battle for points without risking their permanent loadout
Limbo:
 A new social space where Runners can connect, test weapons and gear in firing ranges, and challenge each other in the dueling pit
Bishop Runner shell:
 A new Runner shell built around the fantasy of a fast, surgical blade-wielding robot
New weapons:
 The 
V17 Flamethrower
 brings the heat and short-range area denial while the 
Maverick
 introduces a new revolver archetype with a hand-cannon feel
And more!
Open Play Week & Crew Up
Open Play Week begins alongside Symbiosis’ launch on December 8, giving everyone the opportunity to experience Tau Ceti through December 15. Additionally, players who crew up with their Bungie, PlayStation 5, XBOX Series X|S, and Steam friends can earn a suite of limited-time rewards through December 22. More info on Crew Up is available here.

Bungie has served up our first proper look at Marathon's big 
Symbiosis update
, which is set to go live on December 8 with a permanent PvE mode, plus an all-new Team Deathmatch mode and a social space called Limbo.
Symbiosis represents the start of "the evolution of Marathon," Bungie said, with "a plethora of updates and changes" to the game. First and foremost is the addition of Marathon's PvE mode, now permanent and with a new name, Expedition, that's described in the teaser as a "co-op PvE roguelite." Details beyond that haven't been revealed just yet, but Bungie called it "phase one" of the team's PvE plan, suggesting that it remains a work in progress.

Also notable in Symbiosis is the addition of Limbo, a "social space" where players can hang out without getting aired out (although there does appear to be a little area set aside for friendly shootin'), and its firing range, a handy way to test the game's guns without having to venture into its less friendly locales.

Speaking of which, the Perimeter map is being expanded with new points of interest and tunnels, and a new sword-wielding runner shell, Bishop, will debut with the update. For those who prefer the PvP life, the update will also kick off a beta for Marathon's Team Deathmatch mode.
Overall, Symbiosis looks to be a pretty big deal, and while today's tease is light on detail, the early reaction among players on 
Reddit
 and social media seems quite positive. Bishop is already racking up fans:

One other bit of good news: The rollout of Symbiosis on December 8 will also mark the start of an "open play week," during which the game will be fully free to play, including the new modes and content. If you dig it and decide to stick around (ie., buy the game), any progress and items earned will carry over.

===== https://www.psu.com/news/marathon-symbiosis-update-out-dec-8-with-new-pve-pvp-modes/
(status 200)
Bungie has announced that the Symbiosis update for 
Marathon
will launch on December 8, 2026, and comes with a wealth of fresh content including brand new PvE and PvP modes.
First up, the perimeter zone has been updated with fungal growth and alien life transforming the area, with new payable spaces such as the underground Burrows and submerged Reservoir now available. Expedition meanwhile is the first phase of a new PvE-only mode built around a three-run series, where you’ll play solo or with a crew and face new combat and environmental challenges.
Also new is Deathmatch, an experimental PvP mode that offers 3×3 Team Deathmatch bouts where two crews compete for points without risking their permanent loadouts. Also on the table is Limbo, a new social space where Runners can meet up, hit up the firing range to test weapons and gear, and challenge each other in the duelling pit. Players can also obtain the Bishop Runner shell, plus new weapons such as the V17 Flamethrower and Maverick.
Finally, Bungie revealed that Open Play Week will run December 8 — 15 so you can take Marathon for a spin during this time.
