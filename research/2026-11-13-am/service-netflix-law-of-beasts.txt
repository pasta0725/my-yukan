One of the shortest 
Netflix
 dramas of the month is also one of the heaviest in subject. 
The Law of Beasts
, a four-part Polish drama from writer and director Kasper Bajon, premieres on Netflix on Wednesday 18 November, and Netflix released its official teaser on 3 November. With four episodes of about 45 minutes each, the full series runs roughly three hours.
The story
The series opens in 1988. Police Major Anna Bersz is investigating the murders of four boys, and her friend, the psychiatrist Agnieszka Grabska, is asked to assess whether the suspect can be held criminally responsible. The decisions each woman makes end their friendship. Twenty-five years later, the convicted killer is due to be released, and both are forced back into the case and what it cost them.
The drama is inspired by the case of Mariusz Trynkiewicz and by the law it prompted in Poland, known as the Beasts Act. The two women at its centre are fictional. That distinction matters: this is a drama built around real events, not a reconstruction of them, and its interest lies in the people who had to decide what justice required.

How it was announced
Netflix first unveiled the series on 26 August, already with its 18 November date, as a four-part drama based on the true events that led to Poland’s Beast Law. The teaser followed on 3 November, a little over two weeks before launch.
The cast and the team
Agnieszka Grochowska plays Anna Bersz and Agnieszka Żulewska plays Agnieszka Grabska. Michał Pawlik plays Trynkiewicz.
Bajon directs and wrote the series with Anna Kasińska. Edyta Krajewska produces for the Polish company Entrego. Bartłomiej Kaczmarek is director of photography, Piotr Waglewski composed the score, Anna Marzęda designed the production and Magdalena Bem-Jankowska the costumes.
Where it fits
Netflix’s autumn slate already mixes true-crime documentary, such as 
The Fall of El Mayo
, with international drama, such as Lee Chang-dong’s 
Possible Love
. 
The Law of Beasts
 sits between those poles: a scripted series, made in Polish, about a legal and moral question rather than a manhunt. For viewers who prefer their crime drama compact, four episodes is a modest commitment.

Two friends, two perspectives, and a case that resurfaces after 25 years. Agnieszka Grochowska and Agnieszka Żulewska star in a four-episode drama exploring the limits of friendship and justice. Netflix presents the teaser for 
The Law of Beasts 
directed by Kasper Bajon. The series premieres November 18 on Netflix.
The Law of Beasts
 is a scripted series about the limits of friendship and justice, and the responsibilities of those who represent the state and its institutions. The story is inspired by the case of Mariusz Trynkiewicz and the events that led to the legislation known in Poland as the “Beasts Act.” The story unfolds through the perspectives of two women: Anna Bersz and Agnieszka Grabska. Both face the question of how to protect society while upholding the rule of law, and their decisions put their friendship and professional convictions to the test.
What is 
The Law of Beasts 
about?
In 1988, Major Anna Bersz leads an investigation into the murders of four boys. Her friend, psychiatrist Agnieszka Grabska, is tasked with assessing the criminal responsibility of the suspect, Mariusz Trynkiewicz. Under pressure from both time and the system, the two women must make decisions that end their friendship and shape the rest of their lives.
Twenty-five years later, the convicted murderer is due to be released. Anna is now a government official, while Agnieszka is a respected expert in psychiatry. Both must confront the case once again, along with the consequences of their past choices.
The Law of Beasts 
follows people representing the state and its institutions as the law, a sense of justice, and the need for safety pull them in different directions. Anna and Agnieszka’s relationship, and the price each pays for her decisions, give this conflict a deeply personal dimension.
Is 
The Law of Beasts
 inspired by true events?
The Law of Beasts
 is inspired by the events that led to the legislation known in Poland as the “Beasts Act.” Anna Bersz and Agnieszka Grabska are entirely fictional characters, and their personal stories and friendship were created for the series. Through the experiences of people making decisions on behalf of institutions, viewers can explore the social, moral, and legal consequences of the case.
Who is in the cast of 
The Law of Beasts
?
Agnieszka Grochowska
 and 
Agnieszka Żulewska
 play the leading roles.
Grochowska plays Anna Bersz, a determined major in Poland’s then police force who seeks to secure the perpetrator’s conviction. Years later, as a government official, she becomes involved in the case again, driven by her sense of responsibility for the safety of others.
Żulewska plays Agnieszka Grabska, a psychiatrist tasked with providing an impartial assessment of the suspect’s mental state. Her professional duties clash with external pressure and expectations of a particular outcome.
Michał Pawlik
 plays convicted murderer Mariusz Trynkiewicz. As the end of his sentence approaches, he once again becomes the focus of events and a dispute over what should happen to him after his release from prison.
All four episodes of 
The Law of Beasts 
will be available November 18, 2026, only on Netflix.
