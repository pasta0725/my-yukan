TL;DR
Samsung’s W27 is a China-only premium foldable based on the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra, with luxury styling and golden accents.
It has an 8-inch main display, 6.5-inch cover screen, Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy, 16GB RAM, and up to 1TB storage.
The phone includes a 200MP camera, 5,000mAh battery with fast charging, satellite support, and starts at CNY 18,999.
Samsung has launched another foldable phone, the Galaxy W27. Exclusive to the Chinese market, it is a more premium version of the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra. It features a dual-tone design with premium golden accents and is 
positioned
 for the business and luxury segments.
So, how is it different from the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra? Let us check out its design and specifications
Samsung W27 is a premium version of the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra
The Galaxy W27 has an 8-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X foldable screen and a 6.5-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X cover display. Both screens feature up to 3,000 nits of peak brightness and support for HDR10+. 
The cover screen offers a 2,520 x 1,080 pixel resolution and a 20:9 aspect ratio, while the foldable screen provides a 20.9:18 aspect ratio and a 2,504 x 2,256 pixel resolution.
Abhijeet Mishra / SamMobile
Samsung W27
The device's frame is crafted from Advanced Armor Aluminum, and it carries an IP48 rating for debris and water resistance. Its cover screen is protected by a Corning Gorilla Glass Ceramic 3 panel. The phone also features a side-mounted capacitive fingerprint reader and the “Heart to the World” emblem on the back.
Samsung has equipped the phone with a 200MP primary rear camera with OIS, a 50MP ultrawide camera with autofocus, a 10MP telephoto camera with 3x optical zoom, and two 10MP selfie cameras. 
All of the cameras can record 4K 60fps video, while the primary and ultrawide rear lenses can shoot up to 8K 30fps video.
Under the hood, the device is powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy processor, paired with 16GB of RAM and either 512GB or 1TB of internal storage. 
It runs Android 17 and One UI 9.0 out of the box and will receive seven major Android OS updates. The phone also supports Galaxy AI and Samsung DeX.
The W27 packs a 5,000mAh battery that supports 45W fast wired charging and 20W Qi2.2 fast wireless charging. Other connectivity features include GPS, 5G, 4G LTE, Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6.0, UWB, NFC, and a 5Gbps USB Type-C port. It even offers direct satellite connectivity via Tiantong satellite communication for emergency calls and messages.
Pricing of the Samsung W27 in China
Samsung will offer the W27 in China in two colors: Jihu Blue and Jixia Hong. Pricing starts at CNY 18,999 (~$2,850) for the model with 16GB of RAM and 512GB of storage, while the 1TB storage variant is priced at CNY 21,999 (~$3,300).

Samsung W27 Display and Design
The main folding display measures 8 inches and has a QXGA+ resolution of 2504 x 2256 pixels. The 6.5-inch cover display has an FHD+ resolution of 2520 x 1080 pixels. Both screens support adaptive refresh rates from 1Hz to 120Hz, HDR10+ and brightness of up to 3,000 nits.
Corning Gorilla Glass Ceramic 3 protects the rear and cover screen. The phone weighs 215g and measures 8.9mm when folded and 4.1mm when unfolded. Its dual-tone red and blue finish is paired with gold-finish trim. Samsung includes a Kevlar magnetic case and a 45W 
charger
 in the box.
Snapdragon Processor, Cameras and Battery
Samsung W27 uses the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy, built on a 3nm process and clocked at up to 4.74GHz. Graphics are handled by the Adreno 840 GPU. The phone comes with 16GB RAM and a choice of 512GB or 1TB storage.
The rear camera setup includes a 200MP main camera with an f/1.7 aperture and optical image stabilisation, a 50MP ultra-wide camera and a 10MP telephoto camera with 3x optical zoom. There are 10MP cameras on both the inner and outer sides for selfies and video calls. Video recording supports up to 8K.
A 5,000mAh battery powers the foldable. Charging options include 45W wired charging and 20W wireless charging. The W27 runs 
Android

Satellite Communication and Other Features
The Samsung W27 supports Tiantong satellite communication. Eligible activations include one year of satellite access, 300 minutes of satellite voice calls and 100 satellite text messages. Availability and activation conditions apply to the satellite service.
