Buenos Aires, November 24, 2026
 – 
Gordon
, the new series made in Argentina, starring Rodrigo de la Serna and directed by Pablo Trapero and Pablo Fendrik, is now on Netflix. This thriller, inspired by true events, required a major technical and artistic effort to recreate the historical settings of 1970s Argentina. Hundreds of professionals took part, with extensive work on set dressing, makeup and costume, and visual effects.
The numbers behind the seven episodes
After four months of pre-production, 
Gordon
 shot for 
109 days
 between July and December 2025. The crew included a core team of 
163 technicians
, joined by 
310 additional technicians
. In front of the camera, 
258 actors and 3,720 extras
 brought to life the different historical settings the story moves through.
One of the production's main challenges was recreating 1970s Argentina. To bring back the spaces and everyday objects of the era, the team worked across 
98 locations
 and more than 
200 dressed sets
, using 
611 period vehicles
 and more than 
50,000 props
. Recreated spaces include the Devoto prison, the Bariloche bank and the Automotores Orletti clandestine detention center. The chapel of Father Carlos Mugica was also built from scratch in under two weeks.
The action scenes also demanded significant technical effort. They involved 
28 stunt performers
 and 
230 prop weapons
 adapted to fire blanks, with approximately 
1,100 shots fired
 during filming.
The series includes 
six long takes
. The standout is the Bariloche bank robbery in the first episode, about six minutes long. It required taking over the city center and coordinating more than 
200 crew members and suppliers
. The scene was shot 21 times to reach the final result.
Pablo Trapero said:
 "Our shot needed the same precision as Gordon's plan."
Extensive post-production followed, with 
720 visual effects shots
. These were key to recreating the historical settings, removing contemporary elements and completing the series' visual world.
Synopsis:
Inspired by true events, this thriller follows the transformation of Aníbal Gordon, a common criminal who, without ever ceasing to commit crimes, became a ruthless para-state agent amid the political violence of 1970s Argentina. Based on the novels 
Gordon
 and 
Gordon, el aniquilador
 by Marcelo Larraquy.
