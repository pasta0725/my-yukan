Say what you want about Elon Musk, and you do, he makes vehicles that remain popular buys, even as used cars.
The Tesla Cybertruck, even though it was recently canceled, is the number one-selling used car in America. That’s based on analysis of over 1.1 million used-vehicle sales from October 2026 by the site iSeeCars.com. It includes the fastest-selling trucks, and the 25 fastest- and slowest-selling vehicles overall.
In fact, EVs take 25 percent less time to sell than gas vehicles in the used market, and among those EVs there are three Teslas in the top 25, including the Cybertruck, Model 3, and Model Y. And 16 of the 25 fastest-selling used vehicles are EVs.
What does that say?
“Why do so many electric vehicles, including the recently canceled Cybertruck, sell so rapidly on the used market? The combination of sustained high fuel prices and a dramatic drop in market value essentially ‘flips the script’ on EVs when they transition from new to used status,” said iSeeCars Executive Analyst Karl Brauer.

Here are some numbers:

Used EVs spend 25.2 percent less time on the market than gasoline vehicles, while hybrids spend 10.7 percent less time on the market than gasoline models.
