Netflix announced on Wednesday, Nov. 18, that production has started on Patricia, a film starring Penélope Cruz and written and directed by Juan Diego Botto.
Cruz also produces the film through Moonlyon, the production company she launched in 2022 under the umbrella of IMAGINA, the name Grup Mediapro has used since Jan. 1. Netflix said Patricia is Moonlyon’s first narrative feature. Cruz said it is her first film with Netflix. It is the second film Botto has written and directed, and the two worked together before on En los márgenes.
Netflix describes the story this way: Cruz plays Patricia, a woman who escapes a twelve-year abusive relationship and then faces a court fight in which her children’s welfare is at stake. The streamer’s release, issued from its Spain office, gave no further plot detail.
The cast includes Goya Toledo, Rafa Castejón, Pilar Gómez, Dylan Radley and Ramón Barea, according to Netflix, among others. The release lists earlier credits for several of them: Toledo in Amores Perros and Marsella, Castejón in The Good Boss, Gómez in Tarde para la ira, Radley in The Nest and Barea in Lullaby and Everybody Knows. For Cruz it names La Bola Negra, Bunker, Volver and Pain and Glory.
Botto said he spent two years writing the script and that the research included hours of interviews with women who shared their stories. “It is a privilege for me to tell this story with this cast and this creative team,” he said in the release.
Cruz said she had spent more than two years developing the film. “It is a very necessary story, and an immense privilege to play this character,” she said. She added that she was grateful for Netflix’s response in Spain since the company read the script. “This is my first film with them, and we couldn’t be happier with the care and attention they have shown us,” she said.
Diego Ávalos, quoted in the release, said he was honored to join Botto and Cruz on “their first film for Netflix in Spain.”
Netflix has not announced a release date, a filming location or whether Patricia will play in theaters before it streams. Netflix’s release says Moonlyon has several projects in development led by Cruz. When Netflix sets a date, this story will be updated with where to watch in the US and the UK.
What happens next
Release date: Netflix has not announced one, and has not said whether the film will have a theatrical run.
Filming: Netflix announced the start of production and gave no location or wrap date.
