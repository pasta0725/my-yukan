Netflix has set December 3, 2026, for Jão: Tales from the Void. The documentary is listed as 
Jão: Memórias Póstumas
 in Brazil. Netflix’s new trailer follows the singer through a break from public life, the loss of his father, work on an album and a return to the stage.
Where to find the trailer and watch the film
Netflix’s title page
 is the place to look for the trailer and the film itself. The December 3 date comes from Netflix’s Brazilian announcement. Netflix’s English press release confirms the name 
Tales from the Void
. The announcements give no exact streaming hour or full list of regions and subtitle languages.
The teaser and the new trailer are separate releases. The teaser debuted at Jão’s September 25 comeback show in São Paulo. The November trailer arrived with the December premiere date. That September show matters to the story too, since Netflix describes the film as following the period that led back to the stage.

How the album and the book fit in
Memórias Póstumas
 is also the name of Jão’s 14-track album, released on July 26, 2026. The documentary follows the making of that record among other events. If you want to hear the music before watching the film, 
the album’s tracklist on Apple Music
 is a useful starting point.
In a July interview with Hugo Gloss, Jão traced the album title to Machado de Assis and said he already had the idea for the title song in 2024, before his father died. That chronology matters because the loss became part of the record’s story after the idea had taken shape. Machado’s novel appears in English as 
The Posthumous Memoirs of Brás Cubas
. The literary connection is through the title, while Netflix’s film follows Jão’s own life.

Almost a decade later, the murder of María del Carmen Martinez, the widow of a rich banker, remains a mystery.
In December 2016, Martinez was shot twice in the head in a family-owned car dealership in Alicante, Spain. Her son-in-law, Miguel López, who managed the dealership, was the sole suspect, though he was acquitted by a jury in 2019. Since then, the case has been shrouded in mystery.

Jones sadly passed away earlier this year on May 22, 2026, aged 95, making this documentary a poignant exploration of his life.

Jean-Michel Basquiat

Not many artists’ names come with as much esteem and weight as Jean-Michel Basquiat. This documentary debuted at the Tribeca Film Festival before heading to Netflix in December.

A Deadly Affair: The Pamela Smart Story
Picture Credit: Netflix
Coming to Netflix:
 December 16
In 1991, Gregg Smart was shot dead in the New Hampshire home he shared with his wife, Pamela. Soon after, she and her teenage lover, Billy Flynn, were accused of his murder. She would become known as the ‘most hated woman in America,’ and her subsequent trial sparked one of the biggest media storms at the time. 
Fast forward to today, and Pamela gives her own account of her husband’s death and the trial. The documentary pieces together decades of interviews and archival footage from the police department and the courtroom. It’s the most in-depth documentary on the case yet, asking the question: Was Pamela Smart a grieving wife or a criminal mastermind?
“As one of the first murder trials to be televised gavel-to-gavel, the Pamela Smart trial was in many ways the birth of true crime as entertainment,” Lyttanya Shannon said. “In an age where this genre has become so familiar, what interested me from the outset was the opportunity to understand more about how this dynamic played out while it was still a novel context, and to what extent our main contributors believed it influenced the outcome.”
