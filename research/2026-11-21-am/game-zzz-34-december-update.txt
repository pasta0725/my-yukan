===== https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/zenless-zone-zero-version-3-4-update-the-finality-of-the-day-launches-december-2
(status 200)
The version 3.4 update for 
Zenless Zone Zero
, dubbed “The Finality of the Day,” will launch on December 2, developer 
miHoYo
 announced. 
Here is an overview of the update, via miHoYo:
The story of the Sky Island of Roscaelifer reaches its final climax, as Sunbringer, a new Void Hunter and the first Enchainer Agent, joins the fight. The new version also kicks off the 
Zenless Zone Zero
 × 
Honkai: Star Rail
 collaboration, introducing two crossover Agents, Robin and Kafka, along with themed events. It also brings new combat gameplay, updates to permanent game modes, and a variety of version rewards.
In Version 3.4, Roscaelifer’s main story is about to reach its climax. From the Bangboo corruption incident to High Ambitions Corp.’s invasion, the Proxies and their allies have weathered crisis after crisis together. Now, the enemy has stepped out of the shadows, and Roscaelifer faces a decisive battle for its very survival. For the first time, the three Sunbringers — Dracaene, Lindverne, and Eldreda—will fight side-by-side, joining the Proxies to take on a formidable foe and defend Roscaelifer. New characters and menacing enemies are taking the stage, while the arrival of an unexpected character leaves even more mysteries hanging over this new chapter. How will this storm change the fate of the Celestial Nexus? What trials await the Proxies and their allies? All will be revealed in “The Finality of the Day.”
Once this version update goes live, Sunbringer, leader of Roscaelifer, joins the fray as an S-Rank Genesis Flare Armorer Agent, bringing a unique offensive and defensive rhythm through a three-member synergistic combat style: Eldreda, Lindverne, and Dracaene take turns stepping in with a lance, greatsword, and shield, seamlessly balancing offense and defense. Their Armorer attacks can stack Gash on enemies, and consuming Gash triggers Maim while building up Wishfire. Once Wishfire is sufficient, Eldreda can hold Basic Attack to hurl her lance and make her entrance, following up with a synergy Chain Attack. When Decibels are sufficient, they can unleash a two-stage Ultimate that detonates all Embermark stacks on the enemy at once, triggering Emberburst for massive damage. When the new version launches, Proxies can obtain Sunbringer and the S-Rank Armorer W-Engine Dragonfire Mirage via Signal Search. On top of that, the new Bangboo Sleepwalkerboo will also arrive in the Bangboo Channel, summoning Bangboo soldiers to back you up in combat and boosting Sunbringer’s damage when Sunbringer is on the squad.
The two crossover Agents also bring their own distinct combat styles. Robin can keep up her Concerto performance off-field, granting teammates shields while boosting their DEF and DMG, and can extend her support effects by re-entering the field. Her Ultimate can also restore Sharpness for Armorer Agents. Meanwhile, Kafka, an S-Rank Electric Agent, builds her offense around Attribute Anomalies and Disorder DMG. While in the Hunter’s Overture state, she can choose between slashing or shooting attacks, enhancing direct DMG or speeding up Attribute Anomaly buildup, respectively. When her slashes hit an enemy afflicted with an Attribute Anomaly, she can also trigger a Lightning Surge to deal additional DMG. Robin’s brand-new outfit, Cosmic Beat, will also be made available. Sunbringer’s and Robin’s Signal Search channels will open on the first day of the update and run throughout Version 3.4, while Kafka will arrive in the second half of the version. As for reruns, the S-Rank Physical Anomaly Agent Alice and the S-Rank Anomaly W-Engine Practiced Perfection will return in the second half of the version.
❮
❯
Beyond combat, Dracaene, Lindverne, and Eldreda will each have their own Trust Events, showcasing the distinct daily lives of the three Sunbringers. Once all three reach max trust level, Proxies will also unlock a special side quest to help secretly plan their birthday party. Their new daily wear outfit, Moonlit 
Promenade
, reveals a side of the three Sunbringers as they step into city life, letting Proxies experience up close Dracaene’s gentle sweetness, Lindverne’s playful personality, and Eldreda’s bold, free-spirited charm. The Moonlit Promenade outfit set and its matching Starlit Stroll Limited Gift will also be available. The latter includes a three-person Accompany 
Experience
 and an exclusive Idle Action, so Proxies can enjoy their everyday company on the streets of New Eridu. A dynamic wallpaper and three matching Inter-Knot avatars will also arrive with this version.
As for events, the collab event “Lost in a Theatrical Dream” between 
Zenless Zone Zero
 and 
Honkai: Star Rail
 kicks off in Version 3.4. Proxies will team up with Robin, Kafka, and their New Eridu companions to film a series, featuring stealth raids and Agent interactions. Complete the event to score rewards like the collab Bangboo Pom-Pom and Polychromes. On top of that, the new version also brings the “Opening Act: Enter the Knight” combat mode and a new “Dark Dungeon Survival” season for Enigma of the Labyrinth. Proxies can join in to earn Polychromes, a profile icon, new accessories, and more.
As for rewards, after the Version 3.4 update, log in to claim the limited collab S-Rank Agent Robin for free, along with the upgrade materials needed to raise her to Lv. 40 and a full set of Drive Discs with matching Main Stats. During the version, Proxies will also receive overclock materials for the W-Engine Twilight Afterimage and all remaining Agent Focus: Pyrois for free, and check-in and web events will be available. To thank Proxies around the world for sticking with us, starting January 1, Proxies can claim up to 1,600 Polychromes in total through daily login check-ins. During the version, Zenless Zone Zero will also partner with Make-A-Wish on a charitable initiative, inviting the community to join Sunbringer in helping support wishes for children with critical illnesses. The “Filmgoer Thank-You Gift” cumulative top-up event will also run during the version.
