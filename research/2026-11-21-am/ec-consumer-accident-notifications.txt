Japan Reports 38 Serious Consumer Accidents: Multiple Mobile Battery Fires on a Train and in Homes
Portable power banks are everyday essentials for commuters and students in Japan. A November 19, 2026 report from Japan's Consumer Affairs Agency logged 30 newly notified product fires, including a mobile battery catching fire inside a train.
On November 19, 2026, Japan's Consumer Affairs Agency announced 97 newly notified consumer accidents, including 38 serious incidents (30 product fires and 8 service-related accidents).
Among the 30 fires were 3 lithium mobile battery charger fires (in Mie inside a train, Hokkaido, and Osaka), 2 portable power station fires while charging, and 1 smartphone fire while charging in Shizuoka.
The agency notes these are preliminary notifications under investigation and urges residents to check recall notices and handle and dispose of lithium-ion batteries properly.
