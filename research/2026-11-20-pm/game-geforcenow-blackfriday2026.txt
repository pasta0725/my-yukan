NVIDIA has announced new Black Friday 2026 promotions for GeForce NOW, bringing back discounted six month memberships and introducing a separate offer for customers subscribing through Amazon. The company has also expanded its cloud gaming service to additional Fire TV devices and added nine games to its supported library.
The Black Friday promotion offers 30% off six month Performance and Ultimate memberships for new subscribers and eligible customers upgrading their plans. The discount is available until November 30, 2026, or until the promotional allocation runs out.
Meanwhile, customers purchasing eligible GeForce NOW memberships directly through Amazon can receive a 90% discount on their first month. This separate offer remains available until December 31, 2026.
GeForce NOW Six Month Plans Return With Black Friday Discounts
NVIDIA is temporarily bringing back its six month Performance and Ultimate subscription options as part of its Black Friday campaign.
Both membership tiers are available at a 30% discount for qualifying customers.
The promotion is aimed at new subscribers and eligible customers who want to upgrade their existing subscriptions.
The offer provides an alternative to paying for a subscription every month, although NVIDIA has not specified the final promotional prices in its announcement.
The two Black Friday offers have different eligibility requirements and expiration dates.

NVIDIA GeForce NOW Expands to More Amazon Fire TV Devices
Alongside the discounts, NVIDIA has expanded GeForce NOW compatibility to additional Amazon streaming hardware.
The service now supports the Fire TV Stick HD, Fire TV Stick 4K Select, and Fire TV Stick 4K released in 2026.
The expansion includes support for Amazon's Vega OS platform, allowing compatible Fire TV hardware to access games through NVIDIA's cloud servers.
You can get started by installing the GeForce NOW application on a supported Fire TV device, signing in to your account, and connecting a compatible gaming controller.
Because the games run remotely, the Fire TV device does not need the processing power required to render modern PC games locally.
Instead, it receives the gameplay stream over an internet connection while sending your controller inputs to NVIDIA's servers.
The experience will depend on factors such as connection quality, network latency, and the membership tier being used.
The new compatibility options make it possible to access supported PC games on a television without connecting a dedicated gaming computer.

GeForce NOW Still Requires Game Ownership or an Eligible License
An important distinction between GeForce NOW and services such as Xbox Game Pass is how access to games works.
A GeForce NOW membership generally provides access to NVIDIA's remote gaming hardware rather than a complete library of games included with the subscription.
To play a supported title, you typically need to own it through a compatible digital storefront or have access through an eligible subscription such as PC Game Pass.
This means that subscribing to GeForce NOW does not automatically provide ownership of the nine games added this week.
There are also monthly usage limits associated with the service, so the amount of available cloud gaming time depends on your membership terms.
The latest update gives NVIDIA customers more hardware options, additional supported games, and temporary subscription discounts.
With the six month promotion ending on November 30 and the separate Amazon offer running until December 31, customers have two different ways to reduce their initial GeForce NOW subscription costs during the 2026 holiday shopping period.
