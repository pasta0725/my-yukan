Uber has opened its ride-hailing service to children as young as 10 in three U.S. cities as the company tests whether there’s a market for shuttling tweens without an adult by their side. The program, called 
Uber Kids
, launched quietly over the past few weeks in Austin, Texas, Nashville, Tennessee, and Madison, Wisconsin.
The program, which builds on Uber’s existing teen ride-hailing service for passengers ages 13 to 17,  is the latest bid by Uber to expand its customer base. Uber is describing this as a limited pilot program, which means it may never expand beyond these cities or even exist by next year. But if successful, it could open up a potentially lucrative market for Uber: busy families with active kids. 
The Uber Kids program is stacked with mandatory requirements for parents and drivers. Parents or guardians are the only ones who can order a ride for their child, which they can do by adding the child to their family profile and completing a mandatory onboarding that explains the built-in safety features. A parent or guardian must be present at the pickup locations for the trip to begin and also have to include a note to the driver that explains the drop off. 
Meanwhile, only highly rated drivers who have been invited are allowed to participate. Those drivers are required to complete an education module that includes step-by-step instructions and must be opted into live video streaming so parents can watch the trip in real time, according to Uber. 
Uber said it also requires PIN verification for every Uber Kids trip, and says all recordings will be encrypted and not available to the driver, and will use sensors and GPS data to detect if the ride goes off course, stops unexpectedly, or ends early.
Drivers who are willing to adhere to those rules to shuttle kids will get a pay bump for the effort, according to Uber. Eligible drivers will earn $7 more on every Uber Kids trip compared to a standard one, the company says on the dedicated Uber Kids web page. Drivers will receive an extra $20 for the first Uber Kids ride they complete.
The extra compensation underscores a key challenge Uber faces as it expands into this younger age group — how to convince drivers to take on that added responsibility. If the program does expand it could eventually compete with existing transportation services such as HopSkipDrive and Zum. 
Uber opened its ride-hailing service to minors in 2023 when it launched 
Uber for Teens
, although the company had conducted early pilots several years prior. As the program expanded nationwide, Uber introduced a live video streaming feature.

FAQs
Who can ride with Uber Kids?
Down
The Uber Kids pilot, where available, offers dedicated rides for children ages 10–12 who don’t require a booster seat. As the guardian linked to your child’s profile, you request every ride and stay in control of booking, destination changes, and communication with the driver. You or the adult assigned to pickup, as well as other riders ages 10–17 with their guardian’s permission, can also ride along with your registered child.
Can other people ride with my child?
Down
Yes. You or the adult assigned to pickup, as well as other riders ages 10–17 with their guardian’s permission, can ride along as long as your registered child is on the trip. All minors need permission from their parent or legal guardian. Uber Kids trips must be direct, single-stop rides.
How is Uber Kids different from a regular Uber ride?
Down
Children ages 10–12 aren’t allowed to ride unaccompanied on regular Uber ride options such as UberX. Uber Kids is designed for children in this age group, with rides requested and managed by their linked guardian. Your child’s ride includes: eligible drivers who complete mandatory Uber Kids education, required audio and video recording, live video for guardians, an assigned adult and PIN verification at pickup, guardian-controlled destinations, and access to live human support. You can follow the ride, contact the driver, and share trip updates with trusted contacts from your own Uber app.
Where is the Uber Kids pilot available?
Down
Uber Kids is currently available to select riders in pilot cities. Check the Uber app for availability, and make sure you’ve selected your child as the rider to see the Uber Kids option while booking a ride.
How do I request an Uber Kids ride?
Down
Create a profile for your child in your family hub. When booking, select your child from the “For Me” dropdown, or tap “Book a ride” from your child’s profile. Enter the trip details, select Uber Kids, and follow the prompts to add pickup and dropoff information.
Does my child need their own Uber app or account?
Down
No. Your child doesn’t need their own Uber app or account to ride with Uber Kids. You create their profile, request their rides, and manage their trips through your Uber app. They will receive trip information and status by SMS in the phone number registered for them.
What hours are Uber Kids rides available?
Down
You can request Uber Kids rides from 5 AM to 9 PM daily. Ride requests aren’t available between 9 PM and 5 AM. Rides are subject to driver availability.
What are some ways that I  can help my child prepare for their first Uber Kids ride?
Down
Before the ride, talk through who will be with your child at pickup and what to expect at dropoff. Explain that the pickup adult will help them identify the car and share the PIN with the driver before the trip begins. Remind your child to buckle up and keep their seat belt on throughout the ride.
Let your child know that you can follow the trip, contact the driver, and watch live video from your Uber app. Make sure they know how to contact you, call the support number in their trip text messages, or call 911 in an emergency. You can also take a practice Uber Kids ride together to help your child get familiar with the experience.
Talk your child through dropoff, too: if you’ve assigned an adult to meet your child, they should meet that person before getting out of the car. If no adult is assigned, explain where your child should go after getting out in a safe place, and ask them to let you know they’ve arrived.
Does an adult need to be at pickup?
Down
Yes. You or an adult you assign in the app must be with your child at pickup. The adult helps your child find the car and gives your unique PIN to the driver. The driver must enter the PIN to start the ride. If the assigned adult isn’t there, the driver can’t pick up your child.
You don’t need to personally meet your child at dropoff. You can assign another trusted adult, and we recommend having an adult meet your child when they arrive. Dropoff notes are required; add clear instructions when requesting the ride and follow the app’s prompts for any required dropoff adult.
Can my child ride if they need a booster seat?
Down
No. Your child must meet your state’s 
legal requirements

Age alone doesn’t determine whether a child is ready to ride without a booster. The child needs to fit the vehicle’s seat belt properly—usually around 4 feet 9 inches tall. Legal requirements vary by state. 
If your child still needs a booster for the seat belt to fit properly, Uber Kids isn’t an option for them yet. Learn more about 
booster seats and seat belt fit from the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety.

You don’t need to be the adult who meets your child. Follow the app’s prompts for any required dropoff adult. If you assign someone to meet your child, they’ll need to confirm their name with the driver before your child gets out of the car.
Dropoff notes are required. Add clear instructions so the driver knows where your child should go when they arrive.

Can my child or the driver change the destination?
Down
Only you can change the destination in the app. If your child needs to go somewhere else, update the destination yourself and let your child and any assigned dropoff adult know about the change.
Your child and the driver can’t change the destination. Drivers also aren’t allowed to cancel a trip midway, end it early, or drop your child off away from the designated dropoff location. You can follow the trip and contact the driver through your Uber app. If you have concerns during the ride, contact Uber Support or an ADT safety agent through the app. In an emergency, call 911.

Can I contact the driver during the ride?

Yes. You can call or message the driver through the Uber app. Your phone number and the driver’s phone number stay private. Keep your phone nearby in case the driver needs to reach you.

You can connect with Uber Support and ADT safety agents through the app during the ride. If there’s an emergency, call 911.
Your child can also call the support number in their trip text messages directly or call 911 in an emergency. Before the trip, make sure your child knows how to reach you and get help.
How can my child get help during a ride without their own Uber app?
Down
Your child can call you or the support number in their trip text messages. They don’t need an Uber account or app to make these calls. Before the ride, show your child where to find the support number and talk through when and how to get help. In an emergency, they should call or text 911.
