We've spent most of 2026 seeing the consequences of other developers trying to get the hell out of 
GTA 6
's way – which, for most of us players, has meant a whole lot of new games to play in September and October and very few to choose from in November. One of the few brave enough to take on GTA 6 head-to-head is Binding of Isaac developer Edmund McMillen, and he's not 
too
 worried about going up against the titan.
The Binding of Isaac: Repentance+ is a free DLC expansion that launched on PC back in 2024, and is now coming to consoles on November 19, the same day that GTA 6 launches. Apparently, this was the idea of publisher Nicalis, and McMillen agreed that it would, if nothing else, be pretty funny, as he tells 
Eurogamer
.
"There's a possibility [Repentance+'s release date] wasn't that stupid an idea," McMillen says, "and if it was a stupid idea, it's a funny stupid idea." But he does have experience with having a game's thunder stolen by a much bigger competitor, as was the case when he launched strategy roguelike Mewgenics just a few weeks ahead of roguelike deckbuilder Splay the Spire 2 earlier this year.

"My heart says it doesn't matter, but my brain has shown me it does," he says. "When Mewgenics came out it blew up; we went past two million sales almost right off the bat. It was such a blasting, skyrocketing thing for like three weeks. Then Slay the Spire 2 came out, and it sucked everybody out. The same players who liked Mewgenics, they were Slay The Spire players. So they all went woosh and right over."
Clearly, it's a much different prospect with Binding of Isaac DLC and GTA 6 – a cult roguelike with a devoted audience versus the biggest entertainment launch in, well, maybe ever. That's very different from two hot new roguelikes trading the same group of genre enthusiasts back and forth, and McMillen is hopeful, as many industry analysts are, that GTA 6 could help get people buying and playing console games again.
"Logically, there's a record number of people who are going to be on the console the game is on at launch [because of GTA 6]," McMillen says. "They might be like me, play [GTA 6] for a while, and say, 'Okay, I have a console with one game, what am I going to play next?' Then, you know, a rising tide will raise all ships. I think Grand Theft Auto will bring people back to consoles, because consoles are abysmal right now. Sales are bad and no one is playing anything."

The Binding of Isaac: Repentance+ comes to PS5, Xbox Series X|S and Nintendo Switch 2 on Thursday, November 19, 2026, the same day on all three. Nicalis has not announced an unlock hour for any platform, so plan on the night of November 18 in North America, when digital console releases usually go live.
This is the complete game, Rebirth plus every expansion up to Repentance+, with online co-op for up to four players, which has never been on console before. Below: each platform’s date, format and price, what time to expect it, what is in the package, and what does and does not carry over from the version you may already own.
When Repentance+ Comes Out on Each Platform
Nicalis set the date in its 
announcement teaser
 on August 18, naming November 19 for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Nintendo Switch 2 alike. The same post says Switch 2 gets both physical and digital copies, while PS5 and Xbox are digital only.

What Is in Repentance+
Nicalis’s announcement and the Nintendo eShop listing describe the same package:
The complete game.
 The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth plus every expansion: Afterbirth, Afterbirth+, Repentance and Repentance+, with the rebalanced items and fixes from years of updates.
Online co-op for up to four players
 in all game modes, including daily runs and challenges, and Greed mode according to the Nicalis Store. You can play with friends, start or join a match with strangers, or use 
Quick Match
 to join random players.
Filters for hosting and joining
 online matches, and new online leaderboards.
An Item Information option
 that shows what items you have already picked up do.
The numbers:
 over 700 items, 34 playable characters, more than 330 enemy types and 120-plus bosses and mini-bosses, per the eShop listing, plus a Bestiary that records the enemies you meet.
What Carries Over, and What Does Not
Owners of the 2021 console version.
 Nicalis has not announced an upgrade path, discount or save transfer from The Binding of Isaac: Repentance on PS5, Xbox or Switch to Repentance+. Nicalis calls Repentance+ the first time Isaac has online co-op on console, so the 2021 version is local co-op only.
PC players.
 On Steam, Repentance+ has been out since November 18, 2024, as a 
free DLC update
 for Repentance owners. The console release is sold as a full game instead.
Crossplay.
 Nicalis has not said whether console players can play online with each other across platforms or with PC.
Online subscriptions.
 The Nintendo eShop listing marks online play as a Nintendo Switch Online feature, with cloud saves supported. Online multiplayer on PS5 needs PlayStation Plus, and on Xbox it needs Game Pass Essential or a higher tier.
Should You Wait for Launch Night?
If you play on Switch 2 and want a physical copy, pre-ordering is the only way to have it in hand on the day, since retailer stock decides when it reaches you. If you play digitally, there is no pre-order bonus listed anywhere, so there is no harm in buying on November 19.

Frequently Asked Questions
When does The Binding of Isaac: Repentance+ come out on PS5?
 November 19, 2026, as a digital release, per Nicalis. The PlayStation Store listing was not up yet as of November 11.

 Nicalis has not announced a time. Digital console releases usually unlock around midnight on release day, so the night of November 18 into November 19 in North America is the time to check.
Is Repentance+ coming to Xbox?
 Yes. Nicalis lists Xbox Series X|S for November 19, digital only.

 Yes. Up to four players online, in all game modes, with Quick Match and online leaderboards.
Is Repentance+ free for people who own Repentance on console?
 Nicalis has not announced a free upgrade or discount for the console version. On PC, Repentance+ was a free DLC update in 2024.
Is Repentance+ on the original Nintendo Switch?
 Nicalis lists only Nintendo Switch 2 among the Nintendo platforms.
