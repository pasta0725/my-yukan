Mark Zuckerberg is bringing Meta’s first AI hardware device, 
the Muse Charm
, to the market soon. While we wait to get an official release date for the pocket-sized device, a new report has revealed the potential pricing for the Muse Charm, and the additional perks it may bring with it.
As per a report from The Information, Meta may price the Muse Charm at $300 (roughly Rs 28,000) in the US. But, the report adds, that this price will not just bring you this pendant-like AI device. Rather, the Mark Zuckerberg-led firm is also planning to give you up to 12 months of free 5G data from US carrier Verizon.

The Muse Charm comes with a built-in 5G modem that allows the AI agent to work on the device even without your smartphone’s connection. Though this remains unconfirmed for now.
What is Meta’s Muse Charm?
The Muse Charm is part of the growing category of AI-native devices that many companies are building today. It is a pendant-like handheld device, about the size of an AirPods case, designed to fit on a keychain or inside a pocket. The Charm packs a roughly 2-inch touchscreen with built-in speakers and microphones. You also get a fingerprint sensor.
The purpose of the Meta Muse Charm is for you to access the company’s Muse personal AI assistant. Through the Charm, you can speak to the AI agent and get it to do things for you without needing to open your smartphone.
On the screen, a customisable Muse character appears in a digital avatar, known internally as Jolly. This avatar may remind tech enthusiasts of the Japanese digital pet toy, Tamagotchi, where users would have to take care of a virtual pet in a similarly-sized handheld device.
But 
Muse is far more than just a pet
. It can carry out tasks including online shopping, booking flights and filling in forms. The Muse Charm was first showcased by Mark Zuckerberg during Meta Connect in September, claiming that it was a fast way to get things done with the AI agent. “We’ve packed a lot of technology with this little guy,” he said. “So if you’re not wearing glasses, this is going to be by far the fastest way to talk to your Muse and to show what’s going on around you.”
The Muse Charm marks Meta’s move into dedicated AI hardware as it seeks to build its own consumer AI platform. The company has also released open-source code allowing users to make their own Muse gadgets. Separately, Meta is working on a smartwatch, though The Information reported that its launch has been pushed to 2027 as the company focusses on releasing Muse Charm.
Meta is expected to launch the Muse Charm in December this year. But it is unclear if the device will be available outside the US. Meta is also making Muse the primary interface for its smart glasses, allowing users to access the agent through glasses, phones or the Charm.

At $300, the Muse Charm would cost more than some competing AI wearables in the US. Friend, which allows users to text and speak with an AI persona, sells its wearable for $250 (roughly Rs 23,000). Amazon’s Bee Frontier, which transcribes conversations and tracks tasks, is listed at $75 (roughly Rs 7,000), while its optional subscription costs $99 (roughly Rs 9,200) a year.
Other companies including Nothing and OpenAI are also working on AI wearables, albeit with different plans. 
Nothing’s first AI device is rumoured to be an AI voice recorder
, while 
OpenAI is said to be building a smart speaker
. AI wearables have struggled to reach a broad audience so far, with devices like the 
Humane AI Pin
 and the 
Rabbit R1
 failing to live up to expectations.

キーホルダー型ウェアラブル端末「Muse Charm」（写真=Meta）
Metaが年末に発売するAI端末「Muse Charm」を300ドル（約4万5000円）で投入し、購入者向けにVerizonの5Gデータプランを最大12カ月無料で提供する方針であることが分かった。米The Informationが19日、関係者の話として報じた。
MetaはMuse Charmを通じてハードウェア事業をてこ入れするとともに、AIエージェント「Muse」の利用拡大につなげる考えだ。
報道によると、Muse Charmの購入者にはVerizonの5Gデータプランを無料で付与する。端末を常時ネットワークに接続できるようにし、屋外でもMuseを利用しやすくする狙いがあるという。
マーク・ザッカーバーグCEOは9月の年次開発者会議「Meta Connect」で、Muse Charmを初めて公開した。
MetaはMuse Charmをデバイス戦略の最優先製品と位置付けている。このため、今年の投入を目指していたスマートウォッチの発売は来年に延期した。
AIハードウェアを巡っては、Meta以外の企業も開発を急いでいる。OpenAIはジョニー・アイブ氏のデザイン会社LoveFromと組み、画面を搭載しないスマートスピーカーを来年投入する見通しとされる。Appleも10月、299ドル（約4万4900円）の「HomeView」ディスプレイを発売したほか、ロボット型の家庭向け機器を開発している。
