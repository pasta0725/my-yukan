Harbinger has secured more than $100 million in financing from Erebor Bank and Pinegrove Venture Partners to accelerate production and support its growing commercial vehicle business.
The transactions mark an important step in Harbinger's evolution from an electric vehicle manufacturer to a broader industrial technology platform company reinventing the critical systems the world depends on across mobility, power, defense, autonomy and components. Harbinger develops advanced electrification technologies, including all-electric and hybrid medium-duty vehicle platforms, battery and drivetrain systems, energy storage and auxiliary power solutions, autonomous driving technology and defense applications.
Harbinger is now scaling production and fulfilling major commercial orders, including its 
recent multi-year order for 2,000 all-electric trucks
 from 
FedEx
. Harbinger has added 400 jobs this year, primarily at its Orange County, California headquarters, and plans to continue rapid growth next year, with the goal of creating hundreds of additional jobs around the country.
"Erebor finances companies building the future of American industry—businesses that are making real products, building factories, and scaling U.S. manufacturing," said 
Palmer Luckey
. "Traditional lenders have struggled to support the unique capital needs of businesses developing real technology and scaling domestic jobs. Harbinger is exactly the kind of business we built Erebor to back: an American manufacturer building world-class technology. We're proud to provide the capital to help build more of America's industrial future here at home."
The debt financing comes on the heels of Harbinger's 
recently announced
 Series D funding round, and represents an expansion beyond the equity-driven funding model Harbinger has historically utilized as an emerging technology company. By accessing financing structures more commonly associated with established industrial manufacturers, Harbinger is building a more diversified capital strategy to support long-term growth while demonstrating how a new generation of American manufacturers can finance production at scale.
"Building an industrial technology company at scale requires a different financing approach," said 
John Harris
, Co-Founder and CEO, Harbinger. "The capital needed to ramp production and deliver vehicles to customers shouldn't have to come entirely from equity. Major vehicle and aerospace manufacturers have long relied on debt financing to support their production operations, and we're bringing that same approach to Harbinger. Erebor is now our primary banking partner, and together with Pinegrove, they're helping us build the financial foundation to support our next stage of growth."
Erebor is a nationally chartered bank financing the companies rebuilding American industry. Erebor serves customers across 43 states, including businesses in manufacturing, defense, energy, AI, robotics, and aerospace, industries critical to America's national security and economic growth. Built on modern technology, regulated by the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC), and a member of the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC), Erebor has grown to $8 billion in assets by expanding access to capital for businesses historically underserved by legacy banks.
"Harbinger is building an impressive industrial technology platform, and we're excited to partner with John and the team as they enter their next phase of growth," said 
Jim Ellison
, Head of Credit, Pinegrove. "Our flexible convertible structure was designed around the company's capital needs and provides Harbinger with additional resources to continue scaling production and investing behind the significant opportunity ahead."
Pinegrove operates as an integrated investment platform with over $15B of assets under management across venture fund primaries, venture and growth secondaries, and private credit. Its private credit strategy provides financing solutions to growth-stage companies, including businesses with significant capital requirements. Its participation in this transaction introduces an additional source of flexible financing for Harbinger as the company expands production, builds manufacturing capacity and pursues new commercial opportunities.
About Harbinger
Harbinger is an American industrial technology platform company reinventing the critical systems the world depends on across mobility, power, defense, autonomy and components. The company develops advanced electrification technologies, including all-electric and hybrid medium-duty vehicle platforms, battery and drivetrain systems, energy storage and auxiliary power solutions, autonomous driving technology and defense applications.
Harbinger engineers systems from first principles, using deep vertical integration to design safer, more reliable, more capable and more efficient solutions at economic parity. Its technologies serve a range of applications, including medium-duty trucks, recreational vehicles (RVs), mobile healthcare units, power infrastructure and defense vehicles. Harbinger's mission is to solve the problems industry stopped questioning and raise the standard for what customers should expect.
