Meiji initiated a pilot deployment of ALGO ARTIS’s planning software OPTIUM. OPTIUM is planning software developed by ALGO ARTIS that automates operational planning for blending, sterilization, and fermentation processes in milk beverage manufacturing. The pilot deployment takes place in the market milk product manufacturing lines at Meiji’s Aichi factory.

Background of Meiji’s Pre-Filling Operations
Prior to this pilot, pre-filling processes such as blending, sterilization, and fermentation required veteran planners to balance product-specific rules, tank limits, and cleaning cycles. Meiji and ALGO ARTIS began collaborating on AI-driven production schedule optimization in 2024 to address these workflows.
Automated Monthly Schedule Generation with OPTIUM
The system generates one-month operational plans using AI while accounting for complex constraints across equipment, tanks, and inventory. It accounts for scheduled production quantities, equipment and tank usage rules, and inter-process constraints. The software optimizes equipment operating hours and cleaning frequencies to schedule equipment use efficiently.

Visualization and Planner Adjustments
OPTIUM visualizes the operating status of individual equipment and tank usage across the floor. Human planners review and adjust the generated draft plans before applying them to actual planning workflows.
Shift Reduction and Maintenance Allocation
Comparative simulations against traditional plans show that certain processes can be scheduled with up to 17% fewer operating shifts than under previous staffing plans, 
ALGO ARTIS says
. Operating shifts represent the number of eight-hour time blocks per day that require operator staffing for scheduled equipment runs. Meiji and ALGO ARTIS aim to systematically secure non-operating shifts for equipment maintenance and factory improvement activities. The companies say this system reduces reliance on experienced planners by standardizing complex scheduling workflows.
ALGO ARTIS and Meiji Background
ALGO ARTIS
 was founded in July 2021 and is based in Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo, where Kentaro Nagata serves as Representative Director and President. In June 2026, ALGO ARTIS raised 1.538 billion yen in a Series B funding round to expand its planning software across industries, followed by the September 2026 launch of OPTIUM MPS to support master production scheduling for manufacturers. ALGO ARTIS says it was named to the Forbes Asia 100 To Watch 2026 list in September 2026, and it ranked third among students and second among working professionals in the AtCoder Competitive Programmer Popularity Ranking 2026.
