What happened
On November 10, 2026, Google said it is expanding two Gemini in Gmail features to all Google Workspace users aged 18 and older: AI Overview summaries at the top of long email threads, and non-personalized Help me write drafting. The features are on by default, admins and users can turn them off, and rollout takes up to 15 days from November 10.
What it means for a 10-to-50-person business
For most small businesses this is a small change. Two AI buttons will show up in Gmail, and Google's post lists no price change. Take a 24-person HVAC shop on Google Workspace with 4 office staff: a dispatcher, two customer service reps and a bookkeeper. Their long threads are supplier back-orders, warranty claims and homeowner disputes. A summary at the top of a 30-message thread could save the bookkeeper a few minutes per thread. Summaries can drop details, though, so anyone acting on a part number, price or warranty date should still read the thread. 'Non-personalized' means Help me write drafts from your prompt, not from your past emails, so expect generic wording. Who should look harder: law firms and accounting firms whose client agreements or policies limit AI processing of client email. They should confirm the admin setting before November 25, because the default is on. Who can ignore it: Microsoft 365 shops, and crews who rarely open Gmail.

Finding a correction in the thread. Illustration by MustHave.ai.
A summary is most useful when it catches the correction at the end of a long email thread.
Google began expanding 
two Gemini features in Gmail
 to more eligible work and school accounts on November 10. The change adds summaries of long email threads and a non-personalized version of Help me write. The features serve different tasks. A summary reads an existing conversation, while Help me write turns an instruction into a draft.
Who should see the features?
Google says the expansion covers eligible users aged 18 or older with Gmail smart features enabled. It lists a gradual rollout of up to 15 days for both Rapid Release and Scheduled Release domains. Google lists all Workspace customers in the expansion. Check the Gmail settings for the account you are testing. The rollout is on by default where eligible, subject to the documented admin and user controls.
A thread summary is separate from 
Gmail Search AI Overviews
. The former helps with an already open conversation. Search AI Overviews use a search query and relevant mail. Check the feature you need in the account where you plan to use it.
Try a thread with two conflicting answers
Create a test thread with a date, an attendee list, and a final message correcting both. Use two eligible accounts with the intended settings. Compare the summary to the messages and see whether it reports the final date. Check whether it still mentions people removed later in the thread. Use made-up names and appointments so you can share the test with an administrator.
For Help me write, give both accounts the same instruction for a short reply. Check whether the draft adds an owner, decision, or deadline you did not supply. If the two accounts see different controls, record their settings, age eligibility, and release track before treating the difference as a permanent restriction.

Expanding Gemini in Gmail to more Google Workspace users
Gemini
Gmail

Rapid Release

We’re expanding the availability of some Gemini in Gmail features to more Google Workspace users aged 18 and older. Previously available to consumer accounts and specific Workspace editions, these tools are now being brought to the core Workspace experience by expanding availability to all Workspace users 18 years and older to help users with a school or work account manage their communications more efficiently.
Specifically, eligible users will now have access to:
AI Overview conversation summaries: 
AI Overview at the top of long email threads synthesize key points and replies in the conversation.
Help me write (non-personalized): 
Generate complete email drafts or refine existing text based on simple prompts, such as "Draft a professional follow-up and thank-you for the interview.
Managing an overflowing inbox and composing refined replies can be a significant hurdle for educators, students, and professionals. These features act as an intelligent assistant, removing the friction of manual sorting and drafting.
Getting started
Admins: 
As an admin, you can choose to disable Gemini features in Gmail. Visit the Help Center to learn more about 
managing access to Gemini features in Workspace services
.
End users: 
These features will be ON by default and can be disabled by the user. Visit the Help Center to learn more about 
drafting emails with Gemini in Gmail
 and 
catching up on email threads with AI Overview conversation summaries
Rollout pace
Rapid Release and Scheduled Release domains:
 Gradual rollout (up to 15 days for feature visibility) starting on November 10, 2026
Availability
Available to all Google Workspace customers, Workspace Individual subscribers, and users with personal Google accounts who are 18 years or older and have 
smart features in Gmail enabled
.
