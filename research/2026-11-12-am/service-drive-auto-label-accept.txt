発表

ファイルにラベルが 1 つだけ自動適用されている場合、ラベルを変更できる権限を持つユーザーは、バナーの「Accept (適用)」ボタンをクリックするだけで承認できます。
目次
バナーに「適用」と「編集」を追加
これまで、
Gemini を使ったデータ分類
を含む AI 分類や既定の分類ルールによってラベルが付いたファイルのバナーには「Review」ボタンしか表示されませんでしたが、今回の変更で自動適用されたラベルが 1 つの場合、バナーに「適用」と「編集」の 2 つのボタンが表示されます。
「適用」を選ぶと、ラベルがその場で承認され、ラベルの値を変えたい場合は「編集」を選ぶことでサイドパネルでラベルが開き、そこで内容を変更できます。サイドパネルには新たに「ラベルを適用」ボタンも追加され、バナーを使わずにサイドパネルからも承認できます。
自動適用されたラベルが複数ある場合は、従来どおりバナーに「Review」が表示され、サイドパネルで内容を確認できます。
展開スケジュールと対象プラン
即時リリースドメインおよび計画的リリースドメインの両方で、2026 年 11 月 9 日から最大 15 日間かけて段階的に展開されます。対象プランは以下のとおりです。
Business: Standard、Plus
Enterprise: Starter、Standard、Plus
Education: Standard、Plus
その他: Frontline Starter、Standard、Plus / Enterprise Essentials、Enterprise Essentials Plus / 非営利団体
既定の分類は上記のプランすべてで利用できます。AI 分類は、Enterprise Plus、Frontline Plus、AI Security または Google AI Pro for Education のアドオンが対象です。
この機能に管理者向けの新しい設定はなく、既定の分類または AI 分類を Google ドライブのラベルと組み合わせて使っている組織では有効になります。

Google
 is making it easier for organizations to manage automatically applied classification labels across 
Google Drive
, 
Docs
, 
Sheets
, and 
Slides
. A new update introduces one-click confirmation, allowing eligible users to accept automatically assigned labels directly from a file’s top banner instead of opening the Labels side panel.
Previously
, files receiving classification labels through Google’s AI classification or default classification rules displayed a Review button in the banner. Users had to open the Labels side panel to inspect and confirm the applied classification, adding an extra step to what should be a relatively straightforward process.
With the latest update, Google is 
introducing
 separate 
Accept
 and 
Edit
 buttons when a single classification label is automatically applied to a file. Users with permission to modify the label can click Accept to confirm it immediately, while Edit opens the Labels side panel, where they can review or change the assigned value.
The change is particularly useful for organizations that rely on automated classification to manage sensitive information and maintain consistent document metadata. Instead of navigating through additional menus every time an automated label appears, employees can confirm the suggested classification with a single click.
Image: Google
There is also an option to accept the classification from within the Labels side panel using a newly introduced Accept label button. However, the experience changes when Google automatically applies multiple labels to a file. In those situations, the banner will continue to display Review, allowing users to inspect the different classifications together in the side panel before confirming them.
No new admin settings required
Google says the feature will be enabled by default for organizations already using Drive labels with AI classification or default classification rules. Administrators do not need to enable a separate setting or configure a new control, and end users will not find an additional preference to manage.
The update applies to both Rapid Release and Scheduled Release domains. Google began the gradual rollout on November 9, 2026, with feature visibility potentially taking up to 15 days.
Availability covers Google Workspace Business Standard and Business Plus, Enterprise Starter, Enterprise Standard and Enterprise Plus, as well as Education Standard and Education Plus. Frontline Starter, Standard and Plus, Enterprise Essentials, Enterprise Essentials Plus, and Nonprofits are also included.
There is one important distinction between the two classification systems. Default classification is available across the eligible editions listed above, while AI classification requires Enterprise Plus, Frontline Plus, or an eligible AI Security or Google AI Pro for Education add-on.
This is a relatively small 
Google Workspace
 improvement, but it addresses a practical friction point in document management. Automated classification is only useful when organizations can efficiently review and maintain the labels assigned to their files. Making confirmation a one-click action should help reduce unnecessary interactions while keeping classification metadata accurate across an organization’s shared documents.
