Googleによれば、この「Find Hub」対応のAndroid端末は世界で10億台。経由する他人のAndroid端末からは近くのタグを見つけたことも、誰のものかも分かりません。
同じ部屋のなかなど、ごく近くにある場合は、AppleのAirTagと同じくUWB(超広帯域無線)チップでもっと高精度に、方向や10cm～単位の距離まで分かります。近くにあるはずなのに見当たらない場合、音が聞こえない場合等には便利です。
この機能にはUWB対応スマホ、たとえばPixelならばPro系などハイエンド寄りの機種が必須となっています。
ユーザー交換可能なコイン電池で1年以上駆動することも含め、おおむねAppleのAirTagに近い製品ですが、技術的に面白いのはBluetooth 6.0の新規格『Bluetooth Channel Sounding』(Bluetooth CS)に対応すること。
比較的新しいBluetooth端末が対応する機能で、Bluetoothの電波のみを使い、センチメートル単位で端末との距離を計測する技術。タグを発見しやすくなるはずですが、こちらもBluetooth 6.0が必要なので、対応は最近の端末に限られます。

TL;DR
Google’s Pixel Tag is finally on sale today (November 11), months after the accessory was first unveiled.
The tracker uses the Find Hub network, has an IP67 rating, and delivers precision finding via UWB and Bluetooth Channel Sounding.
The Pixel Tag costs $29, although you can also buy a four-pack for $99.
Google unveiled the 
Pixel Tag
 way back in August alongside the 
Pixel 11
 range. However, the company’s tracker wasn’t actually available to purchase at the time. Thankfully, that changes today as the Pixel Tag is finally on sale.
Google’s first tag (unsurprisingly) uses the 
Find Hub
 crowdsourced network of Android devices for location tracking. The Pixel Tag also brings an IP67 rating for dust and water resistance, UWB and Bluetooth Channel Sounding for more precise tracking, and a replaceable CR2032 battery that supposedly lasts for over a year.
C. Scott Brown / Android Authority
The tag also sports some cross-device functionality. For one, you can press a button on the Pixel Tag to locate your misplaced phone. You can also use your Pixel Watch or Pixel Buds to ring and locate your tag, reducing the need to take out your phone.
It’s worth noting that the Pixel Tag requires an Android device running Android 9.0 or newer as well as Bluetooth 6.0 or higher. The Android requirement shouldn’t be a problem for the vast majority of Android users, but that Bluetooth requirement rules out many budget phones.
The Pixel Tag is now on store shelves and will set you back $29 for one. Google’s tracker is also available in a four-pack for $99, saving you $17 compared to buying four individual tags.
